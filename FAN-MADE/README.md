# FAN-MADE

Original fan fiction set in the world of *Surviving the Game as a Barbarian*. The prose is written from scratch: it borrows the setting (the game *Dungeon and Stone*, Rafdonia, the labyrinth, the "cheat version" played by the other evil spirits) but not the text of the novel.

## Eric Dupont — the dwarf
Eric Dupont, 32, from France, played the original game for 8 years as a dwarf tank (shield and axes). He can reach the 10th floor reliably, but the final corridor before the Abyss Door is lined with instant-death traps. With the cheat version and only its *Experience ×2* toggle, he gains enough levels and essences to survive a trap and reach the door, which teleports whoever reaches it to Rafdonia: that is why nobody has ever reported a clear. He wakes up in Rafdonia in the body of a 20-year-old dwarf.

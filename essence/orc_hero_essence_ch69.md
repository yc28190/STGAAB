## Variants

The three named Orc Heroes drop different colored essences:
- **Danish** — red; contains **Battle Cry**.
- **Belta** — blue; contains **Banner of Struggle**.
- **Tarugas** — green; contains **Gigantification**, the core ability used by Bjorn's shield-Barbarian build.

## Active ability
- **Gigantification** — Increases the user's size, threat level, and physical statistics proportionally.

## Abilities

### Passive
- **Source of Darkness** — The user can resist death while the heart remains intact.

### Active
- **Offering Imprint** — Temporarily increases physical abilities according to the number of creatures marked with the user's blood.
- **Eternal Life** — Temporarily increases regeneration in proportion to decreasing health.
- **Master of Blood** — Direct bloodsucking can temporarily absorb one of the target's skills.

## Notes

The Vampire essence is also associated with guardian-essence mechanics in later chapters.

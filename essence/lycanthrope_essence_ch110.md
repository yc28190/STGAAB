## Abilities

### Passive
- **Blood of the Snowfields** — Multiplies Cold Affinity by 1.5.

### Active
- **Ice Crush** — Deals increased damage to targets affected by Frostbite, and much greater damage to Frozen targets.
- **Indomitable** — Removes incapacitating status effects; consumes a significant amount of Soul Power.

## Synergy

The essence has strong synergy with the Yeti essence, particularly through Cold Affinity and Cold Condensation.

## Documented mechanics

- Rank 3. Primordial Cell reduces the mana cost of transformation-type abilities and increases the highest stat by 1.5x. Soul Dive regenerates Soul Power in proportion to the amount consumed. Blood of Giants greatly increases physical stats and size, but does not work with Unification.

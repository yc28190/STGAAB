## Stats

| Stat | Value |
|---|---:|

| Appetite | +30 |
| Jumping Power | +30 |

## Abilities

### Passive
- **Inheritance** — Permanently increases stats when consuming magic stones. The total bonus obtainable through this skill is capped at 200 stat points.

### Active
- **Leap** — Temporarily increases Jumping Power tenfold and releases a powerful shockwave on landing. For users above 500 kg, the special terrain effect Recoil is also applied to the damage radius. The user's threat level temporarily increases after landing.

## Notes

The monster's signature Random Skill Spam ability is not transferred through the essence.

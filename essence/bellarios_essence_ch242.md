## Description

Bellarios is an Eastern dragon resembling an Imoogi. Its essence is primarily defensive and is heavily focused on Magic Resistance.

## Abilities

### Passive
- **Cycle of Reincarnation** — Allows the user to accumulate stats over time. The ability becomes practically relevant when hunting Grade 3 monsters or higher.

### Active
- **Scales of Greed** — Temporarily manifests scales over the user's skin and increases Magic Resistance in proportion to the user's Magic Resistance stat. At very high Magic Resistance, the ability can contribute toward magic immunity.

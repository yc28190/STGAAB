## Description
Wide plains of green grass dotted with waist-high bushes — the real-world counterpart of the 7th floor's Gray Plains, but far less apocalyptic than the Labyrinth version.

It holds the abandoned Noark Pioneer Settlement and Daylan's Ravine.

## Monsters
- [[Vaiterion]] — grade 3 beast with high physical resistance that uses magic; rare here.

## Mechanics
- Same terrain and monsters as the 7th-floor field (ch. 666).
- Vaiterion's hand-teeth "claws" are a material for the level 8 Immortal Mark (ch. 666–667).
- Heading northwest, the party met monsters once or twice an hour (ch. 667).

## Notable events
- ch. 666–667: The party crosses the plains via the Noark settlement and heads northwest.

## Description
A ghost town on the Grey Plains: flattened ground with log houses laid out in a grid, an artificial spire-like structure of unknown origin, several wells, fields outside a huge wooden palisade that is now half broken, and a small river to the east. The houses are damaged and black bloodstains are everywhere.

It was the village Noark founded after fleeing the city. Their crops barely grew, so they raided the Labyrinth for magic stones and essences, until the Reaper destroyed the village several times and the Noark lord gave up and returned to the city. Ibaekho once lived here.

## Monsters

## Mechanics
- Holds a dimensional stele: several mages pouring synchronized mana into it open a portal lasting about five minutes (ch. 666).
- Only Auril Gavis could make it open a portal into the Labyrinth (ch. 666).

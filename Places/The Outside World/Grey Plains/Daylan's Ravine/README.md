## Description
A wide crack in the ground like an earthquake fissure, nearly 70 m deep, its floor barely touched by light. A small hidden hollow in the cliff served as the hideout where "Daylan" claimed to have lived for about two months.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- A dimensional stele, like the settlement's, stands at the bottom; mana from the Doom Scholar and the GM opened a portal (ch. 668).
- There was no stele on the far side, so there was no way back (ch. 668–669).

## Notable events
- ch. 668: The ravine proves a trap: "Daylan" (whose ID card read Hans, a common name, possibly "L") vanishes as the party crosses the portal.
- ch. 672: Daylan is later found dead as a Bayon in the Star Grave Replica.

## Description
A cave opening on high ground at the end of the underground passage. From it one sees a sunset half hidden behind a mountain range (perhaps the Dragon Mountains), a stream glowing red, birds, and Rafdonia's wall far to the east. Its smooth inner wall bears Korean (Hangul) graffiti scratched with a knife: "Was here / Daehan Geona Ibaekho", and later a crude insult aimed at Rafdonia's king.

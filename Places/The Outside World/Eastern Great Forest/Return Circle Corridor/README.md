## Description
A wide circular corridor, big enough for a giant serpent, running beneath the city but outside its protective circle. One end wall is sealed and carved with unknown patterns — the return magic circle. Heading away from it, the corridor narrows, levels off, widens again and finally slopes upward toward wind and light, emerging at a cave mouth in the forest.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Arrival point of the Noark Plaza circle; it works the same way as the plaza circle but is exposed and easy to damage (ch. 660).
- Restoring it normally takes at least a year (ch. 660); it can be fixed faster with an Eldritch Force Core, a Soulwinder's Mana Core and a Ghoul Road Life Stone (ch. 664).

## Notable events
- ch. 660–661: Bjorn's group arrives through the circle and walks out to the surface.
- ch. 664: The circle is found blown apart by a saboteur (scorch marks like dynamite); the saboteur's body lies in nearby bushes, half melted by strong-acid fruit powder, an ingredient of the royal "Blue Curse".

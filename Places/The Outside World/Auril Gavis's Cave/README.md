# Auril Gavis's Cave

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | cave |
| **Also known as** | Auril Gavis's meaningful place; Crystal-Cave-like cave |
| **First appearance** | ch. 695 |

## Description
A dripping cave outside the walls that strongly resembles the 1st-floor Crystal Cave; the red light of a campfire flickers in one of its passages. Deeper inside lie the Monument Cavern and the Hall of Record Fragments.

It is a place of great personal meaning to Auril Gavis, who refuses to reveal its exact location.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- The Return Scrolls found in the Golden Ruins send their users here, to a "designated location" (ch. 695).
- Located somewhere outside the walls; Auril Gavis will not give the exact spot (ch. 696–697).

## Notable events
- ch. 695–696: Auril Gavis waits by the campfire; he reveals that Aures comes from Ruin Genesis's world and that Briot was a native "guide", warns Ibaekho away from the palace and tells him to seek his answers in the Labyrinth.
- ch. 697–698: Auril shows the party the Record Fragments and sends them back through a monument.

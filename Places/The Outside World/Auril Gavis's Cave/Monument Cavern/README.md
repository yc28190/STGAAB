## Description
About a minute deeper into Auril Gavis's Cave lies a cavern big enough to play soccer in, its walls lined with hundreds of dimensional monuments.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Each monument is a portal; Auril picked the one leading back to "the first place", where the return magic circle had been destroyed (ch. 697–698).

## Notable events
- ch. 697: The party leaves through one of the monuments.

# Hall of Record Fragments

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | hidden chamber (altar) |
| **Also known as** | altar of the Fragments of Records |
| **First appearance** | ch. 697 |

## Description
Behind a stone door that opens at Auril Gavis's touch lies a space that looks like outer space. A straight staircase climbs to an altar where hundreds of Record Stone fragments float behind a thin veil, slowly circling like the stars of a galaxy.

Auril collected all of the fragments himself.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Auril can call a fragment into his hand to read it (ch. 697).
- One fragment tells of the otherworldly evil spirit "———" (Bjorn) losing three companions in a single event before understanding his path; a large inverted-triangle fragment says that on the day Karnon (the Imperial City) burned, he was betrayed by trusted comrades (ch. 697–698).
- No fragment mentions Ibaekho (ch. 698).

## Notable events
- ch. 697–698: Auril reads Bjorn's fragments to the party.

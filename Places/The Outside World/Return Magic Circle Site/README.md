## Description
The outside end of the magic circle linking back to the city, set inside a cave whose entrance is concealed by the teleportation circle, surrounded by forest. Near it stands a gigantic tree that seems to reach the sky; beneath its branches the stars look like the Milky Way. Anabada set up a camp with barracks here.

It is also where the outside world's most terrifying monster eventually arrives: before it came, the wind turned fierce, the sky darkened and huge, apocalyptic storm clouds gathered.

## Monsters
- [The Reaper](../../../Bestiary/reaper_ch665.md) — the outside world's "truly terrifying monster": shackled feet scraping the ground, countless blinking eyeballs, souls writhing in pain around every step.

## Mechanics
- Someone destroyed the circle and killed himself, so returning to the city was impossible (natural recovery estimated at a year); leaving the city still worked (ch. 691, 702).
- Havelion repaired it in a week; Pomplura spores could be replaced by Rapion's Wind Crystals (ch. 704). Opening the portal takes about 3 minutes (ch. 705).
- The great monster has no habitat and eventually appears wherever people go; the more people there are and the longer they stay outside, the likelier it comes. It is what drove Noark back into the city (ch. 703).

## Notable events
- ch. 698–699: Bjorn fights Ibaekho's whole team for more than four hours.
- ch. 700–701: Anabada arrives through the circle and turns the fight; Ibaekho pays 2 billion stones in compensation.
- ch. 702: Bjorn wakes beneath the giant tree by a campfire.
- ch. 703: The first "Anabada Market", a midnight gear-sharing event, is held here.
- ch. 704–705: After two months of waiting, Sven Parav's precognition fires and the clan flees through the repaired portal as the monster arrives.

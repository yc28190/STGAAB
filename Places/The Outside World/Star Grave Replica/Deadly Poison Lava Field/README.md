## Description
A northern field of the Star Grave Replica where pools of green lava boil and thick poison gas hangs in the air like smoke. Small yollam fruit trees grow here and there. The gray boundary cuts the field in half.

The text treats it as one of the fields of the 9th-floor Star Grave.

## Monsters
- [Pophensis](../../../../Bestiary/pophensis_ch674.md)
- Grade 3 monsters; grade 2 in the dangerous northern part.

## Mechanics
- Field effect "Deadly Poison Lava Field": poison resistance fixed at 0, soul (MP) recovery reduced by 99%, explosion warning (ch. 673).
- Everything explodes: the ground swells and bursts into poison lava, monsters swell and blow up mid-fight, and random blasts go off in midair without warning; each sprays poison causing moderate poisoning, so mages and clerics must keep casting Detox and healing (ch. 674).
- Each yollam tree bears exactly three fruits; eating one restores soul energy (ch. 674).
- Skills like [Soul Diving] get around the MP penalty (ch. 674).
- Leaving the field removes its effects, so parties rest outside — but only along the southern edge; the north is cut off and harder (ch. 674).
- Monsters chasing from outside (Bayons) stop at the field boundary (ch. 673).
- A monument like the Primeval Land's stands here, with a different, unreadable script; it is extremely sturdy and its location was recorded (ch. 674).

## Notable events
- ch. 673: The party crosses into the field to escape the Bayon flood.
- ch. 674: They map almost the whole field and find nothing but the monument.
- ch. 688: Ibaekho wakes here after the failed teleport.

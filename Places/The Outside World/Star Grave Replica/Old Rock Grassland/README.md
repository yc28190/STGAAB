## Description
Open grassland in the west of the Star Grave Replica, about four days from the center, cut in half by the gray boundary. Meteors can fall here even mid-fight.

## Monsters
- [Bayon](../../../../Bestiary/bayon_ch556.md) — grade 3 humanoid with dark skin and a black spider clinging to its face (Essence of the Guardian Pianil); source of [Transcendence]; here they are the transformed remains of explorers.

## Mechanics
- In the Labyrinth version this field has a "Spirit Gate"; whether one exists here is unknown (ch. 671).
- Bayon's Parasitic Spider is a level 8 Immortal Mark material (ch. 672).
- After the party killed a group of six Bayons, a "monster flood" of more than 30 closed in from every direction as if targeting them; they stopped chasing at the boundary of the Deadly Poison Lava Field (ch. 673).

## Notable events
- ch. 672: About 70% of the field is searched; human footprints lead to a Bayon with Daylan's face. Legend says Bayon are explorers who died in the Labyrinth.
- ch. 673: The Bayons prove to be Deilan's band of 100+ explorers, who died and turned into monsters months earlier, so the "Hans Deilan" guiding the party was an impostor leading them into a trap. Briot finds a gap to the northeast and the party escapes into the lava field.

## Description
A gray southern field of the Star Grave Replica, reached by boat across the lake and cut in half by the gray boundary. Along the shore, small bone fragments pile up like grains of sand and the ground crunches like gravel; meteor strikes scatter the bone heaps and leave craters.

It is home to the Bone Dragon, one of the infamous "Three Star Graves".

## Mechanics
- Field effect "Yonggol Mountain": status [Dragon's Curse] halves physical resistance and seals one random essence; a sealed essence takes 10 days to reset (ch. 682).
- Monsters appear less than five minutes apart; overall difficulty is moderate except for the Bone Dragon (ch. 682).
- Outside the walls corpses do not vanish, so killing a Bandedemon lets you loot its Numbers item; in the Labyrinth Distortion would be needed for an essence chance (ch. 682).
- Bone Dragon: passive [Dragon Pierce] punishes level gaps (level 5 or lower dies outright, level 6 stun plus soul exhaustion, level 7 rigidity, level 8 stunned 10 s, level 9 paralyzed), repeating over time; passive [Dragon Claw] adds magic damage equal to physical damage dealt; [Bone Breath] (narrow black laser); [Blessing of Dark Mana] (black orbs that fire lasers) (ch. 683–684).
- The Bone Dragon is immune to magic until phase 3; breaking a leg, horn or wing removes 10% of its HP; at the end of phase 1 it casts [Mastery of Magic], making magic in range ten times stronger (ch. 683–684).
- Rewards: dragon bones, dragon heart, achievements (ch. 684).

## Notable events

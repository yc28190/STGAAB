## Description
The central forest of the Star Grave Replica and the only field not cut by the gray boundary. Its climate changes constantly and is always harsh — one spell was a heatwave so extreme that raw flesh smoked and creatures burst into flame — and meteor showers fall periodically.

It connects to every other field and holds the Central Monument, near where the party first arrived.

## Mechanics
- In the Labyrinth version, the Primeval Dragon can be summoned here under certain conditions (ch. 671).
- As the hub linking all fields, it served as the rally point for the scattered party (ch. 685).
- Flame Orb fire resistance protects against the heatwaves (ch. 686).
- Its monument is the reference for the one in the Deadly Poison Lava Field (ch. 674).

## Notable events
- ch. 669–671: The party arrives near the central monument.
- ch. 686: A burnt, torn cloth, apparently hung by Briot, tells survivors to go to the monument.
- ch. 687: Briot's "corpse" is found here — a fake.

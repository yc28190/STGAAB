## Description
Reached by climbing the seemingly endless stairs back up for more than 30 minutes, then following a narrow passage about 10 m long to where the magical darkness lifts at a stone gate. Behind it lies a square, enclosed room covered in sinister murals, with a golden coffin about 3 m long at its center.

It reproduces the hidden piece of the Golden Ruins rift.

## Monsters
- [Makairo](../../../../../../Bestiary/makairo_ch688.md) — grade 4 mid-boss; found already dead.

## Mechanics
- In the original rift, climbing back up the entrance stairs reveals this chamber; opening the coffin summons Makairo, and beating him gives a battle buff until leaving the rift and a rare chance at a Numbers item (ch. 688).
- Here the stone gate — durable in the game — broke in one blow, and the coffin held only a bleached skeleton with no mana left, meaning it had been dead 4,000+ years or was drained by black magic or Karui (ch. 688).
- No. 777 Makairo's Storage Box (mystery box) lay in a corner; it produced No.???? Aegis Dragon Armor (ch. 688–689).

## Notable events
- ch. 688–689: The party finds the dead Makairo and opens the storage box, obtaining the Aegis Dragon Armor.
- ch. 690–691: The corpse vanishes from the coffin, pointing to a hidden intruder.
- ch. 691–692: Ibaekho tests his comrades with No. 7234 Broken Trust.

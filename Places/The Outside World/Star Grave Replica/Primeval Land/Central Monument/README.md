## Description
The familiar monument lies buried in a crater at the heart of the Primeval Land. Later a new structure appears to its left: what looks like a temple doorway standing on bare ground, with a staircase descending into darkness. The top line of the monument's undecipherable hieroglyphs begins to glow red, and the structure radiates the aura of an "ancient existence".

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- The entrance was not there before (ch. 686–687).
- Descending the stairs cuts off the way back: a magic-engineered rope tied outside was severed, and after 213 steps down, more than 240 steps up never reach the exit (ch. 687).
- The effect matches the entrance of the 4th-floor rift Golden Ruins (ch. 687).

## Notable events
- ch. 686–687: The party waits 3 days, then 3 more, then 1 more for stragglers before going in.

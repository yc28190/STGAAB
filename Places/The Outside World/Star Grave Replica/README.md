## Description
A sealed land outside the walls that copies the 9th floor, Star Grave. It is dark like the Labyrinth — light does not carry far and the sun had not risen by 9:18 a.m. — and covered by a forest pocked with huge craters. Storms of every kind sweep it: lightning, downpours, blizzards, gales, hail, earthquakes, hurricanes and floods that raise the water level. The gray boundary surrounds it completely and cuts through its outer fields.

It contains the same five fields as the 9th floor: the Primeval Land at the center, the Old Rock Grassland (west), the Deadly Poison Lava Field (north), the Dreamlike Waterfall (east) and Dragon Bone Mountain (south).

## Mechanics
- Reachable only through the portal in Daylan's Ravine (ch. 668–669).
- Not inside the Labyrinth: monsters are intelligent, bodies remain, and the Labyrinth was sealed at the time (ch. 670, 672). Killing monsters still gives EXP (ch. 670, 672).
- Field effect: a red light marks a circle about 50 m wide before a meteor strikes; anything within 5 m of the center dies instantly and the edges take fixed damage; lightning then strikes the crater and spawns a Gigazerros (ch. 670–671).
- Aggro barely works because monsters learn to target the damage dealers (ch. 670–671).
- Fully enclosed by the gray boundary with no physical way out; mapping the edge took 17 days and looped back to the start. The party searched for a dimensional stele or a Spirit Gate (ch. 671–672).
- A large monument with unreadable pictographs like the Crystal Cave one stands near the arrival cave; it survived [Star's Extinction] unscratched, and a pocket dimension violently rejects it (ch. 669, 671).

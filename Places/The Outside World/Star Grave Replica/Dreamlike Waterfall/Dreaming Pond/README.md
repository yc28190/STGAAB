## Description
The area surrounding the central waterfall of the Dreamlike Waterfall — the home ground of the special boss Dryzen.

## Monsters
- [Dryzen](../../../../../Bestiary/dryzen_ch674.md) — grade 1 special boss; effectively unkillable here.

## Mechanics
- Within the [Dreaming Pond] range, every harmful effect and all damage aimed at Dryzen are nullified (ch. 674).

## Notable events
- ch. 674: The ship flees Dryzen at full propulsion; the noise wakes the dormant monsters.

## Description
A calm lake that glows as though it held the Milky Way, its waves glittering with starlight. In the middle, the water pours into an enormous hole, forming a giant waterfall like the edge of the world in old myths. The ash boundary cuts across the falls and freezes two-thirds of it. Near the shore the lakebed is muck that swallows the feet to the ankles.

It is the eastern field of the Star Grave and its final trial, since the portal to the 10th floor lies beyond it.

## Monsters
- [Dryzen](../../../../Bestiary/dryzen_ch674.md) — grade 1 illusion-type special boss, found only near the waterfall; swims up the falls.
- [Hoppfish](../../../../Bestiary/hoppfish_ch685.md) — grade 5; lives only here and in the 6th-floor sea.
- Sleeping underwater monsters.

## Mechanics
- Field effect "Dreamlike Waterfall": characters fall asleep at random intervals and must endure visions alone, much like Kariadaea's [Soul Extraction] at Ice Rock — but allies can wake a sleeper from outside, and rare wake-up items exist in the field (ch. 674).
- The lake is crossed by boat; speed or noise wakes the monsters sleeping underwater, and only a specially built boat can cross both quietly and quickly (ch. 674).
- The current always flows toward the central waterfall, so walking against it leads to land (ch. 685).
- The portal to the 10th floor lies beyond the waterfall (ch. 674).
- Inside the [Dreaming Pond] range, all harm and damage against Dryzen are nullified (ch. 674).

## Description
A dark cave with a soggy mud floor, as if after a rain-soaked landslide; water lines on the walls and ceiling show that it floods from time to time. It is the arrival point of the portal from Daylan's Ravine.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Portal arrival point; there is no stele here, so no way back (ch. 669).
- The mud does not slow movement and the walls sound solid; the exit is about 20 minutes' walk (ch. 669).

## Notable events
- ch. 669: Bjorn takes command of the party here and leads them out in single file.

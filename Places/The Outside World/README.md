## Description
The land beyond the walls of Rafdonia: living nature with sunlight, forests, mountains, valleys and running streams, birds singing, insects eating leaves, worms in damp soil and wild animals — and no walls anywhere. The night sky looks the same as anywhere else. Far to the east, everything past the coast has turned gray.

For thousands of years it was believed that no one could survive outside the Protective Magic Circle. Noark's exiles became the first to leave, and the faction later settled there. Ibaekho's maps show that the Dark Continent of the 7th floor matches the southeast of the real continent, while the north and west remain unknown; the outside also holds a sealed replica of the 9th floor.

## Mechanics
- Outside the Protective Magic Circle anyone was believed to die of the Witch's Poison within a day; in the game it was blocked by the system (ch. 193).
- Stepping out triggers "[Character has entered a forbidden area]" (ch. 661).
- Reachable through the city's underground tunnels (ch. 501); Noark opened a portal outside the walls that leads directly to the 7th floor (ch. 342).
- Farming is possible, so people can live without magic stones (ch. 544).
- Outside monsters are few (none near Rafdonia) but very intelligent: they use terrain, ambush, test opponents, retreat, and leaders command groups; individuals vary in size and scars. Aggro barely works on them and kills must be confirmed (ch. 664, 667, 671).
- Their bodies remain when killed and always drop byproducts; hunted under Distortion magic they can also give essences and mana stones (ch. 664, 667).
- The Reaper behaves like a floor lord with spawn conditions; it wrecked Noark's settlement several times (ch. 665).
- Dimensional steles act as portals between distant points (ch. 665–666).
- Unknown whether kills here count for Affectionate Weapon stacks (ch. 785).

## Notable events
- ch. 256: On 2 June, year 154, the Noark exiles become the first people in thousands of years to leave the city, rescued by a mysterious wizard.
- ch. 258–261: Noark spreads the news; Clown confirms it at the Round Table and the rumor spreads through Rafdonia.
- ch. 370: Clown reports the land is exactly like the Dark Continent.
- ch. 501–571: Lee Baekho goes out with Noark people to search for Auril Gavis, who lives there.
- ch. 656–667: Bjorn ventures outside; Ibaekho shares his maps and warns that "no one can live outside the walls".
- ch. 784, 812: On his last trip outside Bjorn obtained two Phase 8 engraving materials and the [Aegis Dragon Scale] armor.

## Description
Beyond the cliffs at the eastern edge of the continent, past District 10, the sea, sky, waves and clouds have lost all color. Everything looks frozen in place like a black-and-white photograph; only the rising sun is still red. The sight is deeply eerie. The same ash-gray boundary also cuts across the Star Grave Replica, freezing two-thirds of the Dreamlike Waterfall.

The boundary is evidence that the world is dying in a way unrelated to the "witch's poison".

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Anything that touches the boundary instantly loses its color and hardens like stone; contact is believed to mean instant death (ch. 662).
- It is slowly spreading and at that rate would reach the District 10 walls in about ten years (ch. 662, 671) — though Ibaekho's markers suggest it creeps in and out like a tide (ch. 691) (conflicting accounts).
- It surrounds and cuts through the Star Grave Replica (ch. 671–674).
- A Return Scroll landing inside it was considered the worst-case outcome (ch. 695).

## Notable events
- ch. 661–662: Bjorn and the GM see the gray sea; the GM remarks that the world has never really been whole.
- ch. 665: Ibaekho says it is not that dangerous.
- ch. 691: Ibaekho goes outside to test whether it is spreading and marks several spots.

## Description
A windowless mansion of hallways and rooms. Bjorn arrives in a study that smells of wood (on his second visit a white mask hangs there for him). There is a separate tea room, a main hall on the first floor, and a round-table room with dozens of seats — nearly identical to the later Round Table, with the same edge patterns and strange paintings, but smaller and at first without a truth jewel. Visitors appear according to their self-image: the Ruin Scholar is a child and Bjorn appears as Lee Hansu.

It was Auril Gavis's secret meeting place, the forerunner of the Round Table; after ownership passed to the new GM it was reshaped into the Ghostbusters community.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Entry by invitation only; meetings are held at midnight on the 15th, and only seconds pass in the real world (ch. 289–291, 311).
- The owner can create objects and drinks (Sprite, Coke) — until he spent his authority on the jewel; afterwards they taste bland or of nothing (ch. 307, 309, 333).
- The jewel glows green for truth and red for lies; it became the Round Table rule of sharing information that more than half the members do not know (ch. 307–309).
- Ownership passed to the GM; Gavis "created it but didn't create it", having had an accomplice (ch. 308–309).
- Royal family interference shut it down for months (ch. 315–316, 327).
- Gavis's protective buff does not work here because the mind is exposed (ch. 327).
- The GM can banish members and shut down the server (ch. 332–334).

## Notable events
- ch. 289–291: Bjorn is invited and meets the members: Master Auril Gavis (over 300 years old), Orculus captain Ricardo Liuhen Praha, the Ruin Scholar Belvev Ruinjenes, Kagureas (Dragonkin, holder of two Genesis Artifacts) and Rabi.
- ch. 308–311: Information shared: the Earth Witch is alive, "where everyone's hopes lie"; a portal lies in the royal palace basement; the Labyrinth runs on life force; Regal Vagos killed the Ancient Dragon; a royal envoy visited Noark's Lord.
- ch. 332–334: The GM banishes the old members; Bjorn, posing as the Master, designs the Ghostbusters layout for the new GM (Oliver Wiseman = Yurven Habelion) — computer interface, marketplace, letters with pills, password and spy filtering.

## Description
A dim field shrouded in fog and lit by a single bonfire. As the vision ends, a cold wind distorts the scenery. Its true location is unknown.

Here the Earth Witch, Ellis Groundia, appears to Bjorn as a pale, ghostly girl.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Reached by Bjorn alone when he stepped into the fireplace of the Witch's Cabin after earning the Riakis achievement (ch. 464).
- The system reports an error, cannot identify the character's location and suspends log transmission until he returns; his teammates saw nothing (ch. 464).

## Notable events
- ch. 464: Ellis Groundia introduces herself telepathically and warns him that the Gate of the Abyss must not be opened.
- ch. 472: Bjorn uses this warning at the Round Table.

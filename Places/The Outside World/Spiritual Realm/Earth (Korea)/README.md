## Description
Bjorn's home world, modern-day Korea, where he lived as Lee Hansu. It appears in three ways: in the real-world aftermath of his disappearance (his Seoul studio apartment and a police station), in his fading memories (a pediatric ward, the amusement park where he was abandoned, a lonely room and a cockroach-infested basement), and in an extended vision after he falls asleep at the Dreamlike Waterfall, where he lives again in Lee Hansu's ordinary body.

Whether that vision was a dream or a real parallel world is left uncertain; Auril Gavis insists it is real.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- An unplugged monitor in Lee Hansu's room keeps showing Bjorn's in-game system messages (ch. 89); in the vision a police monitor with no cables shows his live game logs and later vanishes from the evidence room (ch. 677–678).
- An unnamed organization run by an old man (with a female aide) recovers the monitor, erases police memories, regards [Dungeon and Stone] as "Auril Gabis's legacy" and watches Bjorn's action log on a DOS screen (ch. 93).
- Entered in the vision by falling asleep in the Dreamlike Waterfall; the Earth Witch (Elise/Alice Groundia) appears as he sinks, offering to grant his dream and telling him not to come back (ch. 675).
- Auril Gavis says the world is real, has no "cheat mode", and only a sincere wish lets one return; the way out is to clear Dungeon and Stone again with twenty barbarian characters and press YES at the Gate of the Abyss (ch. 678–679).

## Notable events
- ch. 89–93: Detectives puzzle over Lee Hansu's disappearance (CCTV shows him entering but never leaving, windows locked, clothes in his bed; similar cases abroad).
- ch. 205–222: Bjorn's memories of home fade; he recalls a childhood in hospital and compares the plaza to Gwanghwamun Plaza.
- ch. 500–501: Lee Baekho wants to go back to Earth; Bjorn does not.
- ch. 675–679: The dream-Korea episode: arrest, psychiatric hospital, Auril Gavis's visit, and over 60 days playing the game on five laptops until he reaches the Gate of the Abyss; Hyunbyul, his mother and Team Leader Kang appear as kind versions of themselves.
- ch. 722–763: Memories of the Stone Eben forum, where "Elfnuna" posted guides, the New York financial district and his past with Hyeonbyeol.

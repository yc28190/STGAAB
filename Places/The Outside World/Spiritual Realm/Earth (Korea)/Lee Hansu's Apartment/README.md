## Description
Lee Hansu's apartment in the dream of Korea, behind a door with a keypad lock. At first it looks raided: books knocked over beside the computer desk, the monitor gone, wardrobe and drawers open with clothes on the floor, the living room ransacked, and a fridge switched off so long that it stinks. It contrasts with the neat copy of the room in the Ghostbusters community. Later his mother tidies it and lives there with him.

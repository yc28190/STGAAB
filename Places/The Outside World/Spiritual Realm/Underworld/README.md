## Description
Three green moons hang split in the sky, so close and sharp that it feels like being in outer space; seen up close, the moons are made of writhing corpses. Their green light falls over an endless, bleak, dry plain with nowhere to hide. Green magma bursts from cracks, green gas spouts from the ground and the groans of zombies form a constant background. When the symbol holder dies, the horizon begins to crumble and the whole green world collapses.

It is a temporary realm of the dead conjured by the Staff of the Underworld, used by the Corpse Collector in his final fight against Bjorn.

## Mechanics
- Created by the Staff of the Underworld (Double Number No. 17, the necromancer's graduation weapon) through "Call of the Underworld", which has four options; it targets the caster and one designated target, and outsiders cannot interfere (ch. 733).
- Bjorn's [Aegis Barrier] made him the target in place of Vivian (ch. 733).
- Field effects: non-living beings get +200% physical stats, living beings -20%; summoned undead revive after a short time and a destroyed mount takes hours to return (ch. 734).
- The symbol holder counts as non-living and cannot die, though counters exist; the green gas curses the living and strengthens the symbol holder's blessing (ch. 734).
- Hidden pieces: striking odd bumps in the ground raises resistance; killing an Underworld Fire grants temporary dark resistance (ch. 734).
- The Underworld King asks the living why they entered the immortal land and slams his palm down, but misses anyone hiding inside the green gas, then leaves (ch. 734).
- Lasts up to 24 hours; if nobody dies, everyone returns to where they were; if the symbol holder dies, it collapses and sends survivors back to Rafdonia (ch. 734, 738).

## Notable events
- ch. 733–734: The Corpse Collector opens the Underworld in burning District 8 to escape Astarotta; Bjorn takes Vivian's place as target.
- ch. 734–735: Bjorn chases him across the plain for just over an hour and catches him with Aegis Dragon Armor's spectral warrior.
- ch. 735–738: Bjorn reveals he was the Lion Mask, takes the dying man's subspace, hears his real name (Jester Arlecchino) and story, then kills him; he spent about two hours inside.
- ch. 753: Astarotta later refers to it as the Netherworld.

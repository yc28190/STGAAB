## Description
A private chat room in the Ghostbusters community styled as a luxurious noble's mansion, furnished to its owner Lee Baekho's taste: a crackling fireplace with sofas in front of it, and a mirror. Nicknames float above the heads of those present. Hyunbyeol later likes to lie on one of the sofas and lend out books.

It is the meeting place of the Korean players — Bjorn, Lee Baekho and Kang Hyunbyul.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- The owner tests visitors with a Korean word-association quiz (ch. 124).
- Entry can be temporarily restricted with a countdown; the room list shows how many users are online (ch. 196, 368, 370).
- A newcomer cannot leave without knowing how (ch. 372).
- Abilities are sealed, so lie detection does not work, but killing intent can still be projected because souls connect and amplify aura; visitors can even fall asleep there (Bjorn slept 1.5 hours) (ch. 437–438).
- Lee Baekho's early-version pill limits him to about an hour (ch. 123, 470).

- ch. 370–372: Bjorn reunites with his ex-girlfriend Kang Hyunbyul (HS123).

- ch. 435–438: Lee Baekho guesses Bjorn is Bjorn Yandel, refuses and then agrees to return Misha; they exchange killing intent. Hyunbyul is revealed to work for Viscountess Ragna Peprok.
- ch. 469–470: Baekho reveals he blackmailed the Marquis, whose son is a player, over the evil-spirit announcement and the integration policy.
- ch. 499–501: Baekho explains the Record Stone and admits he knew Bjorn was "Elfnunna".
- ch. 544: Baekho reports from outside the walls.
- ch. 570: Bjorn confronts Ibaekho about the Resurrection Stone.

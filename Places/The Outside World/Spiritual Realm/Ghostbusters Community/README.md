## Description
A spiritual space that newcomers first experience as an endless pure-white void like outer space, with no up or down — only an old computer showing a black DOS screen with a password prompt. Players appear in their original Earth bodies and clothes (Bjorn as Lee Hansu in a navy suit), and moving at first feels like piloting a robot. Each member wakes in a personal "mindscape" room built from the place they feel safest: Bjorn's is Lee Hansu's Seoul studio apartment with a bed, a mirrored wardrobe, a computer desk and a fridge of tasteless cola; Versil's is a dim room with wooden blinds and a cheap mattress; Parab's is a hotel-suite-like room. Chat rooms carry their own scenes, such as a bare clearing with a campfire for newcomers, a book-scented study, or a noble's mansion hall; a hallway leads to the Round Table room. Later it took on the look of a web forum, with a free board, inbox and logout window.

It was a secret gathering place for players from Earth ("evil spirits") trapped in the game world, where they traded information and items and shared memories of home. Founded by "Ghost master" (Elfnunalove, the Magitechnician Yurven Habelion), it ran on a jewel given to him by Auril Gavis and was a modification of an older space — Auril Gavis's spiritual domain. After more than 20 years of use it was shut down for good when Auril Gavis revoked the authority he had lent the GM.

## Mechanics
- Entry by taking the pill (GB-027) sent with an English invitation letter; the letter's text vanishes after about 10 minutes, and letters go to explorers suspected of being players (ch. 75–76, 90).
- Members are summoned at midnight on the 15th of each month while their bodies sleep ("the character's soul resonates and is drawn to a specific world"); a session lasts up to 12 hours, and logging out early is possible (ch. 76, 90, 92, 123, 343).
- Time ratio: about one hour inside per second outside (ch. 175, 387–389); an early account gave 1 Earth month to about 5 months (ch. 92) (conflicting accounts). Members' bodies lie defenceless during the session (ch. 387–389).
- The password ("Rafdonia's king, Mother fucker", from the letter's P.S.) is a bluff; the guide SoulQueens verifies newcomers by their mindscape, clothes and manner (ch. 90–91).
- Members are anonymous soul forms; nicknames are permanent and shown by willing it. Non-players who sneaked in were once killed by separating soul from body, later only banned from summoning (ch. 76, 86, 91).
- Thoughts and killing intent have real force because minds are connected (ch. 91, 125–126).
- The computer offers a forum/free board (hot posts with GP rewards), announcements, an inbox and private messages, an Exchange for information and items paid in Ghost Points (100,000 GP ≈ 2 million stones), and chat rooms by race, class, country, age or rank — some locked by password or rank. Ranks rise with activity (Deadman → Goblin after 10 trades) (ch. 91–93, 123, 156, 544).
- Default outfits reflect self-image; other clothes cost GP (ch. 371). Messages can only be read on one's room computer (ch. 372).
- Early-version pills limit some users to one hour; room owners can lock chat rooms and admins can ban users (ch. 196, 344).
- The GM modded the jewel's circuits to see nicknames in anonymous rooms with admin rights and tried to log locations; current functions cannot hand players over to the royal family (ch. 347, 370).
- Players dying in reality could post their last moments there (ch. 175).
- Closure: the device lost power after Auril Gavis revoked the GM's authority; an urgent notice told users to log out themselves, warning that forced disconnection could cause "unforeseen problems" (ch. 572, 575, 579).

- ch. 90–93: First visit; Bjorn registers as "Elfnunna", sells the 1st-floor Floor Master strategy (70,000 GP) and Skull Island information (69,000 GP), and learns the dimensional-collapse rumor was planted by the Tarutein school. Ghost master turns out to be searching for Elfnuna — Bjorn himself.
- ch. 123–135: Bjorn explores the race, class and country chat rooms; hidden-piece information spreads here.
- ch. 155–175: Lee Baekho tells Bjorn about Noark and warns him off the subjugation; Bjorn reads the dying Canadian player Mac Davis's thread.
- ch. 196–200: Lee Baekho is banned; his room "Long Live Korean Independence" remains.
- ch. 236: After the Dimensional Plaza ambush, activity drops to about a third.
- ch. 343–372: The GM's identity as Yurven Habelion and the royal family's knowledge of it come to light.
- ch. 387–389: The Marquis exploits a session to test Bjorn; Bjorn logs out after 20 minutes (0.3 s real time) to catch Eltora's intrusion.
- ch. 569–579: A GM notice announces closure with GP refunds; the board floods with panic; Hyunbyeol messages Bjorn "See you outside"; the server shuts down for good. Auril Gavis says players had grown too comfortable to seek home; Bjorn suspects the aim was to cut off Ibaekho.
- ch. 582: Versil passes on the rumor that the evil spirits' assembly has been disbanded.
- ch. 792–795: Recalled as the place where Bjorn once sold Skull Island boss information.

## Description
Bjorn's personal space in the Ghostbusters community: a recreation of his old dark, gloomy studio in Korea, with a soft bed pushed close to the computer desk, a mirror, and a PC that turns on instantly with no boot time. Over time it begins to feel small and claustrophobic to him.

It is where he wakes each month when the community opens, and from its computer he browses the forum and exchange, reads messages and enters chat rooms before going on to the Round Table.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Arrival point when the soul is "drawn to a specific world" each month (on the 15th); the community is reached by logging in on the computer (ch. 123, 435).
- About 12 hours inside equal 12 seconds in reality; the Round Table opens at 3:00. The body stays helpless during those seconds, which the GM tries to exploit (ch. 258).
- Messages and the inbox are read on the room's computer; chat rooms are entered by password (ch. 368, 578).
- Logging out sends him back ("Character is being transferred to Lafdonia") (ch. 441).

## Notable events
- ch. 258: A Noark poster reveals on the forum that the outside world is safe.
- ch. 342: Bjorn reads 2.5 years of forum posts here after his return.
- ch. 578: After the last Round Table, Bjorn reads Hyunbyeol's (HS123) farewell message here.

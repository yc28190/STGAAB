# Customization Room

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | room (spiritual space) |
| **Also known as** | dressing room |
| **First appearance** | ch. 571 |

## Description
A dressing room in the Ghostbusters community whose walls are lined with dozens of costumes and even more masks. A red-carpeted hallway leads from it to the Round Table room.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Members put on their Round Table disguise here; a newcomer instinctively picks the mask closest to their own "fate" — for Bjorn, a navy suit and a male lion mask (ch. 571).

## Notable events
- ch. 571: Bjorn idly imagines masks for his comrades, picturing Misha with a scarred dog mask.

## Description
Members first pass through a customization room whose walls are hung with clothes, accessories and a whole wall of masks. A door opens onto a long red carpet (or a quiet hallway with a small reception room of facing sofas) leading through a thick wooden door into a hall with a large round table of dozens of seats, a clock on the wall and a jewel set at the table's center. Four doors lead into the hall. An early version, seen twenty years in the past, looks like a medieval noble's office with a full-length mirror and no costumes. When the gathering finally ended, the hall stood empty and echoing, its seats and jewel gone.

The Watchers of the Round Table is a secret, masked club of veteran players inside the Ghostbusters community, created by its "Master" (nickname 0720) — later revealed to be Auril Gavis — to trade high-value information anonymously. Bjorn attended as "Lion". It ended with the closure of the community.

## Mechanics
- Entry requires an invitation code (codes expire after a month, except one the Master gave Lee Baekho); the room opens at 03:00 and the doors slam shut at 03:10, starting the meeting (ch. 124–125, 196–197, 342–343).
- Masks are mandatory and anonymity is guaranteed; a newcomer's mask reflects their "fate" (ch. 124, 158).
- Members share one piece of information each in turn, clockwise (arranged so Lion speaks last); after each round they decide whether to continue (ch. 125–126, 343, 369).
- The jewel glows green when the speaker sincerely believes the information and fewer than half of those present know it, red otherwise; it judges sincerity rather than truth, so misinformation can pass, and a red speaker may retry (ch. 125, 198, 238, 344, 439).
- Lies are allowed only before the meeting starts; afterward a lie breaks the speaker's mask and gets them banned (ch. 124).
- Too few attendees ends the session ("Minimum number of participants not met"); members who drop out lower the majority threshold (ch. 175, 345, 370).
- [Whisper] telepathy works only in this space; killing intent hits dozens of times harder and can cause "mental contamination" (ch. 125–126, 199, 237).
- The Master can appear after the door closes and vanish like a hologram (ch. 196–197).
- Unwritten etiquette: past sessions are not repeated to absentees (ch. 439, 471).
- At the end Auril Gavis reclaimed the jewel and full authority over the space, conjuring objects at will (ch. 575–578).

- ch. 196–200: The GM flags the Master's nickname "0720"; Clown is exposed as the Corpse Collector (ch. 238); Crescent Moon is presumed dead and the Queen (SoulQueens) joins.
- ch. 258–259: Clown confirms the outside world is safe; Goblin exposes Bjorn as the oracle bearer; Lion says the Stone of Resurrection can revive two people.
- ch. 288: Twenty years in the past, Bjorn is pulled into an early version of the space.
- ch. 342–345: Lion returns after 2.5 years, reveals the GM's name and forces the Queen to submit; he later proves he joined 22 years earlier and claims to have met Auril Gavis (ch. 369–370).
- ch. 397–430: Goblin is identified as Sven Parab and Fox as Bersil Gowland.
- ch. 440–441: Revelations: the Master is Auril Gavis; Noark plans to invade; Ragna is Marquis Terserion's illegitimate daughter; Elfnunna is in this world.
- ch. 471–472: Wolf, Black and Butterfly join; claims about royal summoning of players, deliberate dimensional collapses and two portals in the palace basement; Lion says he met the Earth Witch.
- ch. 501–502: Ten members; Gavrilius built the Stone of Honor; Noark killed the 8th-floor Lord.
- ch. 545: Bjorn leaks B1 information to undermine the royal ban.
- ch. 571–576: Final meeting: Crescent Moon and Deer Antlers walk out; Bjorn names the Jester as Abet Nekrafetto; Auril Gavis enters (the room shakes), unmasks everyone but Bjorn and runs an OX quiz with a trip home as the prize.
- ch. 577–578: Fox, Butterfly, Goblin and Queen quit, so Wolf wins; Gavis speaks alone with Bjorn and warns him away from Ibaekho; Bjorn leaves his mask on the table.
- ch. 647–660: Black Mask is revealed as Kang Hyunbyul; Wolf was probably Gavis's agent and Butterfly possibly Ibaekho's informant.
- ch. 736–792: Recollections: the Jester once asked to serve the Lion and was refused; Bjorn met Versil and Beleg (Crescent Moon) here and spread Skull Island information.

## Description
A luxurious, cozy study with an antique atmosphere and a faint smell of books, decorated by SoulQueens, with sofas facing one another. In its later form it is a fairly roomy study with no doors or windows, reachable only by magic — and with a sofa that collapsed under Bjorn's weight.

It is the GM's (Elfnunalove / Yurven Habelion's) private meeting room inside the Ghostbusters community.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- A secret chat room entered by name and password sent by message from "Ghost master"; the password once given was 0720 (ch. 368, 469).
- The room's user count is visible; Bjorn caught Hyunbyul joining when it rose from 2 to 3 (ch. 388).
- The later hidden study is reached through winding alleys: the third door beside a red-roofed building, stairs down, then a magic circle like those leading into secret markets (ch. 660).

## Notable events
- ch. 368: Bjorn threatens the GM, giving him a three-month deadline to unban Lee Baekho.
- ch. 372: The GM and SoulQueens recruit Hyunbyul to spy on "Elfnunna".
- ch. 388: The GM and SoulQueens meet Black Star here.
- ch. 469: The GM asks whether Lion is "Elfnunna" (Bjorn denies it) and mentions Auril Gavis's story of the Record Stone.
- ch. 660: Bjorn and the GM meet here like Cold War spies.

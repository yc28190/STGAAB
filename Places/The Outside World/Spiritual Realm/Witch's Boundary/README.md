## Description
A space of total darkness in which light slowly spreads over a horizon like dawn while a fierce wind blows without end. With a cracking sound, a boundary forms between the darkness and the light.

It is the transitional space between the dream of Korea and Bjorn's return to the game world.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Entered after pressing YES at the Gate of the Abyss in the dream; every step Bjorn takes forward pushes the darkness back (ch. 680).
- The witch fades into the darkness and he wakes on the ship (ch. 680).

## Notable events
- ch. 680: The Earth Witch (Alice Groundia) tells Bjorn that he chose to live as Bjorn Yandel, that he will suffer more and lose something precious, and that he is no different from "us".

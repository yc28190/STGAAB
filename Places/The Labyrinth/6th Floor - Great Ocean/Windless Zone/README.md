# Windless Zone

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | sea zone |
| **Also known as** | No Wind Zone |
| **First appearance** | ch. 265 |

## Description
A special stretch of the Great Ocean in dead calm: there is no wind and almost no current, so sailing ships cannot move through it on their own.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Too dangerous for a small rowboat; it requires a larger vessel such as Partslan's boat (ch. 265).
- Crossing requires a mana propulsion device fed with magic stones (ch. 379, 524).
- The party's crossing took 2 days (ch. 379).

## Notable events
- ch. 379: The party fits three small propulsion devices taken from captured ships and crosses the zone in two days.

## Description
A tiny island with no monsters, dominated by three strange pillars that seem larger than the island itself, arranged in a triangle around a small central altar. The pillars are normally black but glow blue inside the Memory of the End. Explorers and mages have long studied it as one of the labyrinth's mysteries.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- The closest island to Catacoma (Skull Island) (ch. 799).
- In the Memory of the End, spilling blood on the altar makes glowing, incomprehensible symbols appear on the pillars while unstable magic sparks into a portal to Arta's Secret Base (ch. 799).
- Sailing from the apocalypse island took 4 h 40 min with Auyen's buffs ([Tailwind], [Trade Route], [Ghost Ship]) and 3 h 50 min back (ch. 799, 808).

## Notable events
- ch. 799: Bjorn's group opens the portal to Arta's Secret Base.
- ch. 808: The group sails back.

# Easternmost Sea

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | sea region |
| **Also known as** | eastern sea; icy waters |
| **First appearance** | ch. 399 |

## Description
Frigid waters full of ice floes at the far east of the Great Ocean. Breath turns to white clouds and crews wear fur coats while a warship's icebreaker bow crushes the floes.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Slows ships, unlike the ordinary ocean; it is not an official "Frozen Sea" area (ch. 399).
- Includes a windless zone that the warship crosses quickly (ch. 399).
- About 12 days and 7 hours from Laimia by Type 7 warship; ends at a blue portal into the 7th floor's Ice Rock (ch. 399).

## Notable events
- ch. 399: The royal expedition crosses it to the 7th floor.

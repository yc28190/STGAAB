## Description
A group of islands in the southwest of the Great Ocean, where orc and goblin crews pilot ships and ambush explorers. It lies off the route to the 7th floor.

## Mechanics
- Orcs and goblins sail ships here and ambush explorers; it is a source of ship-related essences for navigators (ch. 799).
- Not on the route to the 7th floor (ch. 799).

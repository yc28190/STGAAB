## Description
A small island about 15 minutes' sail east of Laimia, the closest to the start after Parune. Its monsters are low-level and also appear on lower floors.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- About 15 minutes east of Laimia; the first stop on the eastern route, with Anos Island about 3 hours further northeast (ch. 375).

## Notable events
- ch. 375: The party skips hunting here and anchors only because of Bjorn's seasickness.

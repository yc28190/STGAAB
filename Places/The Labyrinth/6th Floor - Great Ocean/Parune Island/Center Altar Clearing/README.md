# Center Altar Clearing

## Description
An open space hidden in the forest at the centre of Parune Island, with a white stone tower-like altar in the middle and dark bloodstains on the ground. During the final phase of the "Wrath of the Sea" event it is the last dry ground left on the island.

## Monsters
- [[Stormgush]] — final boss of the event, fought here

## Mechanics
- The event's offering stone sits on the altar (ch. 271).
- In the final phase, Stormgush's [Call of the Storm] raises a tsunami wall, after which "a sanctuary is created", an air pocket under the water like a fish tank (ch. 279–280).
- Stormgush's passive [Origin] boosts natural regeneration while touching seawater, only on the 6th floor (ch. 282).

## Notable events
- ch. 270: The ambush site, with traces of Drowus's melted corpse.
- ch. 272: Bjorn's rallying point for the scattered party, where he leaves the note "Going east".
- ch. 279–281: The final battle against Stormgush; the sanctuary bubble cracks and floods, and Bjorn and Amelia tie themselves down with a nail and rope.
- ch. 282: The Fragment of Records flashes white and both vanish into the past.
- ch. 285: The returning party finds only the abandoned equipment, the nail and the rope.

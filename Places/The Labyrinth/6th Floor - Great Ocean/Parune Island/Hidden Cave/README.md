# Hidden Cave

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | cave |
| **Also known as** | Parune Hidden Cave; small cave |
| **First appearance** | ch. 284 |

## Description
A small cave about thirty minutes' walk from the centre of Parune Island, roomier inside than it looks from outside.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Amelia conceals the entrance well enough that Clan Elvis search parties walk past without noticing (ch. 284).

## Notable events
- ch. 284: Twenty years in the past, Bjorn and Amelia hide here until the labyrinth closes on day 60. Amelia names the stone the "Fragment of Records" (a legacy of Archmage Gavrilius handed down through the Lords of Noark), and they take identities from looted ID cards: "Amelia Berrywells" and "Nibels Enche", a 21-year-old barbarian.

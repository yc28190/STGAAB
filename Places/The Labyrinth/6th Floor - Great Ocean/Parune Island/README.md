## Description
The island closest to the starting point, north of Laimia, larger than it was in the game. It has sandy beaches with beautiful sunsets, reefs offshore and an inland forest of tall trees with dark blue leaves. During its hidden event the island is battered by storms, rising waves, hundreds of tornadoes, rain and flooding until only its centre stays above water. Twenty years earlier it was a flat forest without hills and served as the base of the looter clan Elvis.

Ordinarily it is a low-level island of insect monsters that drop many magic stones, but it is best known as the place where Bjorn and Amelia were thrown 20 years into the past, causing Bjorn's two-year disappearance.

## Mechanics
- Normally hosts only monsters below 8th grade (ch. 265–266).
- Hidden piece "Wrath of the Sea" (a Gavrilius' Arrangement): destroying the offering stone on the central altar with fewer than 20 people starts a defence event in which sea monsters march inland and waves push back fleeing boats; escape is not blocked, but most boats sink (ch. 267, 271).
- Difficulty scales with headcount: 5–9 people adds more normal monsters; 10–14 adds a fourth wave and a final boss; 15–19 removes some of the final boss's penalties (ch. 278).
- The event lasts until sunrise two days later; wave 1 brings the Siren Queen, wave 2 the Guano Sea Serpent, wave 3 an unnamed 4th-grade boss, and wave 4 Stormgush alone as the water rises (ch. 271–279).
- The 6th floor closes on day 60 (ch. 284).

## Notable events

- ch. 271: Carmilla and Belverson betray Amelia, and Belverson destroys the offering to trap her.
- ch. 273–280: Heyna, Hans Aulock (killed by Erwen), Belverson (by Amelia), Carmilla and Nebarche (eaten by Stormgush) die during the event.
- ch. 281: Bjorn absorbs Stormgush's essence; a white glowing stone emerges from Amelia's subspace.
- ch. 282–284: The Fragment of Records sends Bjorn and Amelia back 20 years; they wake naked in the dry centre of the island and kill a five-man Elvis team for their gear and ID cards.
- ch. 285: In the present, the party finds only Bjorn's and Amelia's gear, the nail and the rope.
- ch. 580: Misha later meant to come here to confess her feelings.

# 7th Floor Portal

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | portal |
| **Also known as** | 7th Floor Portal (6th Floor) |
| **First appearance** | ch. 352 |

## Description
A portal floating above the sea in front of a vast plain whose border is lined by a dark purple barrier. It is one of the gateways from the Great Ocean to the 7th floor and is guarded by a gatekeeper monster.

## Monsters
- Gatekeeper monster (unnamed) — must be killed before ships can pass

## Mechanics
- Fast ships go ahead with the first explorers to kill the gatekeeper and keep Noark out; over half of the fast ships had been destroyed a few weeks earlier (ch. 352).
- Foghorns signal ships to enter one at a time, and explorers on deck tie themselves on with ropes (ch. 352).

## Notable events
- ch. 352: Ships enter the portal one by one during the crossing to the 7th floor.

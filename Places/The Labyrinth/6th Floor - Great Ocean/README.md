## Description
A vast ocean dotted with islands under a blue sky, bright sun, salty wind and crashing waves. Unlike the lower floors it has seasons, occasional rain, snow on a few islands, and at night the Milky Way spreads across the sky. Time here flows continuously and differently from the city.

Exploring the floor means sailing between islands toward the 7th-floor portals, so ships and navigators are essential and clans are recommended. Notable routes include the eastern chain from the starting island Laimia through the Windless Zone to the reefs of the Grand Rock. The Great Navigator Pikma charted the entire floor, and his nautical chart is famous.

## Mechanics
- Does not reset each month: time runs continuously, even while the labyrinth is closed, and corpses and changes persist (ch. 265, 510, 795).
- A ship is required; without one an explorer cannot climb past the 5th floor (ch. 350, 791).
- Ships are summoned through a Summoning Engraving (a Bonding magic tool) and must be unsummoned on the 6th floor itself (ch. 399, 430).
- Compasses stop working once you leave the starting area; navigators use stars, sun, temperature and weather, and without one you must island-hop to confirm your position (ch. 375, 379).
- Rough seas include the Windless Zone and the Frozen Sea (ch. 265).
- Fishing from a moving ship catches 0-grade fish monsters and occasionally real sea monsters (ch. 379).
- Sea Merchant: throwing a set amount of magic stones where it swims yields Blue Stone; its essence grants [Unfair Contract] (ch. 315).
- Floor Lords from here up are endgame raids; this floor's Floor Lord can strike anywhere on the ocean but does not block portals (ch. 460, 808).
- Pikma clearing every monster on the ocean opened two new routes to the 7th floor, and Emon Mulmarin's Sea Dragon kill revealed a new island; hidden islands such as Rainbow Island exist (ch. 491, 608).
- The floor's "final day" is day 60 (ch. 809).

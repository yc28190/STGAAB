## Description
The sea around Pione Island, scene of the dawn naval battle with the Huntsman Clan, with stars still behind and the sun's glow rising in the east. The Huntsman flagship was about three times the size of the party's vessel, with three sails, magic cannons on its sides and bow, an icebreaker bow and a magic barrier.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- As an Elemental Barbarian (Water), Bjorn can walk on water and breathe underwater; sinking into the sea triggers Stormgush's [Origin] for huge regeneration (ch. 378).
- With [Risk] and [Transcendence], every magic-cannon hit is reflected (ch. 378).

## Notable events
- ch. 377–378: A critical reflection breaks the flagship's barrier and a reflected Lightning Spear destroys a second ship; Bjorn captures the flagship and two damaged ships, though the party's own ship sinks.
- ch. 378–379: Bjorn takes the navigator Auyen Lokrob and a mage, who transfers ship ownership with rare magic.

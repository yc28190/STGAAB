# Pione Island

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | island |
| **Also known as** | Treasure Island |
| **First appearance** | ch. 375 |

## Description
A relatively flat island, unlike its neighbours with their central mountains, small enough to walk around in about 3 hours. It is covered by a vast field of colourful flowers swept by a refreshing sea breeze, and its wide open ground makes intruders easy to spot.

Its fame comes from its single, rare monster, which always drops a Numbered Item. Clans have informally carved the island into six territories.

## Monsters
- [[Lafremimic]] — 4th-grade rare, non-aggressive chest monster; always drops a Numbered Item

## Mechanics
- The seventh island on the eastern route, about 10 days from the start (ch. 375).
- Lafremimic: the chest is its true body; it appears at most once a month on the whole island, always drops a Numbered Item, and its essence is worth billions. Without a mage it must be killed in one shot or it burrows underground (ch. 376).
- Apart from rifts, the only place to obtain Numbered Items (ch. 375).
- Divided informally into six clan territories (ch. 376).

## Notable events
- ch. 376: The Huntsman Clan under Eksher Melik demands the party leave; Erwen kills a Lafremimic, which drops No. 6111 Fate Tracker ("Traffic Light Ring").
- ch. 377–378: The clan ambushes the party at sea.
- ch. 379: The party returns to repair the captured ships for two days and disposes of the useless prisoners.

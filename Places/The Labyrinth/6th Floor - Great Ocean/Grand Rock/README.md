## Description
The easternmost region of the Great Ocean, full of reefs, where about 99% of the islands are rocky or volcanic; the sea here has a charm of its own. It is home to the Mermals and contains Catacoma (Skull Island) as well as the sunken city that rises when the Mermal nests are destroyed.

## Mechanics
- Each time the labyrinth opens, three red rock islands (Mermal nests) are generated at random here; completing the event on all three raises the Underwater City (ch. 380).
- Contains Catacoma in the far eastern seas (ch. 793).

## Notable events
- ch. 379–380: Bjorn's party reaches the region via the eastern route and raises the Underwater City.

## Description
A sunken, ruined and deserted city reached by a dive of about ten minutes into pitch-black depths where compasses fail. Its surviving pillars glow as visitors enter. Stairs climb a pyramid-shaped temple to a small room at its top that holds the portal to Atlante.

## Mechanics
- Raised by clearing all three Mermal nests in the Grand Rock (ch. 380).
- Requirements: a ship able to reach the Grand Rock, a means of diving freely (magic, spirits or skills) and patience for repetitive tasks (ch. 380).
- Light attracts monsters during the descent; the city is about 2 hours' walk from the drop point with a Guide (ch. 380).
- Underwater-breathing magic lasts about 4 hours for three people (ch. 380).

## Notable events
- ch. 380: Bjorn's party dives to the city and reaches the temple portal.

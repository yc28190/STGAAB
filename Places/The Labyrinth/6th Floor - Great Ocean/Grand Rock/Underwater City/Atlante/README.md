## Description
A deep-sea realm beyond the reach of a Light Gem: muddy ground, submerged buildings and fields of red eyes glowing in the dark. At its heart lies a vast open space where dozens of massive pillars stand in a circle around a giant blue egg, several times larger than Bjorn in giant form, which turns out to be a pulsing heart.

It is normally tackled by expedition forces, and is where the shield barbarian can obtain a second core essence after [Gigantification].

## Mechanics
- Entered through the Underwater City temple portal; a hidden field, not a rift, so there is no Guardian, normal drop rates and no rift stones or Numbered Items. Once opened it stays open until the labyrinth closes (ch. 380–381).
- Field effect "Atlante": [Hyperventilation] lets you move and speak as on land but doubles all resource use; [Challenger] prevents escape during battle, marked by a red aura on the ground (ch. 380).
- Boss: stepping inside the pillar circle raises a giant whirlpool that blocks the exit and summons the Deep Sea Giant, which speaks an unknown language. Attacking the heart drains its [Life Spring] heals and lowers the giant's defences; the boss has a drop-rate bonus (ch. 381, 383).
- Witnessing the egg unlocks "Mythical Discovery (1/5)", +10 Soul Power (ch. 381).
- On the 9th floor, Deep Sea Giants roam in packs with hidden hearts (ch. 381).

## Notable events
- ch. 380–381: Bjorn solos the area using reflection damage.
- ch. 382: The boss drops a red essence; Lee Baekho and the Ruin Scholar arrive and trade him a green Deep Sea Giant essence ([Soul Dive], passive [Primordial Cell]) for it, then leave by Multiple Teleportation.
- ch. 383: Five days of hunting bring large magic-stone income and a 4th-grade essence; Bjorn kills the captured mage and keeps the navigator, controlled with a fake poison.

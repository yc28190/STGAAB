## Description
A clearing in front of an entrance built from massive bones, like a dinosaur theme-park ride. Visitors walk into a huge gaping skull, inside which stands a single iron door with a keyhole.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Opened with the key from the Key Keeper or the special skull; turning it clockwise sends an eerie roar across the whole island, and the door slams shut behind those who enter (ch. 795).
- After the Skull King dies, the cave stays accessible during the respawn interval (ch. 797).

## Notable events
- ch. 793: Standoff with the Golden Tree Clan's 90 members in the clearing.
- ch. 795: Anabada opens the door.

## Description
An island built entirely of bone. Its ground is littered with shards of human and monster bones of every kind, some skulls are the size of boulders, and eerie vine-covered undergrowth spreads across it. It lies in the Grand Rock region of the far eastern seas.

The Great Navigator Pikma called it "either the easiest of all, or the most difficult". Its boss is tied to a special event for those who later reach the 10th floor.

## Mechanics
- Part of the Grand Rock region; about 9 days' sailing from Laimia without stops (ch. 792–793).
- Average monster grade 6, mostly grades 7–8 (ch. 793–794).
- Hidden piece: the Key Keeper drops the key to the central cave if killed with [Distortion] (ch. 795).
- Guaranteed method: find the single special boulder-sized skull near the centre (two horns, square jaw) and smash it for the key; there is only ever one, and the 6th floor does not reset (ch. 795).
- Beating the boss and then reaching the 10th floor grants "something special"; Bjorn sold this tip via Ghostbusters and the Round Table (ch. 502, 792).

## Notable events
- ch. 792–793: The Golden Tree Clan (90 members under Bill Ironred, one of the Seven Rivals) arrives first, plants its flag at the entrance and ambushes Anabada to assert dominance; the two sides agree that whoever first opens the cave door gets the challenge right.
- ch. 794–795: Avman Urikfrit is revealed to be indebted to and mistreated by Golden Tree; the clan offers 400M stones for first entry, and Bjorn asks for Avman instead and is refused.

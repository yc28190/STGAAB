## Description
The final chamber of the Catacoma Cave. Like the rest of the dungeon its walls, ceiling and floor are bone, lit by candles in skull sconces, but here the floor is a sea of skulls and a massive skull, far larger than the one at the cave entrance, is mounted on the front wall.

## Monsters
- [Gravekeeper Alleton](../../../../../../Bestiary/gravekeeper_alleton_ch796.md) — gatekeeper standing before the door
- [Skull King](../../../../../../Bestiary/skull_king_ch795.md) — Grade 3 boss that rises from the skulls

## Mechanics
- Normally, when Gravekeeper Alleton dies, the curses of the most-cursed character spread to the party, so kills are rotated; hidden piece: if the Cursed One kills it, the curse does not spread (ch. 796).
- Only one team of six can be inside; the door shuts, the room quakes and the Skull King rises (ch. 796).
- The Skull King's soul orb is its life: at 80% it casts a massive curse, at 60% summons adds, at 40% uses instant death, and at 20% [Soul Absorption] for invincible recovery; then [Eternal Liberation] grants damage immunity for about a minute while the orb drains, after which it freezes and the orb can be shattered (ch. 796).
- It respawns after an average of 7 more labyrinth openings (ch. 797).
- A hidden treasure chest must be found by combing the floor after the kill (ch. 797).
- Hidden boss condition: the Cursed One kills both the Gravekeeper and the Skull King ("A fragment of a long story has been completed") (ch. 796–797).
- The Skull King essence is worth about 500M stones (ch. 795).

## Notable events
- ch. 796: Erwen's [Chaos Circuit] infinite-MP burst skips three phases, clearing the boss in about two minutes.
- ch. 797: The hidden chest holds No. 444 Deadrius's Invitation, a charm that keeps undead from attacking the holder and whose wax seal, broken in the labyrinth, drags you to the hidden field Hell.

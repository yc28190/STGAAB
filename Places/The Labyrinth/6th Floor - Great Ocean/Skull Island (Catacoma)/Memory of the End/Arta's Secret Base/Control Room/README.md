## Description
An upper-level room of Arta's Secret Base, fitted with devices that display the interior of the laboratory below and a speaker system connected to it.

It works as the base's command post: from here the sealed laboratory door can be opened and announcements can be made into the lab.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Normally inaccessible; its door opened only because the destruction of the laboratory made the base's doors malfunction (ch. 805).
- The sealed door of the Destroyed Nuclear Laboratory and the lab's speaker can be operated from here (ch. 805).

## Notable events
- ch. 805: Auyen stumbles into the room by chance and opens the lab door just in time to save the party.

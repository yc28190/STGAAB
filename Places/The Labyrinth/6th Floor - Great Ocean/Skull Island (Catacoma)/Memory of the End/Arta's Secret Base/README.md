# Arta's Secret Base

## Description
A dark, quiet facility like a giant submarine, bunker or research lab. Its floors are plated with metal and its walls lined with pipes, hatch handles and mana conduits as thick as industrial cables. After the laboratory was wrecked, its doors malfunction and open and close on their own.

The Arta family built it at the point closest to the Core of the Earth to seal the Beast with the Divine Relic, a duty passed down for generations until a monster broke into the lab. Arta, the only NPC in the labyrinth, speaks to visitors through a static-filled speaker in the ancient tongue; later he appears as a 190 cm robot with an exposed metal frame, red lens eyes and a disturbingly human face.

## Monsters
- [[Mechanical Behemoth Arta]] — optional raid boss, roughly as hard as a 9th-floor Floor Lord

## Mechanics
- Reached by the portal from Arta Island's altar (ch. 799).
- Field effect "Arta's Secret Base": all hostile actions are prohibited; attempting one freezes the body until hostility is dropped (ch. 800).
- Dialogue choices decide the quest: deny being pilgrims (pilgrims face impossible "proofs"), claim to be survivors of the fallen Empire, know how to kill the Beast (End Serpent Sniktura, First Class) and know the Divine Device must be activated (ch. 800).
- A hidden floor hatch then opens onto an emergency ladder (the elevator is broken), about a two-hour climb down to the lab (ch. 800–801).
- After the Beast dies, giving Arta the byproduct earns a reward and seals the entrance forever; refusing triggers the Mechanical Behemoth Arta raid, whose reward is a mechanical heart, a top-grade mana core comparable to a Dragon Heart (ch. 803, 815).

## Notable events
- ch. 800–801: The party passes Arta's questioning and learns the base's history.
- ch. 802: Arta seals the party inside when they try to retreat.
- ch. 815: Bjorn returns and tells the mechanical Arta that "the you of the past" sent him; Arta keeps asking whether visitors are Pilgrims, traitors who sided with the witches.

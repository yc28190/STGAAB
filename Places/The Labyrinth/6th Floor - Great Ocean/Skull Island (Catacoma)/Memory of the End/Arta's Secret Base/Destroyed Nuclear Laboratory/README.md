# Destroyed Nuclear Laboratory

## Description
A spacious dome-shaped laboratory inside Arta's Secret Base, full of machines covered in buttons and echoing with constantly repeating sirens. At its center gapes a bottomless, sinkhole-like pit; a strong wind blows down into it as the damaged Core drains mana. As the fight goes on the room turns into a scene of red emergency lights, sparking machinery and geysers of steam.

It houses the failing mana core of the base and serves as the arena for the battle against Sniktura, the Serpent of the End.

## Monsters
- [[Sniktura]] — "End Serpent / Serpent of the End", First Class boss; ejected from the central pit; uses [All-Poison Physiology], [Seed of Destruction], [Venom Spray], [Form Shift] (changes size and morphs into other Grade One monsters), [Life Leech], [Will of the Undying], [Execution], [Fatal Poisoning] and the Cryptid skill [Harbinger of Corruption].

## Mechanics
- Field effect "Destroyed Nuclear Laboratory": all resources (MP, soul power) of both sides are fixed at 0 (ch. 800).
- Pressing a machine button triggers an emergency restoration that fails and forcibly ejects Sniktura from the pit (ch. 801).
- Phase 1: survive while pressing the lit buttons to restore the equipment, about 2 hours (ch. 801).
- Phase 2: the field becomes "Destroyed Core Lab"; resources fluctuate with distance from the pit (up to ~10%), so Sniktura must be pinned at the center to keep its skills sealed (ch. 801).
- Phase 3: below 10% HP Sniktura uses [Final Radiance] — stats x2, immunity to status and damage, immobile for 60 s, HP drains away over ~5 minutes; the party only has to survive (ch. 803–804).
- Retreat is normally allowed, but Arta seals the door if the party tries to flee a winnable fight (ch. 802).
- Smashing the machines damages the mana core, lifts the divine-weapon seal and turns the field into "Core of the Earth": all resources fixed at 100% (normally only seen at the end of the true final raid against Mechanical Arta) (ch. 802–803).
- [Harbinger of Corruption] permanently corrupts one essence, which becomes unusable for 180 days (ch. 804).
- Reward: Sniktura's two-color First-grade essence ([All-Poison Physiology], [Final Radiance], [Form Shift], [The Last Day]) (ch. 804–807).

## Notable events
- ch. 800–801: The party enters, the restoration fails and Sniktura emerges.
- ch. 802: Beleg is downed by [Seed of Destruction]; the machines are smashed and the field flips to Core of the Earth.
- ch. 804: Bjorn's Bayon essence ([Transcendence]) is corrupted with a 10% mutation. Elwen's [Focused Shot], charged over ten minutes, brings Sniktura below 10%; Sven's [Agent of the Star] lets Reatlas descend and raise a barrier.
- ch. 805–807: Sniktura dies behind the closed door; its essence drops and goes to Amelia.
- ch. 806: Arta says the lab will have to be rebuilt.

## Description
The center of the island in the Memory of the End. When the Beast awakens, the ground heaves like ocean waves and lava erupts through it; the creature rises out of the blaze, and the battlefield is lit by lava, falling meteors and lightning.

It is the lair of the Ancient Beast, the apocalyptic monster of this hidden scenario. A strong mana signal at the center persisted even after its death.

## Monsters
- [[The Beast]] — Ancient Beast; unranked ultra-large (~9 m) bipedal dinosaur-like creature with a massive tail, built like Chaos Lord Liakis; raid boss of the island center.

## Mechanics
- Aura [Song of the Apocalypse]: all time is accelerated, so the death countdown and fire damage tick several times faster; the raid is effectively capped at 30 minutes (ch. 811).
- [Ultimatum] roar drains physical stats for 5 minutes; only the Rank 5 curse [White Tone] dispels it, but it deafens everyone, so the team fights with hand signals (ch. 811).
- Other skills: [Knot of Ruin], [Devour], [Tremor] (phase 1), [Horn of the Apocalypse] summoning an army (phase 2), [Ghost Return] (phase 3) (ch. 811–812).
- Phase 4 (below 20% HP): the Beast regains Authorities, the first always aimed at the Cursed One — a weakened Authority of Annihilation (erases something; it erased Adnus's hair) and a random Authority of Mutation (temporarily turned Adnus into a woman) (ch. 812).
- Being unranked, it gives no EXP and its corpse remains unless an essence drops (ch. 812).
- Loot: hide equal to Grade 4 drake leather over the whole body, head hide equal to Grade 5 ogre leather, a skull that can be forged into a blade as hard as adamantium, the heart and other byproducts (ch. 812).

## Notable events
- ch. 807–810: A strong mana signal is detected at the island's center.
- ch. 811–812: Raid against the Beast; it is killed after about 21 minutes by Bjorn's [Aegis Dragon Scale] reflect. Its essence would have been a graduation option for Misha.

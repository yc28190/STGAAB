## Description
A version of Skull Island at the moment of its destruction. The cave has the same layout as Catacoma but with every bone gone; outside, the sky is blood-red, the earth is cracked and splintered with lava running through the fissures, and the whole island erupts like an active volcano. Lava pours into the sea and hardens into black rock, and steam and smoke reduce visibility along the shore to nothing. From the sea, the island looks detached from reality, cloaked in thick dark-red smoke.

It is a hidden field tied to the ancient Empire and the Arta family's attempt to seal a primordial Beast, and to an apocalypse described as a dimensional collapse of the 6th floor.

## Mechanics
- Entered through the blood-red portal behind the 44-torch wall in the Catacoma Cave; a hidden field like Larkaze's Labyrinth (ch. 798, 807).
- Field effect "Apocalypse": [Eternal Scorch] deals continuous, growing fire damage, and [Terminal Countdown] / [Doomed] kills the character after 24 hours (ch. 799).
- Leaving through the entry portal removes the effects but prevents re-entry (ch. 799).
- Casting [Distortion] on a slain monster burns it to ash and releases a red shimmer, "Original Sin", which resets the countdown to 24 hours and gives +1 fire resistance the first time (ch. 808).
- The Fire Orb halves the continuous fire damage within 15 m (ch. 799).
- Players can summon their boat at the beach and sail away (found after hundreds of trial runs), reaching Arta Island (ch. 799).
- Killing monsters weakens the Beast in 10% steps; at 90% its power is nullified (ch. 809–810).
- The Beast appears after the party lingers in its central territory, normally after 1–2 hours of roaming (ch. 811).
- Quest chain: originally just kill the Beast; the hidden route runs through Arta's Secret Base and its quest, kills the Beast for an extra reward, then refuses the reward to unlock the Mechanical Behemoth Arta raid (ch. 811).
- Being inside shielded the party from the 6th-floor Floor Lord event (ch. 808).

## Description
Arrivals appear in midair above a vast beach of nearly white sand, with wet mud along the shore and an emerald sea beyond. The sun is scorching and the wind cool, humid and salty. A mountain rises in the middle of the island, with a large graffiti-covered rock near the summit; it is usually bald and covered in thousands of stumps, but grows densely forested after a long closure. There are shade trees and beaches, and a meeting point on the east side overlooks the 6th-floor portal.

It is the arrival island of the 6th floor (the Great Ocean). In normal times it is crowded with clans building ships or waiting for comrades; during royal war recruitment more than a hundred galleons line the coast and registration desks stand along the eastern shoreline.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Everyone arrives here regardless of the 5th-floor route taken; the portal from the 5th floor lands on the island (ch. 265, 792).
- The summit grows a special tree as hard as 3rd-tier metal yet light and buoyant, ideal boat material; it is logged to stumps and does not regrow within a month (ch. 265, 791).
- Royal war recruitment: arrive by Day 25, register with an ID card at the eastern desks and choose a ship from the bulletin board. Newcomers may only board 3rd-class ships (rear support); 1st-class ships carry famous explorers; triple cabins are popular because they fit two 6-person teams (ch. 351).
- The war fleet departs on Day 25, leaving the island nearly empty (ch. 375).
- Parune Island is visible to the north from the summit (ch. 265).

## Notable events
- ch. 265: Bjorn finds the island empty this time and the summit trees already logged.
- ch. 351: Erwen's fame causes a stir at registration; Amelia registers as "Emily Raines" and Bjorn as "Lihen Schuitz".
- ch. 398: The expedition teams gather here (Raviyen arrives on day 16 after speedrunning alone); by the 20th all five teams meet and Bjorn reveals the mission — enter the Dark Continent from the rear through Ice Rock and the 8th floor.
- ch. 791–792: Anabada Team 1 and 47 Meiruta "volunteers" log the regrown forest for days; Team 2 arrives five days late; Anabada sets sail on Day 21 as royal warships launch.

# 3rd-Class Warship 3-21

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | ship (galleon) |
| **Also known as** | ship 3-21; the galleon |
| **First appearance** | ch. 351 |

## Description
A massive royal galleon with cramped cabins whose door hinges are rusty, a deck used for formation training and a hold below. At the end of a passage in the hold, down a flight of stairs and lit by torchlight, is artifact No. 399, the Cage of Silence. Upstairs is the officer's cabin, built for secret conversations, holding a crystal-ball military message stone linked to the command center.

It carried explorers assigned to escort the 3rd Magic Corps on the war expedition to the 7th floor.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- The voyage to the 7th-floor portal takes about 15 days, with formation training on deck (ch. 351).
- Every warship has teleport redirection: anyone using a spatial-movement ability within range is redirected into the Cage of Silence (ch. 359–360).
- The Cage of Silence seals magic, divine power, Aura and magic tools for 2 hours (long cooldown); attacks from outside are blocked while it is active (ch. 359–360).
- Ships are unsummoned after the troops disembark (ch. 353).

## Notable events
- ch. 351: Bjorn is seasick for the whole voyage.
- ch. 359–362: After teleporting back to the ship, the trio is trapped in the Cage of Silence; Raven questions them with No. 7234 Distorted Trust, which malfunctions whenever Bjorn speaks, then frees them.

# Witch's Cabin

## Description
A hidden pocket space in the Witch's Forest, entered through a tree that looks like all the others except for a faint purple glow in its bark. Touching it brings a purple mist and then a warm spring breeze, opening onto a sunny meadow full of wildflowers, leafy bushes and chirping crickets. An ordinary cabin stands in the middle, furnished with a bookshelf of books, a table, a carpet, a fireplace, a small bed and a rocking chair. After Riakis is defeated, the meadow turns into a cold expanse of snow with bare, snow-laden trees, though the interior stays the same.

It is the only safe zone in the forest. The boss-room hut of the White Temple copies it exactly, and it is tied to the Earth Witch.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Appears at random places and intervals and lasts 8 hours; its purple light fades as the entrance ages. Enter by placing your hands on the bark (ch. 62).
- Field effects are switched off and no monsters appear inside (ch. 62, 301).
- Holds at most 5 occupants; the entrance closes once full (ch. 63).
- The fireplace is its timer: when the last embers die, "The Witch's Cabin is closed" and occupants are returned to the forest (ch. 62).
- Burning a corpse in the fireplace is an "offering" that adds 8 hours (ch. 64).
- Hidden trigger: the seventh offering (Special Condition "Seven Offerings") summons the Lord of Chaos Riakis onto the 3rd floor (ch. 65, 457–458).
- After Riakis is killed (time-limited), stepping into the fireplace completes a hidden achievement granting a permanent +50 Mental Resistance, then sends the visitor back to the forest and closes the cabin (ch. 463–464).
- A barrier protects the area, making it a private training ground that withstood Dicloe and a full-charge Focused Fire (ch. 463).
- A dimensional-disruption barrier surrounds the hut (ch. 611).

## Notable events
- ch. 62–64: Bjorn and Misha shelter here; Bjorn kills Davis' three surviving followers and burns them in the fireplace.
- ch. 65: With four earlier offerings already made by others, the seventh offering summons Riakis.
- ch. 463: After defeating Riakis, Erwen tests her Chaos Essence behind the cabin and Amelia and Ainar have a snowball fight.
- ch. 463–464: Stepping into the flames, Bjorn alone is pulled into a strange field and meets the Earth Witch.

# Thornreed Field

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | field |
| **Also known as** | the reed forest |
| **First appearance** | ch. 313 |

## Description
A flat, open stretch of tall thornreeds covering part of the western side of the Witch's Forest. The reeds are thick enough to hide entire teams of ambushers, while the ground beneath has no other obstacles. Teams pass through it on the way to the Witch's Forest.

## Monsters
- [[Riakis]] — lured here by decoy teams as a raid site

## Mechanics
- About 4 days from the labyrinth entrance via the 2nd-floor Rocky Desert, the Pilgrim's Path and the Twilight Slope (ch. 313).
- Its flat, open ground makes it an ideal raid site for Riakis; decoy teams lead him here from the forest, and the Sawtooth Clan burned the reeds to clear a battlefield (ch. 458).

## Notable events
- ch. 313–315: Barker Clan uses it as a gathering point and looting base, running Bjorn's "Fashion Barbarian" bait (fake lost barbarians with painted tattoos shouting "Behel-laaa") against the dwarf Aitomz's team.
- ch. 458–462: The Sawtooth Clan under Vice-Captain Malid Kevron runs an apparently unauthorised raid on Riakis here, refuses Bjorn's help and loses 14 members.

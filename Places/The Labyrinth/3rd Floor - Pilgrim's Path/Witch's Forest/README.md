## Description
A huge, eerie forest at the heart of the 3rd floor. Barren thorn bushes are wrapped in snake-like vines, rotten creepers hang everywhere and the trees themselves move, so everything seems alive and shifting. The air is chilly, the soil grows damp near the edges, razor-like wind rustles the branches, and mages feel ominous magic vibrating through the whole forest. Hallucinations warp the surroundings like a funhouse mirror maze while mocking voices whisper constantly. In a large clearing at its centre stands a giant tower hidden by the trees, with a huge door and a multicoloured portal in front of it: the gateway to the 4th floor.

Its monsters are mostly rank 7–8 and largely spirit-type and immune to physical damage, so it is a poor hunting ground and mainly a place to pass through; it is nicknamed "the graveyard of warriors". Residents are few and meeting other teams is rare. When the Floor Master is summoned, the forest falls silent and bright under a red ceiling.

## Mechanics
- Surrounds the 4th-floor portal; about 5 hours' hurried march from the edge of the Orc Colony, and about 1 hour from the eastern outskirts to an open plain (ch. 53, 56, 65).
- Entering applies the field effect "Witch's Forest": [Disoriented], [Auditory Hallucination], [Visual Hallucination] and [Pain Amplification]; it also cancels every non-field status effect, wiping curses and tracking spells (ch. 56).
- Hallucinations worsen with fatigue and begin showing people from one's memories (ch. 59).
- Compasses fail and the trees move, so navigation relies on special senses (e.g. Rotmiller smelling magic, fairies hearing the portal's heartbeat-like pulse); parties tie themselves together with rope (ch. 58, 61, 115–116, 163).
- The Numbered Item Guardian's Bracers overrides the field effects for about 20 seconds (ch. 58).
- After 12 hours inside the field strengthens with [Witch's Eye]: staying in one spot too long spawns a Cannibalo; the allowed time shrinks from about 1 hour at the start to about 10 minutes at level 5 (84 hours) and about 3 minutes at the maximum level 10 (ch. 60–62).
- Most incorporeal monsters are evil-attribute and vulnerable to holy water (ch. 59).
- The Witch's Cabin is the only safe zone; burning seven offerings there summons Riakis (ch. 62–65, 457–458).
- The tower portal takes a party (six people per batch) when its colour shifts, then changes colour for the next team; once it turns red, latecomers go to a different instance. Entering without permission can get a team treated as marauders (ch. 116, 790).
- The Thornreed Field covers part of its western side (ch. 313).

## Description
A huge open-world floor with no roads and terrain that never changes, normally drowned in deep darkness that thickens toward the interior and thins near the edges. The closed ceiling shows something like the Milky Way, a view only the earliest explorers see. During the light show that begins on Day 3, mountains, fields, a river, dense forest and a tall spire reaching the ceiling become visible; from a hilltop the whole landscape is bathed in silver light. When the Floor Master is active, the ceiling glows an ominous red and black rain falls.

The floor contains more than ten named fields, each a separate monster habitat (Steelrock Hill, Orc Colony, Witch's Forest, Greentail Marsh, Pitch-black Estuary and others). All 1st- and 2nd-floor routes converge here, each arriving at a different starting point, and the 4th-floor portal stands in the tower at the centre of the Witch's Forest. It is considered the best floor for looting, which makes it a hunting ground for pillagers.

## Mechanics
- All four 1st-floor routes and every 2nd-floor field lead here, each to a different start point (ch. 12, 51, 398).
- Monsters are mostly grade 7 with rare grade 6; solo play becomes impractical from here (ch. 37, 44).
- About 3–4 days of pure travel to the centre, where the 4th-floor portal stands in a tower in the Witch's Forest; wait for the portal to change colour before entering or the team inside may take you for marauders (ch. 51, 115, 163).
- The floor closes at the end of Day 15 (ch. 53, 115).
- From midnight on Day 3, countless silver orbs rise from the ground to the ceiling for about a minute; mages call it a meaningless mana supersaturation phenomenon (ch. 51, 146, 164).
- Floor Master Riakis is summoned by burning 7 offerings in total in the Witch's Cabin fireplace; the method has been kept secret by the royal family and guild for about 150 years and unannounced summoning is a crime (ch. 65).
- Once summoned, "Erosion begins": normal monsters vanish, Chaos Spirits awaken and chase targets, all portals shut and the floor becomes a boss room until he dies; he uses the instant-kill [Crack of Evil] (ch. 65, 114, 458, 808).
- The guild rarely approves Floor Lord raids; the Explorer's Oath obliges strong explorers to protect weaker ones in an emergency (ch. 458).
- Pillagers lure monsters onto portal arrival points to ambush teams (ch. 51).
- Its only rift is the Hundred Colors Temple; a rift opened during the reopening about an hour from the Witch's Forest entrance and leads to the White Temple (ch. 298, 790).
- The unknown statues can be enchanted with Distortion magic and drop three types of emblem fragments, the keys to the B1 Archive; the route is via the Fragmented Temple (ch. 544–545).
- Taking over a hunting ground without royal recognition is illegal; the crown can grant labyrinth rights to clans (ch. 52).

## Description
A highland area of the 3rd floor whose cliff edge offers a view over the entire floor during the Day 3 light show. It is the first area reached from the Beasts' Lair route and lies uphill from the Orc Colony.

## Mechanics
- The first area reached from the Beasts' Lair portal (ch. 50).
- Where two habitats meet, overlapping territories leave fewer monsters, making it a good camp spot (ch. 51).
- The way to the Orc Colony runs downhill from here (ch. 51).

## Notable events
- ch. 51: Pillagers wait at the arrival portal; Rotmiller shows the team the midnight orb display from a nearby cliff.
- ch. 53: Suggested as a fallback hunting ground.
- ch. 457: Bjorn's party passes through on the way to the Orc Encampment.

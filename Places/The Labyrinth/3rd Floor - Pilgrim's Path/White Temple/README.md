## Description
The only rift of the 3rd floor, a temple whose first impression is overwhelmingly white despite its name. The entry hall has a very high ceiling, white marble pillars and about a hundred statues lined along the walls. The stage rooms beyond are stone and marble chambers of similar size, each holding a single statue with a jewel in its hand whose colour reveals the room type (a blue jewel spreading fog-like light marks a boss room). The final boss room is entirely different: a large dome containing an artificial garden of grass and fruit trees, a hut at its centre, and five doors set in the circular wall.

The hut is an exact copy of the Witch's Cabin in the Witch's Forest, down to the furniture; its books are blank but carry titles in the ancient language. The rift is prized for its huge experience rewards, and its talking boss believed the labyrinth was the real world and called explorers "dogs of the Empire".

## Mechanics
- When it opens the ground rumbles; a Guide can sense where (ch. 298, 790).
- Five portals open on the floor; those entering through the same portal form one team, so parties often get split (ch. 298).
- The inner stone door stays shut until all other portals close (ch. 299).
- Competitive: only one team may enter the boss room, first come first served; if it fails, another boss-room door opens for the next team (ch. 298, 300).
- Stage-based: clearing a room opens the next; rooms hold a boss-type monster, timed waves, traps or mental-attack monsters (ch. 299).
- Each cleared stage either curses the party or permanently buffs the next stage's monsters (Bjorn's [Swing] was sealed); hidden symbols in rooms and at intersections let you choose rooms that lift curses (ch. 299–300).
- The Golden Room's door takes about 3 hours to open (ch. 299).
- An unlit jewel on the boss-door statue means you are the fastest team; smashing the statue opens the door sooner (ch. 300).
- Elite monsters give first-kill EXP; about a hundred different essences can drop (ch. 300).
- Knight of Doom: phase 1 rides the Spirit Horse, which shares its stats; phase 2 fights on foot with random essence skills; phase 3 its passive [Chivalry] destroys its equipment and heals it (ch. 300).
- Drop: No. 87 Kraul's Demon Crusher, a Double Numbered Item (ch. 300).
- The exit portal appears in front of the hut and returns you to where you entered (ch. 300–301).
- Hidden pieces: Gavrilius' Arrangement; the Hundred Fruits, white fruits that randomly re-roll one essence's base stats (usually for the worse); Fragment of Earth (+1 Earth and Physical Resistance); Book of Souls (+3 EXP); other potions must be distorted before they can be taken out (ch. 300–301, 347).
- Its debuffs make fights harder than usual (ch. 537).

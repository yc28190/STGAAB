## Description
A hellish space where every tile is permanently corrupted, opened by the Floor Master Riakis during the second phase of his fight. Raiders dragged inside suffer severe mental corruption.

## Monsters
- [Riakis](../../../../Bestiary/riakis_ch65.md) — the Chaos Lord, in a strengthened form inside the gate

## Mechanics
- When Riakis's HP falls below a threshold in phase 2, he opens the Gate of Chaos and drags everyone in; raiders must hunt down a stronger Riakis amid heavy mental corruption (ch. 460).
- If every raider holds a Purification Torch (No. 8645), they can wait it out: after two in-game days the gate reopens and returns them to the field, but Riakis returns at full HP and stronger (ch. 460).
- The wider fight: phase 1 aura [Rain of Despair] (black corrosive rain dealing chaos damage, lowering Magic Resistance and causing confusion), Chaos Spirits corrupting tiles, healing orbs, the instant-kill [Crack of Evil] and an immobilised "prey"; phase 3 rain disables equipment (Numbered Items stop working until closure and Adamantium becomes like steel); phase 4 is a fast, lean form that uses 12 of his 36 "Tainted Body Parts" to heal, countered by a 10-minute berserk "Blessing of the Pilgrim" purification (ch. 460–461).
- Killing him announces "The Ancient Pilgrim has perished" and the red glow fades; Floor Lord drops are a Floor Treasure, a Floor Stone (opens a rift on that floor) or a Floor Essence (ch. 460–461).
- Achievements: first Floor Lord kill (+50 Soul Power) and a hidden one (ch. 461).

## Notable events
- ch. 460–461: Bjorn's party defeats Riakis; the Chaos Essence goes to Erwen.

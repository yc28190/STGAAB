## Description
A tangle of dozens of shallow ditches and streams flowing in every direction, with murky, knee-deep rivers about 5 m wide separated by damp strips of land. A strong sewage smell hangs over everything. The only good resting spots are mounds of soft, dry, reddish soil where explorers gather when the tide rises.

It is the 3rd-floor starting point for those coming up the Land of the Dead route.

## Monsters
- [Frogman](../../../../Bestiary/frogman_ch115.md) — 8th grade, bipedal frogs with harpoons, very agile; [Slimy Mucus] makes blunt weapons nearly useless
- [Frogman Shaman](../../../../Bestiary/frogman_shaman_ch115.md) — its eyeballs are a valuable collection item

## Mechanics
- Starting point of the Land of the Dead route; the party headed northeast from here (ch. 115).
- Frogmen are almost impossible to hit with blunt weapons because of [Slimy Mucus]; freezing them works (ch. 115).
- Tides: the water rises late in the day, forcing a halt around 23:00; it peaks at 00:00, stays high for 6 hours and recedes at 06:00, so explorers wait on dry mounds (about 30 people at one) (ch. 115).
- Monster corpses normally vanish; the "Distortion" spell rarely keeps one whole but yields no essence or magic stone. Frogman Shaman eyeballs were a 2-million-stone request (ch. 115).

## Notable events
- ch. 115: Bjorn and Misha run into Hikurod, Dwarkey and Rotmiller by chance; Rotmiller dissects a shaman for by-products.

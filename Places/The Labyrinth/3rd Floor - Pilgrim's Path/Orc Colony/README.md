## Description
A 3rd-floor field of tall bushes and forest, downhill from Steelrock Hill. At its centre stands an altar, the "Warrior's Vessel", in front of a statue, with a separate Orc Lord statue nearby. During the Riakis disaster a crimson beam from the ceiling wiped much of the encampment out, leaving only scarred, ruined ground.

It has the highest monster count on the 3rd floor, with nothing below rank 8, and is rated the floor's second-hardest area and the best hunting ground for teams not going on to the 4th floor. Its hidden boss made it a target for clans seeking Orc Hero essences.

## Mechanics
- Highest monster count on the floor, only rank 8 or higher; groups grow toward the centre (ch. 51).
- Orc shamans cast [Frenzy], tripling physical resistance for 10 seconds (ch. 51).
- Hidden boss: offering 777 magic stones at the central altar (orc great warrior stones in ch. 68, Orc Warlord stones in ch. 457, conflicting accounts) summons the Orc Heroes. Danish has a red essence with [Battle Cry], Belta a blue essence with [Banner of Struggle], and Tarugas a green essence with [Gigantification] (ch. 68–69).
- Special condition "Overflowing Vessel": the statue crumbles and all three Ancient Heroes awaken together as 5th-grade high variants with complementary 4th-grade essences (Anguish, Sacred Battlefield, Guardian Shield), wielding Numbered Items such as the Sword of Control, while orc hordes swarm (ch. 457).
- At very low probability the Orc Lord statue activates instead; outsiders must stay away from the altar (ch. 68, 457).
- Riakis fight: [Rain of Despair] deals continuous chaos damage, lowers Magic Resistance and erodes the ground, cancelling field effects; eroded ground causes [Confusion] (ch. 66–67).
- Dark Orbs rise from eroded ground and Riakis absorbs them; ranged attackers must destroy them mid-air (ch. 67).
- If he absorbs 10 or more orbs he casts [Crack of Evil]: a 5 km field of total darkness that pulls everyone toward a lit "center of evil"; anyone not 5 km away within about 45 minutes dies. Clones explode on their killers with [Riakis's Hatred] (ch. 68, 70).
- Afterwards he marks one survivor as [Prey], incapacitating them and locking his aggro onto them (ch. 70–71).

## Notable events
- ch. 52, 68: The Dzarwi Clan illegally claims the field to hunt the Orc Hero for a noble family's request, driving other teams out.
- ch. 66–67: About 500 explorers take refuge here during the Floor Master crisis; the clan charges 1 million stones per person, and a riot over confiscated gear breaks the defence.
- ch. 68: Vice-leader Iraz McGrane, "the Iron Lion", lets Riakis eat the orbs to save his people; 27 of his roughly 50 members die.
- ch. 69–70: Bjorn takes the Tarugas essence and, as a giant, leads clan members, Misha and about 40 explorers out; 30 survive. A crimson beam razes the encampment.
- ch. 457: Bjorn's party clears the hero trio in 35 minutes without loot, while the mage Lewis Siyur waits with a test tube.

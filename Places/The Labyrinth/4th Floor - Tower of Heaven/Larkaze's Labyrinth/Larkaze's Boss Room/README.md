## Description
A huge stone chamber at the end of a long straight corridor, with three entrances. On one side is a menacing stone door carved with geometric patterns that glow when it opens; beyond it lies a cave with a summoning circle on the floor and a brilliant red jewel set at its centre.

## Monsters
- [Larkaze](../../../../../Bestiary/minotaur_ch136.md) — master of the labyrinth, a named high-variant Minotaur about 4 m tall (roughly grade 4.5) with a double-edged axe

## Mechanics
- Its location in the maze is random (ch. 136).
- Iron bars drop over the three entrances on entry ("the master of the labyrinth senses challengers") and lift when Larkaze dies (ch. 136).
- Larkaze uses the Ifrit skill [Burning Hide], a fire aura whose damage builds over time; fire resistance and the [Cold Blood] spell counter it (ch. 136).
- No essence dropped; the real reward is the jewel "Tempered Wish" in the inner cave. There is no portal here (ch. 136).

## Notable events
- ch. 136–137: The party kills Larkaze in under 10 minutes; Regal Vagos appears at the boss room right afterwards.

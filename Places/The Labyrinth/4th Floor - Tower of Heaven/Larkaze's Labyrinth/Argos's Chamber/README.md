## Description
A torch-lit stone chamber in the 11 o'clock corner of Larkaze's Labyrinth, laid out like the Baphomet room, but instead of a goat's head it holds a reeking pile of corpses whose eyes have been gouged out. It is the corner closest to the boss room.

## Monsters
- [Argos](../../../../../Bestiary/argos_ch138.md) — high-variant mid-boss

## Mechanics
- Opened by a wall mechanism; the mid-boss is summoned by dropping blood on the magic circle (ch. 138).
- Drop: a scroll that seals one essence of a chosen target for 48 hours (ch. 138–139).

## Notable events
- ch. 138–139: The party kills Argos and uses the scroll to seal Vagos's Lava Giant essence.

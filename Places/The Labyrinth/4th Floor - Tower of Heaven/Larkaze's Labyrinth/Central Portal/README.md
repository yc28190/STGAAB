## Description
A shimmering portal at the centre of Larkaze's Labyrinth, the way out of the hidden field.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- The exit of the hidden field, marked by a blue dot on the maze map (ch. 136, 141).

## Notable events
- ch. 138–141: Regal Vagos sits at the portal for 12 hours, then leaves Hector Ludwig to guard it so no one can escape.
- ch. 141–144: Ludwig watches the battle from here through visions sent by Karui.

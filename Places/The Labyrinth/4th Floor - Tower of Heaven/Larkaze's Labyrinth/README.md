## Description
A square maze hidden within the Tower of Heaven. When the entrance door closes, total darkness falls, then lifts to reveal straight corridors branching in every direction, lit by torches hung at regular intervals and heavy with an eerie mood. Each corner of the square hides a secret stone chamber with a geometric magic circle on its floor, and many other hidden rooms dot the maze.

It is one of three hidden fields of the 4th floor, home to herds of 6th-grade Vykuntus and ruled by the Minotaur Larkaze. It is remembered as the place where Dwarkey died fighting the Dragonslayer Regal Vagos.

## Mechanics
- Reached only at a special stage where all three doors after a trial are Stairs of Fate; three Fate doors normally never appear together, and Fate itself appears less than 5% of the time (ch. 132–133).
- Guides' abilities do not work inside (ch. 133).
- Field effect "Guardian of Balance": everyone's total stats are pooled and shared equally, which favours mages; it updates whenever someone enters, so a sudden stat change reveals an intruder (ch. 133–135).
- Failing to clear it does not trap you; hidden fields share the closing timer of their floor (ch. 133, 508).
- Each corner hides a switch opening a stone chamber; dropping blood on its circle summons one of four random mid-bosses (ch. 134–135, 138).
- Other hidden rooms hold one-time consumables or rare monsters (ch. 138).
- The final boss room appears at a random spot; the exit portal stands at the centre of the maze (ch. 136, 141).
- Final reward: the jewel "Tempered Wish" (ch. 136–138).
- An exit also runs through a trap room with a portal back to the Tower of Heaven (ch. 146).
- Added as a hidden field after Armella first defeated the Harmonizer Gregory (ch. 508).

- ch. 144–145: Ludwig gives his body to Karui to open a black dimensional gate that pushes Vagos out to Noark.
- ch. 345–346, 376, 565, 807: Recalled as where Bjorn first met the Dragonslayer and where his gamble changed others' fates.

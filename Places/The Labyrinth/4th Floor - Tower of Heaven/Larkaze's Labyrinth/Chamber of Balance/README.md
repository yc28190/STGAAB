# Chamber of Balance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | spiritual space |
| **Also known as** | Tempered Wish space |
| **First appearance** | ch. 138 |

## Description
A spiritual space reached through the jewel "Tempered Wish". It resembles a Tower of Heaven chamber, lit by a bluish flame floating in mid-air, with three stone doors at its edges. Each door bears an uncoloured mural of one of the user's absorbed essence monsters, with a jewel above it in that essence's colour (black, green, or rainbow for a guardian). A short corridor beyond leads to three more doors bearing murals of a Physical-type, a Mental-type and an Ability-type monster.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Entered by crushing the "Tempered Wish" jewel (ch. 138).
- The user sacrifices one essence of 4th grade or lower and receives a choice of random essences of the same grade; a guardian essence raises the grade on offer, and an essence "that no one currently possesses" brings better options (ch. 138).

## Notable events
- ch. 138–139: Bjorn sacrifices his Vampire (guardian) essence and is offered Lich (guardian), Soul Colossus (guardian) and Ogre; he takes the Ogre.

# Trap Room

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | room (hidden chamber) |
| **Also known as** | Trap Room (Larkaze's Labyrinth); small hidden stone chamber |
| **First appearance** | ch. 138 |

## Description
A tiny stone chamber of 3–4 m² behind a hidden wall in Larkaze's Labyrinth. There are no monsters and nothing to see except a single chest.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Opening its chest sends the party back to the maze's starting point; Bjorn kept it in reserve as an escape route (ch. 143).
- It took the enemy about 4 hours to reach it from the boss area (ch. 139).
- A portal from the trap room leads back to the Tower of Heaven (ch. 146).

## Notable events
- ch. 138–139: The party rests here before the final fight with Vagos, and Bjorn uses the Tempered Wish.
- ch. 143: Bjorn holds the chest as a fallback in case Vagos and the priest attack together.

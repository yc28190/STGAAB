## Description
The second rift of the 4th floor, a lower-tier rift famous because "money pours out" of it. In the game it opens in a dense forest where missions reveal a temple; seemingly endless entrance stairs descend to a corridor and a golden stone gate opening onto a main hall with golden walls, lit by golden light from the ceiling when the gate opens.

A replica of the rift was later found outside the city walls. There, everything was already open and abandoned: thick dust covered every surface, the golden walls were chipped and broken, and the ceiling light never came on, leaving the halls dark.

## Monsters
- [[Makairo]] — hidden mid-boss, grade 4 (found already dead in the replica)
- [[Kalpion]] — rift guardian, summoned by the side-gate devices
- [[Snake]] — mid-boss with a lever in its room
- [[Mummy]] — in the right-wing room

## Mechanics
- Forest missions reveal the temple; descending the entrance stairs closes the entrance behind you (ch. 687–689).
- Climbing back up the stairs for a long time reveals Makairo's chamber, the hidden piece (ch. 687–689).
- In the main hall, devices in the side gates summon the guardian and open the treasure room, which normally holds three Numbered Items, one chosen with a key (ch. 689).
- Other pieces include a lever in the snake mid-boss room, a golden orb under a lion mural and an eagle candlestick in the trap room (ch. 689).
- In the replica the walls resisted even [Star's Vanishing]; only a few weak spots took physical damage (ch. 693).
- Multi-teleport preparation was possible inside; the party escaped using Return Scrolls found on Briot (ch. 695).

## Description
A tower of stacked trial stages connected by staircases. Each stage is a stone chamber of about 40 m² with a tightly shut door in each wall, every door carved with a symbol; between trials the routes pass through abyss-like darkness. Some chambers are much larger, like the troll chamber on stage 12. Explorers sleep on the stone floor between fights.

The floor is an independent instance: once a team's portal changes colour, no one else can follow, which makes it one of the safest floors for resting and a refuge even when war rages on the lower floors. Because every 3rd-floor monster appears here alongside rank 6 monsters and some unique to the tower, Bjorn calls it the "Tower of Training".

## Mechanics
- Reached through the portal at the centre of the 3rd-floor Witch's Forest; each team is taken to its own separate space (ch. 82, 164).
- Mostly rank 7–8 monsters, with rank 6 monsters about once every three days; 5th-grade monsters rarely appear above stage 100 (ch. 53, 89, 117).
- Each stage is a trial: a mixed group of monsters attacks and the doors open when all are dead (first trial: seven 9th-grade, three 8th-grade and one 7th-grade) (ch. 116).
- Doors: Courage (monster stage, then a 4-hour wait; the symbol lights when ready), Wisdom (a trap-and-monster maze skipping about 5 stages, no cooldown), Fate (many variables) and Patience (heat, cold, sadness and bad memories; widely avoided) (ch. 116, 163).
- Taking only Courage would need 16+ days to reach stage 100; it is only worth hunting from about stage 20, and difficulty stops rising at stage 100 (ch. 116–117, 163).
- Monsters do not respawn and no one else can enter, so teams can rest without a watch; a chamber door closes if no one stays inside (ch. 116, 164).
- After stage 100 a Guide can sense which doors to open, in order, to reach the portal room, a boss room with a stele that always holds a 5th-grade monster; fewer than half of explorers reaching it are said to survive (ch. 165).
- Trials get harder from 6 people (bigger mazes, more monsters) and waits grow from 7 people, so big clans split into teams of five and regroup on the 5th floor (ch. 263).
- Rifts appear only from stage 25 up and are announced by the whole tower shaking; the two rifts are the Doppelganger Forest and the Golden Ruins (ch. 178–179, 790).
- Combat cannot be skipped here, unlike on lower floors (ch. 790).
- The floor closes at the end of Day 23, returning everyone to Rafdonia (ch. 89, 118).

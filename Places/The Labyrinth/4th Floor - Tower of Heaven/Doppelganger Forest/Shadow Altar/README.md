## Description
The hidden altar of the Doppelganger Forest. When it is found, a black shadow envelops everyone and a giant barrier with a radius of about 500 m rises around it. It is the stage for the rift's trust game: unmasking the expedition member possessed by the sealed marauder.

## Monsters
- [[Doppelganger]] — the sealed marauder, hidden inside one expedition member

## Mechanics
- "The sealed marauder" secretly possesses one expedition member; offering the right person at the altar destroys it, while offering the wrong person seals their soul above the altar until the rift is cleared (ch. 184).
- A Stone of Truth appears at the altar every midnight and forces one truthful answer, but each use strengthens the Doppelganger and raises the later unsealing rate by 10% (ch. 184, 187).
- If the host dies the Doppelganger is released; doppelgangers are good at faking death (ch. 186).
- The item No. 7234 Misplaced (Distorted) Trust stops anyone within 10 m from lying for 10 minutes (ch. 184).
- Doppelgangers cannot approach the altar, making it a safe spot (ch. 188).

## Notable events
- ch. 184–186: The Noark dwarf is exposed; Hans Krisen and the fairy archer Meilin (both players) side with him. Bjorn kills the dwarf and the survivors surrender and are executed.
- ch. 186–187: The possessed turns out to be the Narman-school mage Kyleman Parteian, who was faking death; a potion exposes him, and pressing his head to the altar ends chapter 3 as the shadow escapes.
- ch. 188: The new Guide, Miss Sarman, waits safely by the altar.

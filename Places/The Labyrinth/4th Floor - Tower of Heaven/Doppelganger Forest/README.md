## Description
One of the two rifts of the 4th floor, entered through a violently shimmering green portal in a tower chamber. It unfolds in chapters: a dark cave system (Shadow Cave), then a forest that turns dark in the final chapter, the hidden Shadow Altar, and finally the guardian's cold cave beneath a sinkhole. In the last chapter the forest fills with doppelganger clones disguised as soldiers chanting about "the Empire".

Its theme is mistrust: doppelgangers copy expedition members' skills and personalities, the sealed "marauder" secretly possesses a participant, and teams must decide whom to trust. Its guardian is the only source of the rare [Self-Replication] essence.

## Mechanics
- Up to 3 teams (15 people) enter through three portals; each team starts in a giant cage that opens once the rift is full (about 5 hours here) (ch. 179).
- Chapters: Shadow Cave, the forest, the Shadow Altar, then the guardian (ch. 180–188).
- Field effect "Doppelganger Forest" applies [Distrust]: damage dealt to party members is doubled (ch. 182).
- In the forest the goal is to kill the three mid-bosses and find the hidden altar (ch. 182).
- Teams compete for loot until the final boss, which they fight together; only the boss drops magic stones or essences (ch. 180).
- Chapter 4: the freed Doppelganger flees and the seal weakens by about 1% every 10 minutes; at 100% the boss is at full power. Clone soldiers (archers, spearmen, swordsmen, mages, crossbowmen, shield warriors) with high stats but no skills fill the forest (ch. 188).
- White Sealing Stones that emit an eerie sound appear across the forest; each one activated cuts the unsealing rate by 10%, and one is always hidden near the sinkhole entrance. The [Record of Offerings] at the altar recommends splitting the party between the boss and the stones (ch. 188).
- Clearing it opens a portal back to the 4th floor; the cleared rift can still be searched for herbs (about 10,000 stones per root), a Mandrake (elixir ingredient, must be pulled with protection) and Sealed Shards of Evil from destroyed Sealing Stones (about 1 million stones each; eating one gives +1 Magic Resistance, up to 5) (ch. 190–191).
- The Numbered Item No. 8667 Outlaw of the Wasteland was obtained here (ch. 204, 226).

The dwarf wins the Manticore essence, Bjorn later kills him, the possessed Kyleman Parteian is exposed, and the guardian is defeated without a drop. Apple Nark returns to the tower on Day 14.

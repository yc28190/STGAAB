## Description
A duel space conjured by a priest of the evil god Karui. Everything goes dark, then lights up as if a switch were flipped, and the combatants find themselves standing on the palm of a skyscraper-sized statue of Karui whose face looms above them. It is not a fixed location; it is recorded here because it appeared inside the Doppelganger Forest.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Created by an ability exclusive to priests of Karui; it pulls two mutually hostile targets into an isolated duel (ch. 185).
- They return only when one wins or the ability is cancelled from outside; a dead body is sent back to where it started (ch. 185).

## Notable events
- ch. 185: Bjorn kills the Noark dwarf here and takes the Guardian Corps Insignia (No. 2988) and the Manticore essence.

## Description
The guardian's lair, reached by dropping into a sinkhole that appears in the forest. A cold, foggy cave leads along a straight passage of about forty steps into a low-ceilinged cavern whose floor is covered by a giant magic circle, with Stones of Truth scattered about like toys. In the middle crouches what looks like a crying child, its eyes, nose and mouth upside down.

## Monsters
- [Nameless Marauder](../../../../../Bestiary/doppelganger_ch177.md) — the 4th-grade Doppelganger guardian (boss)
- [Doppelganger](../../../../../Bestiary/doppelganger_ch177.md) — clones spawned throughout the fight

## Mechanics
- Phase 1: the boss copies each person in turn, adding 5th-grade monster stats; [Flesh Explosion] spawns clones, and about every 10 clones raise the unsealing rate by about 5%, signalled by the floor circle flashing (ch. 188–189).
- Phase 2: [Swap] trades places with a clone copying one person; the real one can be spotted with a Stone of Truth or because Doppelgangers cannot copy Numbered Items (ch. 189).
- Phase 3 (berserk): mass [Self-Replication] fills the cave with weak clones, then the main body (which can grow two heads) uses [Crystallization], cutting magic damage by 99% and physical by 90%; if the crystal is not broken in time the cycle repeats (ch. 189).
- At 100% unsealing every clone becomes as strong as the boss and can multiply (ch. 189).
- Reward: 33% chance of an essence or Numbered Item, 66% chance of a Rift Stone (ch. 190).

## Notable events
- ch. 188–190: With only seven people, Apple Nark commits fully to the boss; Raven's stone wall funnels clones toward Bjorn, who uses Outlaw of the Wasteland (up to 771% melee damage) with [Leap], [Wild Release] and [Swing] to shatter the crystal in about a minute under the mages' Mana Shields. The first clones were all Hans Krisen; nothing dropped.

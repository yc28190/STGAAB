## Description
The first chapter of the Doppelganger Forest: dark cave passages with long straight corridors that, after about half a day, reach a five-way fork. Weeds and moss grow thicker on the walls toward the centre, a vast cavern holding an altar.

## Monsters
- [Doppelganger](../../../../../Bestiary/doppelganger_ch177.md) — clones of expedition members

## Mechanics
- Teams start in different places and meet in the central cavern; arriving first lets a team claim rewards along the other routes (ch. 181).
- The main reward is magic-stone chests scattered through the cave; a hidden-piece room exists but was not found (ch. 181).
- Following the weeds leads to the centre (ch. 181).
- When everyone touches the central altar together, vines part to reveal an uphill path to the forest (ch. 181–182).

## Notable events
- ch. 181: Bjorn smashes Hans Krisen doppelgangers on sight, fearing the "Hans jinx".

## Description
A corridor about 100 m long in the Tower of Heaven that dissolves, step by step, into illusions drawn from each walker's past, such as a hospital ward, a burning apartment or an amusement park. People from one's life appear and say hurtful things.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- An illusion trial: reaching the end completes it, and a staircase follows (ch. 179).
- It drains a great deal of stamina; most explorers avoid this door (ch. 116, 179).

## Notable events
- ch. 179: Raven is badly shaken, while the barbarians and Avman are mostly unaffected.

## Description
A peaceful land of lush green hills and meadows opening onto a vast plain, with a warm breeze and a pleasant scent — a shock after the cold of Ice Rock. It is probably the smallest field in the Labyrinth, and the portal to the Dark Continent can be seen from the hill.

Outside its rifts it has no monsters; the floor's real content is the single rift that is always open. Noark held the floor alone for over a year, using it to raise high-level fighters. It shares its name with the witch pilgrims' paradise, the Land of Dawn.

## Monsters
- Floor Lord — the 8th-floor Floor Lord, killed by Noark on its last expedition.
- [Dragon](../../../Bestiary/dragon_ch137.md)s — appear as Rift Guardians.

## Mechanics
- Achievement "Reached 8th Floor": Soul Power +50 permanently, a "Baptism" from the Tower (ch. 406).
- No monsters outside the rift; the portal to the Dark Continent is about 30 minutes' walk from the hill (ch. 406).
- One rift of random type is always open; it opens as a warm spring breeze swirling into a vortex of light. Clearing it opens two portals (on to the 9th floor, or back to the 8th) and a new rift spawns (ch. 406, 414).
- Holding the exit of the 7th-floor portal ("entrance blocking") kills anyone coming through (ch. 414).
- The skill [Iron Fist] comes from an 8th-floor rift (ch. 420).
- Dragons appear as Rift Guardians (ch. 785).

## Notable events
- ch. 406–407: The expedition arrives; Noark's elite force is farming the rift.
- ch. 414: After clearing the rift, Vagos's elites chase the expedition into Ice Rock; 8 of his men die to entrance blocking.
- ch. 502: Clown reports at the Round Table that Noark killed the Floor Lord.
- ch. 618: Hamsik wishes to live in the pilgrims' Land of Dawn reading books.
- ch. 777–789: Noark's year-long push here is recalled; Anabada sets the 8th-floor rift as its expedition goal.

## Description
An 8th-floor rift shaped as a radiant city of light, ringed by an outer wall and watched by Guardian Angels, with a Heavenly Magic Circle and a final boss room.

It is a top-secret rift, known to fewer than 50 people.

## Monsters
- Mirayel — Heavenly Spear Bearer (also Milayel/Maliel); rift guardian in the final boss room; uses Numbers items.
- [Guardian Angel](../../../../Bestiary/guardian_angel_ch541.md)
- Legion of Light spirits — pour in during phase 3.

## Mechanics
- Mirayel's phases: phase 1 Return; phase 2 Blessing of Light plus Comet Split; phase 3 Descent — he takes flight, the outer wall collapses and the Legion of Light floods in, followed by Shell Break and the spear's true form (ch. 537, 541).
- Mirayel can drop No. 7 Verdant Spear (under 3%) (ch. 537).
- Guardians drop guardian essences, rift stones and Numbers items (33%) (ch. 541).

## Notable events
- ch. 544–545: A Noark elite team fails this rift with heavy losses.

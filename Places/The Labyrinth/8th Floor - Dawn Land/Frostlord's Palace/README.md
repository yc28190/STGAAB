## Description
An 8th-floor rift ruled by the Frostlord, mentioned only in recollections.

## Monsters
- [Frostlord](../../../../Bestiary/frostlord_ch27.md) — rift guardian; his essence was a farming target.

## Mechanics
- Reached through a rift on the 8th floor (ch. 27).
- From the 8th floor up, every rift has high variants (ch. 33).

## Notable events
- ch. 27: The chief nearly died here in his youth; Bjorn recalls farming it in the game for the Frostlord's essence.
- ch. 450: Gudnulf Olga raided it when young and earned a big-clan offer.

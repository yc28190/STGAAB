## Description
The last floor of the labyrinth, home of the Gate of the Abyss. In the game it was the portal to the final boss room, and passing through it is what pulled Bjorn into this world. Its difficulty is hellish: most 1st-grade monsters live only here, some only in its hidden fields. It is the ultimate goal of the game and of Bjorn's journey. Rumor holds that opening the Gate lets you return to your original world, though the Witch of the Land warns that it must never be opened.

## Monsters
- [[Abyssal Skeleton]] — 10th-floor monster
- [[Tol-Lafupa]] — found in the floor's hidden fields
- [[Sniktura]] — one of the many monsters here
- About 80% of 1st-grade monsters appear only on this floor, including a luck-dependent monster that drops the [Airship] essence (ch. 508, 799)

## Mechanics
- In the game, reaching it asked "Gate of the Abyss – Do you want to enter?", warned "You may not be able to return again", then showed "You have reached the abyss. Tutorial complete" and sent the player into this world (ch. 1, 3, 455, 679).
- The six Genesis Treasures (Genesis Artifacts), one per race, are the keys to the final floor. At least the barbarians' one was stolen, and reportedly none still exist (ch. 43, 198). The 6-person Bonding spell would raise the number of "keys" to six (ch. 197).
- The floor is reached through the Dreamlike Waterfall (ch. 674).
- When Abyss Seeker Lud El Rimenin first reached it, the souls of all the Lords woke and Floor Lords began to appear. Rifts started after Floor Lords were defeated (ch. 235, 491, 601).
- Its last section has a cruel puzzle in which failure wipes the whole party (ch. 618).
- Opening the Gate is one way back to Earth. Dimensional magic, the method Auril Gavis used, is another (ch. 471–472, 647).
- Reaching the floor after beating Skull Island's boss grants "something special" (ch. 792).
- The final-tier navigator essence [Airship] drops here (ch. 799). Bjorn's game strategy was to avoid combat and run for the Gate (ch. 801).

## Notable events
- ch. 1–3: In the game, Bjorn opens the Gate and wakes up in this world.
- ch. 436–437: Lee Baekho asks for Bjorn's help reaching the 10th Floor and suspects the Earth Witch waits there as the final boss.
- ch. 570–576: Ibaekho says Bjorn must break through the last floor to get his wish. Auril Gavis offers other players a "ticket home", but not Bjorn.
- ch. 585: The Witch of the Land warns, "You must not open the Abyssal Gate."
- ch. 681: Ruin Genesis says Bjorn is fated to open it. He and Ibaekho have a "Plan B" for going home without it.

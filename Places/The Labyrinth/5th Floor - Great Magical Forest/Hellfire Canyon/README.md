## Description
A fire-themed canyon field. Paths about 10 m wide, like suspension bridges, slope downward past cliffs radiating intense heat, with lava flowing far below; the trails fork again and again.

It was Team Apple Nark's hunting ground for six months, prized for the rare Millarodden whose mirror opens the hidden Mirror of Fire.

## Mechanics
- Field effect "Hellfire Canyon" applies [Embers Mark]: every resource cost (Soul Power, mana, divine power, stamina) is 1.5×, and Fire Resistance −30; Raven's [Cold Blood] counters it (ch. 166).
- The Orb of Fire blocks fire damage here (ch. 205, 214).
- Hidden piece: where red and green moss meet with a marble-sized black moss between them, a detection spell cast after the black moss vanishes reveals a Millarodden every time (ch. 168).
- Distortion keeps a Millarodden corpse only about 20% of the time (under 30% with High-Grade Distortion); its mirror opens the Mirror of Fire (ch. 168, 191, 205).
- One of the four paths to the 6th floor (ch. 264).

## Notable events

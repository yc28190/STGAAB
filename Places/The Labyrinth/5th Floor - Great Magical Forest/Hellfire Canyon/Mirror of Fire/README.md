## Description
A world inside a Millarodden's mirror: a vast wasteland opening onto several fields, the Burning Forest, the Ash Gray Mountains, the Lava Lake and the Altar of the Dead. Magic stones here are darker than usual. A charred diary found inside suggests the world was once home to survivors from another dimension, destroyed "at the Witch's hand".

It is an open world with no guardian, and a lucrative hunting ground that clans also camp in.

## Mechanics
- Entered by channelling mana into a Millarodden's mirror after casting Bonding (ch. 168, 205, 380).
- Field effect "Otherworld": magic-stone drop rate greatly increased and essence drop slightly increased; stone density is about double (ch. 168–169).
- No guardian; monsters come in waves at first, and a cleared field takes a day to respawn, becoming a temporary safe zone (ch. 169).
- Compasses do not work; the exit portal is at the Altar of the Dead (ch. 169).
- Only one item can be taken out, by casting Distortion on it once (ch. 169).
- Inside the mirror, explorers are safe from other players (ch. 176).

## Notable events
- ch. 169–172: Apple Nark clears the Burning Forest, finds the Burnt Brick House and the Orb of Fire, and earns about 25 million stones in magic stones in about 8 days.
- ch. 205: One later run yields a 5th-grade essence worth 8.5 million stones per member; the team finishes the Ash Gray Mountains and Lava Lake over six months.

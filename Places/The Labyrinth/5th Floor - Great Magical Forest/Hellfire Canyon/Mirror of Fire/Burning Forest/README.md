## Description
A forest of widely spaced trees wreathed in black flames that burn all year round, giving it a desolate beauty. A giant stele stands in its central area, matched by similar steles in the other mirror fields. It is the easiest field in the Mirror of Fire.

## Monsters
- [Veritas](../../../../../../Bestiary/veritas_ch169.md) — 5th-grade rare demon (two horns, over 3 m tall, black wings over its upper body, four arms); appears only here
- 6th–7th grade monsters — general population

## Mechanics
- Fire damage is negligible with [Cold Blood] (ch. 169).
- Veritas appears only here (a clan sees one about once a year) and always drops an essence with [Mark of Explosion], [Wings of Greed], [Legion of Hatred] and [Doom Ray] (ch. 169–170).
- The central stele is just a landmark (ch. 171).
- A hidden item near the centre (in the Burnt Brick House) makes the other mirror fields clearable and opens access to an island on the 6th floor (ch. 169, 171).

## Notable events
- ch. 170: Ainar absorbs the Veritas essence.
- ch. 171–172: The party finds the Burnt Brick House and the Orb of Fire.

# Burnt Brick House

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | building |
| **Also known as** | survivors' house |
| **First appearance** | ch. 171 |

## Description
A two-storey brick house about two hours' search from the Burning Forest's central stele. Its wooden door is charred and the interior is a ruin, with sofa and walls burned to their frames; a bedroom sits on the upper floor. A cleared passage leads down to a basement storeroom of about 3 m² holding a valuable-looking box.

It was built by survivors from another dimension who waited in vain for rescue.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- A bedroom drawer holds a charred diary in the ancient language with four readable pages (ch. 171).
- The basement box holds the Orb of Fire, which binds to whoever touches it first; activated by a command word, it reduces fire damage within 15 m at the cost of Soul Power (ch. 171–172).

## Notable events
- ch. 171: The diary tells of survivors (the narrator, Zephyros and the mage Tarvian, who became pregnant) who built the house while the Orb protecting it failed and their world ended "at the Witch's hand"; they ask the finder to use the basement item to save the world.
- ch. 172: Raven resolves to research the Orb; clans that own Orbs keep their source secret.

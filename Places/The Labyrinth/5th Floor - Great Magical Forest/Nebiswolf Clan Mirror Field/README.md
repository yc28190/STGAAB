## Description
A 5th-floor field arranged around a giant mirror glowing red, through which monsters from the mirror world enter the labyrinth. It is held by the Nebiswolf Clan, about 30 members under a blue wolf emblem.

## Monsters
- Monsters from the mirror world — pour out of the mirror
- [[Veritas]] — rare 5th-grade demon that can come through

## Mechanics
- Monsters from the other world enter the labyrinth through this mirror (ch. 170).
- The clan hunts in 24-hour shifts, drives other explorers off, sells cheap essences to nearby teams and charges for experience (ch. 170).

## Notable events
- ch. 170: Bjorn pulls Veritas back to his side of the mirror before it can come through to the clan.

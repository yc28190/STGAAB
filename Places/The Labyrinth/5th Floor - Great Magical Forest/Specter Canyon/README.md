## Description
A dark canyon of cliffs, ledges and ravines that trembles periodically and echoes with the howls of specters like background music, leaving visitors with the unpleasant feeling that their skin has grown thin. At one three-way split a gap of over 30 m must be crossed.

It is the shortest route through the 5th floor to the 6th, but a poor hunting ground.

## Mechanics
- Field effect "Specter Canyon" (Canyon of Wraiths): the ground shakes violently every few minutes, and the status [Bound Spirit] / [Earthbound Spirit] gives −100 Darkness and Physical Resistance (ch. 264, 790).
- Abilities open shortcuts: Bjorn uses Leap to cross the 30 m gap and strings a sloped rope the others slide down on hooks (ch. 264).
- Monsters attack about every 20 minutes at night (ch. 264).
- The Soul Eater has spawn conditions, spawns only once per opening and gives 100–200 random EXP on first kill (ch. 264).
- About 10 days to reach the bottom, then half a day more to the portal (ch. 264).

## Notable events
- ch. 264: The party takes this route to the 6th floor; Bjorn kills the Soul Eater for 177 EXP, reaching level 6.
- ch. 790: Compared unfavourably with the Frost and Hellfire Canyons as a hunting ground.

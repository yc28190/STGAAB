## Description
An open-world floor shaped like a flat, tray-like rock (the starting point) with four "slides" running off it. Each slide splits into dozens of narrow, maze-like paths that break off and rejoin, sometimes needing ropes to cross, while wide disc-shaped named fields sit among them, some reached through caves in the cliffs. The starting point glows red like a butcher's shop, and the labyrinth's darkness lies beyond. Despite its ominous name it is a relatively safe layer, with canyons descending from the centre.

The starting area is a safe zone used as a campsite, at times as large as a village and crowded with clan tents. From this floor explorers work in clans; most fields used to be held by clans that charged for hunts, until the war with Noark wiped most of them out.

## Mechanics
- Reaching the floor grants a "Baptism": +20 Soul Power for the achievement, plus mana for mages and divine power for priests (ch. 166, 406).
- The starting point is a safe zone where explorers camp and leave markers for teammates (ch. 166, 790).
- Monsters spawn from mirrors in each field and are released into the canyons (ch. 168).
- Clans traditionally control most fields and charge for one-time hunts (ch. 167, 350).
- Four paths lead to the 6th floor; which are open depends on the party's abilities (ch. 264).
- Passages are too narrow for Luberta's Dove Boots and combat cannot be avoided (ch. 397–398).
- The floor closes after Day 30 (ch. 165).
- Floor Master: the Lord of Silence. His Essence of Silence grants [Flameless Spirit], a 30 m silence cancelling every skill except the caster's own (undispellable, ineffective on monsters; a Bond spell makes an ally immune), and the passive Silent Control, which disables the user's other essence skills and doubles essence stats; fewer than ten people in history have held it (ch. 320, 324–326).
- Floor lords appeared only after the Abyss Seeker Remenin reached the 10th floor (ch. 601).
- The rare hidden boss Harmonizer Gregory can appear; Armella Powell Merhenia's first kill opened Larkaze's Labyrinth (ch. 503).
- Better than the 3rd floor for farming Magic Crystals (ch. 505).
- Killing a Millarodden yields the mirror into the Otherworld hidden field (ch. 168, 380).
- Its rifts include the Soul Fortress, Burning Forest and Rift of Light (ch. 601).

## Notable events
- ch. 166: Many groups arrive short-handed after the 4th-floor attacks; the safe zone becomes a village of tents.
- ch. 176, 191: The war with Noark wipes out over 90% of field-holding clans, leaving the floor crowded with monsters.
- ch. 263: In July no clans control the hunting grounds, and normally paywalled monsters roam freely.
- ch. 324: Bjorn says he killed the Lord of Silence about 300 times in the game.
- ch. 599: 5th-floor explorers are described as the most sought-after grooms.
- ch. 790–791: Anabada reaches the 6th floor on Day 16–17, a record pace; the Meiruta Clan's leader is executed here for an attempted raid.

## Description
A multi-floor dimension reached through the portals of the Dimensional Plazas, and the center of the explorers' world. The floors range from the 1st Floor's Crystal Cave up to the 10th Floor (the Abyss), with hidden basement floors below. Each floor is its own landscape (caves, deserts, forests, an ocean, star fields), and many explorers believe they are copies of real places outside the city made by someone; Clown says the land outside matches the 7th Floor.

The royal family presents the labyrinth as a passage to another dimension that they created, though some scholars suspect the Archmage discovered rather than built it. Explorers hunt monsters here for magic stones and essences, the kingdom's economic lifeblood. Inside, explorers usually avoid one another, pair up as temporary "night friends" to keep watch, and fear pillagers, since what happens inside goes unseen and unpunished.

## Mechanics
- Explorers enter through the portal in the Dimensional Plaza. The starting point is random unless the party has binding magic (ch. 4, 22).
- Each floor stays open for a set time and then sends everyone home: 1st floor 168 hours (7 days), 2nd floor 240 hours, and longer higher up. The 5th floor closes after Day 30 and the 7th after Day 75 (ch. 13, 166, 366).
- The number of monsters per group grows each day (ch. 8, 23). Hunger and thirst apply only inside (ch. 6).
- Each monster type gives experience only the first time it is killed. Opening a portal for the first time is a repeatable achievement worth 2 EXP (ch. 22).
- Essences drop very rarely in normal areas and much more often in rifts. Stairway and portal areas swallow light (ch. 24, 31).
- Monster categories include variants, higher species, rare species and high variants. High variants appear in the 3rd Floor's White Temple and in every rift from the 8th Floor up (ch. 33).
- "Homeostasis": damaged terrain is restored when the portal next opens (ch. 162).
- From ch. 177, Lafdonian explorers carry identification tags: steel pendants with the royal emblem and a rank number (1 low, 2 mid, 3 high). Anyone without a tag can be killed without penalty, knights check tags, and some people kill explorers to steal theirs (ch. 177–182).
- Players can open rifts intentionally (ch. 374). The Bonding update made six-person teams standard (ch. 351). Hidden achievements exist, such as "Mythical Discovery (1/5)" for Soul Power +10 (ch. 381).
- The royal council sealed the labyrinth because the mysterious mage might activate Viphron's dimensional plaza. The Marquis compares the labyrinth to a "server" and portals to "logins" (ch. 645, 657).
- After a long closure, rift spawn rates spike abnormally, according to records of the Iron Wall Rebellion and of the Dawn King creating the 13th Month (ch. 789). Hidden pieces run on cooldowns (ch. 789).

## Notable events
- ch. 3–5: Bjorn's first entry into the labyrinth.
- ch. 645–657: After Noark's invasion the labyrinth is sealed. Prices crash, explorers can't pay their taxes, and protests follow.
- ch. 773–774: It stays closed for about a year after Viphron's fall.
- ch. 789–790: The labyrinth reopens. The royal force aims to take all three prime farming islands on the 6th Floor, while Anabada targets the 8th-floor rift.

## Description
The last floor before the 10th, where the Abyss Gate lies. Meteor showers fall periodically, and the floor is so lethal that it is said anyone can die there just by breathing. It is divided into five fields: the Primeval Land at the center, the Old Rock Grassland to the west, the Deadly Poison Lava Field to the north, the Dreamlike Waterfall to the east and Dragon Bone Mountain to the south.

Few have explored it — Titana Akuraba's legendary team and the Golden Generation among them — and Noark has begun conquering it. A sealed replica of the floor exists outside the city walls (see Star Grave Replica).

## Mechanics
- Reached by clearing the 8th-floor rift (ch. 406).
- The Stone of Resurrection can be obtained here, but only under very specific conditions with an absurdly low rate (ch. 198, 340, 780).
- The portal to the 10th floor lies beyond the Dreamlike Waterfall (ch. 674).
- An Ice Dual Swordsman is useful in the latter half of the floor (ch. 436).
- [Hands of Regret] (1st-grade skill) comes from an essence found on the 9th floor or higher; the Abyssal Tongue, sold for a record 3.2 billion stones to the Mage Tower, came from a 9th-floor rift (ch. 488, 499).
- Its cracks occasionally yield the "Holy Altar" holy potion (ch. 539).
- Lamansis appears only by using Gavrilius's hidden arrangement inside a rift; its essence's latent [Best Friend] blesses one nearby target and the user at maximum affection (ch. 639).
- A 9th-floor rift is the only source of the Crimson Formalwear (lets the wearer reshape their body), behind a bizarre hidden-piece condition (ch. 775).

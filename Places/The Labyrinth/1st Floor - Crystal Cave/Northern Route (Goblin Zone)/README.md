## Description
Crystal-lit cave passages in the north of the floor, with rocks to hide behind and crude goblin traps left in plain view, narrowing toward the north portal to the Goblin Forest. A pond beside a large rock is a common resting spot. During the Noark war, the passages were strewn with knights' and soldiers' corpses used as bait and lookouts, and later sank into the pitch-black Abyssal Fog, where hundreds of glowing eyes appeared as Verzak drew near.

## Monsters
- [[Goblin]] — ambush from beside their badly hidden traps; numbers rise daily until the floor closes
- [[Abyssal Goblin]] — 5th grade; lays undetectable traps and throws Monster Pouches
- [[Abyssal Watcher]] — 9th-grade giant floating eye
- [[Verzak]] — Lord of the Abyss
- [[Troll]] — released from a Monster Pouch
- Corpse puppets — the Corpse Collector's

## Mechanics
- Goblins attack from ambush near their traps, and their numbers rise every day until the floor closes (ch. 6, 8).
- During the war, the Corpse Collector guarded the passage to the 2nd-floor portal (ch. 208–210, 215).
- Abyssal Goblins lay [Random Trap]s that neither eyes nor magic can detect, set off about every 20 steps, each casting a random offensive or curse skill of 5th grade or below (e.g. Extinction Flame, Leech Flame, lightning). Their "Monster Pouches" release a random monster of 5th grade or below in front of the target. Casters and archers cannot survive at the front (ch. 222).
- Killing an Abyssal Watcher draws Verzak's strong interest (ch. 222).
- The north portal stayed open during the war and led to the Goblin Forest, Noark's chosen exit because it has no walls and is closest to the 3rd floor (ch. 222, 224).

## Notable events
- ch. 10–11: The Crystal Union gathers its members here.
- ch. 208–210: Team Apple Nark clashes with the Corpse Collector.
- ch. 222–224: Bjorn tanks hundreds of traps with his magic resistance, fights Verzak for 10 minutes, then escapes about 3 km by Mass Teleport to a pre-planted coordinate medium and goes through the north portal.

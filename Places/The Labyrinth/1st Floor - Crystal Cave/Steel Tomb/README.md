## Description
Narrow passages like the inside of a pyramid, except the walls are metal rather than stone, inscribed in an unknown language. It is dark enough to need a light spirit and full of traps.

## Mechanics
- A 1st-floor rift, opened here at the Great Sage Monument (ch. 456).
- Traps are normally disarmed carefully; Ainar simply set them off and tanked the damage (ch. 456).
- Two hidden pieces: Mercurial Liquid (Magic Resistance +3, does not stack) and Blessing of Iron (Physical Resistance +3, does not stack) (ch. 456).
- The final chamber can be reached in about 3 hours (ch. 456).

## Notable events
- ch. 456–457: The last 1st-floor rift Bjorn had not cleared. The boss drops No. 9999 Beginner's Luck, one of the "109 treasures", a charm that binds to its first owner (Ainar) and adds about 5% to the essence drop chance of the first monster killed.
- ch. 584: A boss-only version appears in the Hall of Guardian Statues.

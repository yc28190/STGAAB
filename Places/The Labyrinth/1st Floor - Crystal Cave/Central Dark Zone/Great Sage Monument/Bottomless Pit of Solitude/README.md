## Description
A sealed room in total darkness: the usual special field for the fourth phase of a small-party Dreadfear raid. Bjorn knew it from the game, but his party never reached it because the Pilgrim's Cave hidden piece triggered instead.

## Monsters
- [Dreadfear](../../../../../../Bestiary/dreadfear_ch212.md) — larger form, no longer constrained by the cave
- Token monsters — spawn in swarms

## Mechanics
- Reached through Special Condition "Ancient Memories" in a raid of five or fewer at phase 4 (ch. 482–483).
- Dreadfear grows larger, token monsters swarm, and he uses dangerous area and unavoidable skills, all with known counters (ch. 483).
- An escape route exists but only works just before the labyrinth closes (ch. 483).

## Notable events
- ch. 483: Bjorn recalls the field from the game.

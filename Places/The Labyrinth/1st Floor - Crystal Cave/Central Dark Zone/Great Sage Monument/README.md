## Description
A rare open chamber about 30 m across in the middle of the Crystal Cave, reached by three passages. At its centre stands an old stone monument with an altar, the only built structure in the labyrinth. Its inscription honours the "great first step" of Diflun Groundel Gavrilius (spellings vary), the last of the great sages and first discoverer of the labyrinth. The Immortal King built it, and Raven sees it as proof that space-time magic was once far more advanced.

It is both a hidden-piece shrine for opening the 1st-floor rift and the arena where the Floor Lord Dreadfear is raided.

## Monsters

## Mechanics
- Rift hidden piece: place mana stones from all four 1st-floor monsters (goblin, gnome, ghoul, blade wolf) plus one 8th-grade stone (e.g. from the Rocky Desert) on the altar, and a black portal to a 1st-floor rift opens if none is on cooldown. The 8th-grade stone decides which rift: a deathfiend stone opens the Blood Citadel. Cooldown is at least 3 cycles (3 months); the rift usually opens on its own every 5–6 cycles, and at most about 8 (ch. 27, 218, 456).
- Dreadfear phase 3 casts the aura [Veil of Betrayal]: Mental Power, Ability Power and Physical stats keep falling (loss amplified by 50%) while his Rage climbs. Everyone is in Stealth and invisible to each other, communication is blocked, and damage to non-monster targets is ×5 and ignores Resistance, making friendly fire deadly (ch. 481–482).
- Maxed stat losses inflict [Terror], [Lethargy] (no MP regen; active skills cancelled and put on cooldown) or [Exhaustion] (-30% Physical; actions cost MP instead of stamina). Max Rage doubles his damage (ch. 481).
- His skills include [Shell Game] (teleports a target), [Inner Mirror] (fixed damage scaled to lost Mental Power) and [Memories of the Dead] (floating bombs). When the aura lifts ("the light of trust dispels the darkness of fear"), he retreats into [Protective Shell], a groggy window for damage. Phase-3 buffs (five possible) raise his "Hatred", and max Hatred adds a very dangerous extra buff (ch. 481–482).
- Hidden piece: with five or fewer characters in range at phase 4, Special Condition "Ancient Memories" moves the party to the Bottomless Pit of Solitude. If a character has been poisoned with Goblin Paralyzing Poison, "Distorted Memories" sends them to the Pilgrim's Cave instead (ch. 482–483). Bjorn's clear permanently removed Distorted Memories (ch. 490).
- Five-man clears are legendary: only nine before Bjorn, including Rimenin, Armella, Mulmarin and the Last Sage (ch. 482).
- After the clear, the inscription was unchanged and touching the monument did nothing (ch. 503).

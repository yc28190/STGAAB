# Nameless Pilgrim's Chamber

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | spiritual space (final chamber) |
| **Also known as** | torch-lit cave with the stone altar |
| **First appearance** | ch. 489 |

## Description
After the Pilgrim's Cave collapses there is only a dark void. Then dozens of torches flare up on the walls of a cave, lighting a stone altar with a book resting on it.

## Monsters
- [[Nameless Pilgrim]] — the original Dreadfear, who seems to have a will of his own

## Mechanics
- Reading the ancient-language book plays the pilgrim's memory and permanently grants reading and writing of the ancient language; the book then burns to ash (ch. 489).
- The Nameless Pilgrim offers two stone doors: one undoes everything lost (dead companions return), the other makes it all real for a bigger reward. He claims killing him would give even more (ch. 489–490).

## Notable events
- ch. 489–490: Bjorn reads the book and takes the revival door. The Pilgrim hints that labyrinth monsters may be copies of originals and that what happened was his "avatar".

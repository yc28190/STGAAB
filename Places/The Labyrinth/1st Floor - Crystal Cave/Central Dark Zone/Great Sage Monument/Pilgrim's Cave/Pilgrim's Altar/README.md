## Description
Entered through a swirling purple fog, it is the Dreadfear raid chamber, now empty. Where the monument should be stands a statue of the Witch more than 4 m tall, shaped like a young girl: the one Bjorn met briefly after defeating Riakis. Its pedestal carries an ancient-language inscription about all being equal beneath one star, one sun and one moon, which the Centurion quotes.

## Monsters
- Centurion Argarsil Dreadfear — knight with white Aura, unbeatable head-on here
- Imperial soldiers

## Mechanics
- The system announces "Character has entered the Pilgrim's Altar." Touching the statue does nothing at first (ch. 486).
- Centurion skills: [Persuasion] (raises the target's Trust in his words), [Ownership Revocation] (freezes a target with a look), [Hour of Execution] (strong fear aura) and [Eternal Nightmare] (full heal below 15% HP) (ch. 486).
- He stabs every pilgrim, then offers a goblin-poisoned dagger: kill one fellow pilgrim and walk free (ch. 486).
- The altar wakes when a pilgrim's blood touches the statue ("an unknown entity embraces the noble pilgrim's soul"), freeing the pilgrims as the cave shakes (ch. 486).

## Notable events
- ch. 485: Erwen and Ainar are brought here as captives.
- ch. 486: Bersil cuts her own throat to awaken the altar.

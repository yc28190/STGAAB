## Description
An exact copy of the Crystal Cave lit by glowing crystals. The pilgrims see clearly, but the light blinds the Imperial soldiers, to whom the cave looks dark. Each pilgrim wakes at one of four light-filled entrances, where a transparent barrier holds back crowds of shouting Imperial soldiers in leather vests and Imperial-emblem cloth, armed with maces, shields, spears and hammers. Beyond each entrance lies the matching 2nd-floor field; Bjorn's faced a dense forest like the Goblin Forest. When it is cleared, the cave collapses: walls crumble, the ceiling cracks and everything shatters into a kaleidoscope of colours.

It replays the memory of a pilgrim who sided with the Witch in the Witch's War, killed his comrades out of fear and was turned into Dreadfear.

## Monsters
- Imperial soldiers — hunt the pilgrims; can always spot one, even in disguise
- Centurion Argarsil Dreadfear — the pursuing commander and final boss
- [Goblin](../../../../../../Bestiary/goblin_ch4.md) and other cave monsters — friendly to pilgrims and protect them

## Mechanics
- Reached through Special Condition "Distorted Memories" during the phase-4 transition of a raid of five or fewer, triggered by Goblin Paralyzing Poison (ch. 482).
- On arrival, all stats are fixed at 15, essences are sealed, equipment is replaced by rags and a bone awl, and the subspace pocket will not open. The party is split across the four entrances (Rocky Desert, Goblin Forest, Beast's Lair and Land of the Dead sides) (ch. 484).
- Each pilgrim gets a hidden monster skill: Bersil [Blooming Flesh], [Assimilation] and emotion sensing; Erwen sensing everyone's location; Bjorn [Narcolepsy]; Ainar [Prophet of Misfortune] (ch. 484–488).
- Every 5 soldiers killed gives +1 to all stats (later every 10) (ch. 483–485).
- The labyrinth closing is no escape, and the event lasts over 7 hours. The goal is the hidden Witch's Altar at the centre, about 8 hours from the entrances (ch. 484–486).
- Each pilgrim who dies gives the survivors +400 to all stats. Fleeing fails: the Centurion seems to teleport ahead, and neither the barriers nor the walls can be broken (ch. 486–488).
- With one pilgrim left, "Perfect Bond" triples all stats and the order becomes "Slay Centurion Dreadfear". Fought alone, he cannot regenerate and dies in about 10 hits: +100 EXP for Dreadfear, +15 Floor Lord bonus, "the Nameless Pilgrim accepts defeat" (ch. 488–489).
- Killing a fellow pilgrim would strengthen his avatar. The intended solution is to clear it without betrayal; the dead return if the survivor picks the revival door (ch. 489–490).
- Final rewards: No. 12 [Trust], a Double Numbered bracelet bound to the four participants; the Nameless Pilgrim and Distorted Memories permanently removed; a hidden area unlocked; a first-time feat on the Stone of Honor; then return to Lafdonia (ch. 490).

## Notable events
- ch. 484–485: The pilgrims fight their way inward separately; Erwen and Ainar are captured.
- ch. 486: Bersil kills herself to awaken the altar.
- ch. 486–488: Erwen dies as a decoy; Ainar carries the downed Bjorn and dies at the entrance.
- ch. 488–489: Bjorn, alone, slays the Centurion.
- ch. 490: Bjorn chooses revival, and all three companions come back in the plaza.

## Description
A pitch-black maze at the heart of the Crystal Cave, reached by dark passages with colourless crystals scattered in their walls. At its centre lies a cavern about 30 m in radius holding the Great Sage Monument. Torches are needed, and few rookies come here because it is riskier and torches cost money.

During the Noark war, it was turned into a fortified plaza. Mages collapsed the surrounding walls until the cavern was hundreds of times bigger (Bjorn compares it to Gwanghwamun Plaza). It was lit with torches and packed with clan tents, with a large tent flying the Lafdonia royal flag at the centre where leaders met around a round table. Explorers rested shoulder to shoulder in the narrow outer passages.

## Monsters
- [[Goblin]], [[Ghoul]], [[Gnome]], [[Blade Wolf]] — all four of the floor's monster types appear here
- [[Dreadfear]] — Lord of Fear, summoned nearby (e.g. in the south passage) and raided at the monument

## Mechanics
- All four 1st-floor monster types mix in this zone (ch. 27).
- During the war, a defensive magic circle kept the poison fog out. Knights guarded the entrances and vetted arrivals by ID tag or a knight's message ring. Noark pursuers stopped at the dividing dark passage (ch. 214–215).
- The royal army painted location signs on the walls (e.g. "North 172-21") and assigned sleeping spots and rotating watches in the outer passages. A temporary infirmary with conscripted priests healed for free under wartime rules (ch. 215–216).
- Dimensional Gates (from 6 schools such as Delta, Spheris and Jacquard; cast by 4th-grade mages and up; 30 people each; usable once in a mage's lifetime) were opened one after another here, since each absorbs the mana around it; the city was visible through them (ch. 216–217).
- Holds the Great Sage Monument chamber, where Dreadfear is raided (ch. 484).

## Notable events
- ch. 214–215: Bjorn's group breaks through to the safe zone; Marco Elburn (3rd Royal Knight Order) commands from here.
- ch. 217–218: On the night of Day 5, the royal army secretly leaves by Dimensional Gate. Abandoned explorers riot and lynch the knights left behind until a gigantified Bjorn shouts "Stop!", echoed by the barbarians.
- ch. 218–219: Leaders such as Melter Pend (Nartel Clan), Lacey Naret (Heindel Church) and military mage Kyle Pebrosk rally around Bjorn; survivors split into groups of 300, and the Noark blockers withdraw.
- ch. 503: High-level clan explorers crowd the zone as their first stop when hunting for the hidden area.

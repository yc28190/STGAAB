# Outer Fortress (Castle Town)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | rift area (ruined town) |
| **Also known as** | castle town ruins |
| **First appearance** | ch. 29 |

## Description
The ruins of a castle town's public buildings: smithies, taverns, inns, churches and merchants' stables, with no farmland or housing. In the middle stands a dry fountain full of pebbles with a statue at its centre.

## Monsters
- [[Deadman]]
- [[Skeleton Archer]]
- [[Skeleton Mage]]
- [[Ghoul]]
- [[Deathfiend]]
- [[Cambormier]] — the Vampire Duke comes up to the surface here after sunset

## Mechanics
- Street fighting: monsters come in groups of dozens, and noise draws hundreds more within a minute (ch. 29).
- Hidden piece: breaking the fountain statue gives a small box holding the Tear of the Goddess, a single-use holy item that heals or deals heavy holy damage and works only inside the Blood Citadel (ch. 30, 33, 35).
- Hidden piece: the Golden Mask (changes the wearer's face for 30 days; about five uses; sells for about 500,000) is hidden in the ceiling of a watchtower on the outer walls, reached by ladder. It only appears after the boss is killed under special conditions (ch. 36, 45).
- After sunset the vampire comes up to the surface (ch. 33–34).

## Notable events
- ch. 29: Raven collects antiques, books, soil and deadman remains for research.
- ch. 34–35: The final battle with the Vampire Duke takes place in the town.
- ch. 36: Bjorn obtains the Golden Mask.

## Description
A large stone room behind a door at the end of the Demonic Shrine. A hooded figure sits waiting in the darkness at its centre.

## Monsters
- [Death Knight](../../../../../../Bestiary/death_knight_ch32.md) — normal guardian
- [Cambormier](../../../../../../Bestiary/cambormere_ch32.md) — Vampire Duke (written Cambormere here), level 5 named "high variant" special guardian

## Mechanics
- If the door's knob is an unusual colour, opening it shakes the floor and awakens the Vampire Duke instead of the Death Knight (ch. 33).
- The Duke uses black magic: spiritization (mist form), materialization, exploding crows that mark targets as "sacrifices" to boost his strength within 100 m, [Eternal Life] (regeneration that rises as he loses stamina), [Master of Blood] and pain sharing (from a Nightfla essence). He can only be killed by destroying his heart, and he will not follow anyone to the surface until sunset (ch. 32–35).
- Killing him gives EXP +5, a high-variant bonus +1 and a guardian bonus +3, plus the vampire guardian essence (ch. 35).
- The vampire guardian essence carries all four skills: [Dark Origin] (passive; the user cannot die while the heart is intact), [Mark of Sacrifice], [Eternal Life] and [Master of Blood] (ch. 44).
- The variant guardian's room holds a treasure chest of extra rewards (ch. 36).

## Notable events
- ch. 33: Bjorn, who had only seen the Duke in another player's recording, opens the oddly coloured door.
- ch. 34–35: The Duke is finally killed on the surface after sunset; Bjorn takes the essence.
- ch. 36: Raven empties the chest and strips the room for research.

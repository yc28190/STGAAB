# Inner Fortress

| | |

## Description
About a third the size of the outer fortress and completely ruined: every building has collapsed and no monsters remain. It once housed the armoury and the lord's vassals, knights and soldiers.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- About 30 minutes of clearing rubble uncovers wooden planks hiding stairs down to the Citadel Dungeon (ch. 30).

## Notable events
- ch. 30: The party digs out the dungeon entrance.
- ch. 33: The party passes back through during the escape.

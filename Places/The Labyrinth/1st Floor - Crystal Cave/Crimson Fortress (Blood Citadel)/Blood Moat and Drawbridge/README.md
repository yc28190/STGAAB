## Description
A 50 m bridge over a dry moat stops 20 m short of the castle gate, where a hinged drawbridge hangs raised on chains. Once the event starts, the moat fills with dark crimson blood and hands reach up out of it. The citadel takes its name from this blood.

## Monsters
- [Deadman](../../../../../Bestiary/deadman_ch29.md) — climb onto the bridge from the blood

## Mechanics
- Blowing the commander's horn starts lowering the drawbridge and activates a water magic circle that floods the moat with blood (ch. 29).
- Deadmen swarm the bridge, and the party must hold out until the drawbridge comes down; anyone who falls is lost (ch. 29).

## Notable events
- ch. 29: Bjorn's party clears the first chapter of the Blood Citadel.

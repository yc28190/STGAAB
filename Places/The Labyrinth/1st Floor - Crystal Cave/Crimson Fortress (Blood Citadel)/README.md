## Description
A red, end-of-the-world sky full of crows, with a black fortress looming among mountain ridges at the end of a mountain road; at night a red moon rises. Beyond a blood-filled moat and drawbridge lie the outer fortress (castle town), the inner fortress with its dungeon, and the lord's castle with a demonic shrine beneath it. An invisible barrier stops anyone from leaving the map, though the land beyond can be seen.

The rift is modelled on the real ancient Cambermier Duchy, whose duke became a vampire. It was Bjorn's first rift, where he met Raven and Hikurod.

## Mechanics
- One of four 1st-floor rifts. Instances hold five members matched first-come, first-served; when it opens the labyrinth shakes and thousands of portals appear, each closing once its team is full (ch. 27–28).
- Monsters are grade 7 or lower (ch. 28).
- Four chapters: the drawbridge, street fighting in the outer fortress, the dungeon under the inner fortress, and the demonic shrine under the lord's castle (ch. 29).
- The only way out is to kill the guardian and use the exit portal; afterwards the party may stay until the labyrinth closes, then returns via the 1st-floor entrance to Rafdonia (ch. 34, 36).
- The guardian can drop an essence (about 33% in the game), Numbers items or rift stones (ch. 29, 35).
- Most items (junk like bricks and decorations) can only be taken out with distortion magic; real items such as the Necronomicon are an exception (ch. 30, 32, 374, 614).
- Special guardian: an oddly coloured doorknob on the guardian-room door means Vampire Duke Cambormier waits inside instead of the Death Knight; a coffin replaces the knight (ch. 33, 587).
- Hidden items: Tears of the Goddess, from a statue in front of a fountain, and the Golden Mask (changes your face for 30 days); their entries in Raven's "Rift Records III" / Compendium of Rifts have faded (ch. 44, 78, 257, 315).
- The rift has a dimensional-disruption barrier (ch. 611).

## Notable events
- ch. 27–35: Bjorn, Ainar, Hikurod Murad, the wizard Arua Raven and her porter Tarzine clear the rift and kill the Vampire Duke, using Tears of the Goddess and holy water. Bjorn eats the corpse golem and vampire essences; Ainar eats the bone knight essence.
- ch. 36: Bjorn is unconscious for three days. Hikurod receives No. 3112 Guardian's Vambraces (normally a 4th-floor-plus rift item worth about 50 million stones); Raven takes the rift stone.
- ch. 257, 775: Bjorn's Golden Mask is traced to this rift.
- ch. 300, 315: Raven suspects Bjorn knew the Compendium because he beat the Vampire with no essence.
- ch. 490, 813: Bjorn recalls the vampire speaking to him and believing the labyrinth world was real.
- ch. 539: Bjorn's second expedition here, where mutants appeared.
- ch. 585: Cambormier's origin as a real ancient duke is revealed in the Hall of Guardian Statues.

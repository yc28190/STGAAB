## Description
The dungeon beneath the inner fortress. The first level is a maze of cells full of chained, rotting deadmen, with skeletal rats scurrying everywhere. The second resembles a mad scientist's laboratory crammed with grisly props. The third hides a door behind a wall that leads under the lord's castle.

## Mechanics
- Killing a banshee leaves a "mark" that summons dozens of others; bumping into banshees causes a horrible experience (ch. 30).
- A map normally found in the guardroom shows the hidden door on the third level (ch. 30).
- The dungeon is a maze; Bjorn knew the escape route by heart (ch. 33).

## Notable events
- ch. 30–31: The party fights through to the Corpse Golem.
- ch. 33: Bjorn leads the escape through the maze.

# Tombstone Clearing

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | clearing (rift altar) |
| **Also known as** | the tombstone |
| **First appearance** | ch. 374 |

## Description
An empty space about 30 m in radius with a single tombstone in it. During the old Noark incident it was much larger, because mages had widened it. It appears to be another description of the central monument site.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- To open a rift, kill an 8th-grade monster on the 2nd floor for its magic stone, return, and lay it before the tombstone like an offering. The whole floor shakes around the tombstone and a green portal opens into a 1st-floor rift (ch. 374).
- Other explorers on the floor can rush in too (ch. 374).

## Notable events
- ch. 374: Bjorn's party opens the Green Mine rift here; he recalls the remaining explorers gathering here after the army and large clans escaped through the Dimensional Gate.

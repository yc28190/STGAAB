## Description
The rift opens on a frozen lake whose ice cracks underfoot, beneath a snowy mountain, with snow falling and harsh sunlight glaring off the ice; at night a dark blue Milky Way shows overhead. Chapter 1 is an ice cave behind a cave mouth, with pillars at regular intervals. An ice staircase leads down to chapter 2, a bright cavern whose ice walls glow white as if the whole cave were glass. The walls are nine layers of ice with beasts frozen inside, and a totem stands in the middle. Chapter 3 is a dark, slippery, three-dimensional ice cave of steep slopes, rope crossings and dead-end side paths, joined to chapter 2 by an underground river with rapids and a waterfall. It ends in a huge cavern where three Ice Orcs stand frozen in clear ice pillars.

## Mechanics
- One of the four 1st-floor rifts. It opens when an 8th-grade monster's stone from the 2nd floor's Beasts' Lair is offered at the altar; natural openings usually start from Day 3, so a Day-1 rift means someone triggered it. Cooldown is about 3 months (ch. 101–102, 106).
- Chapter 1: the ice cave copies the Crimson Fortress prison layout. Following left, left, right, right, left, seven straights, left takes about an hour (the party took 6) (ch. 103).
- Chapter 2 hidden piece: break the third pillar from the top on the ice staircase for a Magic-infused Ice Shard (+3 Cold Resistance, permanent) (ch. 103, 109).
- Touching the totem wakes "the tyrant" and starts waves from the ice walls: about 20 Frost Wolves first, then Sabertooth Tigers, Wormstones and Ice Golems. Fighting with your back to the staircase helps. Wave 9 is Katumba ([Suppression], resisted by Magic Resistance or by biting your tongue; [Blizzard]; Frostbite), whose blue essence gave Cold Condensation (ch. 103–104).
- In the last wave the area grows four times larger, the ceiling caves in and Tarunbas uses [Ice Crush]; the floor collapses and drops the party into the underground river, costing HP (ch. 104).
- Chapter 3 field effect "Glacier Cave" / [Hypothermia]: up to -30 Agility and double cold damage for anyone without cold resistance; fires cannot warm you. Rubble blocks the way back (ch. 106).
- Killing the last of the three Ice Orcs (axe, hammer, and a shaman with [Steel Ice Wall]) starts chapter 4 (ch. 107).
- Chapter 4: Tarunbas (1.5 times a normal Lycanthrope; his club frosts any shield it hits) breaks out of a wall. His [Ice Crush] frees about a dozen Frost Wolves from the walls. His Lightning Resistance is negative: a Lightning Scroll paralyzes him, and since [Indomitable] removes paralysis but not stun, Storm Explosion lightning bombs keep him down (ch. 108).
- Rewards: guardian Lycanthrope essence (Blood of the Snowfields); the Heart of the Glacier and the Frozen Breath essence drop only here. Afterwards, an ice passage opens back to chapter 2 (ch. 106–110).
- Signature item: break the small pond near the start for a red Ice Crystal (+3 Cold Affinity, permanent, edible up to 3 times) (ch. 109).
- Essences in rifts drop often but vanish after 30 minutes. A cleared rift is the safest place in the labyrinth to sleep (ch. 104, 109).

- ch. 587: A boss-only version with the hidden guardian Kirdyu appears in the Hall of Guardian Statues.

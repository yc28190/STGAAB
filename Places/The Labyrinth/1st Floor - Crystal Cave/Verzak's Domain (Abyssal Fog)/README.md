## Description
Black fog that limits sight to about 2 m in every direction. Within roughly 2 km of Verzak, hundreds of disembodied eyes float in the darkness. Verzak himself is about 2 m tall, wrapped in a ragged robe like burial clothes, with dark blue smoke where skin should be and a single huge crimson eye filling the space under his hood.

## Mechanics
- Appears after the 9th Lord of Fear is killed (special condition "Nine Fears") (ch. 217, 219).
- The fog seals active skills and usage effects and cuts mana efficiency to 1/4 (ch. 221, 223).
- Every floating eye shares Verzak's vision; he picks targets through Abyssal Watchers, and once a group meets one, his aggro cannot be shaken off (ch. 224).
- Meeting his eye can inflict Fear. At 9 stacks a character permanently becomes his monster subordinate, even after he dies (ch. 224).
- Attacks: shadow-stiletto strikes that pierce a Moonstone shield, shadow nets that drag victims into the dark, and [Abyssal Call], which pulls every trash monster in sight to one spot. Extra patterns unlock from Day 7 (ch. 223–224).
- Weak to the Sun attribute (fire + holy imbuement), and Aura hurts him. Beating him takes late-game preparation: a solo tank, Aura users, many priests, mages and at least 5th-tier gear. Lower raw power than Riakis but several times harder (ch. 223–224).

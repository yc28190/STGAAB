## Description
A mine cave whose ceiling has collapsed at the start, lit only by the arrival portal, and creepier than it was in the game. Rail tracks run along the floor among scattered wooden debris, past a broken bridge over a cliff, side passages, mineral deposits and a spiral staircase down the cliff edge. It ends at a collapsed tunnel that opens into a vast chamber about 50 m in radius, where green slime writhes.

## Mechanics
- One of the four 1st-floor rifts; progress by following the tracks through 4 chapters (ch. 374).
- Chapter 1 hidden piece: where the tracks are broken, smash the wall to find a narrow one-person passage to a chest holding a green jewel worth about 3M stones (ch. 374).
- The two-thirds-collapsed bridge can be skipped with a mid-level movement skill; otherwise take the side passage (~30 min) (ch. 374).
- Chapter 2 hidden piece: three Gob Quartz hidden among the minerals near the mid-boss room; eating one gives Poison Resistance +3 permanently (does not stack) (ch. 374).
- Chapter 3 ends at the bottom of the spiral staircase. The collapsed final tunnel is normally opened with explosives hidden in the mine; high-level players can smash its foundation instead (ch. 374).
- "The Green King awakens": the King Slime fights while spawning trash mobs (ch. 375).
- Valuables like the jewel leave the rift without distortion magic; junk needs it. Rift terrain rarely breaks, so a breakable wall usually hides something. Essences fade about 30 minutes after dropping (ch. 374–375).
- The entry portal closed after only two more people entered (ch. 374).

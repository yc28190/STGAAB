## Description
An enormous cave system, as twisting as an anthill, lit by crystals set in the walls and ceiling. The crystals usually give a soft purple (sometimes faintly iridescent) glow, bright enough in the inner areas to go without torches. The passages are mostly narrow, the air is cool and slightly damp, and dripping water collects in small ponds that serve as drinking water. Both the outskirts and the centre hold "Dark Zones": pitch-black stretches with dead ends where a light orb or torch is needed. When the Floor Master is summoned, the whole floor trembles and the crystals turn blood red. Most of the layout reshuffles each opening, but a few landmarks never move: a square crystal intersection, a pond beside a large rock and a moss-covered puddle. The Gabrielius monument stands at the centre.

The Crystal Cave is the entry floor of the labyrinth, reached from the city portal. Most explorers here are human rookies (fewer than 100 barbarians at a time). The floor is divided by direction among goblins, ghouls, blade wolves and gnomes, with a portal at each edge leading to a different 2nd floor. Experienced explorers simply race through it toward the 2nd-floor portals, while looters and gangs prey on newcomers.

## Mechanics
- The floor is open for 7 days and closes at 24:00 on Day 7, returning explorers to the city; the return portal still works on Day 7 (ch. 13, 53, 114, 230, 503).
- Parties are capped at five members (ch. 11).
- Four portals at the edges (E, W, N, S) lead to four different 2nd floors: east to the Beasts' Lair, west to the Rocky Desert, north to the Goblin Forest (closest to the 3rd floor), and another toward the Land of the Dead; all routes meet again from the 3rd floor (ch. 12, 50, 216–222, 455). The starting direction hints at which 2nd floor you are headed for (ch. 455).
- A portal is opened by touching the monolith (Floor Stele) at a dead end, which turns into a sphere of light (ch. 5, 22).
- "Dimensional instability" exploit: being the first to touch an untouched stele opens its portal and gives a permanent +2 EXP, but you start on the far edge and face a long walk (almost two days) back to the centre (ch. 101, 178, 204). The Magic Tower later stabilised the portal, so the start is no longer in a dark zone and the trick stopped working (ch. 263).
- Opening the 2nd-floor portal first grants a first-clear achievement (ch. 398).
- Navigation: take the wider passage at forks, avoid mounds (likely dead ends), and head toward darker passages with fewer crystals from the outer areas. Guides also read wall textures by touch (Rotmiller's technique). With a compass, an ordinary explorer reaches a portal within a day; Rotmiller's record is 6 hours (ch. 151, 163, 789).
- Floor Master (Dreadfear / Lord of Fear): may be summoned by chance after Day 3 wherever 5+ people gather. While it is out, portals stay open. It respawns every 6 hours, takes about 30 decent (7th-grade, or 4th-floor level) explorers to kill, drops no essence, and its first kill promotes a clan to 4th grade. Raids must be approved by the Explorer's Guild in advance. A failed raid lets it quietly vanish (ch. 65–66, 110, 216–219, 505).
- Special condition "Nine Fears": killing the 9th Lord of Fear summons Verzak, who roams the floor (ch. 217–221, 505–506).
- Field effect "Abyssal Fog" (after Verzak appears): visibility about 2 m in light-swallowing darkness; all usage effects (active skills, item and scroll effects) are disabled while passives still work; mana efficiency drops to 1/4; monsters touched by the fog evolve into 5th-grade Abyssal forms (ch. 221, 506).
- Rifts: there are four 1st-floor rifts (Crimson Fortress, Glacier Cave, Green Mine, Steel Tomb) with roughly 3-month cooldowns. They usually open naturally from Day 3, making the floor shake and thousands of portals appear, or earlier through the hidden piece at the Gavrilius monument in the central dark zone using 8th-grade (or 2nd-floor) magic stones (ch. 100–102, 218, 789).
- The four portals first appeared when Gavrilius cleared every monster in the cave, as recorded on the Stone of Honor (ch. 491). Hidden areas always appear on the floor where the feat was done (ch. 503).
- Unlike the rest of the labyrinth, damage to special points such as the crystal caves is not restored (ch. 554).
- The dimensional stele accepts three Nameless Fragments to open the 1st Basement Floor (ch. 790).
- Royals check ID cards of explorers entering here (ch. 342).

## Notable events
- ch. 3–12: Bjorn's first entry into the labyrinth; he meets Hans A and kills his first person.
- ch. 28: Erwen trains here with her dagger under Tersia.
- ch. 49–53: Bjorn's new team (Hikurod, Dwalky, Misha, Rotmiller) starts here.
- ch. 101: Bjorn meets "Hanst" (formerly Hans G).
- ch. 110: The Jagun Clan beats the Floor Master using a strategy bought from the community.
- ch. 113–114: Karon baits marauders with the night-companion trick.
- ch. 178: Five explorers killed for their tags are found on the route to the Goblin Forest.
- ch. 207–230: Noark's great ambush. A poison fog covers the floor, Orculus members guard the 2nd-floor portals, and Bjorn leads a charge into the safe zone, killing Serpent Knight Kesial. The royal army escapes by Dimensional Gate, Bersil Gowland's Mass Teleport leaves 15 behind by dice roll, and Verzak appears. Bjorn leads about 1,000 people through the Abyssal Fog with a 12-person rear guard, earning the title "giant". Fewer than 2,000 explorers return from the cycle.
- ch. 263: The Magic Tower patches the dimensional-instability start.
- ch. 478–479: Looters hunt barbarian newcomers for their hearts; Bjorn and Ainar go undercover, wipe out two looter teams and learn of a 2-billion-stone bounty on Bjorn's heart.
- ch. 503–506: Crowds hunt for the hidden area after Bjorn's feat; Dreadfear is summoned on Day 3, the ninth kill brings Verzak back, and ten clans help evacuate the floor.
- ch. 789–790: The labyrinth reopens through the Karnohn plaza portal; the stele is used to open the 1st Basement Floor.

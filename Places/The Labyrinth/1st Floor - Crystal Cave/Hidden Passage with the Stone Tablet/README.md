## Description
A small crevice in a passage wall, too narrow for a barbarian until Bjorn broke the wall open with his hammer. Behind it a passage about 10 m long ends at a stone tablet that looks like a portal stone. An ancient-language inscription buried in the ground beneath it repeats the words on the Witch statue and in the Witch's Cabin: all are equal under one star, one sun and one moon.

## Monsters
- [Abyssal Watcher](../../../../Bestiary/abyssal_watcher_ch222.md) — present while Verzak is on the floor

## Mechanics
- The broken wall does not regrow, which marks the spot as a hidden piece (ch. 504).
- Touching the tablet alone does nothing, and Mana Detection finds nothing (ch. 504).
- Touching it while holding all three Nameless Fragments (Sun, Star and Moon, farmed in the 3rd floor's Sculpture Temple) opens a multicoloured portal: "Hidden area portal opened for the first time. EXP +99" (ch. 507).
- The portal is one-way and closes for good after 60 people pass through; it is not time-limited (ch. 507–508).

## Notable events
- ch. 503–504: A weak clan finds the passage before Day 3. It killed six explorers who came across it and dissolved the bodies; Bjorn hands their testimony to the guild instead of executing them.
- ch. 506–507: Dozens of explorers wait here despite Verzak. One accidentally kills an Abyssal Watcher, which risks drawing Verzak. Bjorn opens the portal to the 1st Basement Floor.

## Description
When the Lord of Terror spawns, the cave walls shake, dead crystals flare crimson, the air turns cold and the darkness is bathed in red light, with vision shrinking to 2 m. Dreadfear himself is a humanoid about 5'4" tall, crawling with maggots, carrying a bone greatsword and stepping with wet squelches.

## Monsters
- [[Dreadfear]] — Lord of Terror, Floor Lord
- [[Giant Maggot]] — head-sized tokens
- Smoke ghosts

## Mechanics
- Special Condition "Five Believers": five or more explorers gathered at a specific spot after Day 3. The spawn chance rises sharply over time, with warnings like "Something in the darkness is watching". The standard five-man plan starts the ritual on the evening of Day 6 for a sure spawn on Day 7 (ch. 478, 480).
- On spawn, the Bonding spell is cancelled and vision is limited to 2 m (ch. 480).
- Phase 1: maggot aura [Gnawing Terror] drains Mental Power while Rage grows with damage taken; fire such as [Holy Flame] dispels it. Giant maggot tokens appear (ch. 480).
- Phase 2: [Infiltrating Dread] aura drains Mental Power, lowers accuracy and can redirect attacks to the nearest ally, so the tank stays closest. A stacking buff every 5 minutes ("Resentment deepens") makes speed essential (ch. 480).
- Phase 3 begins with "The hatred within Dreadfear awakens" (ch. 480).
- A five-man clear gives the best rewards. Only 2–3 clans attempt it per century, and it is considered harder than Riakis (ch. 468).

## Notable events
- ch. 467–468: Bjorn plans a five-man Dreadfear raid.
- ch. 478–480: Clan Anabada (Bjorn, Ainar, Amelia, Erwen, Bersil) runs the raid on a recording crystal. It doubles as bait for Count Alminus's watchers, who move in when phase 3 begins around 20:00.

# Pantelion Ruins

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | field (ruins) |

## Description
Nearly abandoned ruins of stone temple buildings set in jungle, west of the River of Souls. Monsters lie dormant inside and wake when intruders arrive.

The ruins were once an Empire laboratory, which explains their mechanical and magic-engineered monsters. The jungle of Rainbow Island looks like their intact prototype.

## Monsters
- [[Gigawolf]]
- Modified metallic beasts
- Giant magic-engineering golems
- Mechanical flying (drone-like) monsters
- [[Mechanical Giant]] — Grade 3; [Magic Beam], [Support Request], [Reactor]; essence passive [Mechanization] turns magic stones into MP.

## Mechanics
- Low-level monsters for the 7th floor at the entrance, with many unique species (more than 20 new types in one patrol) (ch. 365).
- Patrol shifts last about 10 hours; the 30+ patrol fought in formation with a shield wall and a joint magic beam; loot tokens are paid out at the city's Achievement Office (ch. 365).

## Notable events
- ch. 365: The 4th Patrol Team is assigned here.
- ch. 505: Compared to the Sculpture Temple.
- ch. 610–618: Rainbow Island's jungle is recognized as its intact prototype; Panthelion is revealed as an Empire lab.

## Description
A narrow canyon thick with an ominous, gloomy fog, so tight that ships must pass through it in single file.

It is the required entrance to the Dark Continent from the sea and leads to the Blood-Red Lake.

## Mechanics
- Field effect "Ghostly Canyon": all healing and regeneration disabled, divine power sealed (ch. 352).
- Undead respawn constantly and climb the hulls; ghost types are immune to physical attacks (ch. 352).
- Noark bombards the fleet throughout, so ships must hold their barriers the whole way (ch. 352).
- Over half the monsters also appear on the 6th floor, but EXP comes faster here (ch. 352).
- The standard route into the Dark Continent; a single ship cannot cross without the main force (ch. 398).

## Notable events
- ch. 352: The royal war fleet fights its way through to the lake.
- ch. 409: The royal main force is noted to have entered the continent this way.

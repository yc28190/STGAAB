## Description
A lightless continent wrapped in black fog, with deep mountainous interiors. The surrounding sea is black, as if coated in oil, and fog lies thick even at the shore; the entire coastline is cliffs. It looks even more apocalyptic than the real Grey Plains outside the walls. Explorers need the 5th-grade support spell [Insight] to see.

The main region of the 7th floor and the theater of the war between Noark and the royal army. Noark held more than half of it for years. Its terrain closely matches the real continent outside the city walls.

## Monsters
- [[Devil Wolf]] — 5th-grade pack beast.
- [[Bellarios]] — found in the Dragon Mountains region.
- [[Vaiterion]] — inhabitant of the continent.
- [[Bayon]] — main habitat is the Land of Immortality.
- [[Deadred]] — Lord of Darkness, one of the two floor lords; essence gives [Star's Extinction].

## Mechanics
- Fleets land under a royal defensive barrier costing tens of millions of stones per minute while Noark shells them with magic cannons from the cliffs (ch. 352).
- The only normal way inland is the Ghostly Canyon to the lake, which branches into the River of Souls, the Great Forest and the Pantelion Ruins (ch. 365); the expedition instead entered from the rear through the 8th floor (ch. 398).
- Without a priest, camps rely on a blocking magic circle (ch. 340).
- Noark has its own starting point here, like the Crystal Cave, and can start on the 7th floor from day one; it also opened a portal from outside the walls leading here (ch. 342, 398, 406, 656).
- The floor closes after day 75 (ch. 366, 413).
- Two floor lords; Deadred was last summoned about eight years earlier, probably by Ibaekho (ch. 671).
- Crossing from end to end takes only one or two months (ch. 658).

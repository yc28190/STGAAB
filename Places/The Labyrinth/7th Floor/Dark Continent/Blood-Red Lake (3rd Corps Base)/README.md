## Description
A vast lake lying just past the Ghostly Canyon. Waterfalls thunder down the surrounding cliffs in water stained red like blood, and mist hangs everywhere until mages clear it with magic. Tents line the shore, with the six-person tents to the east near the water.

After the main force moves inland, the royal 3rd Army sets up its rear base here, protected by Raven's golden iron dome and the anchored warships.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- One of the few safe zones on the Dark Continent: no natural monster spawns, but not safe from people (ch. 354).
- Alarm magic and fog-clearing magic are maintained around the base (ch. 353–355).
- Raven's [Wall of Thousand Gold] (2nd-grade earth + lightning spell; not channeled, repairable and recastable) covers the shore in an iron dome; the first time it held about 8 hours. Teleporting in is blocked, but teleporting out works (ch. 354, 364).
- If attacked, troops board the anchored ships and use their barriers as a fortress (ch. 355, 363).

## Notable events
- ch. 354: Background: early in the war Raven held the base with the Wall and earned the name "Golden Mage".
- ch. 353–358: Orculus ambushes the base with the Corpse Collector's corpses, the Blood Knight, Liranne Vivienne (the Wailing Witch) and the Lighthouse Keeper; Bjorn kills the Lighthouse Keeper and loots ten rings and a necklace.
- ch. 360–363: Viscount Lamreond, the 3rd Corps commander, is killed by Ricardo Liuhen Praha the Traitor.
- ch. 364: The attackers leave by Multiple Teleportation; patrol teams are created.
- ch. 368: Forum debates about the Wall's weakness and the Lighthouse Keeper kill.

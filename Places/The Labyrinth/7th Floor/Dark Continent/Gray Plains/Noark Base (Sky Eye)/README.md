## Description
A giant tower on the Gray Plains guarded by about a hundred Noark explorers. After the expedition's raid it was left as shattered ruins in the haze.

The tower is Noark's communication hub on the Dark Continent.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- The Sky Eye is a mana wave emitter, like a cell tower, letting Noark talk with forces across the continent; it is too large to fit in a subspace pocket (ch. 407).
- Its communication coordinates can be removed and bandwidth maxed to broadcast to every message stone (ch. 410).

## Notable events
- ch. 407: The expedition targets it, expecting light defenses while the elites are away on the 8th floor.
- ch. 409: False reports lure Regal Vagos toward Ironfalls.
- ch. 409–410: The expedition lures monsters into the defenders, raises the royal flag, breaks through and destroys the tower, then escapes by mass teleportation — but never manages to reach the main force through it.

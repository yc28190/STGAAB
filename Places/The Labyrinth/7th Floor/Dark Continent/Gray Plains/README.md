## Description
A vast, barren, dark plain under a gloomy haze, split by cracks and crevices that offer places to hide — and out of which monsters crawl.

It is the central region of the Dark Continent, between the Dragon's Mountain Range and the Dragon's Lair, and holds Noark's base with the Sky Eye tower.

# Deadwood

| | |
|---|---|

## Description
A forest of giant withered trees in the western part of the Dragon's Mountain Range; the branches are big enough for a whole expedition to hide in.

It lies firmly under Noark control and was never breached by the royal army, though it is less heavily defended than the range itself.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Multiple Teleportation for 28 people cannot cross fields and leaves residual mana that can be tracked (ch. 411–412).
- The Gate spell does not work on the 7th floor or above (ch. 411).

## Notable events
- ch. 410: Used as the rendezvous point; the expedition waits three days but no rescue comes. Philip Aintropy dies here.
- ch. 412: While five mages charge the teleport, Mite Million, Fiona Aimers and Niaro Cambel die and Regal Vagos arrives; the expedition teleports to western Deadwood and turns north.

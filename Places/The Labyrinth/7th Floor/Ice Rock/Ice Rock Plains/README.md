# Ice Rock Plains

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | field (plain) |
| **Also known as** | eastern region; starting area; vast plain |
| **First appearance** | ch. 399 |

## Description
A wide frozen plain stretching from Ice Rock's arrival point to the giant ice mountain. Cliff-like walls of ice rise from it and can crumble to release monsters; expeditions pitch their camps against these cliffs.

## Monsters
- [[Stonewinter]] — 3rd grade, super-sized; bursts out of ice walls.
- [[Frozen Soul]]
- [[Tainted Snow Spirit]]
- [[Frost Guardian]]
- [[Lich]]
- [[Glacier Giant]]
- [[Frostfire Turtle]]

## Mechanics
- The rowboat was hidden at the arrival point (ch. 399).
- Super-sized monsters can collapse the ground; a Stonewinter dropped a food sled into the river (ch. 399).
- Crossing to the ice mountain took about 9 days (ch. 400).

## Notable events
- ch. 399: Three Stonewinters ambush the expedition right after the starting area, and one collapses the ground under a food sled.
- ch. 400: The expedition reaches the giant ice mountain after about 9 days.

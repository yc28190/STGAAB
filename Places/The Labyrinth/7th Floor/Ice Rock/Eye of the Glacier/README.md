## Description
A giant sinkhole at the end of the ice-cave maze: a deep cylindrical chasm that seems to plunge toward the earth's core, swept by cold wind. A narrow spiral path, carved into the ice like a staircase and about four people wide at the top, winds down the outer wall. Partway down, a wide ledge juts from the cliff. The floor is marked with a giant eye-shaped pattern with a huge stele at its center.

It is the final gateway from Ice Rock to the 8th floor, and a notorious "tank's graveyard".

## Mechanics
- Field effect "Eye of the Glacier": [Whisper of Immortality] raises the dead as undead that attack their former allies; [Opportunity Denied] removes the death countdown ("golden time"); all healing and regeneration are reversed, so wounds fester and faster regeneration means faster rot (ch. 404, 415).
- About 90% of monsters fly, so ranged attackers and paladins carry the descent; divine spells do 1.5x damage to undead (ch. 404).
- The ledge (first checkpoint) is a spawning area with hundreds of monsters (ch. 404).
- Exploit: monsters killed under [Greater Distortion] (6th grade, 1.5x success) may leave preserved bodies containing a bead, the "Frozen Trace of Life", that heals wounds despite the reversal (ch. 404).
- The descent takes about a week (6 days from the ledge); bonding allows at most 6 people and first-clear EXP is limited to 6, so the expedition drew lots (ch. 405).
- Touching the stele opens a five-colored portal to the 8th floor; the first opener gets EXP +2 (ch. 406).
- Once the portal is opened no monsters remain; climbing back takes about 5 days (ch. 415).
- The boss does not pursue beyond the Eye; the field boundary sits one step inside the top entrance, so enemies inside rot while defenders outside heal (ch. 419–421, 424).

## Notable events
- ch. 404–406: The expedition descends and opens the portal to the 8th floor.
- ch. 415–416: On the return they are chased by 46 Noark men (15 elites with 8th-floor essences); at the midpoint they use the terrain, throw enemies off the cliff and turn the reversed healing against them; Ice Troll Shamans' [Avalanche] covers the retreat.
- ch. 417–419: They throw sleds, food, heating stones and armor over the cliff to outrun pursuers, cauterize wounds and amputate limbs.
- ch. 420–422: Bjorn holds the top boundary against Vagos and Repeles; the Rose Knights strike from the rear.
- ch. 430: The valuables buried inside are lost to the Elder Lich.

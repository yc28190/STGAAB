## Description
An illusory space created by the Glacier Witch's [Soul Extraction]. Each victim finds themselves in a copy of the place that marked them most — for Bjorn it was the 1st-floor Crystal Cave; in the game it could be the Rocky Desert, the Sky Tower, the Great Ocean or the royal palace.

There the victim must face everyone they have ever killed.

## Monsters
- Everyone the victim has killed. For Bjorn: Hans A, Hans Argo, Elisa, Hans G, Zencia Nephryn, Karui fanatics, the Lighthouse Keeper, Pike Neldain and countless looters.

## Mechanics
- The victim must defeat every person they have killed; some of them speak and turn the victim's own doubts against them (ch. 423).
- Bodies outside stay stunned but invincible, and even the boss ignores them (ch. 423–424).
- Anyone who does not wake within 30 minutes dies (ch. 423–424).
- On waking, a random penalty lasts until the labyrinth closes. Seen: active skills sealed (Bjorn), crippled leg (Jun), blindness (James Kala), right hand turned to stone (Aldidi), no resource recovery (Erwen), weapon restriction (Raviyen), deafness (Tsuon Iribun) (ch. 423–424).
- An ordinary Elder Lich's [Soul Extraction] only stuns (ch. 423).

## Notable events
- ch. 423–424: Priests and those with fewer kills wake first; Bjorn fights through his past victims; Mckelly Leyaders never wakes.

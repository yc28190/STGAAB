## Description
The very bottom of the Eye of the Glacier, where a rare boss event can trigger. When it does, the ground shakes and white fog rises up the chasm; thousands of skeletons pour out of it around an Elder Lich with crimson eyes.

## Monsters
- Glacier Witch Kariadaea — Named high-variant 3rd-grade Elder Lich; boss.
- Skeleton army — respawns throughout the Eye; bone explosions deal cold damage.
- Medium giant skeletons, and a large giant skeleton with red eyes.
- [Banshee](../../../../../../Bestiary/banshee_ch23.md)s

## Mechanics
- Kariadaea can appear only between 10 and 5 days before the 7th floor closes, with about a 10% chance (ch. 418–419).
- She spawns at the very bottom and climbs slowly; her appearance respawns a skeleton army in already-cleared areas (ch. 418–419).
- Skeletons explode for cold damage (ch. 419).
- [Frozen Faith] cancels Bonding (ch. 422).
- Destroying her first Soul Vessel triggers phase 2, [Soul Extraction]: everyone in range is stunned and pulled into the Vessel of Punishment; the fight resumes 30 minutes later, and she grows stronger with every soul she absorbs (ch. 422–424).
- She does not chase beyond the Eye of the Glacier (ch. 424).

## Notable events
- ch. 422: Bjorn times her arrival so the ranged attackers' combined barrage breaks her first Soul Vessel while the pursuing Noark force and Rose Knights are caught in the stun.
- ch. 423–424: Everyone in range is drawn into the Vessel of Punishment.
- ch. 430: The valuables buried in the Eye are lost to the Elder Lich.

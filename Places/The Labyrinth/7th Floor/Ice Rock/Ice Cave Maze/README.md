# Ice Cave Maze

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | field (cave maze) |
| **Also known as** | frozen cave; ice cave |
| **First appearance** | ch. 400 |

## Description
A huge cave entered at the foot of the giant ice mountain range of Ice Rock. Its ice is so clear that it feels like a fantasy world, yet the maze-like passages are always dark, and an underground river runs beneath the floor.

It leads to the Eye of the Glacier, and the expedition camped at its far end beside the sinkhole.

## Monsters
- [[Frozen Webter]]
- [[Unmelting Cocoon]]
- [[Frostfire Goblin]]
- [[Meran]] — drops a rare 3rd-grade essence.

## Mechanics
- The floor can collapse into the river below (ch. 400).
- There are no blizzards inside, so rations keep (ch. 405).
- About 13 days from the entrance to the Eye of the Glacier (ch. 401).
- After the boss fight the survivors lived on monster meat preserved by Distortion magic (ch. 424–425).

## Notable events
- ch. 402–403: At the camp beside the sinkhole the expedition tries and beheads Pike Neldain.
- ch. 424–430: The survivors shelter here until the labyrinth closes; the final battle takes place in the Trap Room.

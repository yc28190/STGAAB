# Trap Room

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | room (trap encounter) |
| **Also known as** | Ice Cave Trap Room; dead end; sealed room |
| **First appearance** | ch. 425 |

## Description
A wide chamber in the ice cave, as open as a field, that looks like a dead end and has only one entrance.

Once triggered it becomes a sealed arena, and it was the scene of the final, bloody battle of the Ice Rock expedition.

## Monsters
- Undead summoned by a Karui priest.
- [[Executioner]] — summoned.

## Mechanics
- A random encounter in the ice cave: using any skill inside triggers it (James Kala's [Nimble Fingers] did) (ch. 425).
- The only entrance is then sealed by a frozen wall until it melts, creating a closed arena (ch. 425, 430).

## Notable events
- ch. 426–429: Final battle on day 70 (5 days before close): 18 survivors against 7 Rose Knights and 6 Noark fighters. Jun, Ashid, Didi, Eriabosti, Orman, Rikerburn, Iribun and others die; Bjorn kills Six Rose; Regal Vagos, Manua Repeles and Cale Elbad Jeneger are killed. Ten survive.
- ch. 430: The Liar's Rose Rings (hidden subspace rings) are taken from the Rose Knights; Bjorn removes the Dragonslayer's heart.

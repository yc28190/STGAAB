## Description
A frozen land of bitter cold: a vast snowy plain with ankle-deep snow, cliff-like walls of ice, a giant ice mountain range and, beneath it, a maze of crystal-clear ice caves. Blizzards sweep the region periodically. It is one of the few places in the game with snow.

Ice Rock is a separate 7th-floor field from the Dark Continent and connects to the 8th floor through the Eye of the Glacier. Most explorers avoid it; clans visit only for achievements. It is remembered as the site of the doomed royal expedition in which twenty of Bjorn's comrades died.

## Mechanics
- Entered through a blue portal at the end of the 6th floor's easternmost sea (ch. 399).
- Field effect "Ice Rock": Cold Resistance -100 (ch. 399).
- Status [Frozen World]: space items (subspace pockets, expandable backpacks) are deactivated, so supplies are dragged on sleds in "Preservation"-enchanted boxes (ch. 399).
- Status [Starvation]: food gives a third of the usual resources, so hunger comes three times faster; food supply sets the time limit (ch. 399).
- Ice storms every 30 minutes to 6 hours bring the field effect "Festering Cold", accelerating decay and spoiling even frozen food; only the 4th-grade space-time spell [Block] protects food, at heavy mana cost (ch. 400). There are no blizzards inside the ice caves (ch. 405).
- The ground can collapse under heavy weight (super-sized monsters, trolls), dropping sleds into an underground river (ch. 399–400).
- Paladins' [Purge Evil] keeps monsters away from camps; Heating Stones warm the tents (ch. 400).
- Killing every monster here would still not reach level 8 (ch. 401).
- Fate Tracker (No. 6111), unusable in the city, glowed red from arrival (ch. 401).

## Notable events
- ch. 399–405: The expedition crosses with three months of food in a planned 20 days and arrives on day 20.
- ch. 402–403: Traitor Pike Neldain, who made trolls heavier with Blood Roar's [Bestow], is exposed and executed.
- ch. 413–430: Retreating from the Dark Continent, the expedition uses Ice Rock as a refuge; the survivors leave on day 75 when the labyrinth closes. The official cover story blames monsters for the deaths.
- ch. 447, 474: The Rose Knights are ordered to wipe out survivors; Bjorn kills the Dragonslayer Regal Vagos.
- ch. 546, 553–554: Recalled: the party hunted with Distortion magic for food, and Parab's intuition saved them from the royal betrayal.
- ch. 767–786: Bjorn's grudge against the Marquis, who sent the expedition to die; James Carla bonded with the clan there; Guildmaster Adnus had planted Pike Neldain.

## Description
An island about ten times the size of the Human (Chief's) Island, with cliffs to the north and a southern coast where ships can land. Trees almost ten stories tall, like apartment blocks, stand everywhere. After a rainy season, thousands of magic stones cover its silver-sand coast. A colossal giant over 40 m tall lives at the island's center and rises to roar at ships as they leave.

## Mechanics
- Every monster here gains [Gigantism] (about 1.5x size) as an extra skill, which seems to be an island effect. Their essences look normal (ch. 524).
- It is about 9 hours' sail from Rock Island, or 2 hours with the propulsion device (ch. 523–524).
- Killing a Hippermachant under Distortion can drop its essence ([Crouch]: 3 minutes of x20 resistances and regeneration, 30-minute cooldown; negative fighting-spirit stat). Its bones are strong and light, only slightly better than steel (ch. 525–528).
- The island's secret probably requires killing the giant, which only appears as a party leaves (ch. 528, 540, 542).
- It has its own dimensional stele in a cave (presumed to lead to B2), which the chief says to ignore. If a huge shadow suddenly falls over you, never look up; wait and it will pass (ch. 551, 560, 607).

## Notable events
- ch. 519–525: The Hex Clan's ship breaks up in the rainy season, and 12 survivors are stranded here and call for rescue.
- ch. 526–528: Bjorn's group, the Hex survivors and the Armin team map over 90% of the south in 3 days.
- ch. 538–541: The party collects about 1 billion stones' worth of magic stones. Mirayel is killed in 6 min 17 s and drops No. 7 Verdant Spear.
- ch. 560, 622: The island is considered for Hippermachant farming and for the giant boss, but Bjorn judges the expedition too weak.

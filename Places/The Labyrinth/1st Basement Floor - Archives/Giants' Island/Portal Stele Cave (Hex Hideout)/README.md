# Portal Stele Cave (Hex Hideout)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | cave |
| **Also known as** | Hex Clan Hideout Cave; Hext hideout; Portal Stele (Giants' Island); portal stone; the cave |
| **First appearance** | ch. 525 |

## Description
The only cave deep inside Giants' Island. Its entrance is a rock hidden in the brush and disguised as a wall by magic. Inside, the cave is much bigger than it looks. A small inner hollow holds a man-made portal stone with no inscription; the ground around its base had already been dug up when it was found.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- It opens to a knock signal from inside (ch. 525).
- Monsters do not approach the cave, so it is a safe zone even in the rainy season (ch. 526, 539, 553).
- The stele does nothing when touched, on any day, so some special condition must be met. Ideas include killing the 40 m giant, reaching the top of the library shelves, or asking the Elder (ch. 526).
- On the Hamsik map its symbol has a downward arrow, so it is presumed to lead to Basement 2 (ch. 551).

## Notable events
- ch. 525–526: The Hex survivors hide here, and Wyte Hex finds the stele.
- ch. 539–540: Bjorn runs his Garphas necklace gacha here, spending about 1.55 billion stones' worth. Ainard pulls No. 3 Wall of Aegis.

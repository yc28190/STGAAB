# Rock Island

## Description
A small, barren rocky island about the size of three football fields, with a stone floor and nothing on it: no trees, no monsters, no shelter. Waves break against the rocks, visibility is only about 100 m, and flotsam and scrap wash up on its beach. It is even bleaker than Limia, the 6th floor's starting island, which at least has trees.

## Monsters
None recorded (the island has no monsters).

## Mechanics
- Arrival point of the portal from the Crystal Cave, with no way back (ch. 508).
- Later arrivals come through portals that open in the sky (on day 107, the last day, in this cycle) (ch. 521, 552).
- Ships can be summoned from the shore, since wood floats on the silver sea (ch. 508).
- About 5 hours by ship from the Human Island; the Hex castaways' island lies 19 degrees from it, and the rainbow bears 34 degrees from it (ch. 516, 519, 538).
- The B1 map is centred on it (ch. 551).
- Scavenging its beach could be an income source if the rainy season is avoided (ch. 552).
- The only island with no shelter from rainy-season waves (ch. 607).

## Notable events
- ch. 507–508: Bjorn arrives on B1 here.
- ch. 521–522: Bjorn camps on the island from Day 53 to meet the second wave of arrivals.
- ch. 552: The 180-strong royal expedition arrives.
- ch. 622: Before the floor lord appears, wind and waves surge toward the island; it may be linked to "Record Lord Rekardo".

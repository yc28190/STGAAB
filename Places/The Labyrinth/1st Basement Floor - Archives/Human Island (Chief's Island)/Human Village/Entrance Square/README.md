# Entrance Square

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | plaza |
| **Also known as** | Village Entrance Square (Human Village); gate; village entrance |
| **First appearance** | ch. 529 |

## Description
An empty square in front of the village's rock-wall gate. In the rainy season, a translucent mage barrier covers it like a roof.

## Monsters
- Rainy-season monsters (see Human Island (Chief's Island))

## Mechanics
- During the rainy season the explorers and village warriors fight here under the barrier "umbrella". For the gate rules, see Human Village (ch. 529–530).

## Notable events
- ch. 529–530: The rainy-season battle alongside the villagers.
- ch. 534–536: The battle with the chief. The gate is shattered and the party escapes on day 5 of the rainy season.

## Description
A dark cave hidden beneath the corpse-filled basement. A portal stele (Dimensional Stele) stands at its center, with a black stone, the Heart of Karui, in the middle of the cave.

## Monsters
- The chief — performs his body-stealing ritual here

## Mechanics
- The chief's body-stealing ritual: black smoke flows from the chief into the captive's body through the Heart of Karui, which requires human sacrifice. Setting a one-month condition on the possession lowers the sacrifice needed (ch. 565–566).
- The Heart of Karui is fused to the stele and came loose only when the portal opened, because monsters cannot use the portal (ch. 567).
- On the map this stele has an arrow pointing up, so it is presumed to be the exit to the city (ch. 551).
- Expedition mages found that the stele's mana flow resembles a "bidirectional transmission device" and were given 10 days to try to activate it by force (ch. 569).

## Notable events
- ch. 565: In Reatlas's vision, the possessed Bjorn wakes up here.
- ch. 566: The Heart of Karui turns out to be a royal treasure of the Immortal King, who stayed immortal by taking over the bodies of his newborn children.
- ch. 566–567: The chief takes Jerome Saintred's body here.

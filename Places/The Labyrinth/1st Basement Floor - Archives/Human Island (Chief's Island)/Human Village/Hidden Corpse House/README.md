## Description
A normal-looking, fenced and heavily guarded house about 15 minutes from the chief's house. A staircase leads down to a soundproofed basement that stinks of rot and is piled with mutilated, twisted human corpses. A hidden cave lies beneath the basement.

## Monsters
- Village warriors (guards)

## Mechanics
- According to the chief, corpses fall from the sky every 10–40 years, probably because of dimensional collapse, and do not decay, as if time had stopped for them. A journal found on one of the bodies describes a dimensional collapse (ch. 533).

## Notable events
- ch. 531–532: Amelia is caught sneaking in.
- ch. 533: Bjorn hits the chief, throws a Molotov onto the corpses and brings down the stairs and ceiling as he escapes.
- ch. 565: The cave beneath the basement is revealed (see Hidden Stele Cave).

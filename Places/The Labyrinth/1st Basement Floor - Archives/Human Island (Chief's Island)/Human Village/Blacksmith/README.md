# Blacksmith

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | shop (forge) |
| **Also known as** | Native Village Forge; Village Blacksmith (Human Village); forge |
| **First appearance** | ch. 517 |

## Description
The village forge. Its fires are lit with abilities rather than firewood, and its fire was still burning after the village was emptied. Ainar liked to watch weapons being made here, and her native friend worked here.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- The smiths melt down debris that washes ashore and resources that fall during the rainy season (ch. 517).

## Notable events
- ch. 530–532: An iron dagger with the Silver Lion Clan emblem turns up here, and Amelia secretly puts it back. The chief and the fake Madderkin claim it came through trade (ch. 543).
- ch. 568: The forge fire is still burning in the deserted village.

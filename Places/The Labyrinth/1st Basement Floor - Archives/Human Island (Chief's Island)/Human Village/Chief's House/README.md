# Chief's House

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | building (house) |
| **Also known as** | Elder Brugrid's House; Elder's house; Elder's study; chief's mansion |
| **First appearance** | ch. 517 |

## Description
The chief's spacious but plain house at the head of the village's main street. There is no tea, and talks are held one on one. Its study holds books Brugrid wrote on monster leather, since he cannot touch wood.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- The study holds Brugrid's "Compendium of Monsters", his firsthand and secondhand notes on the island's monsters, including some missing from the Archives (for example Hippermachant, estimated rank 2, with [Gigantification]) (ch. 517–518).
- Brugrid's passive turns any wood he touches to dust, though not living trees (ch. 517).

## Notable events
- ch. 517: The captured Silver Lion explorers are held here.
- ch. 531: Bjorn and the chief play a question "truth game". The chief's version of history: the Sage foresaw the apocalypse, Rafdonia was founded as a refuge, the Sage created the labyrinth portal, and Noark was an "underground shelter". Afterward the house is often empty.

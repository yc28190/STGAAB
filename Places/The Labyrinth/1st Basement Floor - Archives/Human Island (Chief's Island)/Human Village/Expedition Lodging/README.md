## Description
Housing the chief gave to the explorers. The first house was roomy for 7 but as cramped as a refugee camp for 39. On day 64 they were given a new lodging with a living room and private rooms lit by magic lamps. Later, Clan Anabada stayed in a large three-story mansion with a ground-floor dining table; Misha had a corner room on the third floor.

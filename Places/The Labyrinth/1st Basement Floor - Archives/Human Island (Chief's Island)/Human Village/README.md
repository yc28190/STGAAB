## Description
An underground settlement reached through a large crevice under the roots of a giant tree. Inside are lush grass wet with rain, a soft white light and stone buildings, "like Wonderland", or like a small Noark. A main street runs from the chief's house to the entrance, a rock wall that works as a gate. The main entrance is hidden among the trees and looks like solid ground when closed, and a second, secret entrance lies in a gap beneath another tree. There is no wood anywhere: bows are made of horn and sinew, and fires are lit with abilities.

The villagers call themselves "humans": blue-skinned creatures with big eyes, fangs and pointed heads. The children are small, and adults stand up to 3.5 m tall. They speak the ancient language, taught to them by Cornelius Brugrid, the chief, who rules as a near god. After the chief took Jerome's body, the villagers were wiped out and the village was stripped bare, leaving the forge fire burning with no one there.

## Monsters
- Village natives ("humans") — warriors and archers, such as Nyuachichi, the chief's right hand
- The chief — skills Lightning God Step, Thundercloud, Conductor, Extreme Charge and Cosmic Protection (Tol-Lafupa)

## Mechanics
- Only the chief (or the elders) can open or close the entrance. Once the rainy season starts the gate stays locked until it ends, since leaving it open risks permanently damaging the "door" (ch. 514–515, 530).
- The stone gate regenerates quickly. Burst damage fails, so it takes focused damage; Elwen's Concentrated Shot finally shattered it (ch. 534–535).
- It is the safe refuge during the rainy season, which lasts exactly 7 days. Guests are given empty houses and may roam freely (ch. 517, 553).
- The natives don't eat. They live forever on "life water", ground magic stones dissolved in water, the same as the city's mana water. After each rainy season they come up to gather the stones (ch. 515, 518, 562).
- Villagers can absorb essences but cannot level up. They have 3–7 innate essence slots and drop essences when they die (ch. 531).
- Gestures: pointing a finger down means "yes", and touching fingertips is a handshake (ch. 515).
- The village has more exits than the chief admitted (ch. 563).

## Notable events
- ch. 515–518: Bjorn's party is taken in. The chief asks explorers to report what they find in exchange for support. Bjorn meets natives such as Marupichi, Uncle Maura, Nui, Itaphia and Kagudi, and finds Silver Lion survivors sheltering here.
- ch. 531–535: Amelia is caught in the Hidden Corpse House. Bjorn strikes the chief and the party breaks out through the gate, burning the village. The chief does not pursue and repairs it.
- ch. 543, 549: A fake Madderkin, apparently possessed, is suspected to be the chief's doing. Hamsik reveals the chief once flew to Library Island on a dragon with followers, so he had lied about being unable to leave.
- ch. 563–568: The chief, now in Jerome's body, orders his warriors to fight half-heartedly and die. The expedition wipes them out and plunders the village.

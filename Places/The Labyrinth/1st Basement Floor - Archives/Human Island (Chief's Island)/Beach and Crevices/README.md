# Beach and Crevices

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | beach / cliff passages |
| **Also known as** | Human Island Beach and Crevices; small beach below the cliff; three crevices |
| **First appearance** | ch. 512 |

## Description
A small beach below the cliffs of Human Island that takes about 20 minutes to search on foot. Three narrow cracks in the cliff face (front, left and right) lead inland. Squeezing through the middle one takes about 20 minutes, and a barbarian has to duck and keeps hitting his shoulders.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- The beach is the intended landing point. Climbing the cliffs is the alternative (ch. 512).

## Notable events
- ch. 512–514: At the exit of the middle crevice, the bodies of three Silver Lion Clan members (MacHoldin, Prenton and Rick) hang from vines, left by the natives as a warning.

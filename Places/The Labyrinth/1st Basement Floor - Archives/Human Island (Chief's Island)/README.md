## Description
A large island ringed by steep cliffs, smaller than Giants' Island. The only easy landing is a small beach under a cliff. Inland, a tropical rainforest of trees bigger than any Bjorn has seen, knee-high bushes and hanging vines covers the upper surface, and visibility on the ground is about 10 m. After each rainy season the ground is strewn with odorless chunks of flesh, bone and eyeballs from mixed monsters, then briefly carpeted with magic stones; after the second rainy season tens of thousands of mana stones lay scattered while groups of mutated monsters roamed. Legend says a giant dragon once lived here.

The island is home to an underground village of blue-skinned natives who call themselves "humans", ruled by the chief, who claims to be the Dragon Knight Cornelius Brungreed. The village also holds the floor's only working exit stele. Before the floor's deadline, the silver sea around it pulled back violently toward Rock Island.

## Mechanics
- It is about 5 hours by ship from Rock Island and from Library Island (ch. 512, 516).
- The rainy season runs from Day 14 to Day 21 of each cycle. Low-grade monsters burst into light on impact, while stronger ones survive and grow tougher over the 7 days. A mage's barrier under a tree could not hold, so the only safe place is the native village (ch. 516–517).
- Island trait "Persistence": monsters of every grade from 1 to 9 appear, allowing endless farming. There is no priest to erase essences (ch. 535).
- During the rainy season, explorers and about 300 village warriors fought under a mage-barrier "umbrella" at the village entrance. It was a "burning event" with huge EXP: Bjorn reached level 8 and the loot in under 2 hours was 2 third-grade, 7 fourth-grade and 1 fifth-grade essences (ch. 529–530).
- Trees from this island don't float, and a high-grade essence may be found lying on the ground after the rains (ch. 516, 518).
- Its dimensional stele is the only exit from the floor. Mages failed for more than ten days to force it open. It produced a blue portal once Bjorn had the Seventh Archive pact and led to the 7th District Dimension Plaza, since the labyrinth was closed. The chief says the dimensional-collapse portal opens here (ch. 551, 582, 607, 622).

## Notable events
- ch. 512–514: The Hex Clan camps on the coast hunting Diamonds, while the Silver Lion Clan pushes inland and is nearly wiped out by the natives.
- ch. 529–535: Bjorn's party fights through the rainy season with the village, then strikes the chief and escapes, setting the village on fire.
- ch. 536–537: Fleeing on day 5 of the rainy season, the party kills an Inmyeonjo and outruns a Titanus to their ship. The Hex warrior Shellianne Emertown dies.
- ch. 561–567: The royal expedition arrives on day 118 and gathers mana stones. The villagers ambush them, Jerome turns out to have allied with the chief, Reatlas intervenes, the chief takes Jerome's body, and the village is wiped out.
- ch. 569–582: The expedition uses the island as its base while mages study the stele. The Silver Lion Clan is found dead "in the basement".
- ch. 622: Bjorn leaves the floor through the stele.

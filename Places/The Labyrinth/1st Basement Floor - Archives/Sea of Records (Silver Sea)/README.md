## Description
A sea of silver waves that roll like mercury under a dark sky, connecting every island of the 1st Basement Floor. Debris drifts everywhere: wardrobes, fireplaces, tables, books, a rusty sword, a knight's helmet, a single sock, and paintings that somehow stay dry. Below the surface it is dark like the deep ocean, though some stretches are shallow, with a soft muddy bottom that gives way underfoot.

The sea seems to collect what has been lost in the labyrinth (Hans A's family portrait turned up here), and much of the furniture probably comes from ancient 6th-floor ships. Its name comes from the Tentakulan's habitat entry in the Archives books.

## Mechanics
- It is not real water, though it behaves like it. Elemental Barbarian (Water) mode does not work in it, but Oxygen Tank and Soul Outline (for tracking targets underwater) do (ch. 523).
- Different stretches hold different monsters, so routes matter (ch. 512, 524).
- Ships are summoned (wood floats on it) and driven by magitech propulsion fed with magic stones, which burns stones fast: about 20 million stones turned a 6-hour route into 2 hours (ch. 508, 524, 537).
- Magic stones sunk in it shrink and vanish without leaving traceable mana (ch. 538).

## Description
A knot hole in the tree's roots, larger than most caves, opens into winding tunnels deep inside the trunk. The team camped inside around a bonfire.

## Monsters

## Mechanics
- After six days (Day 35), the only "exit" found was a window-like opening at the base of the tree, about knee-high from the outside (ch. 518).

## Notable events

- ch. 518, 521: Bjorn is pulled into the community while sleeping here.

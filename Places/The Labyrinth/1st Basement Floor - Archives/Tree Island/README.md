# Tree Island

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | island |
| **Also known as** | Namu Island; giant tree island |
| **First appearance** | ch. 518 |

## Description
A single gigantic tree rising out of the silver sea, far bigger and taller than the Human Island. Its roots contain cave-sized knot holes leading into tunnels deep inside the trunk. Even after several expeditions it remained half shrouded in mystery.

## Monsters
- Unnamed new species — sprouts from vines in the bark (nicknamed "Nanari")
- Other new monsters, rising to 4th grade before the halfway point and possibly 1st grade higher up

## Mechanics
- Vines shoot from the bark as soon as a ship docks at the base, spawning monsters (ch. 518).
- Difficulty rises with height: 4th-grade monsters appeared before the team was halfway up, and Bjorn suspects 1st-grade ones higher still (ch. 523).
- Exploration hit a dead end (ch. 529).
- There is nothing at the top. If you see or hear something there, wear a blindfold and earplugs and hold your breath to pass safely (ch. 607).

## Notable events
- ch. 518: Discovered on Day 27; the team explores the root tunnels.
- ch. 523: The climb shows how fast difficulty rises with height.
- ch. 529: The party hits a dead end.
- ch. 607: The chief's notebook gives advice about the top of the tree.

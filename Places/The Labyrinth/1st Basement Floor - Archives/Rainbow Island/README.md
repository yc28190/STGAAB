## Description
At first only a straight, cylinder-shaped rainbow was seen in the island's direction. At its charted position there is just open sea. The wind picks up on approach and then dies into a dead calm, above water so deep that an anchor line never touches bottom. From about 200 m up, violent gusts in darkness hide something that seems to look down at anyone watching, perhaps a human-like face.

The real island, reached only by rainbow light, is a jungle around a pyramid temple, the Seventh Archive. Bjorn named it and believed it was the core of B1 and the key to escaping the floor.

## Monsters
None recorded. (See the Jungle and Seventh Archive pages.)

## Mechanics
- The rainbow is visible for only about 5 minutes after the rainy season ends, and only from the sea. Bearing: 21 degrees from the party's position, 34 degrees from Rock Island (ch. 538).
- About a week from Library Island at normal speed, but over a month by ship from the Stone Gate area (two weeks under full magitech propulsion, roughly 500,000 Stones per ship per hour) (ch. 551, 582, 608).
- Levitation, light orbs, drones and weighted recording orbs all fail to get through the calm zone (ch. 608–609).
- Entry: sit out the whole 7-day rainy season inside the calm zone, walled in by silver waves. At exactly 00:00 when it ends, rainbow light moves ships and crew to the jungle island (ch. 610).
- Exit: destroy the huge drone guarding the barrier at the jungle lake, sail onto the lake and wait one full turn of the pocket watch's second hand; rainbow light returns you to the calm zone (ch. 622).
- The chief's notebook does not mention the island (ch. 607).

## Notable events

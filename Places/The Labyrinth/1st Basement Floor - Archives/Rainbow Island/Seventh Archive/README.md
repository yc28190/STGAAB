## Description
A diamond-shaped pyramid temple at the heart of the Rainbow Island jungle. A central stair climbs to a stone door at roughly third-storey height, and from there stairs lead down to a seven-sided hall. Six stone gates line its walls, only one of them open, and a stone altar in the centre has seven rectangular grooves. Past the open gate, a sticky, liquid-like curtain of air leads into a futuristic prison where drones patrol corridors, a mechanical wolf sleeps in a hall and dazed monsters are led around.

According to its director, Panthelion was a secret underground Empire laboratory built to produce loyal biological weapons. It boomed during the witch war, when thousands of monsters were brought in each day. Hamsik (once called Marx) was a test subject who died there during a witch's attack.

## Mechanics
- Sealed by dimensional disruption until a burst of light. The temple's mind control then pulled the whole expedition inside, even from across the river (ch. 615).
- The altar's grooves fit the Golden Book; the party suspects there is one book per island (ch. 615).
- Hamsik remembered a hidden wall tile in a stairwell with a metal key card behind it (ch. 615). The card opens a secret door on the top floor that leads to the Control Room (ch. 617).
- Achievement for reaching 15% progress in the Seventh Archive grants permanent access to the Record Archive. Accepting the director's pact instead makes leaving permanent (ch. 622).

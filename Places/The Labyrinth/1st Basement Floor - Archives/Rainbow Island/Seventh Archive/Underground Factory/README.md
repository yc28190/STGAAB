# Underground Factory

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | facility (factory) |
| **Also known as** | Seventh Archive Factory; dome; underground factory |
| **First appearance** | ch. 615 |

## Description
A domed space the size of a football stadium at the very bottom of the Seventh Archive, reached by four staircases. Hundreds of rails run in from every part of the building. A beam device turns whatever arrives into magic stones, ceiling claw-hooks drop the stones into machines, and boxes of stones slide away on further rails.

## Monsters
- Synthetic monsters — combinations such as goblin+orc+ghoul; about average grade +2 (up to +4 with synergy); stay on the rails unless attacked
- [[Drone]] — repairs broken rails without attacking

## Mechanics
- Monsters on the rails become a single magic stone each. Three machines rebuild three stones into a combined monster, which is broken back down and rebuilt as the originals and returned to the prison, in an endless loop (ch. 616).
- Breaking a rail sets off an alarm; drones come to repair it but do not attack (ch. 616).
- Captured people were sent through too. Those turned to stone lost about 1,000 EXP; anyone recently promoted dropped a level and their newest essence stopped working (ch. 620).
- Custom essence machine: device A's stone sets the stats, B's the passives and C's the active skill. One active skill from C is chosen at random, and the essence's colour shows which (ch. 620).
- Drop rates seem higher than outside, but each combination can only be used a limited number of times (e.g. 21) (ch. 620–621).

## Notable events
- ch. 616–617: Captured expedition members on the rails are woken by slapping or with the Crown of Agony.
- ch. 621: Ainard gets two custom essences quickly; Amelia, Elwen, Misha, Muul Armin and others get theirs, plus 7 for the royal family.
- ch. 621–622: After many tries, Bjorn's tank combo (Arbet stats, Woomdal passive, Orc Hero [Gigantism] active) finally drops a green essence.

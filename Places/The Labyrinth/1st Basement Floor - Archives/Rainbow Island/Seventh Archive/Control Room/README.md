## Description
Hidden about 3 m down a secret passage from an empty hall on the Seventh Archive's top floor. Its walls are covered in rectangular glass screens showing live footage of the whole building. Cockpit-like rows of buttons and levers fill the room, with three unknown controls set apart in the middle. The lab's former director appears here on screen as a bouncing rainbow cluster of light.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Opened by sliding the key card into a thin slit next to an oddly visible wall seam (ch. 617).
- The buttons under each screen move that camera; red stops the rails, green restarts them, the left lever reverses them and the right lever switches to another area (ch. 618).
- A hidden doorless waiting room can be reached only by riding the right rails in the right order; it was found after days of mapping the rails (ch. 618, 620).
- The director offers a pass that opens the exit if Bjorn promises never to return. He warns that within half a year "he" will appear and only about 5 of the group would survive (ch. 618–619).
- Accepting: "Dimensional pact established. Access to Underground First Floor, Record Archive permanently restricted" (ch. 622).

## Notable events
- ch. 617: Bjorn opens the secret passage with the key card.
- ch. 618–619: The director, who says the witch later forgave him, greets Hamsik as Marx, mentions Captain Baekin and offers his deal.
- ch. 622: The pact is made and the exit opens.

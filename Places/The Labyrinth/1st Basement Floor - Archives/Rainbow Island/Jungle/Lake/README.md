## Description
A vast lake in the Rainbow Island jungle, once sealed behind a barrier. The lab's director pointed to it as the way off the island.

## Monsters
- Huge Drone — guardian of the barrier around the lake

## Mechanics
- Destroy the huge drone to open the barrier, launch the ship onto the lake, start the pocket watch and wait one full turn of the second hand; rainbow light returns everyone to the calm zone (ch. 622).

## Notable events
- ch. 622: The expedition destroys the drone and leaves Rainbow Island from the lake.

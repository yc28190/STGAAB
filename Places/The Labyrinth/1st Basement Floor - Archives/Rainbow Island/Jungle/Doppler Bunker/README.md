## Description
A manhole-like hatch with a turning handle, hidden under knee-high brush in a jungle clearing. A 20 m ladder shaft leads to a corridor ending in a round iron door that aura cannot scratch. Inside, the place feels like a submarine, all steel plates, pipes and cables. One corridor is lined with tubes of green culture fluid holding live Dopplers whose eyes follow visitors. A laboratory holds a giant central tube, two levers and shelves of blank books, and a lower level has another round door that will not open.

## Monsters
- [[Doppler]] — weak, languageless beings that look like twisted versions of the non-human races; kept in tubes and roaming the clearing

## Mechanics
- Footprints in the clearing were bait for a Doppler ambush (ch. 612).
- The iron door opened by itself: "Primary record access permission confirmed. Entry permitted." Only Bjorn could enter, since dimensional disruption still blocked the others (ch. 613).
- In the lab, the left lever creates a Doppler in the tube and the right lever removes it (ch. 614).
- Smashing the big tube exposed the Philosopher's Stone. Picking it up knocked Bjorn out for 10+ days and removed the "malignant genetic factors engraved on the character's body" (ch. 614).

## Notable events
- ch. 612: The party is lured into an ambush in the clearing.
- ch. 613: Bjorn alone is allowed inside and studies the Dopplers (horns, extra limbs, scales, lopsided faces, warrior hearts like barbarians, bodies left behind on death).
- ch. 614: Bjorn takes the Philosopher's Stone and begins to suspect that the non-human races were created artificially, possibly by the royal family.

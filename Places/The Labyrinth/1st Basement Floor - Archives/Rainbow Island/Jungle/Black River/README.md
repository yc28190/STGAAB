## Description
A river as wide as the Han, black as an oil slick and undrinkable, running east of the jungle temple. It is extremely deep for its width.

## Monsters
- [Mechanical Giant](../../../../../../Bestiary/mechanical_giant_ch612.md) — seen on the far bank

## Mechanics
- Boats sink unless mages treat them first. Contact was judged harmless, but people avoided it anyway (ch. 611–612).
- Bjorn crossed alone by sinking and walking along the riverbed (ch. 614).
- May be a version of the Aribeona river mouth on the 7th floor, though very different (ch. 611).

## Notable events
- ch. 611: Dopplers are sighted beyond the river.
- ch. 614: Bjorn walks across the riverbed.

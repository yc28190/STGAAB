## Description
A dense jungle with no insects, full of Dark Continent plants and scattered rusty scraps of a strange metal that rots like black flesh. At its centre, dozens of stone buildings surround a huge diamond-shaped pyramid temple, and a black river runs to the east. The expedition's large ship ends up sitting among the trees after the rainbow transfer.

## Monsters

- [Mechanical Giant](../../../../../Bestiary/mechanical_giant_ch612.md) — seen beyond the river

- Panthelion-type mechanical monsters

## Mechanics
- Appears to be a prototype of the 7th floor's Panthelion Ruins (ch. 611).
- A dimensional-disruption barrier encloses the area and the temple (ch. 611).
- A hatch hidden in the brush leads to the Doppler Bunker (ch. 613).
- The "rotting metal" may be linked to alchemy's failed attempts to create flesh (ch. 611).

## Notable events
- ch. 610–611: The expedition arrives and camps by the temple.
- ch. 615: Mind control from the temple takes everyone except Bjorn (inside the bunker) and Hamsik; drones carry the victims into the temple.
- ch. 620: Exploration winds down about three months after arrival.

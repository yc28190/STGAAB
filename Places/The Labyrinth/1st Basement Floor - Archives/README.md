## Description
A hidden floor beneath the labyrinth: a vast, dark silver sea that looks like mercury, dotted with islands and shimmering with silver waves. There are seven islands: Library, Stone Gate, Tree, Giants', Chief's (Human) Village, Rock and Rainbow. Visibility depends on where you are, from about 100 m on the starting island to much farther on the open sea, about 10 m in the island jungles and only 5 m at the library entrance. Items lost or discarded in the labyrinth wash up on its sea. In each rainy season, monsters pour down from the sky and silver waves rise to join sea and sky. When the rain ends, a straight pillar of rainbow light stands on the horizon for about five minutes.

Bjorn's clan were the first to discover it. It holds new species and enormous rewards, including Single Numbered Items, but also mysteries: the silver sea, the rainy season, dimensional collapse, the village chief, and Hamsik (Marx), who lives here and cannot leave. The Dragon Knight Cornelius Brungreed has been trapped here for ages in monster form. The royal family later declared it a restricted area.

## Mechanics
- It is reached through the hidden portal stele in the 1st Floor's Crystal Cave, using the three Nameless Fragments within the first 7 days. The fragments are emblem pieces that drop from unknown statues on the 3rd Floor when they are enchanted with Distortion magic (ch. 507–508, 544–545, 790).
- The entry portal is one-way and takes at most 60 people per opening. The first entry earns an achievement and an Honor Stone record ("Bjorn Yandel and his comrades first discovered the first basement level") (ch. 507, 582).
- The floor does not close when the 1st Floor does, and there is no normal exit. The only working exit is the stele in the Chief's village, and the chief's notebook says to ignore all other steles. The Hamsik map shows a stele in the Giants' Island cave pointing down (presumed B2) and one on Chief's Island pointing up (ch. 508, 551, 607).
- There are two kinds of monster: ordinary ones that drop magic stones and essences, and "intact-corpse" monsters with exaggerated features whose bodies don't dissolve. The two kinds fight each other. Corpse monsters killed under Distortion magic can dissolve and drop loot, though the cast often fails. Many new species have skills borrowed from several existing monsters, and some give no EXP (ch. 509–511, 517–518, 527–528).
- Time cycle: after the first rainy season, time runs 1:1 with the city, with some drift. When the labyrinth reopens, dozens of portals in the sky over Rock Island bring 60 newcomers (ch. 518–522, 552, 622).
- Rainy season: about 7 days per cycle (Day 14–21). Monsters of every grade, including rift and hidden-field monsters, fall from the sky. The first days are the heaviest, and almost nothing falls by day 5. Monsters that hit the ground or sea die and leave magic stones. Stones in the sea vanish, and those on land decompose from 00:00 on the 3rd day after the rain ends. Safe zones are Library Island, the Giants' Island cave and the Chief's village. Ship barriers deflect falling monsters, and mages are drained for days afterward (ch. 522, 529, 536–538, 553, 560–561, 610).
- Chief's rules: if waves surge during the rainy season, stop and hide, since every island except the rocky starting island has a shelter. If a dimensional collapse begins, go to the village island, where a portal to the surface opens (ch. 607).
- Its system lets you store only an essence's stats, without its skills (ch. 625).
- Leaving permanently under the Seventh Archive pact bars Bjorn from ever returning. When the deadline passes, "Record Lord Rekardo begins wandering the floors", and a floor lord may appear after half a year (ch. 619, 622).
- Entries in the library books (e.g. "Bjorn, son of Yandel, the first explorer to enter…") suggest a link to the mythical Record Stone (ch. 510–511).

## Notable events
- ch. 499–502: Melbeth pays Bjorn in advance for information about the floor.
- ch. 507–512: The first 60 enter: Bjorn's 7-member clan, the Silver Lion Clan (20, Matherkin Lillgrams), the Hex Clan (19, Wyte Hex), two five-person teams (one led by Myrtane) and a four-person team.
- ch. 515–516: Bjorn meets Cornelius Brungreed in monster form.
- ch. 522: A second wave arrives: the Armin Exploration Team (20, Mul Armin) and the Einperial Clan (40, Magnon Nias).
- ch. 544–545: The royal family closes B1 to large clans on pain of death to stop wartime desertions. As the Investigator at the Round Table, Bjorn reveals that he found two single-number items here, setting off a rush.
- ch. 552–562: The royal 1st Expedition Army (180 people under Jerome Saintred) arrives at the end of day 107, with Anabada as its temporary 4th squad. The warships cross through the peak of the rainy season.
- ch. 577–622: Royal expedition arc. Bjorn takes the Seventh Archive pact to leave.
- ch. 625–638: Over a hundred essences come back. Most are handed out to District 7 explorers, and the best are kept in secret.
- ch. 783–790: Bjorn recalls exploring with Saintred. At the reopening a team is seen heading for B1.

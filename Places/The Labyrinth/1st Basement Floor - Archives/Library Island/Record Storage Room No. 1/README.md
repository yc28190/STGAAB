# Record Storage Room No. 1

| | |
|---|---|

## Description
A small circular chamber hidden behind the library's shelves. When the right books are pulled halfway out, a sealed stone door opens and blinding white light spills out like a portal. Inside, the ceiling is so low that barbarians must stoop. Shelves of colourful books line the walls, more books stand in towers on the floor, and there is a desk.

It is the reward room for the library's hidden piece, the "legacy of Gavrilius". After the battle with Kashan, it was seen through the light of the shattered stone gate looking as if a typhoon had swept through, and royal soldiers later stripped it bare.

## Monsters
- [[Kashan]] — grade 1 guardian of the official route, summoned from the top-shelf book; luck-manipulating boss
- [[Hamsik]] — the room's keeper, who opens the secret door

## Mechanics
- Hamsik's door is a hidden shortcut: it opens only through Hamsik, you leave by the same glowing door, and it closes once Hamsik steps out (ch. 550–551, 559).
- Walls, floor and ceiling hide nothing else (ch. 550).
- Contents: a golden book in the ancient language meant for a "qualified" person; a fountain pen that guarantees an essence of whatever color is painted on a summoning-book drawing (grade 3 or lower, 2 uses); 7 special summoning books (one with 10+ mimics, colored-monster books guaranteeing that color's essence, and "???" books); a map of B1; and a damaged picture frame that cannot be restored (ch. 550–551).
- Official route: defeat the grade 1 monster from the top-shelf book. This reveals the "hidden First Expeditionary Record Archive" and grants record-viewing rights to the top contributor (ch. 558).
- The top contributor becomes "qualified"; Hamsik then hands over the golden book, though its contents stay the same (ch. 560).
- Kashan's kit: Ominous Prophecy fixes luck at -200; beams from its eyed tails; Life Leech threads that can be cut with aura; Flame of Doom pillars and fire rain; Death Sentence (scales with luck); Cross of Fate, which brings plague wolves (ch. 558).

## Notable events
- ch. 548–549: The hidden piece "legacy of Gavrilius" is identified.
- ch. 550–551: Hamsik opens the secret room for Bjorn, who takes items from it.
- ch. 558–559: The official route is cleared. Kashan bites Bjorn's head and Jerome lands the killing blow; royal soldiers then loot the room.
- ch. 560: Bjorn, as top contributor, is deemed qualified and receives the golden book from Hamsik.

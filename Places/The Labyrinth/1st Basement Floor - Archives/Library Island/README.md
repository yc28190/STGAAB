## Description
A single temple-like building raised on two pillars above shallow water. Its only entrance is wrapped in a shadow that pulls back as you approach. Inside, a wide giant-sized staircase of more than 200 steps, big enough for Gigantification, descends through dusty air into a vast underground library. Bookshelves rise tier upon tier, connected by stairs. At the center stands a summoning altar facing a stone door about 20 m tall, through which summoned monsters emerge. After visitors leave, the scattered books tidy themselves again, and footsteps echo in the silence.

It is the home of Hamsik, the library "ghost", and was Bjorn's farming base on days 76–103. The royal expedition later set up a makeshift mage lab inside and climbed to the top shelf.

## Mechanics
- Ships must anchor offshore and send a small boat in. Visibility at the entrance is about 5 m (ch. 510).
- Placing a summoning book from the shelves on the altar summons its monster through the stone door. Higher shelves mean harder monsters, from grade 9 up to grade 1 at the top. Used books open stairs to the next shelf (ch. 543, 548, 551).
- Books and stairs reset over time, so farming is effectively endless but reaching the top is hard. If you sense something nearby, leave for a day and the shelves will be back in order (ch. 543, 607).
- The island is a safe zone in the rainy season (ch. 553).
- Between 11 PM and 3 AM, a "ghost" (Hamsik) appears anywhere in the library, including the stairs outside. Each viewer sees a different horrifying form, which vanishes when they look away (ch. 548).
- Breaking the stone door, as the royal expedition did with the grade 1 spell Sphere of Upheaval, removes the summon delay. It regenerates from the edges. The expedition reached the grade 4 zone in 16 hours, about 5 times faster than Anabada (ch. 554).
- The last book holds 7 grade 1 species. Killing Kashan turned the stone gate into a portal to the reward room (ch. 556–559).
- After the island is cleared, the Golden Book can be looted from Hamsik's house (ch. 615). Library Island is about 4 weeks from Rainbow Island (ch. 609).

- ch. 546–548: Bjorn farms here and meets the ghost.
- ch. 554–559: The royal army rotates squads between guarding the door and fetching books. It reaches grade 3 by day 111 and the last shelf on day 114. Bjorn uses the fountain pen on a Bellarios book. Seven people die in the climb and 28 in the Kashan fight.
- ch. 605–622: Bjorn persuades Hamsik to come aboard, then drops him back here when leaving the floor.
- ch. 800, 806: Hundreds of explorers are recalled fighting Kashan here with heavy losses.

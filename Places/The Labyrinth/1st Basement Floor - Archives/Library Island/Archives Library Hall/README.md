## Description
A vast cylindrical hall whose lights switch on all at once when someone enters. Its ceiling is as high as the entry staircase is deep, and curved bookshelves cover the walls all the way up, holding thousands of light, person-sized books, nearly all of them blank. A table-like stone slab sits in the middle of the floor, and a large stone door is set between the shelves opposite the stairs.

The hall is effectively a self-service monster arena. Certain books are bestiary entries, and placing one on the slab summons the monsters it describes. Because there are no respawn waits and plenty of time to prepare, it became one of the richest hunting grounds on the floor for those who reached it first.

## Mechanics
- Some books are encyclopedia entries: a picture plus ancient-language text giving the monster's name, habitat, exact base stats, every essence color with its skills, the origin of its name, and who named it on which floor (ch. 510).
- Placing an entry on the stone slab makes it glow blue; the stone door opens and the monsters described come out. Killing them closes the door, reveals the staircase to the next bookshelf level, and turns the used book blank (ch. 510–511).
- The shelves can be climbed, but books cannot be taken that way; progress must be earned level by level (ch. 511).
- Higher levels give stronger and more numerous monsters: 100+ per book in the mid-upper levels and pairs of 3rd-grade monsters near the top Bjorn reached (ch. 511).
- No respawn timers and room to prepare; Bjorn's team earned over 10 million stones and 7 essences in about 3 days. Beginner's Luck rarely triggers here (ch. 510–511).
- Only species that someone has already discovered get entries, and rare monsters such as the Bellarios never appear (ch. 511).
- Taking the top-level books stops anyone else from climbing past that level (ch. 511).

# Ham's House

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | building (dwelling / reward room) |
| **Also known as** | Hamsik's house; Hamsik's room |
| **First appearance** | ch. 582 |

## Description
The living quarters of Hamsik, the strange caretaker of the library on Library Island. It served as a reward room holding valuable items, and the expedition left it wrecked when they departed.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- The map of the floor that Bjorn took came from here (ch. 582).
- It held the "Golden Book", an ancient-language book that later turned out to fit one of the grooves on the Seventh Archive's altar (ch. 596, 615).

## Notable events
- ch. 582: Bjorn takes the floor map from Hamsik's house.
- ch. 596: The Golden Book's story (a brother who leaves his village to become a wizard and a sister left grieving) is linked to the witch story of the 3rd Archive rift.
- ch. 607: The chief's notebook says the golden book will not help anyone escape.
- ch. 615: The book's size matches a groove on the Seventh Archive altar.

## Chapters
582, 596, 607, 615

## Description
A four-phase rift. Phase 1 is a dark maze hall branching left, right and straight, with seamless walls that fire magic cannons, leading to a square stone chamber holding a watermelon-sized magic stone. Phase 2 is an elegant mansion hallway mirrored endlessly, its rooms full of boxes. Phase 3 takes place inside a giant worm's body, with a slippery, writhing floor and acid oozing up from every surface. Phase 4 is a void like outer space around a transparent glass pyramid staircase, with a giant eyeball-shaped magic stone at the top.

## Mechanics
- Two-person rift; the normal version opens only rarely. Its [Defense System] grows stronger the longer the fight lasts (ch. 591).
- Smash each phase's magic stone to move on; a banshee wail turns everything black between phases (ch. 591).
- Phase 2 boxes: finding the stone usually takes about 500 boxes. A roughly 1-in-100,000 cursed box drains 5,000 EXP (level loss, random essence slots locked) and strengthens the guardian. An equally rare golden box gives "Dreaming Soul" (+10,000 EXP once you reach level 10, needed for level 11) and weakens the guardian (ch. 591).
- Phase 3: acid keeps rising, so reach the heart stone quickly (ch. 591).
- Phase 4: climb the stairs through waves of monsters, whose grade depends on how fast and how lucky you were earlier. [Target Lock] and pushback pulses; falling off means death (ch. 592).
- The guardian essence's passive [Soul Contract] gives an instant level 8 but no further EXP; one once sold for billions (ch. 592).

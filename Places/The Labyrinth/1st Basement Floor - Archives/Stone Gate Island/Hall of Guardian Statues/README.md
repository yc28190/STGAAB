## Description
A huge straight indoor corridor, at least three times the size of the Hall of Glory, with smooth marble floor, walls and ceiling, wrapped in darkness. Endless rows of statues of rift guardians line both walls: Death Knight, King Slime, Lycanthrope, Iron Cavalry, a drake, a troll, Cambormier and many more. At the far end stands a massive stone gate that even first-rank magic cannot dent.

It served as the expedition's base for weeks, with barracks tents set up inside.

## Monsters
- Statues of [King Slime](../../../../../Bestiary/king_slime_ch374.md), [Death Knight](../../../../../Bestiary/death_knight_ch32.md), [Lycanthrope](../../../../../Bestiary/lycanthrope_ch104.md), [Iron Cavalry](../../../../../Bestiary/iron_cavalry_ch582.md), a drake, a troll, [Cambormier](../../../../../Bestiary/cambormere_ch32.md) and others — each opens a boss-only rift of that guardian

## Mechanics
- The hall is not inside a rift. Its terrain repairs itself and the statues give off faint magic (ch. 583).
- Touching a statue opens a portal to a boss-only version of that guardian's rift (ch. 583).
- If no one enters, the portal closes after 10 minutes. It cannot reopen while someone is inside. Statues have no use limit (at least 7 clears tested) (ch. 584).
- Floor 1–4 rifts are solo; 5th-floor rifts admit two people (two portals). A cleared rift cannot be re-entered; you just come straight back out (ch. 584).
- Only essences drop (no rift stones or Numbers items), and the guardian's essence is guaranteed. The guardian-kill bonus of +3 EXP applies (ch. 588).
- A light comes on above each statue the first time it is cleared. There are 41 statues: 27 known guardians from floors 1–5 plus 14 unknown Grade-5-type guardians such as Cambormier (ch. 584, 590).
- Clearing all 41 opens the stone gate onto a red rift portal (ch. 592). A mural inside the door was read by mages as a five-person limit (ch. 593).
- After the 3rd Archive is cleared, the statues stop opening portals (ch. 607).

## Notable events
- ch. 583–589: Bjorn solos almost every statue rift; the mages time and rank every kill (Amelia's 47 seconds on the Glacier Cave), Raven screens other challengers, and the Anabada members get as far as the 3rd floor.
- ch. 590: Duke Cambormier talks sensibly with Bjorn once, then is "hotfixed" into silence for everyone after.
- ch. 592–593: All 41 statues cleared; the stone gate opens onto the red portal.
- ch. 604–605: Bjorn takes over as commander in a barracks tent in the hall.

# Statue Rift - Glacier Cave

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | rift (statue rift, boss-only) |
| **Also known as** | Statue Rift: Glacier Cave (Kirdyu); glacier cave (troll variant) |
| **First appearance** | ch. 583 |

## Description
Cold seeps up through your soles, and the ice walls shine white like glass. A narrow path opens into a huge frozen cavern strewn with torn Empire soldiers' corpses under bloodstained Empire banners, along with dead ice orcs, frost wolves, yetis and the body of Tyrant Tarunbas, all heaped around a mound of corpses in the middle. The cave goes dark in the final phase.

It is the unknown-guardian (hidden boss) version of the 1st floor's Glacier Cave rift.

## Monsters
- [[Kirdyu]] — Corrupted Beast, a black guardian troll over 5 m tall; high-level mutant
- [[Tyrant Tarunbas]] — the regular guardian (has its own statue); present as a corpse
- [[Ice Orc]], [[Frost Wolf]], [[Yeti]] — present only as corpses

## Mechanics
- Solo rift (ch. 587).
- Kirdyu wields Tarunbas's club (No. 9712 Snowfield Club, adds cold damage) and, as a high-level mutant, uses the Ogre skill [Swing] (ch. 587).
- Attempts to talk to it failed in modern speech, the ancient tongue and its own language (ch. 587).
- Its cooldowns disappear in the darkened final phase (ch. 587).
- Bonus EXP: +1 for the mutant and +3 for the guardian (ch. 587).

## Notable events
- ch. 587: Bjorn beats Kirdyu in a 30-minute fight. Amelia later posts the fastest recorded clear (47 seconds).

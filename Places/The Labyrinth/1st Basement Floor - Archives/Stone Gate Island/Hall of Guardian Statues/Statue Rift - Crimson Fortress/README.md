## Description
Brick walls like a medieval castle, candleholders along the walls and old bloodstains on the floor. It resembles the Blood Castle's final chapter (the Demon Worship Room), but instead of a square loop it is a straight corridor leading to a boss room behind a door.

## Monsters
- [[Cambormier]] — Vampire Duke, unknown (hidden) guardian
- [[Blatter]] — Cursed Knight; has his own separate statue in the hall

## Mechanics
- Solo rift (ch. 586).
- On Bjorn's run, Cambormier woke up sane, talked with him for 3 h 21 min and asked to be killed before his madness returned. Later challengers found him silent and mad from the start (ch. 586, 590).

## Notable events
- ch. 585–586: Cambormier tells his story. He ruled a neutral border duchy in an ancient Empire era. Karui's priests "cured" his sick daughter, who swelled into a flesh-eating monster kept first in the dungeon and then in a reservoir. The duke became a vampire and his home became known as the Blood-Red Fortress. The Tri-God Church's army killed him beneath it, and he prayed to the witch for his daughter. He says only humans existed in his time, with no elves, dwarves or other races, and guesses that guardians are summoned whenever explorers fight them.
- ch. 590: The guardian is "hotfixed" and no longer speaks to anyone.

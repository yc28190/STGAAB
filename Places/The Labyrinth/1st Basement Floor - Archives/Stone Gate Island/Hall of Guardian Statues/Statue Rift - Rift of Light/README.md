# Statue Rift - Rift of Light

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | rift (statue rift, boss-only) |
| **Also known as** | Rift of Light (5th floor rift); Statue Rift: Rift of Light |
| **First appearance** | ch. 589 |

## Description
A cliff with cold air blowing up from a bottomless dark pit. Jumping in drops you into the boss room.

## Monsters
- [[Hell Giant Herja]] — guardian with three horns, one eye, a rotten open chest showing its ribs and a long tongue

## Mechanics
- Two-person rift. The jump into the pit deals no fall damage (ch. 589).
- In the normal rift, Herja usually flees through the rift (ch. 590).
- Herja links to a target with [Wave Link] to spread area attacks; strike its eye while the link is active (ch. 590).
- Its essence has only [Wave Link] (skill sharing, with no extra MP cost for summons and clones) and the passive [One-Eyed] (+200 vision, sees invisible things) (ch. 590).

## Notable events
- ch. 589–590: Amelia kills Herja alongside Bjorn and takes the essence.

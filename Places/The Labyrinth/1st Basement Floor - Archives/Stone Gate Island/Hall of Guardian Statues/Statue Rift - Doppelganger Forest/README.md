## Description
A boss-only copy of the 4th floor's Doppelganger Forest rift, opened from its guardian statue in the Hall of Guardian Statues.

## Monsters
- [Doppelganger](../../../../../../Bestiary/doppelganger_ch177.md) — guardian; copies the challenger, silent this time

## Mechanics
- In the original rift, portals are single-use and bound people move together (ch. 583).
- Solo, but the fight's inside and outside phases run together. The Doppelganger copies you and uses [Self Replication] and [Crystallize] (13 times), but cannot copy Numbers items (ch. 587).
- Its split-team start is later compared to the 3rd Archive (ch. 595).

## Notable events
- ch. 587: Bjorn takes about 7 hours to beat it with borrowed gear.

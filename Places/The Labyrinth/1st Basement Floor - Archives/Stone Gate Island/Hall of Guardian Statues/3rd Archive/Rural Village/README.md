## Description
A country village under attack by an army, with a mountain behind it and a cave to hide in. It is the opening setting of Elwen's chapter as the "witch" girl. Bjorn guessed that this region may later become the Land of the Dead.

## Monsters
- Monsters — present but do not attack the girl

## Mechanics
- Elwen's chapter 1: she escapes the village, climbs the mountain with soldiers in pursuit and hides in a cave, where a knight (Ravigion) gives her a compass (ch. 595).

## Notable events
- ch. 595: Elwen's opening scene in the 3rd Archive.

## Description
The same terrain as the 5th floor's Daemagyeong, but cut by four canyons. Canyon paths drop away into the abyss, with flowers growing along the cliff edges. After the final explosion, everything collapses into ruins and debris, leaving a humid wasteland.

## Monsters
- [Half-demon](../../../../../../../Bestiary/half_demon_ch599.md) — Grade 3, blocks one of the paths
- Grade 4+ monsters
- [Silriat](../../../../../../../Bestiary/silriat_ch320.md) — Lord of Silence, 5th-floor lord; can be spared
- [Soul Eater](../../../../../../../Bestiary/soul_eater_ch264.md) — the rift's guardian; uses [Soul Cavalry]
- [Tempted Priest](../../../../../../../Bestiary/tempted_priest_ch604.md) — Karui's priest (the corrupted Eden Hestia)

## Mechanics
- Field effect "Daemagyeong": Control steadily drops, bringing violent urges and loss of self-restraint (the witch role is immune). Eating the cliff flowers restores Control (ch. 600).
- Gavhin's intuition is cut off until the rift is cleared (ch. 600).
- A whispering voice lures travellers toward the Soul Eater (ch. 601).
- Inside the range of Silriat's [Silent Curse], sound is blocked and all essence skills are sealed (ch. 602).
- Sparing Silriat weakens the guardian. Silriat shielded the team from Hestia's [Unstable Vessel] explosion but was then killed by Hestia (ch. 603).
- After the guardian falls, red and blue portals appear; the blue one shrinks after Silriat's death (ch. 603–604).

## Notable events
- ch. 599–602: The party fights through the canyons under the Control drain.
- ch. 603: The Soul Eater dies and drops a guardian essence never seen before; Hestia's explosion levels the area.
- ch. 604: The Tempted Priest is killed and the party leaves.

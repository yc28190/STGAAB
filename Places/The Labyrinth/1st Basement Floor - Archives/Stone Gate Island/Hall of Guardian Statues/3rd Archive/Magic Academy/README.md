## Description
A school of magic in the 3rd Archive's story, and the setting of Gavhin's opening chapter as the mage. Gavhin, playing the girl's wizard brother, still looked like an old man wearing a student uniform.

## Monsters
- Monsters that attack the academy on a practice day

## Mechanics
- Gavhin's chapter 1: he holds off the monster attack, receives a royal medal and gets a letter saying his sister has gone missing (ch. 597).

## Notable events

- ch. 598: Gavhin's appearance (an old man in uniform) is remarked on.

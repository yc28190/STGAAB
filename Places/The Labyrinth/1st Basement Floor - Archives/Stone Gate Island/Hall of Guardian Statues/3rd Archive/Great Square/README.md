# Great Square

| | |

## Description
A city square packed with thousands of people screaming for the witch's death, with gallows set up in it. A huge glowing magic circle later spreads across the square, and a blinding flash empties it. More than ten years of story time have passed by this point.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Bjorn and a ten-to-twelve-year-old Elwen arrive bound with ropes and stripped of their gear; the only option is to wait for rescue (ch. 598).
- The chief, disguised as a knight reading the emperor's decree, mass-teleports the crowd away using Gavhin's ancient magic (magic has no effect on the witch) (ch. 598).
- A dimensional barrier keeps everyone in the city; the clear condition is to survive until nightfall (ch. 598).

## Notable events
- ch. 596: The Distorted Journey drops Bjorn and Elwen here.
- ch. 598: The rescue at the gallows.

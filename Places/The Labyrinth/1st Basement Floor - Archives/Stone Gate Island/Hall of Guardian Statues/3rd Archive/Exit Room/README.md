# Exit Room

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | room (reward / exit room) |
| **Also known as** | 3rd Archive Exit Room; sealed room beyond the stone gate; stone chamber |
| **First appearance** | ch. 604 |

## Description
A sealed stone chamber behind the stone gate of the Hall of Guardian Statues. Three figurine-sized statues sit on the floor, and a shimmering portal floats in the middle of the room.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- The portal leads back outside; the expedition waited for the rainy season to pass before using it (ch. 605, 607).
- Statues: placing a rift stone in a statue's groove opens a guardian-only rift portal for about 30 minutes. The rift's grade follows the stone (a grade 9 stone gave a Blood Fortress wavelength). Each statue then turns black and slowly regains its colour as a cooldown (ch. 606).
- The statues are compared to the No. 18 Crack Generator (ch. 606).

## Notable events
- ch. 604: The stone gate opens on the Exit Room after the 3rd Archive.
- ch. 606: The statues are tested; Bjorn keeps them, and Raven considers using Cambormier's rift stone.
- ch. 607: The expedition leaves through the portal.

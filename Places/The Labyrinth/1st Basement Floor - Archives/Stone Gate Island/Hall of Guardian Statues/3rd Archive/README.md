## Description
A story rift behind the stone gate of the Hall of Guardian Statues, opened through an ominous red portal. Inside, the party lives through chapters of a tale set in the ancient Empire before the age of witches: a ruined city under monster attack, a village, a magic academy, a desert city, a cave much like the Crystal Cave, a great execution square and a version of Daemagyeong.

The story follows a girl branded a witch (the Earth Witch is suspected) and her wizard brother. The Immortal King Ravigion III and his wife Karnon appear as allies, and the rift also holds a replica of the Immortal King.

## Mechanics
- No natural fading; 5-person cap (ch. 593).
- Field effect "Role assigned" (ch. 593–597): Mercenary (Bjorn): attack ×3, defense −70%, regeneration rises with lost HP. Girl/witch (Elwen): all stats fixed at 10%, healing −90%, cannot be attacked by monsters, turned into a child. Knight (the chief): all stats +200%. Mage (Gavhin): ancient magic plus an intuition about what each chapter requires. Cleric (Eden Hestia): unlimited divine power, cannot heal self, zero mental resistance.
- Teams start in separate chapter-1 scenes and meet up later. Time jumps wildly: the others spent about half a year inside, Bjorn about a week, and 30 days passed outside (ch. 593–604).
- Special condition "The Distorted Journey" lets the party skip ahead (ch. 596).
- Sparing Silriat makes the Soul Eater easy to kill (ch. 603).
- After the guardian, two exit portals appear: blue (safe return) and red (dangerous, extra reward; not taken) (ch. 603).
- Killing the Tempted Priest triggers "Special Condition: Third Record Achieved" and drops the Secret Numbers item "Crown of Agony" (ch. 604).
- Back in the hall, the stone gate stays shut and then opens onto the Exit Room (ch. 604).

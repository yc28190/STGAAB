## Description
The heart of a city reduced to ruins, its streets piled with human and monster corpses while soldiers carry off the wounded. The scene is set in the Year of the Sun, before the witches.

## Monsters
- [[Troll]] — part of the attacking horde
- Monster horde

## Mechanics
- Bjorn wakes as the mercenary "Bjorn Yandel" in the middle of the battle. Saving NPCs is optional (ch. 594).
- The chapter ends when an Empire knight hires him to escort a girl (ch. 594).

## Notable events
- ch. 594: Bjorn's opening scene in the 3rd Archive.
- ch. 596: An NPC mercenary friend from this chapter loses everything when Nelbus Fortress falls and tries to have Bjorn hanged.

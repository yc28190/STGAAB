## Description
A dark, dripping cave very much like the 1st floor's Crystal Cave, but with no monsters and slightly different terrain. Its centre, where the Gabrielius monument would stand, is empty.

## Monsters
- [Hamsik](../../../../../../../Bestiary/hamsik_ch548.md) — ancient form; hidden boss, roughly mid-to-upper Grade 3, who wants to kill the girl

## Mechanics
- The compass points west toward the Rocky Desert exit (ch. 595).
- Going to the centre triggers ancient Hamsik. You can simply survive him, but subduing him earns his conversation and information (ch. 595).
- His potion orb gives the girl some control over "Forsaken" monsters (not high-grade ones) (ch. 595–596).
- He opens an unstable portal, triggering "Special Condition: The Distorted Journey", which skips ahead to the Great Square (ch. 596).

## Notable events
- ch. 595: Bjorn subdues Hamsik, who explains the "Abandoned" (intelligent races such as goblins, kobolds and orcs who gave up their faith) and the "Forsaken" (monsters).
- ch. 596: The Distorted Journey sends Bjorn and the girl to the Great Square.

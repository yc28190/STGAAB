# Outskirts Safehouse

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | story scene (safehouse) |
| **Also known as** | 3rd Archive - Outskirts Safehouse; hidden basement; residence on the outskirts |
| **First appearance** | ch. 597 |

## Description
An empty house on the outskirts of the city, with stairs hidden in the floor leading down to a basement where the party hides after the execution-square rescue.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- The team regroups here, and the archbishop returns Bjorn's confiscated gear in a bag (ch. 598).
- After midnight, Ravigion arrives and hands over a black gem whose mist ends the chapter. The next goal is to reach the "Great Magic View" (Daemagyeong) and face the evil god Karui (ch. 598).

## Notable events
- ch. 598: Ravigion Kemmelvi Lapdonia, the future Immortal King, visits the safehouse.

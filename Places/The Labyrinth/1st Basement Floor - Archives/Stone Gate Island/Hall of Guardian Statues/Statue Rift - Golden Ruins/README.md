## Description
A boss-only copy of the 4th floor's Golden Ruins rift, opened from its statue in the Hall of Guardian Statues.

## Monsters
- [[Golden Fang Calphion]] — guardian with a heavy shell that must be stripped away

## Mechanics
- Solo rift; Bjorn spent about 3 hours peeling off Calphion's shell (ch. 587).
- Killer bonus EXP +6 and guardian bonus +3 (ch. 587).
- Raven barred weaker explorers (e.g. "Big Hammer") from attempting it (ch. 590).

## Notable events
- ch. 587: Bjorn clears it solo.

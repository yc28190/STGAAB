## Description
Not really an island: there are no trees, no soil and no land, only a gigantic stele-like stone structure rising out of the silver waves about a week's sail from Library Island. When the gate is active, a huge swirling portal shimmers inside it like a mirage.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Touching the stele opens a portal to the Hall of Guardian Statues (ch. 582).
- The chief could not open it in his earlier body, and a lone survivor who went in never came back. There is no clear limit on numbers; 100+ people entered (ch. 583).
- Once the archive rift's exit portal was used, the gate closed and sank under the sea as soon as the last person came out. It cannot be retried at once and is expected to rise again when its cooldown ends (ch. 607).
- No Golden Book was found in its reward room, perhaps because the "true ending" was not reached (ch. 615).

## Notable events
- ch. 582–583: The expedition finds the gate and enters.
- ch. 604–605: The expedition returns from the 3rd Archive.
- ch. 607: The gate sinks after the last person leaves.

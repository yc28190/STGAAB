## Description
A dark forest under what looks like a night sky strewn with Milky-Way lights, though it is really a very high ceiling. It stays about as bright as an unlit alley at night, which still makes it one of the brighter 2nd-floor fields. The trees are as hard as rock, the ground is damp, and leaves and bushes hide traps everywhere. Goblins climb out of holes like rabbit burrows. An empty clearing about 50 m across surrounds the portal from the Crystal Cave.

It is the usual fastest route up from the 1st floor and the site of the famous Goblin Forest battle that ended Noark's great ambush.

## Mechanics
- Reached from the 1st floor's northern portal; arrivals are thrown about 3 m into the air, and the trip back down throws people hard (ch. 12, 15, 222).
- Traps are part of the terrain everywhere, not a sign of goblins. Variants and higher grades increase farther from the portal; beyond 3 km a group shows up about every five minutes. Goblins respawn from holes that cave in if dug into (ch. 12–13).
- Light orbs make it as bright as day but draw goblins (ch. 163).
- There are eight portal steles to the 3rd floor; activating one turns the rest into ordinary stones. Without a Guide, finding it means circling the outer edge for 2–3 days on average; with one, about a day (ch. 163).
- The portal back to the 1st floor stays until the 1st floor closes. On Day 7 around 23:50 a return portal opens in the centre of the forest (ch. 12–13, 230).
- Abyssal Fog's seal does not reach here, so skills work again (ch. 225).
- Special terrain effect [Recoil]: landing with a total weight of 1,000 kg or more throws everyone in the impact radius into the air (ch. 227–228).
- One of four 2nd-floor fields that all lead to the 3rd floor's Pilgrim's Path (ch. 398). An 8th-grade monster's stone from here can open a 1st-floor rift (ch. 374).

## Notable events
- ch. 13–15: Bjorn and Erwen farm here on Days 4–7; Erwen absorbs a goblin archer essence, and they kill four ambushing humans.
- ch. 163: Apple Nark camps in the outer area and finds the portal by noon the next day.
- ch. 224–229: The Goblin Forest battle. The Corpse Collector (Abet Nekrapetto), the Ruin Scholar (Belvev Ruinjenes) and Blue Mane wait with low-level Noark explorers. Melter Pend's clan kills Blue Mane; Kyle loses the Ruin Scholar's [Parallel World] duel but is spared; Bjorn (Elemental Barbarian, Earth) and Erwen ([Spirit Transformation]) nearly kill the Corpse Collector until the Ruin Scholar intervenes, kills Daria (who dies saving Elwen) and carries the Clown away.
- ch. 230: About a thousand more explorers arrive by following Bjorn's wall marks.
- ch. 263: Amelia's group finds the trap they set near the portal removed; the party regroups.
- ch. 505: Over a hundred explorers fleeing Verzak gather in the central clearing.
- ch. 628, 775: The battle is remembered as what made Bjorn famous and won him his baron title.

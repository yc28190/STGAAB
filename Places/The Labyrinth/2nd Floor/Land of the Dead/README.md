## Description
A dark, oppressive undead field with the shortest line of sight of any 2nd-floor area. Near the arrival point the ground is black, soggy earth that squelches underfoot, a damp swamp dotted with ruined stone buildings overgrown with vines. About five hours to the north it hardens into rocky, hilly ground where the walls of ruined buildings offer shelter for a camp. The air is musty and damp, and the distant wailing of banshees plays constantly in the background, growing louder further north.

It is the southern field of the 2nd floor, linked to the ghoul district of the 1st floor, and is considered the easiest 2nd-floor route to the 3rd floor's Pilgrim's Path. It is home to undead monsters and is a known haunt of pillagers. Bjorn later wondered whether Erwen's ancient village became this place.

## Mechanics
- Reached through the southern 1st-floor portal tied to the ghoul district; Bjorn once arrived via a dark-zone portal whose message misleadingly said Goblin Forest (ch. 22–23, 110, 296).
- No traps; monster numbers triple from day 3 (ch. 23).
- Attacking a banshee first inflicts the "mark of grief" curse, and banshees then target that person until they leave the field; best hunted on the last day (ch. 23).
- From day 2, corpse flowers bloom on the vines over rubble and hold drinkable water, the only water source in the field (ch. 24).
- Collapsed buildings serve as landmarks for finding the way back to the portal (ch. 25).
- 9th-grade monsters near the start, 8th-grade further north; from day 3 Deathfiends roam in fours ("newbie slayers") using [Call of the Dead] and [Physical Preservation] (ch. 110).
- The 3rd-floor portal lies north; it must be searched for near the northernmost edge (ch. 110, 115).
- A large stele spawns at a random spot in the 8th-grade zone when the labyrinth opens; at 00:00 on Day 7 it summons a Death Knight whose [Grudge] aura greatly reduces healing and regeneration (ch. 113).
- The 2nd floor closes on Day 10 (ch. 113).
- One of the four 2nd-floor fields connected to the Crystal Cave and to the Pilgrim's Path (ch. 51, 398, 484).

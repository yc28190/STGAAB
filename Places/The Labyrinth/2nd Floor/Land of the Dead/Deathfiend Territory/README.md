## Description
The northern part of the Land of the Dead, where the soggy swamp gives way to hard ground and rolling hills and the banshee howls grow louder. It is the territory of the Deathfiends and marks the start of the field's 8th-grade monsters.

## Monsters
- [[Deathfiend]] — 8th grade, over 3 m tall with a crescent-blade arm and a shield arm; regenerates and summons ghouls
- [[Ghoul]] — summoned in tens by Deathfiends
- [[Banshee]] — heard and met throughout

## Mechanics
- Grade 8 monsters appear from here on (ch. 23).
- Deathfiends regenerate with Preservation of the Flesh and summon ten ghouls with Call of the Dead; they die to head strikes, or can be tackled down, after which they cannot get up (ch. 23–24).
- Each Deathfiend is worth about 100 stones, up to 300 including its ghouls (ch. 23–24).
- They come alone on day 1, in pairs on day 2 and in threes from that afternoon, and do not chase anyone out of their territory (ch. 24).

## Notable events
- ch. 24: Bjorn and Ainar kill about 70 Deathfiends on day 1 with the "double barbarian tackle".

## Description
A 2nd-floor desert of rock and sand that crunches underfoot, with hot air rising from the ground and stone ruins casting shadows in a darkness lit only by torches. It lies west of the Crystal Cave and is connected to the 1st floor's gnome district.

It is one of the four 2nd-floor fields leading to the 3rd floor, known for golem and sandworm hunting and as a favoured "experience run" for high-level teams. Misha prefers it to the Land of the Dead.

## Mechanics
- Reached through a 1st-floor portal linked to the gnome district (described as eastern in ch. 89 and western in ch. 219, conflicting accounts); opening its portal gives achievement points (ch. 89, 219).
- Also reachable through a 2nd-floor portal from the Crystal Cave; one of the four 2nd-floor fields joined to it (ch. 101, 484).
- Leads on to the 3rd floor's Pilgrim's Path (ch. 89, 398).
- Killing Sandworms in one area eventually makes the ground quake; a sandstorm walls off escape and the Deathworm emerges. Teams compete for the spawn on a first-come, first-served basis (ch. 456).
- A source of 8th-grade magic stones used to open the 1st-floor rift (ch. 456).
- In the game it was one possible backdrop of [Soul Extraction] (ch. 423).

## Description
The labyrinth's second floor is split into separate fields, each reached by its own portal from the edges of the 1st floor's Crystal Cave: the Goblin Forest (north), the Beasts' Lair (east), the Rocky Desert (west), the Land of the Dead, and Blackrock Mountain (south route). Each has its own terrain and monsters, but every route leads on to the 3rd floor's Pilgrim's Path.

## Monsters
None recorded. (See the individual fields.)

## Mechanics
- Your direction from the centre of the Crystal Cave decides which 2nd floor you reach; going north leads to the Goblin Forest (ch. 455).
- The 2nd floor stays open until the end of Day 10 (240 hours) (ch. 13, 53).

## Notable events
- ch. 455: The choice of 2nd-floor field is discussed by starting direction.

## Description
A canyon of narrow paths winding between high cliffs that tangle into a maze. It reeks of beasts (enough to make Dwalky vomit), the smell clings to visitors, and the entrance is windy. The ground looks flat but is riddled with pits filled in with trash, and visitors leave with its dirt on their shoes. Deeper in, the cliffs turn from reddish brown to black. Most monsters live in caves along the cliffsides.

## Mechanics
- Reached through the portal in the 1st floor's eastern (blade wolf) district (ch. 50, 222).
- No traps, but a scout matters more than in the Goblin Forest because of the pits and maze-like forks. Even Rotmiller needed his sense of smell and advised giving up on it without a top scout (ch. 50, 178, 207).
- Hunting teams go into the cliffside caves to fight groups. In the black-cliffed depths, only higher-species (rank 8) monsters appear (ch. 50).
- Its exit portal leads to the 3rd floor's Pilgrim's Path, near the Steel Rock Hill (ch. 50, 398).
- An 8th-grade monster's stone from here, offered at the altar, opens the 1st floor's Glacier Cave rift (ch. 102).
- Walled on three sides, it is hard to surround, so a group can hold out there until Day 7 (ch. 222).
- The 2nd-floor rift will not open after Day 7, so parties wait until Day 8 before moving up; its guardian essence has about a 33% drop rate (ch. 457).

## Description
The house shared by Lee Baekho's group: Misha Carlstain ("Meowmeow"), the Ruin Scholar and the Guide. It has a dining room and upstairs bedrooms. Lee Baekho's room is kept dark by thick curtains that block the moonlight, while Misha's bedroom is lit by the moon.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- For safety, Lee Baekho sleeps in his No. 1,577 Coffin of Slumber (ch. 441).

## Notable events
- ch. 433: Misha's new life with Lee Baekho's group is shown.
- ch. 472–473: Lee Baekho tells Misha that Bjorn is alive and blackmails her into owing him a "favor".

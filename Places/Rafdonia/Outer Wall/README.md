## Description
The endless, towering outer wall that encircles Rafdonia. Ruins lie beyond it. Along the top run the ramparts, where wall-top carriages travel.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- A transparent barrier runs along the outside of the wall; you can feel it by tapping. Bjorn and the GM followed it overnight to Districts 10 and 9 (ch. 661).

## Notable events
- ch. 661–662: Bjorn and the GM travel along the wall and its barrier.
- ch. 768: While exploring beyond the wall, Bjorn learns royal secrets, including the claim that the ancient chieftain ran off with one of the two Hearts of Karui.

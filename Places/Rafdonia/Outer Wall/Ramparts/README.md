## Description
Open ramparts along the top of the outer wall, empty of pedestrians. At night the festival-lit city glows below and its noise carries up.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Wall-top carriages used to be strictly military but can now be used with permission or a reservation. They work like a highway and are faster than crossing the wrecked city center (ch. 776).

## Notable events
- ch. 776, 780: Bjorn travels by wall-top carriage and takes in the view of the festival city at night.

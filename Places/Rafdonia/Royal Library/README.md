## Description
A huge, quiet building about the size of the National Assembly, smelling of paper and ink, with tall windows and very few visitors. Twenty years before the present it looked exactly the same, with reading tables and counters staffed by many librarians. In Bjorn's early days a single sleepy librarian sat at the front desk. About two years before the later arcs it was renovated, and more than twenty staff now work at a large front desk. It holds books on history, law, monsters and rifts, plus collections of old newspaper articles. A restaurant stands in front of it.

In the game it could not be entered (it was marked as under construction). For Bjorn it becomes a second home and a cover story: he studies here for hours every day and claims his labyrinth knowledge comes from its books. Its librarian was Ragna Ritaniyel Peprok, a mage of at least 5th grade with a secret noble background, who later became a Viscountess.

## Mechanics
- It is open from 8 AM until evening. Books cannot be borrowed in the early period and must be put back; they are reshelved by magic (ch. 19, 86).
- At the entrance the librarian casts a book-detection spell ("Parstyev" / [Book Detection]) that makes books related to what the reader is thinking about seem to draw them in (ch. 19, 49, 100).
- "Bierdo Parsityev" ([High-Grade / Special Book Detection]) reveals restricted books, normally a privilege of nobles and a few authorized people. Ragna cast it secretly for Bjorn and let him read overnight after closing (ch. 86, 100, 123).
- Data on grade 8 and higher essences is not here and has to be bought from the Adventurers' Guild (ch. 19).
- Twenty years in the past, an administrative mage searches records by keyword, since the self-search spell does not exist yet (ch. 286).
- After the renovation, book detection costs 3,000 stones. Borrowing is allowed for people with verified identities, though in practice a deposit is required unless you are a noble or high official (ch. 366, 373).
- As a Viscountess, Ragna can close the library for a private meeting (ch. 447–448).

## Notable events
- ch. 19–28, 49: Bjorn reads here six hours a day for about two months, wearing his armor so it won't be stolen from the inn, to other readers' annoyance. Titles include The Ruined World, The Two Royalties of Rafdonia, Life at Camp Bifron, and the Ninth Grade Essence Compendium.
- ch. 78–79: Through a friend at the Administrative Office, Ragna gets Bjorn the sewer patrol request.
- ch. 85–90: Ragna turns down joining Team Misfits. Bjorn reads "Compendium of Rifts II" by Auril Gabis, the game's developer, printed on 150-year-old paper. Ragna warns him about the dimensional-collapse rumor.
- ch. 286–292: Twenty years in the past, five-year-old Arrua Raven teaches herself magic here. Bjorn stops a man who beats her and tells her labyrinth stories, while Shabin Emure is an apprentice librarian.
- ch. 366–367: In the present, Ragna has retired. Ainar reads strategy and history here, and Bjorn finds news about Nail Urbanes and Julian.
- ch. 447–448: Ragna, now a Viscountess, closes the library to meet Bjorn, guided by her secretary Harin Suevi (Hyunbyul). She reveals her nanny Ritanyel, her unknown noble father and the Evil Spirit Integration Policy.
- ch. 731, 766: Ragna shuts herself in here. Bjorn remembers first meeting her as a grade-9 explorer.

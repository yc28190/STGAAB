## Description
One of the churches of the Three Gods. Its outskirts branch is a small church on the edge of the city used by ordinary people. The church's High Priest, known as the "Agent", is the best healer in the city.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- An old priest at the outskirts branch removes essences for a fee (ch. 349).

## Notable events
- ch. 349: Bjorn picks this branch over the Leathlas Church to avoid being recognized, pays double, and has the Manticore essence removed. The priest doesn't recognize him.
- ch. 397, 417: The High Priest wakes Bersil Gowland from a year-long coma, leaving her in the church's debt.
- ch. 416, 426: Jun serves as its inquisitor.
- ch. 432: Its Archbishop speaks at the victory parade.

# Erwen's Inn (Fairy Dormitory)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | building (inn / dormitory) |
| **Also known as** | Fairy Dormitory; Erwen and Daria's Inn; Erwen's Lodging; Erwen's Inn Room; Erwen's hostel; dorm |
| **First appearance** | ch. 17 |

## Description
A fairy lodging where Erwen lives with her sister Daria, more than an hour's walk from Bjorn's inn. During one dark stretch Erwen kept her curtains drawn for months. A later inn room Erwen paid for occupies a top floor with four rooms and a moonlit window.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Other races are not allowed inside the fairy dormitory (ch. 17).

## Notable events
- ch. 90: Daria checks on Erwen, who meditates with all four spirits summoned, muttering about getting stronger. The obsession is driven by the Obsession stat from her Goblin Archer, Steel Hill Stalker and Cannibalo essences. Daria had been watching a nearby tavern for rumours about Bjorn.
- ch. 119–121: Erwen shuts herself in, then resolves to train harder.
- ch. 340: After leaving Noark, Bjorn and Erwen spend their first night in the top-floor room and drink Return Wine.
- ch. 341: Erwen decides it is not a safe enough hiding place.

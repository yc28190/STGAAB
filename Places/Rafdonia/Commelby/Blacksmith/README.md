## Description
An honest smithy in Commelby, recommended by Hikurod, where the party buys and repairs weapons and armour.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Repairs: shield and breastplate 300,000 stones; stretching leather armour with softener 50,000; ready in 3 days (ch. 122).

## Notable events
- ch. 77: Misha's dual swords cost 550,000 stones, Bjorn's new mace 400,000, and his enchanted Gigantification shorts about 900,000.
- ch. 122: Bjorn has his gear repaired here.

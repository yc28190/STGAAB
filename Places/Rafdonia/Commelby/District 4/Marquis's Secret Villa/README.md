# Marquis's Secret Villa

| | |
|---|---|

| **First appearance** | ch. 762 |

## Description
A modest two-storey house behind high walls, with a carefully kept, blooming garden that someone had tended within the last day or two. Inside it is understated, warm and homey, expensive without being flashy, like the home of a wealthy, loving family. There is a back room for resting, and a portrait of the young Marquis sits on a table.

It is the Marquis's "memory box", where he retreated when things were hard. It was also where he hid Princess Mayrin after taking her from the palace, and where Ragna sleeps in a hidden room.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- It is registered as land owned by Countess Peproc, who does not know it. Its only staff is an old gardener who does not know who hired him (ch. 763).
- A portal in an outer wall leads to the Hidden Room (ch. 766).

## Notable events
- ch. 763–764: Hyeonbyeol, posted here to watch the sleeping Ragna, gives Bjorn the Marquis's diary. Eltora had found the villa through the diary of his body's original owner.
- ch. 765: The diary reveals the villa as Princess Mayrin's hiding place.
- ch. 766: After waiting several days, Bjorn smashes the whole villa in Giant Form in under ten minutes, sparing only the portal wall, to bait the Marquis.

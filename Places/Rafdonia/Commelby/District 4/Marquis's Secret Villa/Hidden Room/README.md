## Description
A plain, modest room with a bed, table and wardrobe, where Ragna lies in forced sleep. It is slightly cramped for the whole party. Hyeonbyeol calls it a bunker beneath the villa.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Reached through a portal in an outer wall that seems to back onto the garden. Hyeonbyeol's bracelet resonates with the wall, which sends out a wave of blue light and opens the portal. The magic cannot be detected until it activates (ch. 766).
- Ragna's forced sleep ends only if the Marquis wakes her himself or dies (ch. 766).

## Notable events
- ch. 763: Bjorn finds Ragna asleep here.
- ch. 767: After several days, the Lord of Noark's bespectacled agent comes through the portal at the Marquis's request.

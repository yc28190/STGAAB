# Leathlas Temple Central Branch

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | building (temple) |
| **Also known as** | Reatlas Temple Central Branch (District 4); Central Branch of Reatlas Temple |

## Description
A ruined temple full of shattered goddess statues, its broken windows looking out onto streets patrolled by Noark. Attached stables hold a supply cart, and a stray horse has wandered off through a broken fence. The main road out front leads straight to the Imperial Capital gate.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- A random summoning circle dropped Rotmiller and the unconscious Bjorn in front of a statue here (ch. 751).

## Notable events
- ch. 751–752: Rotmiller hides Bjorn in the cart under hay and a tarp and drives slowly through the patrols. At the 71st checkpoint soldier a greedy one stops him; Rotmiller kills him and sticks his head on the cart's spike to keep others away. Near the wall a mage blows the tarp off and exposes Bjorn.

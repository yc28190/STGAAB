# Marquis's Prison Building

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | building |
| **Also known as** | Marquis's Prison Building (District 4 outskirts) |
| **First appearance** | ch. 767 |

## Description
A multi-storey building on the edge of District 4, near the wall that borders Viphron. The Marquis lies in a room upstairs, and the whole area is full of hidden Noark troops.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- The Lord of Noark required Bjorn to bring all his comrades with him as insurance (ch. 767).

## Notable events
- ch. 767: Having taken command while the Marquis was unconscious, the Lord of Noark keeps him confined here.
- ch. 768: The Marquis tells Bjorn that the Immortal King and the Dawnbringer King are the same person, and asks whether he wants to become king.

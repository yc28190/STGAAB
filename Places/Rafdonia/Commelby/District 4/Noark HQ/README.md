## Description
A large stone-and-steel building with five floors above ground and two basement levels, a tall-ceilinged ground floor and several stairwells. It is Noark's headquarters in occupied District 4, far from the bank, where the Noark lord stayed and the traitor Marquis was kept in a room with a bed.

The HQ also holds a huge, crude machine: a vertical structure around a shaft that seems to have no bottom, into which lined-up captives are pushed one by one. After Bjorn's Urae strike the building became a heap of rubble weighing many tons, a horrific wasteland.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Captives are fed into the shaft as "fuel" for the magic weapons. Noark soldiers are paid per body, and more for anyone who has absorbed essences (ch. 755).
- The Marquis survived Auyen's attack thanks to an artifact that saves the wearer from a fatal blow at the cost of a two-day coma. Urae could not be fired during those two days (ch. 755–756).
- In [Giant Form] Bjorn smashed down through every floor to B2, dug a pit and shielded his companions with his own body as a lid to survive the Urae blast. The Royal Army dug them out from above (ch. 770–771).

## Notable events
- ch. 755–756: Auyen is about to be thrown into the machine when a knight recognises him and takes him to the Marquis. Left unbound, Auyen strikes the Marquis with [Crush] (Bellarios essence).
- ch. 769: Bjorn kills the Marquis here after receiving Urae's authority and the royal revival ring.
- ch. 770: He kills the bespectacled Noark aide who set off the alarms, then fires Urae just beside the building and brings it down.
- ch. 771: The Royal Army digs the group out of the rubble.

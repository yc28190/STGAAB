## Description
A dark, deserted art gallery, left unmanaged since Noark's invasion, its walls still hung with paintings and moonlight falling through a single window. Bjorn used to come here alone.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- It is Noark point 4-7 (ch. 728).
- Ricardo Luhenpraha's [Silent Spell] creates a zone where all Essence skills are sealed. Amelia's self-replication stopped working once she entered it (ch. 728).

## Notable events
- ch. 728: Separated from the clan, Amelia and Auyen hide here. Amelia stows paintings in her pocket dimension "for safekeeping" and burns the works of a disgraced artist.
- ch. 729–730: Ricardo finds them and they flee. He breaks off the chase to answer Bjorn's taunts. Amelia's right forearm was split open in the chase (ch. 755).

## Description
The central plaza of District 4, normally busy. During the closure it stood more deserted than Bjorn had ever seen it.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
None recorded.

## Notable events
- ch. 632–633: A shelter is set up here for the Viphron residents and District 7 refugees, who live in the street for several days. A Rose Knight takes Bjorn to a fine house nearby, where he speaks with Chancellor Terserion over a video-call crystal.
- ch. 771: The plaza becomes the front line of the standoff between the Royal Army and Noark after more than half of District 4 is retaken.

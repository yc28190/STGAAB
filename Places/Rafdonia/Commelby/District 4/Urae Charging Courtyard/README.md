## Description
A walled courtyard with a colossal magic weapon at its centre, fed by dozens of pipes. Below it, a wide stone chamber under bright light holds a mountain of several thousand corpses, all shrivelled and drained like dried vegetables. A ceiling hatch opens to drop more bodies in, then closes.

The weapon is Urae, one of the royal family's three ancient magic weapons. It is also described as having no physical form: a spell circle built into the city itself (conflicting accounts). When activated, the user's view rises through ceilings and walls to hover high above the city. When fired, a huge projectile launches upward like a rocket from the user's position and falls on the target, followed by a building-shaking explosion and blinding light.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- It charges by grinding up humans. One shot needs the souls of many people, gathered by an "extractor", and can wipe out a palace (ch. 748, 769).
- Only the holder of its authority can fire it, by thought once a target is locked in the aerial view (ch. 756, 770). It went silent for 2 days while the Marquis was in a coma (ch. 756).
- It is part of the city and cannot be used inside the Labyrinth (ch. 769).
- Five shots remained when the Marquis handed authority to Bjorn (ch. 769).

## Notable events
- ch. 725: The ruins of the Palace of Glory are Urae's work. Minor nobles died in the strike (ch. 783).
- ch. 748: The Lord of Noark helped the Marquis seize Urae before the war. The Marquis holds back from firing at the kingdom, waiting for a bigger moment. Its shots come from the direction of District 4.
- ch. 753, 767: The Marquis repeatedly fires Urae at Bjorn and offers it to the Lord of Noark in exchange for protecting Ragna.
- ch. 769–770: Authority passes to Bjorn, who uses it to destroy the Noark HQ. He hides this from Astarotta (ch. 771).
- ch. 775: Two Urae strikes are recalled to have hit the centre of District 7 during the war.

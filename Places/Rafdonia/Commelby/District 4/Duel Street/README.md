## Description
A street on the way to the Marquis's recovery building that, without its barriers, looks just like a village market square. A three-storey inn overlooks it. For the duel the crowd drew back into a circle, and spectators raised dozens of overlapping barriers: [Barrier of Flowing Water], [Skyward Guardian Seal] and a yellowish fog, [Veil of Twilight]. A [Wall of the Dead] made of corpses was dispelled after the audience complained it blocked the view.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Ibaekho forced a one-on-one: if the Evil Eye refused, Ibaekho's group would take Bjorn's side (ch. 760).
- The Evil Eye's Taurobrude essence grants seven eye skills, including Green (shield), Red (laser), Blue (blink) and White (a hallucination in which damage is real and up to nine skills of tier 3 or lower can be faked). [Black Evil Eye] stops time (ch. 753, 761).
- Bjorn broke the hallucination with the Crown of Anguish (Mental Immunity) and released the reflected damage stored in the Aegis Dragon Scale. [Transcendence] plus [Eye of the Storm] pinned the Evil Eye in place and cancelled his blink (ch. 761–762).
- The Evil Eye survived thanks to the Hellase essence's [Grace Period], which prevents death for 900 seconds (ch. 762).

## Notable events
- ch. 759: Noark coalition troops block Bjorn here. The coalition strips the hostage Eltora of his acting command.
- ch. 760: The bespectacled agent of the Lord of Noark offers Bjorn a meeting with the Lord if he surrenders. Ibaekho, Rek Aures, Jaina, the Destruction Old Man and dozens of cloaked figures watch from the inn roof, noting Bjorn's items.
- ch. 761–762: Bjorn defeats the Evil Eye, and the shock wave destroys half the barriers. A mass brawl breaks out; Ibaekho's group stays to fight while Bjorn's party slips away.

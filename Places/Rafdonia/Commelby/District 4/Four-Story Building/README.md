## Description
A four-storey building in Noark-held District 4 whose balcony and roof overlook the road leading to the capital wall.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Roland Banossant's [Black Evil Eye] created an artificial night in which hundreds of eerie eyes opened, freezing everything except himself and Ainard. Her spear thrust shattered it like glass (ch. 753).

## Notable events
- ch. 753: Ainard, who had been scattered to the Dimensional Plaza, leaps from the balcony with wings spread to clear the road for the cart. She stays behind to fight the Evil Eye and is captured.
- ch. 753–754: Further along, Rotmiller runs out of bolts, writes a last message on Bjorn's palm and jumps off to hold back the pursuers so the cart can reach the wall. The wall garrison reports his death, and Noark soldiers take his head as a trophy.

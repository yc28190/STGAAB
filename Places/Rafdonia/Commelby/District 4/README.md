## Description
Normally a busy part of Commelby, full of shops and passers-by, with a bank quarter, multi-storey inns and houses. It borders Viphron, the Imperial Capital wall and District 5. During the Viphron crisis every shop shut and the streets emptied.

In the Noark war it became the fiercest front and the base of Noark's main force. The streets were ruined and looted, and Noark patrols searched every building, tearing down wallpaper and looking behind wardrobes.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- It was the only district closed "for citizens' safety", because it borders Viphron. It reopened the day after the Viphron circle went dark (ch. 632–633).
- Holds Noark point 4-7 (ch. 728). Urae fired from this direction (ch. 748).
- Borders the Imperial Capital wall, held by the royal army (ch. 752), and its edge reaches the wall beside Viphron (ch. 767).

## Notable events
- ch. 728–730: Amelia and Auyen hide in an abandoned gallery and are chased by Ricardo Luhenpraha.
- ch. 734: Ricardo waits here, then leaves for District 8.
- ch. 754–758: Ainard is captured and held in the Alminus Bank vault, then rescued. Rotmiller dies in the district.
- ch. 759: Bjorn walks the streets holding Eltora up as a hostage, followed by over a hundred Noark troops.
- ch. 760–762: The duel with the Evil Eye on Duel Street.
- ch. 771: After Bjorn's Urae strike, the Royal Army retakes more than half the district and a standoff forms around its central plaza. Some teleported clan members, such as Ainard, had landed deep inside it.
- ch. 773: Noark pulls back as the front collapses, leading to a forced truce.

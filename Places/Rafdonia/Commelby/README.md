## Description
The busiest commercial area of Rafdonia: streets crowded with shoppers, shops of every kind, and stalls selling treats such as cotton candy on skewers. It has more explorers and better-dressed citizens than Ravigion, though it is less grand than the Imperial City. Behind its gate a main street runs past restaurants and general stores, with a carriage platform about 30 minutes' walk in.

Commelby covers Districts 2 to 5 and forms a belt around Karnon (District 1). It is the largest part of the city, home to about 70% of the population, including the middle class, master craftsmen and many artisans. It holds the Central Administrative Office, the Alminus Central Exchange, magic shops, blacksmiths, inns and an auction house, and it is cheaper than Karnon, hence the name "free market". The Anabada Clan House stands on its main street. After Districts 7 and 8 burned, it became the nearest surviving area to the Barbarian Sanctuary.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Carriages from the platform stop running in the evening (ch. 72, 119).
- An inn room costs about 9,000 stones a night; the city centre is usually over 10,000 (ch. 72).
- Hidden black-market entrances are tucked into its back alleys (ch. 492).

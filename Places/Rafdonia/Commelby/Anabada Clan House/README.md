## Description
A three-storey building on Commelby's main street, about ten minutes from the Alminus Central Exchange. It is in better shape than its low rent suggested and is enclosed by high walls, with a small stable, a mailbox and a front-door bell. The overgrown backyard was turned into a lush garden (Erwen's) and a training ground (Amelia's) with a round table and three chairs. Raven finds it smaller than Bjorn's previous home.

The house belongs to the Melbeth Trading Company and is rented to the Anabada clan at a discount. The ground floor is the common area, living room and kitchen; Amelia and Erwen live on the 2nd floor; Bjorn and Auyen on the 3rd, where Auyen's window looks over the main street. Auyen Lokrob is the clan's coachman, cook and housekeeper.

## Description
A district of Commelby next to District 13. It is seen as one of the safer areas during the Noark crisis.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- The platform route to District 4 passes through here, about 3 hours (ch. 729).

## Notable events
- ch. 632: Although it borders Noark-held District 13, it stays open, which makes the closure of District 4 look all the stranger.
- ch. 755: Considered a safer place.

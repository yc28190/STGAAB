## Description
A district of Commelby next to Districts 4 and 7. During the Noark war it was a front line. Explosions boomed in the distance while the area around the platform stayed fairly quiet; exhausted, hollow-eyed soldiers carried on with their duties, and the once-busy wide streets were barricaded high with guards on top.

After the war its streets filled with festive crowds and lively merchants. The outskirts still show holes in the roads and collapsed buildings. The west, bordering District 4, is damaged, while the east is mostly intact, though daily purification circles cannot clear the smell of smoke. Gnomtree lies just east of it.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- It stayed open during the Viphron crisis (ch. 632).
- Defences are concentrated around the strategically vital military platform (ch. 726).
- Noark destroyed the sewers, so they cannot be used as a passage (ch. 726).
- Noark radio mentions points 5-1 and 5-3; 5-3 is where royal forces gathered at Bjorn's request (ch. 728). The 5th wall garrison is stationed here (ch. 729).

## Notable events
- ch. 726: Maan commands the front here.
- ch. 737: The Corpse Collector was born here, a shopkeeper's son.
- ch. 745–748: At Bjorn's request the District 5 army moves early and pushes into District 7 from the north.
- ch. 775–778: The 13th Month festival. The royal family hands out free food and drink, and gear prices are badly inflated.

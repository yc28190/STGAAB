## Description
The bank through which the world's money flows. Its private vaults are so packed with treasure that people call it a dragon's nest. It has grand front doors and a lobby, with stairs leading down to an unfamiliar underground layout. The basement vault has a thick door and a holding cell, and there is room to hide in the ceiling.

The bank is run by House Alminus under Count Alminus and offers accounts, anonymous vaults and secured loans. During the Noark war, with District 4 occupied, Noark used its basement vault as a prison.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Accounts can name beneficiaries in case of death (ch. 88).
- Anonymous time-limited vault: store items for a set term without giving your identity and retrieve them with a password; it needs a large down payment (ch. 334). Bjorn's vault had a 22-year limit, which Amelia extended (ch. 341).
- Short-term, high-interest six-month loans secured on property, approved within three days (ch. 349–350).
- Low-interest secured loans backed by jeonse deposits (ch. 649, 657).
- Its security is said to beat the Security Bureau's or the guild's underground prison (ch. 755).

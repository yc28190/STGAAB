# Business District

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | district (shopping area) |
| **Also known as** | — |
| **First appearance** | ch. 18 |

## Description
A shopping area in Commelby with a weapon shop, an armour shop about ten minutes away, and a general store. It is where Bjorn first sold loot and bought gear.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- The weapon shop (recommended by Tersia) stocks the huge one-handed blunt weapons barbarians like (ch. 18, 21).
- The armour shop pays little for used armour. Custom pieces cost about double, and a custom helmet takes three days (170,000 stones) (ch. 18, 21).

## Notable events
- ch. 18: Bjorn sells looted weapons for 350,000 stones, used armour for 180,000 and odds and ends at the general store for 145,000.
- ch. 21: He buys a mace for 220,000 and a sheet-steel half-armour for 360,000.
- ch. 43: He sells scrap and loot here again for about 800,000 stones.

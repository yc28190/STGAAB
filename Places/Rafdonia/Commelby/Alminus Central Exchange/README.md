## Description
A grand building where crowds of explorers sit reading item listings, with a storage area at the back for inspecting goods. Auction Hall No. 1 is usually less than half full. Its chairs have a bidding device on the armrest, and bids appear on an electronic display on the stage.

It is Rafdonia's main marketplace for explorer gear, essences and numbered items, run by the Alminus Trading Company of House Alminus (patron of the Sawtooth Clan). It also has a collateral loan department and handles magic-stone exchange after expeditions.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Take a numbered ticket, then register as a seller or file a search request. Each matching item costs 3,000 stones to look up. Staff bring printed listings, and you inspect and pay in cash at the storage area. The exchange charges fees (ch. 47, 150).
- A special appraiser can be called for 300,000 stones (ch. 172).
- Sealed-bid auctions: bidders write any amount anonymously, a system first used at the Sky Auction House after a feud between noble families. Entry tickets are needed and get scalped. Items are paid for and collected in a delivery room (ch. 331, 335).
- The collateral loan department keeps pawned items in its own vault. Unredeemed items go to auction after the term (two months in Amelia's case) (ch. 344, 347).
- Magic stones are exchanged here after expeditions. Offers such as ship purchases must be made before closing (ch. 366, 373).
- Items registered each day can be reviewed and go on sale the next morning (ch. 784).

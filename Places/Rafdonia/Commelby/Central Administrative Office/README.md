## Description
The city's administrative office in Commelby, which handles property matters. Shabin, a 7th-grade secretary, works here. A tea house and a popular new cookie shop are nearby.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Notarises property transfers and appraises property (ch. 149).

## Notable events
- ch. 149: Dwarkey's house is transferred to Murad here.
- ch. 155: Bjorn, Shabin and Ragna meet about once a month at the nearby tea house. Shabin passes on gossip, such as the likely end of the magic-stone fee discount for experience.

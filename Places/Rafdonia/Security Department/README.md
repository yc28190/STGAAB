## Description
The office of the royal family's secret security service, which hunts evil spirits (people from Earth). It has a cabinet of files and a crystal ball that plays back recordings from button-shaped video devices. City guards bring suspects here for questioning, and the department reports directly to the Lafdonia royal family.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Explorers who become famous or stand out quickly are routinely tested. An agent poses as an "evil spirit" from Earth, mentioning America, Taiwan, Europe and "the game", to bait a confession. Agents carry an Evil Spirit Hunter's badge (ch. 75–76).
- An innocent verdict is marked with a blue seal on the report (ch. 76).

## Notable events
- ch. 75: After Bjorn beats up the fake "evil spirit" in the Central Plaza, the city guards bring both of them here. The guard captain's brother was one of the survivors Bjorn led out of the Crack of Evil.
- ch. 76: Bjorn is cleared, and the agent is suspended for 3 months. The head mentions a "Count" who might take an interest in talented people.

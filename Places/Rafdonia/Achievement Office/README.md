## Description
A royal office in Rafdonia that pays out for war service. It stays crowded for several days after an expedition returns.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Patrol loot tokens and royal-seal vouchers are turned in here for money; on the 6th and 7th floors during the war, loot is exchanged for these vouchers (ch. 365–367).

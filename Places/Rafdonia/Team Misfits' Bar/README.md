## Description
An ordinary bar in the residential district where Team Misfits always sat at a window table. The party switched to it after Hikurod decided their old bar was cursed, following the Moselan knights incident.

## Notable events
- ch. 100: The party moves to this bar.
- ch. 122: The party drinks to its return and splits the loot.
- ch. 131: Bjorn and Rotmiller talk here the night before the expedition.
- ch. 148–149: Team Misfits holds its last settlement and final drinks here and decides to disband. Dwarkey's chair stays empty.

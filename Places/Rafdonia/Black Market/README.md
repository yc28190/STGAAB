## Description
A huge underground complex at a secret location, about the size of three football fields. Visitors arrive in small, closet-sized stone rooms with numbered doors (Bjorn's was 217) that open onto spacious, brightly lit halls. It spans several levels, with heavy steel doors, private rooms for customers and alarm bells that ring when there is an intruder.

The Asmond family has run it for generations; the current head is Mel Asmond of the Shadow Trading Company. Stolen goods, contraband, slaves and anonymous contracts all pass through it.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- It can only be entered by teleport, from hundreds of hidden entrances around the city (ch. 492).
- Masked guards average around 4th-floor level, some fit for the 6th floor, including Aura users and curse-casting mages. They seem to keep coming endlessly (ch. 492).
- Customers place anonymous requests, such as contracts for barbarian hearts, a bounty on Bjorn's heart, and hiring the Sawtooth Clan to sabotage a raid (ch. 482, 493).

## Notable events
- ch. 157, 176: Gear believed to be the Dragonslayer's turns up here after Amelia steals it.
- ch. 348: An organisation posing as a relief effort sells opium here. The Security Bureau raids it before Amelia can rob it.
- ch. 492–493: Bjorn storms the market. Mel Asmond hands over the client list, frees 397 slaves and quits the slave trade.
- ch. 494: The market shuts down for a while.

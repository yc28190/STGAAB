# Baron Martoan's Mansion

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | building (mansion) |
| **Also known as** | Baron Martoan's Residence; Martoan Barony residence; Martoan mansion |
| **First appearance** | ch. 94 |

## Description
A vast mansion with palace-like rooms, maids and a main gate, from which a luxurious carriage sets out escorted by flag-bearing knights on horseback.

It is the seat of Baron Martoan, Dwalky's half-brother. His haughty butler, the "Assistant Manager", runs the household, and the knight Kals Erimoore, a former explorer, serves here.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Reached from the outer districts by several carriage changes at the platforms (ch. 94).

## Notable events
- ch. 94–95: Maids dress Bjorn up as a showpiece barbarian: spiked and beast-skull shoulder guards, a champion belt, a horned helmet like the demon Parseira's, and 7 kg plate boots with lifts. He leads the procession on foot about 6 km to Count Perdehilt's estate.
- ch. 96: Bjorn leaves his mace here and fights the tournament with a double-edged battle axe.
- ch. 98: He returns to collect his gear and his fee: 1 million stones for the job plus 1 million for winning.

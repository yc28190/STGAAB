## Description
A refined, elegant noble mansion with rabbit-eared guards and attentive servants. A large study holds a big round table where Melbeth, the alliance of demi-human noble houses, meets.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Melbeth votes by open show of hands, with every member equal around the round table (ch. 464).

## Notable events
- ch. 464–465: Bjorn's membership passes unanimously (31 votes). Official support for Clan Anabada passes 17 to 15, limited to a loan, a discounted Melbeth building or exclusive quests. Members include Baron Heskaira (fairy) and Baron Brewellin (White Wolf).
- ch. 467: Bjorn stays the night while looking for a lawyer (James Elk) and meets the Baroness's daughter Minia.

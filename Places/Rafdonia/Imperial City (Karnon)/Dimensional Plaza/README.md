## Description
The original dimensional plaza of Rafdonia, in the Imperial City, long unused until it was brought back into service. When the labyrinth opens, a portal appears with a rush of wind at the centre of the plaza.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- The labyrinth used to open at midnight on the 1st and close at noon the next day (ch. 786).
- For secrecy, the labyrinth portal now opens only here instead of at the district plazas, on unannounced dates (no longer the 1st) but still at midnight. Reconfiguring it took the whole time since Viphron fell (ch. 786, 789).
- Only small numbers of vetted explorers are let in, to keep Noark out (ch. 789).

## Notable events
- ch. 789: The labyrinth reopens with 31 clans (2,517 people) and a royal expeditionary force of 1,700, about 4,300 people in all.

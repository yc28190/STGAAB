## Description
An ordinary army tent with a bed, far below what a baron would normally be given. Bjorn was hidden here during the war, after his rescue.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Bjorn is kept in a plain tent purely for security, because every time his location becomes known another Urae strike is fired at it (ch. 753).

## Notable events
- ch. 753: Bjorn wakes here after Rotmiller rescues him. Astarotta tells him Rotmiller is dead and Ainard has been captured, and Bjorn finds Rotmiller's last message written on his palm.
- ch. 754: Raven and Astarotta rejoin him. He finds hidden writing on Hyeonbyeol's Love Note and plans Ainard's rescue.

# Count Perdehilt's Mansion

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | building (estate) |

| **First appearance** | ch. 94 |

## Description
A noble estate about 6 km from the Martoan residence. The outdoor banquet ground is a lawn and garden laid with delicacies, with a band, dancing young people and clowns. The quieter indoor hall, with soft music, holds about 200 guests: titled nobles, heirs, bank presidents and guild branch managers. A central staircase leads to the 2nd floor, and nobles watch from a terrace. For the tournament a stage was built on the lawn with tents for the competitors.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- The apprentice-knight tournament is fought with real swords after duel oaths. Killing is discouraged but allowed, and priests reattach severed limbs (ch. 95–96).
- The prize, Numbered Item No. 7777 Garpas's Necklace, drops only in 5th-floor rifts at very low rates and has a hidden special event (ch. 95–96).

## Notable events
- ch. 95: The banquet for the birthday of the Count's daughter Arabella, with thirteen titled nobles besides the Count. Pal Krovitz praises Bjorn publicly, and the Count invites him into the tournament. Baron Martoan offers 2 million to take part plus 1 million for a win.
- ch. 96: The first match is Sir Arpeon (Hensleben Viscountcy) against Sir Saboan (Serphia Barony). Bjorn faces Sir Silbenia of the Hessen Barony in round one.
- ch. 210, 220: Bjorn recalls meeting the knight Kars Erimore at an earlier banquet here.
- ch. 234: The Count offers Bjorn his daughter Arabella and his patronage.
- ch. 445: Bjorn's last banquet before his disappearance was held here.

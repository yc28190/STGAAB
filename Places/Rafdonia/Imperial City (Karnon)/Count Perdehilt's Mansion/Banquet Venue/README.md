# Banquet Venue

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | arena (banquet grounds) |
| **Also known as** | Count Perdehilt's Banquet Venue; banquet grounds; tournament arena |
| **First appearance** | ch. 97 |

## Description
A festival setting with hundreds of spectators around a stage that serves as the duelling arena. Duellists start about 15 m apart, and a referee signals with a flag. Tents stand to one side: a waiting tent for each patron and a treatment tent where high-ranking priests heal the wounded.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- An apprentice-knight tournament in which noble houses enter fighters. Stepping out of the arena means defeat (ch. 98).
- The entry fee is 2 million stones, paid by the patron. The prizes are a trophy and No. 7777 Garpas's Necklace (ch. 98).

## Notable events
- ch. 97–98: Fighting for Baron Martoan, Bjorn punches out Silbenia and Quartean and breaks a third opponent's spine. He wins when Count Perdehilt's knight Albatross flees the arena in fear of [Gigantification] + [Wild Release]. He earns the nickname "Knight Crusher" and 4.3 million stones in total.
- ch. 98: The Count quietly advises him to turn down other nobles' invitations.

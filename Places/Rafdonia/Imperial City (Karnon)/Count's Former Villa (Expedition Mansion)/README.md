# Count's Former Villa (Expedition Mansion)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | building (mansion) |
| **Also known as** | empty mansion in the Imperial City |
| **First appearance** | ch. 397 |

## Description
An empty mansion that a count once used as a villa before being forced to sell it. It has a drawing room and a reception room. For the expedition's ceremony it hosted only a plain banquet of cold, pre-cooked food, with no orchestra, servants or spectators.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Its location was kept secret. Expedition members stayed two days for formation training (ch. 397).
- After the return, survivors were held here under knight guard and their conversations were recorded (ch. 431).

## Notable events
- ch. 397: The expedition's founding ceremony with its sponsors: the Prime Minister, Duke Keallunus, minor nobles, the Three Gods Church, the Alminus Trading Company and the Explorer's Guild.
- ch. 431: The ten survivors report to the Marquis by crystal ball.

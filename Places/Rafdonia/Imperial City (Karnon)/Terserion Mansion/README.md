## Description
A massive mansion on a huge plot near the royal palace. Its crest, a shield with a thorny rose, hangs at the entrance. Knights guard the main gate, and the 1st-floor entrance hall is brightly lit and lined with pillars. The house has three floors full of statues and costly paintings. Rooms include a lavish reception room, a 4th-floor dining room whose wall of windows looks over the garden toward the distant palace, a 3rd-floor office, the Marquis's windowless study and office, training grounds, infirmary rooms with patterned wooden ceilings and chandeliers, and Eltora's room hung with armor, swords and a mounted deer head. A guest house east of the main building holds several luxurious, individually styled guest rooms. In the garden, servants line up in formation to greet guests. The mansion is famous for its strength: it was built exactly like the King's residence, with outer walls of special materials under many layers of spells, said to hold for a minute even if the world ended.

It was the home of Prime Minister Marquis Ageny Rotten Terserion. After his treason and death it stood empty, too clean to be a ruin and too desolate to be lived in, and served as a secret staging ground for the labyrinth reopening.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- A complex, colorful magic circle under the reception-room carpet appears to turn the whole mansion into a trap or execution device (ch. 385).
- Communication goes through a crystal ball, and servants are sent away at night (ch. 389).
- Unannounced visits break noble custom. When Bjorn camped inside for three days, his meals stopped from the second day and knights tried to push him out (ch. 649).
- A bookshelf in the study hides a thick iron door; knights cut its lock with aura. It leads down to the Secret Library (ch. 711). A hidden groove in the floor opens a panic room (ch. 712, 714).
- During the reopening, labyrinth-department officials guided the chosen clans to tents in the garden, and at midnight carriages took them to the plaza (ch. 789).

## Notable events
- ch. 385–390: Bjorn reveals to the Marquis that he is Bjorn Yandel and strikes a deal: a special forces unit in return for killing the Dragonslayer. Erwen blasts the garden.
- ch. 388–389: On the night of the 15th, Eltora sneaks into Bjorn's room to test whether he is an evil spirit. Bjorn beats him and four knights, and meets Raviyen.
- ch. 391–392: A shaman's apprentice performs Bjorn's 7th-grade Spirit Engraving in a cleared, smoky, candle-lit room.
- ch. 392–396: A leaders' lunch, then Bjorn's 4-vs-1 duel on the training grounds wins him command of the expedition. The founding ceremony is announced.
- ch. 649–655: The Marquis agrees to a construction contest. He thanks Bjorn for saving him, admits he thinks Bjorn is a demon, reveals that his son Eltora is dead (possessed), and asks Bjorn to kill Ibaekho. He also mentions a royal treasure that would revive him at the palace.
- ch. 711–714: Bjorn's squad raids the mansion. The 17 Orculis special forces hiding there surrender to the King's Knight Morgan Karitumore and are loaded onto prisoner carriages in the garden, where Bjorn smashes Banonzant's head.
- ch. 789: The abandoned mansion is the secret rally point for explorers chosen for the labyrinth reopening.

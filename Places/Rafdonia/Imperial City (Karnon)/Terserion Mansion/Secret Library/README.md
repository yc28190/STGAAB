# Secret Library

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | room (hidden library) |
| **Also known as** | Marquis Terserion's Secret Library; secret chamber |
| **First appearance** | ch. 711 |

## Description
A room big enough to live in, at the bottom of a short flight of stairs behind a hidden iron door in the Marquis's study. Bookshelves line both walls, with a small desk and chair. The walls, the shelves and even the ceiling are covered with coated, photo-like pictures of a single black-haired, black-eyed woman, like a stalker's room. One framed portrait is labeled "Meirin Hyunvenia" with the surname scratched out, birth and death dates, and a red-and-blue sphere symbol like a Taegeuk in its lower-left corner. The woman looks like Ragna and is probably her birth mother.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- It is reached through an iron door hidden behind a bookshelf in the study. Knights cut its lock with aura (ch. 711).

## Notable events
- ch. 712: Bjorn searches the shelves. Records include Royal Meeting Minutes 17–18; Explorer Status Report Year 02; the 11th Race Council Minutes (underlined: race leaders kissing the ground before the Immortal King); a Draft Plan for Labyrinth Progress, Year 117 IV (underlined: the Labyrinth Management Department needs countermeasures against fast explorer growth); and a record of the 217th Dimensional Collapse. He takes diaries written in an unknown script.
- ch. 715: The Marquis never brings up the library afterward.

## Description
A spacious, glittering noble mansion filled with music, the smell of good food and finely dressed aristocrats. It hosted a banquet where nobles watched barbarian fighters for entertainment.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- The banquet included a tournament open only to barbarian fighters (ch. 99).

## Notable events
- ch. 99: Dkart, a young barbarian and son of Kafen, is hired and drilled to imitate Bjorn. The nobles decide that most barbarians are "unexpectedly normal" and that only Bjorn will do.

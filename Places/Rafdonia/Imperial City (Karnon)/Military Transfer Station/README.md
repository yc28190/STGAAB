# Military Transfer Station

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | transport station (teleport) |
| **Also known as** | Imperial City Military Transfer Station; Karnon Military Platform; military platform; teleport platform |
| **First appearance** | ch. 385 |

## Description
A military transport station in Karnon that troops pass through; soldiers stiffen at the sight of the royal emblem. It is the receiving end of the district transfer circles, and carriages arrive directly.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Teleport circles link it to the districts, which makes the trip to Karnon about an hour (ch. 385, 391).
- In the war it moved troops to District 5 and District 9 (ch. 726–727). To reach District 4 you go through District 3, at least three hours without fighting (ch. 729).
- Every district outside Noark's control relied on these platforms for supplies, reinforcements and retreat, which is why the District 5 platform was so heavily defended (ch. 726).

## Notable events
- ch. 391: The expedition members travel to the Imperial City via the station.
- ch. 726–729: Bjorn and royal troops move to the front lines through the platform.

## Description
A huge palace complex at the heart of Karnon, visible from all over the city. An outer wall surrounds it, and guests' identities are checked at the gate; on banquet nights hundreds of carriages queue there. Inside, twelve palaces stand around the central Apocalypse Palace. The Palace of Glory is the best known of them. Below ground lie a series of treasuries, prisons and secret facilities. On parade days the gates are thrown open, knights line the entrance and a military band plays on the walls.

The palace is the seat of the royal family of Lafdonia and the center of government; the Prime Minister (Marquis Terserion) works here. The King has rarely appeared in public for more than a decade, and nobles call the court a "den of vipers". The Magic Tower is rumored to have no windows so that it cannot look down into the palace. Gnometree is said to be the most heavily guarded district after the palace itself.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Weapons and armor are not allowed inside (ch. 232).
- Using abilities inside the palace walls counts as an insult to the royal family (ch. 432).
- Rewarded explorers are called in one after another by number to choose an essence or item from the treasury that matches their rank (ch. 241).
- There are exit-only portals in the basement. Kagureas notes they do not seem to connect to the labyrinth (ch. 310; see Basement Portals).
- Jerome Saintred's [Heretic Altar] (Eblus) is believed to be installed inside the palace, so he would revive here (ch. 562, 566).
- Raven used the royal palace library (ch. 295).

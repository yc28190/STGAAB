# Royal Meeting Chamber

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | room (council chamber) |
| **Also known as** | royal council hall |
| **First appearance** | ch. 644 |

## Description
The kingdom's council chamber, built around a long rectangular table (not a round one) with numbered seats. The highest-ranking nobles of the kingdom meet here.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- There are fifty seats, numbered by rank. The top seat belongs to the King (Martanux) and stays empty, so 49 members attend. Bjorn's seat is last (ch. 644).
- Members enter one at a time every two minutes, lowest rank first, and leave in reverse order (ch. 644).
- Votes are public: members flip a gold coin to show the royal emblem. The meeting runs from morning to evening with breaks but no meal break (ch. 644–646).
- Seat ranks: Terserion 2, Kealunus 3, Alminus 6, Kaislan 11, Saintred 14, Ragna 21, Maxiland 25, Huytaylor 27 (ch. 644).
- No Melbes member had held a seat in about 600 years (ch. 635–636).

## Notable events
- ch. 645: The agenda includes the Noark invasion and labyrinth closure (passed, with Bjorn the only "no"), rebuilding Districts 7 and 13, taxes, and the incorporation of evil spirits (rejected).
- ch. 645–646: When the Bjorn–Ragna marriage comes up, Bjorn refuses by declaring his love for a doll.

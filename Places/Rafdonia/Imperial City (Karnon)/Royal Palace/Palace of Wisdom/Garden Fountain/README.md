# Garden Fountain

| | |

## Description
A fountain at the center of the Palace of Wisdom's maze-like garden. At night a cold wind rustles the bushes.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
None recorded.

## Notable events
- ch. 643–644: Bjorn meets Jerome Saintred (Dragon Knight Cornelius Brungreed) here in secret at midnight. He returns the temple relic, the Heart of Karui, and Saintred warns him about the marriage to Ragna.

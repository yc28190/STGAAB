## Description
A cell in the Royal Palace engraved with a sealing magic circle and guarded by knights. Here the royal family held Lee Baekho, their long-time prisoner.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- The seal prevents escape. Lee Baekho broke it with [Extinction of Stars] (Dark Lord Deathred's essence), which absorbs the surrounding mana and wipes out everything within 100 m. He then walked away on air using Spatial Interference (ch. 235).

## Notable events
- ch. 235: After the banquet, Lee Baekho breaks out, planning to visit the Magic Tower and then hide in Bifron.

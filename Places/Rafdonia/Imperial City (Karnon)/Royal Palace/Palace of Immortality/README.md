## Description
One of the palaces of the Royal Palace. Under heavy guard, it stores the sealed crest boxes of the kingdom's noble houses.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Each Baron's sealed crest box is kept here under heavy guard until the kingdom falls or the family commits treason (ch. 443).
- The crests of the thousand noble families are enshrined in the "Ark of the Covenant". The Lirivia crest has been there for 2,000 years (ch. 443–444).

## Notable events
- ch. 443: Bjorn's crest box is sent here after his promotion to Baron.

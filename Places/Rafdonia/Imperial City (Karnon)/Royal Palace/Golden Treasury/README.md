## Description
A royal vault deep underground, at least five floors down. It has no windows, yet it is bright everywhere and so luxurious that it does not feel like a basement. A giant metal door swings open smoothly, as if it weighed nothing. Inside, thousands of essences in test tubes stand in glass cases alongside displays of equipment.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- It holds 3rd-grade essences and items of similar value. One reward grants one essence or one item (ch. 232–233).
- Visitors have 3 hours and may take only ONE essence or item. A guard knight and a mage guide go with each visitor. Theft or damage is severely punished, and visitors are searched on the way out (ch. 241–242).
- About half the stock is 3rd-grade essences, often seven or eight copies of each, plus at least one of every rare rift-only essence and some Triple Numbered Items. Bjorn assumes higher vaults hold 2nd-grade (possibly 1st-grade) essences and Double or Single Numbered Items (ch. 242).

## Notable events
- ch. 233: Bjorn is granted access as a banquet reward.
- ch. 242–243: Bjorn weighs Bellarios, Vol-Herchan and Bion and chooses Bion. He receives it in a box at the title ceremony and absorbs it in the carriage.
- ch. 252: Avman has been here before, which is why he thinks the royal family is far stronger than people believe.

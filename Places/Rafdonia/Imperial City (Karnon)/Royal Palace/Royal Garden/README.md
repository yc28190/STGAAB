# Royal Garden

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | garden |
| **Also known as** | maze garden; palace garden |

## Description
A maze garden in the royal palace grounds, near the Palace of Glory, full of flowers that sleep at night. Its fountain is loud enough to drown out quiet conversation. Everything known about it comes from the Marquis's diary, in which the secret meetings took place at night and broke up at dawn.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
None recorded.

## Notable events
- ch. 764–765: For about a year the young Marquis Terserion meets the nameless girl from the grand balls here, Princess Mayrin. She disappears on July 19. They meet again six years later, and again six years after that, when she has not aged at all. At that last meeting she asks him to take her away, and he smuggles her out of the palace.

## Description
The best known of the twelve palaces, used for banquets, title ceremonies, parades and royal grand balls. The interior is magnificent. The rectangular grand hall holds thousands, with a red carpet running to an empty throne, knights saluting, priests giving blessings and nobles watching from both sides. Even when every head of family attends, only the front two rows fill. Off the hall are waiting rooms and private dressing rooms of about 20 m² where maids restyle guests. Banquets spill into side halls with large round tables, a quieter music hall for nobles, and an outdoor garden of lawns, benches, food and drink. The grounds include a maze garden of sleeping flowers and a fountain.

In wartime it became the Military Command. Gate checks were tightened, and the wide inner grounds filled with officers, communication equipment and map tables around a command center. An Urae strike flattened it during the war, leaving a mound of rubble and bodies; the seal of the Count of Nartuil could still be seen in the debris.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- The palace keeps thousands of enchanted, self-resizing tailcoats for guests who arrive without formal wear (ch. 233).
- By tradition, only nobles may attend a title ceremony (ch. 242).
- In wartime, Supreme Commander Marquis Terserion gave Bjorn a "quest" to find every hidden Noark agent in Karnon in exchange for reassignment, and formed the Special Search Squad (ch. 710).

## Notable events
- ch. 233–234: At the May 10 banquet for the ambush survivors, each survivor receives 10M stones and the right to enter Karnon. Melter gets the Silver Treasury, Kyle becomes a royal mage, and Bjorn receives the Golden Treasury, 50M stones and a hereditary title. Bjorn meets Baron Martoan and Count Perdehilt.
- ch. 242–243: At the 20 May title ceremony, the Marquis's heir Cariot Kudo tries to trip Bjorn and is thrown across the carpet. The Prime Minister performs the ceremony before the empty throne, and at the banquet afterward the GM approaches Bjorn disguised as "Hans Aiphreya".
- ch. 432–435: At the victory parade Bjorn kneels before the families of the dead, reads out the names of the 20 fallen, then uses [Gigantification] to reveal himself as Viscount Bjorn Yandel.
- ch. 443–445: At Bjorn's promotion to Baron, he places his crest in a box that the Marquis seals and sends to the Palace of Immortality. Duke Keallunus offers "friendship", and Melbeth courts Bjorn through Baroness Lirivia. Bjorn challenges Baron Kipriot to a duel over racist insults, and the Duke steps in.
- ch. 709–710: The palace serves as wartime military headquarters.
- ch. 715, 725: Urae hits the palace and it collapses. About half the titled nobles die, and the Prime Minister's fate is unknown.
- ch. 763–765: In the Marquis's diary, a legendary nameless girl appears at grand balls. The young Marquis meets her on July 16. She is Princess Mayrin Hwinnvenia Lafdonia, Ragna's mother.
- ch. 783–784: The deaths of whole noble families flood the market with noble valuables. Jerome Saintred went missing on the day of the strike.

# Royal Underground Prison

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | building (underground prison) |
| **Also known as** | Karnon Prison; Palace underground prison |
| **First appearance** | ch. 714 |

## Description
The royal family's prison beneath the palace. Officially it has five basement levels, but rumor says it holds monsters and criminals who are supposed to be dead. Every level is closed off by thick iron doors. Basement 5 is laid out like a Go board and dimly lit by magic lights set into the ceiling. The levels range from a luxurious cell for respected nobles on Basement 1 to a hidden Forbidden Chamber on a sixth level that does not officially exist.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Noark prisoners are questioned on Basement 5 (ch. 715).
- A mana suppression field covers the prison. Once it lifted, Orculis escaped by multi-teleport (ch. 716).
- Two Urae strikes caused collapses, and the Basement 5 floor gave way under Bjorn and Raven, dropping them into the Forbidden Chamber (ch. 715–716).

## Notable events
- ch. 714–715: Bjorn is detained in the Noble Special Cell. Visitors include the King's Knight, Ragna (bringing cookies), Raven, and the village elder in Jerome Saintred's body, who brings an EMP-like device and warns of "the day everything will burn".
- ch. 716: Unnamed knights free the Orculis members, who escape while Banonzant's [Green Mana] barrier holds Bjorn back. The floor collapses under Bjorn and Raven.
- ch. 754: The Witch of Screams, Liranne Vivian, is held here after coming to the capital with Astarotta, kept alive just in case.
- ch. 783: Bjorn recalls Saintred's cryptic warning in the prison.

# Forbidden Chamber (Basement 6)

| | |
|---|---|

## Description
A huge, empty cube of a room with no bars and no entrance, hidden below the official five levels of the Royal Underground Prison. At its center sits a gray box, neither black nor white, with boxes nested inside it like a Russian doll. Its surface is soft enough to take a mark, yet dozens of mace blows leave it unharmed. The hole Bjorn and Raven fell through sealed itself within minutes. The chamber did not exist in the game.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- It can only be reached by teleport circle, or by falling in from above (ch. 716–717).
- Mana scatters in the room, so multi-teleport fails (ch. 717).
- Raven senses a portal inside the box. This matches Kagureas's old rumor of a portal under the palace that does not lead to the labyrinth (ch. 717).
- A hidden two-way spatial magic circle under the floor leads to the King's underground complex (ch. 717–718).
- Child-sized handprints appear on the box, and a young girl's voice begs to be let out. Bjorn suspects the Witch of the Land, though Raven says no living being would fit inside (ch. 717–718).

## Notable events
- ch. 716–718: Bjorn and Raven fall in, investigate the box, and leave through the hidden circle to the King's underground complex.

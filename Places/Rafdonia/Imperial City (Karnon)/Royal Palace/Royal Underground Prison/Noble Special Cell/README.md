## Description
A cell on the first basement level of the Royal Underground Prison, furnished like a five-star hotel suite: three rooms, a huge bed, a large bathtub and fast hot water. It is reserved for criminals the royal family respects.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Food comes through a slot in the door. Bjorn wore no shackles and could have torn the door off (ch. 715).

## Notable events
- ch. 714–715: Bjorn serves a 2-day detention here for attacking a prisoner.

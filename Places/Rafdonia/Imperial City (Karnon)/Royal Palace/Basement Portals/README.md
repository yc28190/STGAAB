# Basement Portals

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | portal |
| **Also known as** | Royal Palace Basement Portals; the portal in the royal palace basement |
| **First appearance** | ch. 310 |

## Description
Secret portals in the basement of the Royal Palace. Kagureas notes that they do not seem to connect to the labyrinth.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- They are exits only. You cannot enter through them, but entering a certain portal elsewhere brings you out in the palace basement (ch. 436).
- There are two such portals (ch. 472).

## Notable events
- ch. 310: Kagureas mentions the basement portal.
- ch. 436: Lee Baekho explains how it works.
- ch. 472: Butterfly reveals at the Round Table that there are two.

## Chapters
310, 436, 472

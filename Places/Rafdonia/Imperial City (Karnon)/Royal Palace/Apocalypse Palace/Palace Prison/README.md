# Palace Prison

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | room (prison) |
| **Also known as** | Palace Prison (beneath Apocalypse Palace); the prison deeper below |
| **First appearance** | ch. 724 |

## Description
A prison even deeper than the life-support chamber beneath the Apocalypse Palace. A girl who looks about ten years old is held there and begs to be released. She may be the Witch of the Land (unconfirmed).

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- The prison collapsed, and Bjorn entered the palace from underground through it (ch. 724–725).

## Notable events
- ch. 724–725: Bjorn finds the imprisoned girl and later questions the King about her.

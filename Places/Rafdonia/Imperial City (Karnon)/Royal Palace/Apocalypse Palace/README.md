## Description
The central palace of the twelve in the royal castle at the heart of Karnon, and the home of the Immortal King (King of Ruin / King of Apocalypse). Its audience chamber has no windows. The throne stands at the top of a flight of stairs behind heavy silk curtains, so the king shows only as a silhouette. Apart from the elevator there is one entrance, guarded by two Guardian Knights. The rest of the palace is strangely modern: a speaker in the corner plays a modern song, and there is a huge screen, board games and comic books, said to have been made by tortured evil spirits. A garden path leads out to an open-air picnic area with tables and chairs. From outside the palace is magnificent, but Bjorn only sees its exterior when he finally leaves through the main gate.

It is guarded by the Guardian Knights under Commander Astarotta Berun, and in wartime it is called the safest place in the kingdom. Elevators connect it to an underground complex, the king's life-support chamber, and a prison further down. It also has reception rooms and private rooms where guests are kept.

## Monsters
None recorded. (The Guardian Knights are each said to be stronger than a grade 3 monster.)

## Mechanics
- Guardian Knights serve only inside the palace. Of them, only Astarotta can be called out (ch. 719, 725).
- In the original game, the palace's anti-intruder gimmicks always meant game over (ch. 719).
- An elevator runs from the audience chamber down to the life-support chamber (ch. 723).
- When the king steps out into the garden, lightning strikes at him, which suggests the world itself rejects him. A translucent shield blocks the bolts (ch. 723).
- Bjorn entered from underground after the prison below collapsed. Once the king lifted his week-long confinement, he left in secret by carriage with the windows shut (ch. 723–725).

## Notable events
- ch. 719–720: Bjorn's first audience with the King. In awkward Korean the king calls him "junior baron", says he mostly sleeps and that Bjorn was "chosen by her", and threatens Bjorn's people if he does not cooperate.
- ch. 723–725: Bjorn spends a week confined here, playing board games and eating somyeon with the king, and questions him about the life-support device and the prison.
- ch. 765: In the Marquis's diary, the king receives the Marquis in the audience chamber after the royal family discovers his relationship with Mayrin, and offers him the Chancellorship so an insider will keep the royal secret.
- ch. 771–774, 783: Bjorn delivers the Marquis's corpse to Astarotta here and receives the intelligence casualty list. Ragna (unconscious), Elthora and Harin are held here in semi-detention.

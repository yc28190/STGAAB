## Description
A strikingly modern facility beneath the Apocalypse Palace. The arrival room has white walls and ceiling lit like daylight, and one metal wall that turns out to be an elevator door. The elevator needs no mana and has a call button, ceiling lights and a mirror. It rises for about 50 seconds to a hall like a hotel lobby, with a chandelier, a small fountain and polished marble floors. From there a windowless corridor spirals on for more than three hours, built that way for defense. It ends in a large cavern holding a test tube bigger than any floor lord, filled with liquid and with hundreds of wires at its base. A second elevator leads up to the audience chamber.

The technology was taken from captured evil spirits by the Dimension Technology Department.

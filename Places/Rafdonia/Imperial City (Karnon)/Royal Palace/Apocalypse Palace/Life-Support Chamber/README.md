## Description
A room under the Apocalypse Palace, reached by elevator. At its center stands a gigantic test tube like those in the Pantelion Lab, and the King sleeps naked inside it, floating in fluid.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- The device keeps the King's worn-out body alive. It is the secret behind the Immortal King's long life (ch. 723).

## Notable events
- ch. 723: Bjorn is shown the chamber.
- ch. 768: The Marquis says the King's body should have died long ago but is still alive and sleeps more and more, which fits the device.

# Investiture Hall

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | building (ceremonial hall) |
| **Also known as** | ceremony hall |
| **First appearance** | ch. 783 |

## Description
A ceremonial hall in the Royal Palace with a podium, a backstage area and seating for thousands of nobles. Its ceremonies end with a banquet, with a court orchestra and dancers.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- The post-war ceremony ran in four "periods": Kealurnus's speech, the succession of about 200 heirs, a banquet followed by the Chancellor's inauguration, and then promotions. For a promotion, the box wrapped in navy cloth is replaced by one wrapped in blue cloth (ch. 788).

## Notable events
- ch. 783, 788: On the same day, Duke Kealurnus is inaugurated as Chancellor, the war heirs are confirmed, and Bjorn is promoted to Viscount.

## Chapters
783, 785, 788

## Description
Rafdonia's 1st District, behind tall, well-kept walls and a giant gate bearing the royal emblem. In peacetime its wide paved streets are almost as clean as a modern city's, lined with grand shops, huge mansions and elegantly dressed people, and a main street runs straight to the palace gates. Land costs hundreds of millions of stones. During the Noark war its roads were cracked and sunken and strewn with rocks and broken wood, worn down by convoys with no one left to repair them.

Karnon belongs to the royal family and holds the palace, the top nobles, Hero's Plaza and the Sky (Celestial) Auction House. About 90% of its residents are servants or commoners who work for the nobles. Commelby wraps around it like a belt. It has burned once before: on the night of 1–2 June the whole southern district went up in a magical fire under a red sky.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Commoners and low-ranking explorers need permission, enough fame, or a higher rank plus achievement points to enter. Carriages have to detour around it, roughly doubling travel times (ch. 47, 74, 157, 192).
- Bjorn first got in with Baron Martoan's procession (ch. 87, 94). Ambush survivors were granted entry as a reward (ch. 233), and with an appointment he later passed freely (ch. 654).
- Reached by the military transfer station (about an hour) or by a long noble-section carriage ride of about 6–7 hours (ch. 391, 443, 464).
- The 1–2 June fire was magical, with timed explosions, set by the Ruin Scholar (Flame Giant summon). Crossing it took Fire Resistance from Cold Blood, the Orb of Fire, the Lava Shield and Gigantification. Seven school masters called down rain to put it out about 12 hours later (ch. 253–256).
- In wartime, titled nobles called up are assigned to the Military Command here, and leaving without permission counts as desertion (ch. 706, 710).
- The wall facing District 4 is held by the royal army. Reaching it means rescue, because the garrison can open the gate (ch. 752–753).

## Notable events
- ch. 94, 98: Bjorn attends noble events, including Count Perdehilt's banquet.
- ch. 233: Victory reception: the road to the palace is strewn with petals and lined with cheering crowds.
- ch. 253–256: The great fire. Bjorn saves Raven and 32 other survivors while Lee Baekho fights the Ruin Scholar. A royal treasure is stolen during the chaos (ch. 259).
- ch. 432: The victory parade ends here.
- ch. 473: Baroness Lirivia urges Bjorn to build his estate here, but he chooses Ravigion.
- ch. 691–709: Noark aims to march on Karnon and kill the king. A Noark elite squad slips in and vanishes, and a Record fragment foretells the day Karnon burns.
- ch. 753–758: Bjorn wakes hidden in a soldier's tent to dodge Urae. The capital regulars fake a full assault as a diversion for the bank rescue.

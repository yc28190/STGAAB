# 4th Wall Garrison

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | military camp |
| **Also known as** | 7th Legion 4th Wall garrison; temporary tent (crime scene) |
| **First appearance** | ch. 772 |

## Description
A camp of temporary tents near Karnon's western wall, held by the 7th Legion. Its jurisdiction runs along the barrier next to Gnomtree. Inside the crime-scene tent were two movable cots near the entrance, and the tent was in disarray.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Duty here is nominally mandatory but in practice a standby assignment, and many people sought it (ch. 772).

## Notable events
- ch. 772: Astarotta posts Sven, Elwen and other clan members here.
- ch. 772–773: Ibaekho assassinates James Carla in his sleep in his tent and kills Versil Gowland at the entrance.

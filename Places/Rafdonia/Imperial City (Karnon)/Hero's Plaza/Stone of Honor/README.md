# Stone of Honor

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | monument |
| **Also known as** | — |
| **First appearance** | ch. 490 |

## Description
A massive stone tablet in Hero's Plaza, covered in inscriptions of heroic feats, with companions' names in small letters beneath each. In the game it was mere decoration.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- It was built alongside the first Dimensional Plaza with forgotten ancient magic, reportedly by Gavrilius (ch. 501).
- It records major labyrinth feats on its own and works like patch notes for changes in the labyrinth (ch. 491).
- Recorded feats include Gavrilius (Crystal Cave), Emon Mulmarin (Sea Dragon), Ordel Pikma (Great Ocean), Lud El Rimenin (Abyss), Armella (Harmonizer Gregory) and Bjorn (157th year of the New Era: Dreadfear, hidden area) (ch. 491, 503).
- The previous entry was about 600 years old. A person can only be inscribed once (ch. 501).
- Credible records say the stone holds a hidden piece, "Gavrilius's arrangement" (ch. 501).
- The first entry into the 1st Basement Floor also produced a record (ch. 507).

## Notable events
- ch. 490–491: Bjorn's name is inscribed, making him a hero across the nation.
- ch. 786, 789: Bjorn's carved name is referred to again around the labyrinth's reopening.

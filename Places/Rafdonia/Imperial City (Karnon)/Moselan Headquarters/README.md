# Moselan Headquarters

## Description
The headquarters of Moselan, one of the strongest military orders in the city. Team Misfits once spent an entire day waiting outside its main gate. No other physical details are recorded.

Moselan recruits only knights of noble birth, refuses to deal with commoners, and is rumored to take bribes from nobles. It also acts as a judicial and police body: it takes captured criminals away for interrogation and runs a Dispute Resolution Committee that handles disputes between explorers and nobles. It has at least one branch office, in the 7th District.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Moselan accepts only knights of noble birth and does not deal with commoners (ch. 85, 87).
- A Moselan summons must be answered within 15 days. The Dispute Resolution Committee pushes for a settlement, and cases that don't settle go to the Royal Court. Written statements can be prepared with a scribe or lawyer (ch. 466–467).

## Notable events
- ch. 85–87: On the orders of Baron Martoan's butler, Moselan knights arrest Dwalky for "insulting a noble" and revoke his labyrinth permit. He was held at the 7th District branch.
- ch. 245: Moselan takes the captured assassins away for interrogation.
- ch. 466–467: Count Alminus brings a claim before the committee, chaired by Christina Bealon (sister of Count Bealon). He demands 2.135 billion stones, half the appraised value of the Riakis essence, under the Labyrinth Disaster Law.

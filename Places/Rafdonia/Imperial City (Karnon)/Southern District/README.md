## Description
A district in the south of Karnon, and the setting of the great fire started by the Ruin Scholar. Its landmarks include the 3rd Public Transportation Platform (a large stone carriage station), a plaza with a central fountain, and a gukbap restaurant patronized by Lee Baekho. Raven kept her lodging and temporary lab at its center.

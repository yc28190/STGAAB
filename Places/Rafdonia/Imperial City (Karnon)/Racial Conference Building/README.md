# Racial Conference Building

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | building |
| **Also known as** | — |
| **First appearance** | ch. 639 |

## Description
A temple-like building whose roof rests on six columns, recalling a Roman temple. Inside is a conference room built around a large round table. The leaders of the six races meet here to settle matters between them.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- The conference can only be held when all six race leaders are present, so it was delayed while Bjorn was trapped underground (ch. 634, 640).
- Guards at the entrance come from the other races. There are no barbarians among them (ch. 639).

## Notable events
- ch. 639–641: The conference is attended by Dragon Elder Lapir, Albreynib Kalstein (Red Cat patriarch), the dwarf Dumoka Malaku Izor, the elf/fairy Ragsian, and Maxiland for the humans. Topics include dwarves buying land in Gnome Tree, the exiled dwarf craftsman sheltered by the black bear tribe (voted out), Bjorn's marriage, and the Noark mage who inherited the Great Sage's vision.
- ch. 641: Bjorn gives a speech attacking the hypocrisy of "balance".
- ch. 642: After the conference, Bjorn meets Beleg Shusia di Tersia, Elwen's uncle.

## Description
The headquarters of the royal 3rd Magic Corps, a unit directly under the royal family. Raven's spacious office has sunlight streaming through the window and stacks of documents (at one point newspapers about Bjorn); snow falls outside in winter. The headquarters also has training grounds. Raven serves as vice-captain under Captain Febrosk, with Alex Hailo as her assistant.

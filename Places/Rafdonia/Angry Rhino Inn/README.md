## Description
A cheap inn packed with barbarians. Its rooms hold five narrow beds and smell of days-old sweat; a back door opens onto a dirt yard where barbarians spar bare-chested, boxing and wrestling until about 9pm. Ainar lives here.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Costs 300 stones a day; ten barbarians share a five-bed room and sleep in shifts (ch. 20).
- By tradition, barbarians move out once they can afford to (ch. 43).

## Notable events
- ch. 20–21: Bjorn trains in the backyard every day from 5pm, improving his reflexes, flexibility and dynamic vision.
- ch. 43: Two months later more than half the faces have changed, some because their owners died.

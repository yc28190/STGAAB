## Description
A forest clearing in the Barbarian Sanctuary, lit by flickering torches around nightfall, with racks of weapons and armour and a display of shields. It is the place where Bjorn first woke up in this world.

Young barbarians come here at their coming-of-age ceremony to choose their gear, recite the tribe's creed and then head off through the gates toward the city and the Dimensional Plaza. The clearing is also used as a meeting spot for the Anabada clan.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Young warriors choose their weapons here. Over the past ten years about one disguised player a year has been executed at the ceremony (ch. 455).
- The creed: a good looter is a dead looter; don't trust the Explorer's Guild; run from Hans; kill evil spirits (ch. 455).
- After the ceremony the gates open and the new warriors go to the city (ch. 455).
- A torch is added to the starting items (ch. 478).
- Under Bjorn, each warrior gets a full armour set plus a pack of compasses, water bottles and potions, worth about 1 million stones. Bersil bonds them in threes and they are sent through the portal (ch. 502).

## Notable events
- ch. 454–455: Bjorn spots a player "newbie", Bekta, third son of Kiltau, who picks a shield. He later does well on the 2nd floor (ch. 478).
- ch. 502: The new, better-equipped ceremony. Laithium breastplates have become the mark of a seasoned warrior.
- ch. 787: The whole Anabada clan gathers here to hear about the labyrinth reopening, meet recruits (Raven, Beleg, guest Adnus) and get team assignments. The teams plan to regroup on the 6th floor.

## Description
The area of tents around Bjorn's quarters, noticeably calmer than the festival square. Bjorn does his clan work and sleeps in his tent here.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Shouting is banned (Amelia's rule, in her "Emily Raines" guise); offenders are sent to the loud zone (ch. 780).

## Notable events
- ch. 774–775: Bjorn works on clan matters from his tent.
- ch. 780: The quiet-zone rule is introduced during the festival.

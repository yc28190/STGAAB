# Grounds of Proof

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | arena (pit) |
| **Also known as** | Land of Proof |
| **First appearance** | ch. 450 |

## Description
A crude pit dug in the middle of the forest, over 7 m deep and large enough for thousands of warriors to watch from the rim. The floor is uneven and covered in pebbles, and rainwater sometimes pools in it, but duels go ahead anyway.

It is the barbarians' place for sacred duels between warriors, including challenges for the chieftainship.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Sacred duels between warriors are fought here, including chieftain challenges (ch. 450, 781).

## Notable events
- ch. 450–451: Bjorn defeats chieftain Gudnulf Olga, who uses Frictional Heat stacks, Flame Leap, Thunderflame, Hammering (from the Hellsmith essence) and the Golden Sun Engraving. The fight ends in a fistfight and a spontaneous festival with bonfires, drums and roast meat follows.
- ch. 781: At the festival Bjorn fights barehanded challenge duels, promising a house to anyone who draws his blood. He then holds an improvised "sacred drinking duel" with thousands of warriors, which Vekta, third son of Kiltau, wins after more than 160 rounds.

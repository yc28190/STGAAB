# Chieftain's Tent

| | |
|---|---|

| **First appearance** | ch. 449 |

## Description
A hut or large tent with a curtain hanging over the entrance, the residence of the barbarian chieftain. After Bjorn takes over, Ainar cleans the large tent for him and he moves in.

It is where chieftain challenges are issued, and it later becomes the first base of the barbarian administrative office.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
None recorded.

## Notable events
- ch. 449: Gudnulf Olga is challenged for the chieftainship here.
- ch. 451, 454: The defeated former chieftain recovers in his own tent and tells Bjorn about seeing the King at the Tribal Council, and about Balkan/Kagureas.
- ch. 452: Bjorn moves into the large tent.
- ch. 453–454: The tent becomes the seat of the administrative office: Shabin Emure as Chief Administrator, Mary Jayne on statistics, Shepherd Lambden on budget, Rick Anderson on urban development.

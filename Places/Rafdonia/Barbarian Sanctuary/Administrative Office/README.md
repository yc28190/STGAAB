# Administrative Office

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | building (tent) |

## Description
A tent in the Barbarian Sanctuary that serves as the tribe's administrative headquarters. It started out in the chieftain's large tent and grew into a separate office.

Bjorn set it up after becoming chieftain. Its staff are human clerks who had been fired from the city's administrative office: Shabin Emure (head of the team), Rick Anderson, Mary Jayne and Shepherd Lambden. It handles population figures, land sales, the tribe's budget and the organisation of the coming-of-age ceremony.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- The clerks are paid double for night work (ch. 453–454).
- Responsible for numbers, land sales, budgeting and the coming-of-age ceremony (ch. 454).

## Notable events
- ch. 453–454: Bjorn founds the office and hires the four former city clerks.
- ch. 494: Shabin Emure manages the tribe's rapidly growing budget from the tent.

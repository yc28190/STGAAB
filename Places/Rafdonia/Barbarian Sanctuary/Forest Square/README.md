## Description
A forest clearing where the barbarian festival runs at full volume. Shirtless barbarians in traditional dress shout war cries and drink, and rings of cheering warriors form around brawls.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Amelia split the Sanctuary into a "loud zone" (this square) and a "quiet zone" around the tents where shouting is banned (ch. 780).
- Duel circles are held here; the number of fights is said to measure how good a festival is (ch. 780).

## Notable events
- ch. 780: The festival celebrating the clan. Although the clan has many human members, about 99% of the Sanctuary's residents are barbarians.

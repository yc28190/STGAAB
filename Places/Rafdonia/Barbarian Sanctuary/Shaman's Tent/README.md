## Description
A dark tent full of pungent, nauseating incense smoke where the barbarian shaman lives and works. The old shaman, bald and wrinkled, smoked a large pipe and spoke in a high, drugged-sounding voice, while a disciple showed visitors in. Engravings are done on a bed inside.

This is where barbarians get their spirit engravings (imprints). After collapsing during a ritual, the old shaman lies bedridden here, tended by his young apprentice, who later takes over the engraving work.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- The shaman reads the "soul circuit" (the tattoos) and adds an imprint with a needle. It is extremely painful and takes until dark. Once a path such as Undying is chosen, imprints from other paths can no longer be taken (ch. 21).
- Undying Engraving stages are paid for in stones and magic stones. Stage 4 cost 4 million stones (Mental +30, Ability +10, more Soul Power) (ch. 100). Stage 5 cost 8 million (Physical +30, Mental +30, much more Stamina) (ch. 127).
- Stages can be bought up to 6; later ones require materials you gather yourself (ch. 115).
- Phase 8 was performed here over 3 days. The young shaman cannot safely do Phase 9 and beyond, which is carved into the soul itself (ch. 785).
- Reviving the old shaman requires a real dragon heart (a race quest) (ch. 785).

## Notable events
- ch. 21: Bjorn receives Undying Imprint Stage 1 (much better natural regeneration, Body +20).
- ch. 100, 127: Bjorn buys stages 4 and 5.
- ch. 454: The apprentice, caring for the bedridden old shaman, fears that Bjorn has come to kill them.
- ch. 785: Bjorn gets Phase 8 and learns about the dragon-heart quest.

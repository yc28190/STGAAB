## Description
A stretch of forest near the Sanctuary's outer gate, handy for travelling to and from the city. Plots for sale are marked out by four yellow flags each.

It is the core of Bjorn's Sanctuary redevelopment plan, which sells land to barbarians to fund the tribe, with roads and childcare facilities planned.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Each plot is 20 m × 20 m (400 m²) and sells for 2 million stones by lottery (drawing a black lot wins) (ch. 453).
- The deed lets only barbarians inherit the land; without heirs it reverts to the tribe (ch. 453).
- Sales are held the day after the labyrinth closes, when warriors have money (ch. 466).

## Notable events
- ch. 453: 40 of the 73 surveyed plots sell on the first day, raising 80 million stones.
- ch. 466: Resale prices climb from 2M to 3M, 4M and 4.3M within a week, pushed up by Charon's secret "Comrade Association". The next batch, closer to the centre, is planned at 6M.

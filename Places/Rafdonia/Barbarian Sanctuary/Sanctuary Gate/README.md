# Sanctuary Gate

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | gate / wall |
| **Also known as** | City Gates (Sanctuary Side); Sanctuary gate and wall; Barbarian clearing gate battlefield; clearing near the gate |
| **First appearance** | ch. 3 |

## Description
A large wall with a gate that opens slowly on a rattling mechanism, standing about 30 m from the edge of the forest that surrounds the Barbarian Sanctuary. It is the link between the Sanctuary and the city of Rafdonia.

During the Noark assault the ground in front of the gate became a battlefield. The gate was wrecked and then buried when that section of wall collapsed, leaving the ground covered with Noark and barbarian dead.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- The gate opens at night so newly adult barbarians can enter the city after their coming-of-age ceremony, and closes behind them (ch. 3).
- In the defence, elite defenders held the first line in front of the gate and barbarian warriors the second. Magic cannon fire from the wall killed groups of defenders at once (ch. 741).
- Elwen's Wind [Spirit Form] combined with Bjorn's [Sweep] tripled its range for clearing crowds (ch. 743).
- Barbarian guards are posted on the wall; unknown visitors are turned away and only famous people get an audience (ch. 785).

## Notable events
- ch. 3: Bjorn passes through and sees Rafdonia for the first time.
- ch. 741: Rotmiller fights here, deafened by the cannons.
- ch. 743–745: Bjorn wipes out the Noark troops trapped in the clearing.
- ch. 785: Guild Master Ilia Adnus arrives at the gate asking to see Bjorn.

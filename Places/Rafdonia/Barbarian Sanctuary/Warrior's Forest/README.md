# Warrior's Forest

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | forest (funeral ground) |
| **Also known as** | Sanctuary Funeral Forest; forest of warrior souls; funeral forest |
| **First appearance** | ch. 152 |

## Description
A gloomy, eerily quiet forest of thick trees about 30 minutes' walk deep into the Sanctuary, beyond the campsite. The barbarians say the souls of countless warriors play here.

The tribe holds its funerals here.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- At a barbarian funeral the body is propped against a tree. The shaman smears blood on its face, shakes a rattle and sprinkles a liquid that keeps insects away. The body is left to decompose; later the shaman crushes the bones and scatters them through the forest. Mourners cut their hair or let their blood drip on the ground around the body (ch. 152, 248).

## Notable events
- ch. 152: Funeral of Kiduba, third son of Toharu, who died passing his essence to Ainar through Soul Inheritance.
- ch. 153: Ainar spends the night sleeping in the bushes here.
- ch. 248: Kiduba's body has not finished decomposing.

## Description
A familiar path through the dense forest inside the Barbarian Sanctuary, with trees, mud and stumps. Seen from the Sanctuary wall, the canopy hides most of what goes on beneath it.

## Notable events
- ch. 742–743: During the Noark attack, Rotmiller flees down the path alone to draw off pursuers. He is shot in the stomach, climbs a tree to treat himself, is spotted, shot in the thigh and caught at a stump. Bjorn leaps down from the wall and rescues him.
- ch. 745: The warriors crush the heads of the fallen here in case any are faking death.

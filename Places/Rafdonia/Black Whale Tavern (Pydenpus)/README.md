## Description
A busy, smoky tavern south of the Dimensional Plaza, full of drunks, adventurers, music and the odd demi-human. A pillar hides a corner table at the back, and a bell hangs over the door.

For generations it was known as the Black Whale. About ten years ago the owner fell into gambling debt and it changed hands and was renamed Pydenpus.

# 13th District

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | district |
| **Also known as** | District 13; Zone 13; beastman district |

## Description
An outlying district of Rafdonia with more tangled streets than the 7th District and a large population of beastmen and dwarves. It has a plaza with benches and street-food vendors where beastman families stroll, and it borders the beastman sanctuary, the beastkin's sacred site.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- About 3 hours by large carriage from the 7th District (6 hours round trip), since the road must go around the restricted 14th District (Bifron) that lies between them; there is a public transport platform selling tickets (ch. 127, 130, 628).
- District 3 lies next to it (ch. 632).

## Notable events
- ch. 43–47: Bjorn moves to his second inn here.
- ch. 623–628: Noark occupies the district together with District 7; the Orculus leader is believed to be there.
- ch. 633–645: The royal army retakes it on the third day and rebuilds it.
- ch. 725: It is occupied by Ravigion along with Districts 7 and 8.

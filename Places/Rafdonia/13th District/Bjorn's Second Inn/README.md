## Description
An inn in the 13th District with a ground-floor tavern. Bjorn's room is half again as large as his previous one, with windows, a soft bed and a private bathtub. The food is good, though the house beer is weak and lukewarm; adventurers drink there.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Rent is double that of his first inn, but it is closer to the Dimensional Plaza; the weekly set meal costs 300 stones (ch. 43, 46).

## Notable events
- ch. 44: Bjorn meets a man named Hans, who suggests using the guild to find teammates.
- ch. 46: Bjorn feeds the evil spirit Tarikan Lien and gives him 150,000 stones.

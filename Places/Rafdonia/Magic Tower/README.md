## Description
A windowless black spire above a large plaza, taller than the city walls and visible across the city. Inside, elevators chime and staircases climb past the 31st floor. Each floor's lobby is sealed by magic barriers that grow stronger the higher you go. The Tower houses hundreds of separate schools, each behind its own doors, and some own several whole floors. It also has a lobby where mages gather, personal and upper-floor research labs, a training center and an affiliated cafeteria. Mages keep night hours, so 3 AM is like daytime there.

The Tower is the center of the kingdom's magic. Its wizards rank close to nobility, and its schools stand together to protect their shared interests. It tests and sponsors magically gifted children, who live in the Tower; only those accepted are "true wizards", and self-taught mages are looked down on. It teaches spells for a fee, buys rare monsters and materials (including, until Bjorn stopped it, barbarian hearts), and runs projects for the royal family. Known schools include Artemion (Raven's), Ioni, Benocturn, Lengman, Egatile, Hulbunt, Altus, Galatir, Evound, Ornstalt, Wiltom, Triten, Mileun, Bevektalt and the extinct Maireta sect.

## Mechanics
- Entry requires a visitor's pass (ch. 44, 100).
- A warp gate on the first floor sends wizards close to their destination, such as a guild branch (ch. 39).
- The royal family allowed the Tower to be built only if it had no windows, officially so it could not overlook the palace. Raven suspects the real reason is to hide the outside world (ch. 262).
- The floor barriers can be broken by brute force, with about ten [Swing] hits at the 30th floor. The defending mages are mostly researchers and hold back area spells when civilians are present (ch. 496).
- The training center's instructors teach spells for a fee. Dwalky paid for the forbidden "awakening" combination of Thought Acceleration, Mana Amplification and Heart Acceleration (ch. 141).
- As a VIP courtesy, the royal family warns the Tower first when the labyrinth is dangerous (ch. 176).
- The guild asks high-level Tower wizards to cast mind magic on people with strong mental barriers (ch. 37). Stats can be checked at the Tower (ch. 556).
- It found a way to break Noark's barrier, which requires thirty High Priests praying together (ch. 198, 238). At the royal family's request it repaired and stabilized the labyrinth portal, which removed the 1st-floor dark-zone bug (ch. 259, 263).
- When Noark invaded, the Tower raised its barrier to buy time, then teleported all data, equipment, senior officials and local leaders out of the district. The royal family destroyed only the teleport circle (ch. 638).

## Notable events
- ch. 42: Artemion master Deian Tveherion settles an essence dispute with Bjorn.
- ch. 76–78: Bjorn begins weekly experiments on his vampire traits in Raven's lab.
- ch. 154–155: The Tower Master refuses the barbarian chieftain's request to stop using barbarian hearts. Bjorn recruits Raven.
- ch. 235–245: After escaping, Lee Baekho comes looking for the GM (Elfnunalove / Yurven Habelion), who hides in his upper-floor lab and goes out only through a golem.
- ch. 253: Tower mages call down rain on burning Karnon.
- ch. 417: Liard Ashid, who researched here for seven years, finds that the Tower Master colludes with the royal family.
- ch. 494–497: Bjorn leads over a thousand barbarians and civilians to occupy the Tower, with the royal army reaching only the 4th floor. Tower Master Teschlun Lengman Keallunus bans barbarian-heart research, dismisses 36 mages and wins immunity for Lurendel Galinbaret of the Triten School.
- ch. 638: Noark seizes the Tower, the second time in recent years that its boast of never being taken has been broken. The exterior is battered but the interior is mostly intact.
- ch. 776–777: Tower mages are drafted for the war. Raven's master dies, leaving the Master seat vacant, and Raven resigns from the Mage Corps to join Anabada.

# Artemion School

| | |

## Description
A school of the Magic Tower that owns five whole floors of the tower. Its master is Deian Tveherion, a white-haired old man. It is Raven's home school: she joined as a child and became a 6th-grade mage there, and she is the master's youngest and favorite student.

## Notable events
- ch. 42: To settle the essence dispute, the master gives Bjorn the Ring of the Frost Spirit (No. 9425).
- ch. 292: In a flashback, the young Raven passes the Tower's tests and joins the school.

## Description
Raven's personal lab on the 31st floor of the Magic Tower: cramped, dusty and windowless, buried in stacks of documents, flasks of colorful liquids and monster organ samples. Guests drink from titration flasks. Over time it fills with sinister-looking magic tools for diagnosis and drawing blood, including an ominous test-subject chair whose "bracelet" snaps shut on the sitter. Bjorn always takes that chair, and his belongings pile up in a corner. After Noark's invasion the lab was a mess with things missing, and Raven moved most of her work to a lab in Karnon.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- The lab is protected by soundproofing magic, which her master slipped past easily (ch. 42).
- A crystal ball is used for long-distance calls (ch. 41).
- During ability analysis, a mana conductor inserted into a vein monitors vital signs (ch. 468).

## Notable events
- ch. 41–44: Bjorn agrees to come once a week for three hours over three months so Raven can study his essences.
- ch. 98–100: The Artemion master tests Bjorn with a fake "evil spirit detector" (just an odd chair) and drops his suspicion. Bjorn finishes his three months of help.
- ch. 315: Raven finds the Golden Mask and a Whisper+Record tool that Bjorn had replayed 98 times (Hans Krisen's conversation). Misha arrives with news.
- ch. 468: Raven spends a month of daily tests on Erwen's Chaos Essence skill [Fusion], which turns her passives into chaos energy. It gives more Soul Power and natural energy, bonus chaos damage against monsters below 4th grade, and damage absorption while she concentrates.
- ch. 638–639: Bjorn hands over his secretly kept essences for study. Raven reveals that the latent ability of the Hiphramagent essence is [Best Friend], a Lamansis passive. Bjorn sleeps on the warm floor overnight rather than go home.

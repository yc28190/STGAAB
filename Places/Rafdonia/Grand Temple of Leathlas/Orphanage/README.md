| | |
|---|---|

## Description
An old orphanage run by the Leathlas church, surrounded by a wall, with a playground and a shade tree. Staff work at a desk inside, and the windows face a bench across the street. Twenty years earlier it was already decrepit, with a leaky roof unsafe for children. By the present day it is slated for demolition.

Dwarkey (Liol), Rotmiller and other orphans grew up here.

## Notable events
- ch. 146–148: Dwarkey first plans to leave his assets to the orphanage, then changes his will in favour of his teammates.
- ch. 257: The party moves furniture out as a church request for achievement points.
- ch. 287–288: Twenty years in the past, Bjorn (as "Nibels Enche") spends a week repairing the building. He tells grim labyrinth stories to put young Liol off becoming an explorer, and meets Wobu Emiren at the workers' party in a nearby bar.
- ch. 292: Emiren adopts Liol "Wobu" Dwarkey from here.
- ch. 336: The memory-wiped Laura (Shannon Ellaura) works at the desk doing paperwork, having learned to read in weeks.
- ch. 742, 751: Rotmiller grew up here after his parents were executed for not paying taxes, and learned to handle horses.

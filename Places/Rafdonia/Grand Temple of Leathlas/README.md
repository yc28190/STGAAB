## Description
A spotless white building that looks untouched by the world. Inside are a reception room with sofas and, on the 2nd floor, a large chapel normally reserved for priests. A large hall can hold a crowd of explorers. During the Noark war the temple burned.

It is the head temple of the church of Leathlas (Reatlas), the good-aligned Goddess of Stars and god of exploration, whose greeting is "May the stars of the twilight guide us". The church heals, removes essences, hunts evil spirits alongside the royal family, teaches most human children to read, and runs paladin orders and an orphanage. Priests are treated almost like nobles.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Essence removal for a fee that rises with the essence's value (10–15 million stones for a rare one) (ch. 41). Unless you are a saint, essences can only be removed inside a temple, by a priest of bishop rank or higher (ch. 530, 625). Floor Lord essences cannot be removed (ch. 461).
- During removal the person stands naked in a pure white space with a blue dagger. Their essences appear as giant caged monsters, and you cut the thread of light linking you to one. No visitors in the evening; an empty-slot feeling lingers for a few days (ch. 335).
- Healing such as "High-Grade Healing" divine power (ch. 119). Apprentice priests at the entrance ask visitors whether they want treatment (ch. 146).
- Recruiting a temple priest takes a team with at least three members of 5th grade or above, plus enough achievement points with the church, earned by grinding church requests. Titles and rank are only the minimum (ch. 151, 246, 257).
- Evil spirits are reported to the temple (ch. 2). Barbarians send their dead to the Grand Temple (ch. 153).

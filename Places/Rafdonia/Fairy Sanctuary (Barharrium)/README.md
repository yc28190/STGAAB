## Description
The fairies' holy land, outside the walls of District 9. From its outer edge you see nothing but trees: enormous ones, far tidier than those of the barbarian holy land, as if they had been tended for thousands of years. Inside, the buildings resemble the rest of the city but are blended with nature, giving the place an exotic feel. A massive Divine Tree is visible in the distance, the air is clean and smoke-free, and its festival is orderly, with soft, lively string music instead of drums.

It is home to the fairy tribe, its elders and the Pureblood Order, and one of Rafdonia's six holy lands, all of which lie outside the walls. About thirteen years ago, during the Sacred Relic War, Regal Vagos (the Dragonslayer) attacked it alone and killed more than a thousand fairies, including Erwen's parents, an event remembered as "the Bloodbath". Other races are rarely seen here.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- A transparent barrier keeps outsiders out, and the people inside apparently cannot see beyond it (ch. 662).
- The Divine Tree purifies the air (ch. 778).

## Notable events

- ch. 231: Erwen brings Daria's body here for the funeral. Her youngest sister lives here until she comes of age (ch. 240).
- ch. 343: Erwen takes most of the royal support funds by threatening to sell the Sacred Tree Bow.
- ch. 408, 428: The Bloodbath is recounted; Erwen's uncle saved the children.
- ch. 442–446: Erwen resigns from the Pureblood Order, returns the Divine Tree Bow and must repay the money for her house; her uncle helps her against the elders.
- ch. 642: It is recalled that Beleg Shusia di Tersia saved the Fornaci sisters during the Dragonslayer's attack.
- ch. 662: A young silver-haired fairy touches the barrier from inside, frustrated, wondering what lies beyond.
- ch. 778: Fairies openly insult Elwen as a traitor for leaving the Pureblood Order. Bjorn uses [Savage Eruption] and makes an embarrassing public oath.
- ch. 784: Beleg looks after Mei here.

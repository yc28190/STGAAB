| | |

## Description
The grave of Daria, Erwen's sister, presumably in Barharrium (the location is not stated). Unusually, her burned body was buried rather than cremated.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- The Resurrection Stone only works on the recently dead (ch. 784).

## Notable events
- ch. 784: Beleg reveals he buried Daria instead of cremating her and secretly dug her up to cast preservation spells, hoping to revive her. He gave up on learning the Resurrection Stone's limit.

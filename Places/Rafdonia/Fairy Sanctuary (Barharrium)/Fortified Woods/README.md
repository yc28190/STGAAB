# Fortified Woods

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | forest (fortification) |
| **Also known as** | Barharrium fortified woods; former residential zone |
| **First appearance** | ch. 778 |

## Description
Quiet woods in Barharrium that see few visitors even during festivals. They were a residential area until the Dragonslayer's attack ruined the sanctuary, killing Elwen's parents. Elwen's family house still stands here beside a small pond.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- The woods are really a fortification: magic circles are carved into the trees, the loose ground slows movement, and the trees overhead are arranged for defence against attacks from the air (ch. 778).

## Notable events
- ch. 778: Elwen takes Bjorn through the woods to her family home.

## Description
An unguarded cave outside Kiarbys, marked on the map kept in the party's annex. Crystals in its walls give off a blue light like the 1st floor of the labyrinth. Side passages branch off, but the straight path from the entrance leads to a vast cavern with a rock in the middle that vibrates at regular intervals. Behind the rock is a wall with a mural.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- The vibrating rock (Lignaracia) is said to bring good luck, but it is a decoy (ch. 250).
- Hidden piece: smash the mural wall behind it to reach a small hidden chamber with a plain rock. A self-repairing magic circle restores the wall in about 5 minutes (ch. 250).
- Touching the plain rock grants "Imbued with the Earth Dragon's energy (1/3)": Soul Power +10, Earth Resistance +20. With the Earth Dragon's Blessing, Bjorn's essence stat bonus goes from 20% to 30% (ch. 250).
- Requirement: being a noble who can enter Gnometree (ch. 250).

## Notable events
- ch. 250–251: Lee Baekho follows Bjorn in and pushes him into admitting he is a player, then turns the repaired wall to dust with a wave of his hand.

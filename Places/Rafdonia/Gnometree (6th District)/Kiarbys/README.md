## Description
An old holiday village for nobles on the east side of Gnometree's central mountain. It is full of villas owned by titled nobles, plus annexes for rent. There are outdoor hot springs, riding grounds, fishing spots (a deep lake fished at night by lantern) and a hunting ground stocked with pheasants and deer. A brightly lit gambling house stays noisy late into the night.

The village already existed in the game, which was set 150 years earlier.

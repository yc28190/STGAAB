## Description
A huge farming district that smells of fruit and unripe grain. A straight road runs in from the double gates between green fields, past grape vines, windmills and watermills and grazing cows and horses, with a mountain in the middle. Away from the village lights, nights near the mountain are chilly and pitch dark, much like the Korean countryside. It lies just east of District 5.

Gnometree was made a special zone when the city was first planned and is built on the Dragonkin's sacred grounds, which makes the Dragonkin effectively landowning nobles. Fairies with special spirit magic work the farms, dwarves build and maintain the mills, and beastmen run the livestock. The noble resort village of Kiarbys sits on the east side of its mountain.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Only nobles may enter, apart from farm workers. IDs are checked at the double gates, and each noble may bring only three escorts (ch. 249).
- Visitors get a whistle that calls the Gnometree patrol anywhere, at any time, and hand it back at the southwest exit. It is the most heavily guarded district after the royal palace (ch. 249, 251).
- Its income pays for the other races' interest-free tax loans: fairies get 3 years, while barbarians and humans get none (ch. 202, 249).
- Nobles may be buried here; everyone else is cremated and keeps the urn at home (ch. 774).
- The Dragon Sanctuary is reached from here by carriage (ch. 642). The 4th Wall garrison covers the barrier next to it (ch. 772).

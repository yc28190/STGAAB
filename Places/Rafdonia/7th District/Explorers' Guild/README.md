## Description
The organization that registers, ranks and supports Rafdonia's explorers, with hundreds of branch offices across the city. Each district has a central headquarters much larger than an ordinary branch; the 7th District Central Branch, the one nearest Bjorn's inn (about a 30-minute walk) and the Dimensional Plaza, has heavy wooden main doors, a busy 1st-floor hall full of staff and explorers, and creaking wooden stairs up to the Regional Manager's office (4th floor in one account) with a sofa and an old table. On labyrinth-opening day a long queue forms outside before the doors open; after the labyrinth closes the branch fills with silent, unwashed explorers waiting to file death reports.

The guild is organized as tens of thousands of members under hundreds of branch managers, 13 regional chiefs/overseers (such as Nile Urbans of the 7th District) and a Guildmaster elected by board vote (later Ilia/Iriya Adnus, the former third-rank explorer "Black Hunter"). Explorers traditionally distrust it.

## Mechanics
- Binding/Bonding magic: party members touch a crystal ball together (green light = rank 9) to start at the same random point in the labyrinth; it lasts 24 hours and costs 15,000 stones for two rank-9 adventurers, more at higher ranks (ch. 22).
- Rank (9 lowest to 1) is based on the total essence engraved on the soul; it can be raised by a review application (e.g. on the strength of valuable equipment), which other people can see. From 5th grade, promotion requires a test, unlocked with achievement points from guild requests (ch. 22, 40, 48, 57, 77, 205).
- Party finding: staff hand out recruitment listings matching requested criteria, and members must wear video recorders to deter betrayal; recruitment notices are posted and teams/clans register here (ch. 44–46, 159, 257, 261).
- Bjorn's ID card carries a secret "five stars" mark that makes staff very polite (ch. 45–46).
- Bounties (e.g. Elisa's 10 million stones) are paid on ID once a temple confirms the kill (ch. 85).
- Sells information on grade 8+ essences, keeps essence records and publishes standard essence prices (ch. 19, 73, 204).
- Explorers write wills before their first entry; without a named heir, assets go to the guild. A death report takes about 3 minutes (ch. 146, 148).
- Visitors normally draw a number and wait; nobles skip the queue (ch. 257).
- A rule introduced about six months before ch. 161 grants achievement points for subduing troublemakers inside the headquarters (ch. 161).
- Guild records (former team, essences, etc.) are accessible to the royal family; lost administrative records are common (ch. 366, 373).
- The guild must approve killing the 1st-floor Lord of Fear, takes Floor Lord raid requests, investigates looting and pays achievement points for rescuing explorers (ch. 219, 504–506).
- It receives labyrinth information early to prepare selected explorers (ch. 786).

## Notable events
- ch. 37–41: Bjorn is falsely imprisoned, breaks out and takes a hostage; he is compensated with promotion to rank 7 and 6 million stones under a non-disclosure agreement.
- ch. 57, 73: Elisa spots Hikurod's Guardian's Bracers on his rank application; Bjorn and Hikurod later report her as a Karui priest with video evidence.
- ch. 146–148: The Misfits report Dwarkey's death and learn he left them his gear and house.
- ch. 159–162: Team "Bjorn" is registered and renamed Apple Nark. Bjorn smashes the 7th District headquarters' front door, faces down Meltson Nelbo, threatens Regional Manager Nile Urbans with a bribery recording and folds his table in half.
- ch. 261–262: Apple Nark registers as a clan, with Raven doing the paperwork.
- ch. 295: Raven's filing of Viscount Bjorn Yandel's death report causes chaos.
- ch. 373: Amelia blackmails a corrupt executive into erasing Lihen Schuitz's records.
- ch. 403: Guild Master Adnus has Pike Neldain sabotage the expedition's supplies.
- ch. 786–787: With guild influence shrinking during the labyrinth closure, Adnus joins Anabada's Team 1 as a guest, paying with the guild's Dragon Heart.

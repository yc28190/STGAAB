## Description
A prison deep beneath the guild branch, with cages of thick iron bars along corridors guarded by jailers. It mostly holds accused pillagers awaiting investigation.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Prisoners sentenced to death are told the date and asked what they want for a last meal (ch. 37).
- It is rated less secure than the Alminous vault (ch. 755).

## Notable events
- ch. 38: Bjorn melts the bars with acidic Flesh Explosions and frees everyone; about half the prisoners choose to stay.
- ch. 624: During the Noark occupation the prison holds explorer hostages.
- ch. 626: Bjorn's rescue route finds it completely empty, with no guards or prisoners.

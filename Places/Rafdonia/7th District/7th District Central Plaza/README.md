## Description
A square where several roads meet, with paths named by clock direction (such as the "3 o'clock path"). Usually busy or empty depending on the hour, it fills with explorers waiting for teammates on the night before the labyrinth opens and serves as a common meeting point. It also holds a guillotine, where soldiers and crowds gather for public executions.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Tax evaders and evil spirits are publicly beheaded here; a superstition holds that bread soaked in the blood of the executed brings wealth (ch. 18, 74).

## Notable events
- ch. 74: The barbarian Tarik son of Liyen is executed here as an evil spirit, shouting Bjorn's name.
- ch. 162: Team Apple Nark meets here before the 5th-floor expedition; Avman arrives 45 minutes late and pays Raven's 45,000-stone fine.
- ch. 384: The navigator Auyen Lokrob waits here for Amelia after her return.

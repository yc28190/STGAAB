# Leathlas Church Eastern Branch

| | |
|---|---|

## Description
A small Leathlas (Reatlas) shrine in the 7th District with a familiar statue of the Goddess at the entrance. It follows the standard small-branch plan: a 1st-floor chapel with the statue and an awkwardly central staircase, and administrative rooms and priests' quarters on the 2nd floor, the only floor with windows. During the war it stood untouched among burning ruins, wrapped in a dim, glowing translucent barrier that the flames struck like glass, its main door barricaded with chairs, desks and wardrobes.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- It is the only branch with a holy enchantment over the whole building that makes it fireproof; it blocks only fire from outside, so a fire set inside can burn it. Few know of it (ch. 744, 750).
- The door resists magical breaking; Marone added a protective circle (ch. 746–747).
- A hidden recess on the statue slides it back to reveal a secret stairway, an emergency underground passage that leads to a well outside (ch. 747, 752).
- The fastest route from the west crosses a royal army vs Noark battle; any detour is at least three times longer (ch. 745).

## Notable events
- ch. 745–746: Sven and others reach it through the fire under [Prayer of Protection]; 37 refugees shelter inside under a retired rank-7 explorer.
- ch. 747: Over 100 Noark soldiers douse the fire and climb in through the 2nd-floor windows; Marone's [Iron Wall] briefly seals the stairs.
- ch. 750: Sven sends everyone down the passage and stays to fight; he rejects the evil god Karui, the Star Goddess erases Karui's mark and grants [Agent of the Stars], and he sets white [Unquenchable Flame] inside. Avman Urikfrit bursts out of the passage on the giant bear Chulwoong Iradoon and rescues him; the statue is shattered.

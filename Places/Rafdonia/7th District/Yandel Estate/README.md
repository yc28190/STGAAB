# Yandel Estate

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | building (estate, under construction) |
| **Also known as** | Yandel Family Estate; Yandel mansion under construction |
| **First appearance** | ch. 446 |

## Description
The Yandel family's planned estate on land bought in the 7th District near the Dimension Plaza with the help of the Melbes (Melbeth) Trading Company. By the time of the Noark war the house was nearly finished with a handsomely raised roof, but the fighting badly shattered it.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Construction takes at least a year, from blueprints by a Melbes Trading Company architect (ch. 473).

## Notable events
- ch. 446: Amelia asks for a bigger room with a window for the navigator.
- ch. 636–637: After war damage, moving in is delayed again; the original design is now too small for the household, so the Melbes chairman offers to help buy nearby land.

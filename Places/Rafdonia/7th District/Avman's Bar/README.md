## Description
A bar and restaurant in the 7th District run by Avman Urikfrit (later written Abman Siegfried, nicknamed "Bear") and his Black Bear wife. At first it was a small, nearly empty shop that closed late, with Avman serving in an apron. It is open-plan with no partitions or private rooms, so eavesdropping is easy. The menu includes grilled meat, pumpkin stew, rye bread, very sweet rum and the house speciality, home-made (salted) honey mead served with platters of meat; warm honey water is served for hangovers. Later it is described as two floors, a restaurant by day and bar by night with an inn upstairs; other accounts call it a three-storey bar where the team met on the 3rd floor (conflicting accounts).

## Notable events
- ch. 120–123: Bjorn ducks in out of a storm; Avman asks to join his team, and they meet again for a 5-million-stone repayment.
- ch. 151: Avman joins Bjorn's team here.
- ch. 159: Apple Nark negotiates loot shares at a newly opened three-storey bar Avman picked because his own shop was not private enough (possibly a different venue).
- ch. 177, 193–205: The team gathers here before expeditions and holds its loot settlements.
- ch. 252: Bjorn gauges public opinion here and hears the alarm bells for the Karnon fire.
- ch. 262: With a "Closed" sign up, the clan eats here and Raven casts Bonding before an expedition.
- ch. 442–443: Bear, whose wife is pregnant with their second child, declines to rejoin the party and lends Bjorn a raincoat.

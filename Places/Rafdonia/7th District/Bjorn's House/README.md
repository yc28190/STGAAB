## Description
A four-storey building in the inner area of the 7th District, about a 10-minute walk from Commelby. Team Apple Nark rents the 1st and 2nd floors, while an explorer couple occupies the 3rd and 4th floors with a separate entrance. The team's part has a kitchen and dining table, a living room with a sofa and coffee table, a bathtub on the 1st floor and a small bathroom on the 2nd. Bjorn (with a 350,000-stone super-king bed), Misha and later Erwen have rooms upstairs; Ainar's room and a storage room are downstairs. It is the home of Bjorn, Misha, Ainar and, from ch. 241, Erwen.

After Bjorn's reported death, flowers were piled at the door and the curtains kept drawn for mourners, his room left untouched. During the Noark invasion the 2nd floor collapsed and was scorched, apparently by fire magic, while the 1st floor and basement survived behind a partly broken fence.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Rented, since buying was too expensive (ch. 205).
- After Bjorn was named for a title, royal knights guarded it: first 12, later over thirty outside plus three inside, with a poison-resistant food taster at every meal; they were recalled in early June (ch. 240–241, 246, 257).

## Notable events
- ch. 232: Many visitors (Teterud, Melter, Hikurod, Rotmiller, Ragna, Shabin, the chieftain and others) come after the ambush.
- ch. 235: Erwen moves in after her sister's funeral.
- ch. 260: Misha waits up all night after hearing Karon's story; Bjorn confesses to her in her room.
- ch. 295, 342: Mourners and people Bjorn had helped visit after the death report.
- ch. 312–313: Misha shuts herself in; Lee Baekho breaks in, accuses her, and hints there is a way to bring Bjorn back.
- ch. 624: The damaged house serves as Bjorn's hideout; the Noark captive "subordinate G" is held in the basement while the hostage rescue is planned.

## Description
A large open square surrounded by multi-storey buildings, under Rafdonia's gloomy gray sky. On the night the labyrinth opens, thousands of armed explorers gather; at midnight a light appears at the center and swells into a huge glowing sphere, the portal, which shrinks as closing approaches. On return day the plaza fills as densely as a stadium crowd, with families waiting beyond the checkpoint. There are seven such plazas in the city (one per district except Bifron, whose plaza is gone); the 7th District's is the one Bjorn uses. The Black Whale Tavern (now Pydenpus) lies to the south.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- The portal opens at midnight on the 1st of each month; officials announce entry and count down to closing. Tens of thousands enter, and the plaza is nearly empty within about 40 minutes (ch. 3, 22).
- Entering at the last moment causes "dimensional instability", dropping the party into the dark outer zone of the 1st floor next to an unopened Floor Stele (the "experience duplication bug"); it was common 130 years ago until the Magic Tower repaired the dimensional circle (ch. 3–5, 19, 22, 162, 178).
- Parties of up to 5 are linked with a mage's [Bonding] spell before entering; otherwise the guild charges about 100,000 stones for five (ch. 88, 131, 455).
- When the labyrinth closes, explorers reappear here at noon (party members may arrive one by one, far apart) and pass grade-based checkpoints where magic stones are exchanged; those below level 7 go to a separate area. The 5th-grade checkpoint is quick, gives out candy and has baths (ch. 16, 205, 490).
- Engraved ID tags are issued on the wrist at each entry and dissolve into light after return (ch. 205).
- People leaving by Dimensional Gate also exit here, synchronized with the closing (ch. 230).
- The first Dimension Plaza was built at the same time as the Stone of Honor (ch. 491).
- Noark's own return plaza, in the western part of its Lord's Castle, has no checkpoint and opens only with someone of the Lord's bloodline present (ch. 302, 336).

## Notable events
- ch. 71–72: After the 3rd-floor disaster the plaza fills with wounded explorers and patient tents.
- ch. 177: Officials hand out recording devices and ID tag necklaces; Bjorn uses [Gigantification] to carry his team through the closing portal.
- ch. 195: At the Behel-la Union's founding ceremony, a wooden platform collapses under Bjorn and he speaks from a rooftop.
- ch. 230–236: Hundreds of Dimensional Gates open and crimson pillars of fire erupt in all seven plazas, designed by the Ruin Scholar and triggered by Liranne Viviane; only 103 survive, all comatose.
- ch. 251–252: On 1 June only Public Safety guards seal the plaza while the army is underground; a tremor is felt.
- ch. 285: Bjorn fails to appear after the labyrinth closes, and Ainar declares him dead.
- ch. 337: In the abandoned Noark plaza, Bjorn and Amelia meet by a dusty royal-army tent.
- ch. 490: Erwen, Versil and Ainar reappear alive after "dying" in the Pilgrim's Cave.
- ch. 622–623: The checkpoint lies destroyed; Noark ambushes returning explorers, and Anabada meets a 14-man Noark patrol.
- ch. 751–753: Ainar lands here after the random teleport, revealing the summoning circle's misfire.

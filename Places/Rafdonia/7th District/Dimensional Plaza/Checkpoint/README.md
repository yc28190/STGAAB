## Description
The checkpoints at the Dimensional Plaza through which returning explorers must pass. They are divided by explorer grade, with long queues at the lower-grade gates. The 6th-grade checkpoint is a private room rather than an open counter, with fewer explorers and more than twice the staff of the 9th-grade checkpoint, so it moves quickly and the officials are more polite. The checkpoints are a major source of royal income.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Returning explorers pass through by rank and exchange magic stones for money; officials verify the stones' mana with the "Mana Measurement" spell, and loot is inspected (ch. 89, 146, 170, 538).
- Checkpoint records track who comes back; an ID card is needed to pass and to leave the city (ch. 73, 164).
- Team rank is recognised only from 6th grade, allowing a team to use the 6th-grade checkpoint together (ch. 172).
- Inspectors can detain explorers with outstanding arrest warrants (ch. 191).
- The checkpoint is linked to the Marquis's mansion by message stone (ch. 431).
- The Integrated Checkpoint is only for explorers of 4th grade and above (mostly clan members), so there is no line; parties of over 10 must use another checkpoint (ch. 464).

## Notable events
- ch. 114: Karon walks through carrying three bags of marauder loot and rallies the barbarians.
- ch. 172: Apple Nark exchanges seven bags of Burning Zone stones for 25,190,000 stones.
- ch. 191: Apple Nark is held and the Moselan knight Elmeras arrests Bjorn; that run they exchange only 7,080,000 stones.
- ch. 431: Bjorn announces a "royal special forces unit" here to spread rumors; the Marquis's knights arrive within 30 minutes.

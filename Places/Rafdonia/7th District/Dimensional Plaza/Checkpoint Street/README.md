## Description
The street outside the Dimensional Plaza checkpoint, packed with explorers' families and friends on return day. Twenty years in the past the buildings look the same; only the first-floor shop signs differ (a bar stands where there is now a general store).

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Explorers hand over their ID card and exchange magic stones at the checkpoint (ch. 285).

## Notable events

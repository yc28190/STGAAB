## Description
Rows of tables at the Dimensional Plaza where blank-faced officials weigh pouches of mana stones and pay out money very quickly. There are separate counters by rank, including one for rank-nine adventurers.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Returning adventurers must trade their mana stones for currency here; pouches go on a scale-like device that shows their value (ch. 16).
- Officials can press a hidden alarm to call guards if someone's takings look suspicious (ch. 36–37).
- Fairies who entered with a registered relative can name them (ch. 37).

## Notable events
- ch. 16: Bjorn turns in 182,413 stones' worth on his first return (a loaf of bread costs about 20 stones).
- ch. 36–37: Bjorn is arrested here after the hidden alarm is triggered.

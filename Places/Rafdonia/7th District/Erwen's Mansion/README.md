## Description
A detached house in the 7th District behind a high fence and a heavy iron gate, with a private garden and lawn that Erwen tends. Every window was boarded over with thick planks, so it looks dark from outside and the bedroom gets no moonlight. Inside are a living room with sofas, a dining table, many empty rooms, a bathroom on each of the 1st and 2nd floors, and Bjorn's room on the 2nd floor. It is described as three stories in one account and two in another (conflicting accounts). A hidden mechanism under the carpet opens a staircase to a basement storage room; at its end, behind a thick locked iron door, is a room with a large bed, wall shackles, a bathroom and shower that is effectively a prison.

Bjorn lived here as a recluse under the identity of Lihen Schuitz after his return from the "dead", and the house later served as the team's base until it was sold.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Erwen bought it in one day at full price with money from the fairy tribe's royal support fund, choosing a house with a basement "just in case" (ch. 341, 343, 384).
- Bjorn could walk in the garden only at night and only with Erwen (ch. 341).
- It was mortgaged to the bank on a six-month short-term loan to fund equipment (Kraul's Demon Crusher in one account, the Vol-Herchan essence in another; conflicting accounts), later repaid with expedition loot (ch. 348–350, 391).
- Erwen installed recording crystal balls ("CCTV") in the living room and entrance that store one day of footage; Amelia also leaves a hair on the doorknob to detect intruders (ch. 387, 433).

## Notable events
- ch. 345–348: Erwen confronts Bjorn and they fight in the bedroom with [Eye of the Storm]; Amelia picks the front-door lock and joins them.
- ch. 350: Testing Gachabon's [Probabilistic Retaliation], a reflected fireball nearly burns the house down.
- ch. 384–387: The captive navigator Auyen Lokrob is locked in the basement; royal knights under Eltora Terserion smash the front door and arrest "Lihen Schuitz" for colluding with Noark.
- ch. 396: Bjorn welcomes new teammates Liard Ashid and Gruold Aldidi.
- ch. 433–434: Intruders search the house but miss the basement; reporters and fans crowd the gate after Bjorn's return, and his brawl with Ainar wrecks the garden.
- ch. 446–473: Erwen must repay the fairy tribe after leaving the Pureblood Order, so the house is sold and everyone moves out on four carts.

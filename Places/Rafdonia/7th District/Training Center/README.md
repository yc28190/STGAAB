# Training Center

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | building (training hall) |
| **Also known as** | — |
| **First appearance** | ch. 207 |

## Description
A membership training facility in the 7th District with target platforms and Adamantium steel bars that are magically repaired after each use.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- The only place in the city where using abilities is allowed; requires a monthly membership (ch. 207, 242).
- Live targets (such as a pig) must be brought in, while the steel bars are free (ch. 242).

## Notable events
- ch. 207: Misha and Ainar train here daily; Bjorn considers renting a training center for Rotmiller's navigator school.
- ch. 242: The party tests new reward essences: Avman's Hungry Claws linked to Iron Bear, and Ainar's Wild Control cutting Adamantium.

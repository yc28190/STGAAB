## Description
A military facility in the 7th District holding teleportation magic circles linked to the matching stations in other districts and the Imperial City. Carriages drive straight onto the circles, and a separate noble section always has carriages waiting to take nobles directly to their destinations. It was built for rapid troop deployment in emergencies but is mostly used by nobles on urgent business. A separate public transfer station links the districts by ordinary carriage.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Teleports to the Imperial City's station without passengers leaving their carriage, cutting a six-hour carriage journey to about an hour in total; it costs about 1 million stones per person without a carriage and causes slight motion sickness (ch. 387, 391, 453).
- A Viscount is entitled to use it; commoners cannot use the noble section (ch. 387, 443, 453).
- The public transfer station runs carriages between districts, including a noble carriage to Commelby (ch. 490).

## Notable events
- ch. 387–391: Bjorn uses the station to travel to the Imperial City.
- ch. 491: Crowds follow Bjorn's carriage after the Stone of Honor feat.
- ch. 495: The 3rd Magic Corps passes through, crowded among military carriages, on its way to the Mage Tower incident.

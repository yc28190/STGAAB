## Description
A residential district of Rafdonia with paved roads and simpler street layouts than the 13th District, within walking distance of the labyrinth checkpoint. It is Bjorn's home district ("hometown"): it holds his inn and later homes, Avman's wife's bar, the central plaza, the 7th District Central Branch of the Explorers' Guild, a military transfer station and a Magic Tower branch. Before the wars it was where most barbarians living outside the Sanctuary resided, and it contains the barbarians' sacred site. The 8th District lies across the wall, Viphron (Bifron) borders it, and District 5 is next to it.

The district was wrecked twice. After the first Noark occupation and liberation its main road was bloodstained and battle-scarred, barracks were abandoned, and residents slept in blankets in front of collapsed homes; reconstruction then filled it with new studio-apartment buildings and shops. During the later war it became enemy ground patrolled by Noark, its eastern side burned to ash, and two Urae strikes hit its center, leaving uneven rubble paths with soldiers working among the debris.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- About an hour by carriage from its military transfer station; the Imperial City is about six hours away by plain carriage (ch. 391).
- During the Noark occupation, explorers were held in the underground prisons of the guild branch, the Tri-God temples and the police, and civilians were gathered near the walls (ch. 624).
- Reconstruction was awarded to the Yandel Barony; only the Yandel family may build there (ch. 634, 654, 657).
- A military magic circle at the District 7 wall garrison connects to Karnon (ch. 709).
- An ancient summoning circle built into the district pulls in everything within a narrow area and was meant to send it to the Imperial Capital; it needs a long casting time. Fired in haste during the second Urae strike, it scattered everyone randomly across the city (as it had failed during the old Siege of Iron Walls rescue), leaving dozens of barbarians behind (ch. 751).
- After the war, carriages can no longer cross the city center through Districts 7 and 8 (ch. 776).

## Notable events
- ch. 173–205: Bjorn lives here, first at his inn, later at the team's rented house.
- ch. 385–392: Bjorn, as Lihen Schuitz, lives in Erwen's house here.
- ch. 623–633: Noark occupies the district and its walls; vice leader Roland Banossant offers Bjorn passage, but Bjorn chooses Viphron. The royal army retakes the district and Noark flees into Viphron.
- ch. 648–657: Bjorn's real-estate scheme: he buys cheap land from cash-strapped nobles, builds studio apartments rented on "jeonse" deposits backed by Alminous Bank, and buys the 7-2 Commercial District. Tens of thousands of barbarians, Viphron men and dwarf technicians rebuild the district.
- ch. 708–717: Anabada is assigned here under the 3rd Legion; Urae strikes the city.
- ch. 726–751: As Ravigion territory, the district becomes a battlefield; Bjorn's force wipes out Noark units, joins a pincer with the District 5 army, and escapes the second Urae strike via the ancient summoning circle.
- ch. 783–788: Bjorn admits he set Districts 7 and 8 on fire during the war; Sanctuary warriors are assigned to restoration work.

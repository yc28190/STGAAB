## Description
An ordinary inn with a lobby and dining room on the 1st floor and rooms upstairs, used by Bjorn as his base during different periods. The rooms are small, cramped studios with running water, a single bathroom, a window onto the street and a groaning bed; each room has a mailbox by its door, and the wooden doors let snoring through. No cooking is possible, but meals are served downstairs; the rice and stew are cheap and good, and a special 800-stone meal includes a baked cream pie. In the past timeline the room has sunlight through barred windows and a cool breeze.

The notes cover more than one inn: Bjorn's very first inn (room 302, about an hour's walk from Erwen's fairy dormitory), which he left after the innkeeper cleared his belongings, and the later 7th-District inn where he, Ainar and Misha took rooms 201, 202 and 207 before moving to the team house.

## Notable events
- ch. 21–22: Erwen walks an hour to eat the inn's food.
- ch. 37–43: When Bjorn fails to return from the labyrinth, the innkeeper clears his belongings and turns Erwen away; Bjorn demands 30,000 stones in compensation and moves out.
- ch. 119–123: Guild staff fetch Bjorn about Karon; Erwen and Misha clash; Julian Urbans warns Bjorn about Rotmiller; Bjorn's soul leaves for the Ghostbusters community from his bed.
- ch. 159–160: The Dragonkin leader teleports Bjorn to and from his room.
- ch. 177: Amelia Rainwales has a tense standoff with Bjorn in the hallway.
- ch. 204: Misha's family's black-clad beastmen wait for her here.
- ch. 257–259: Bjorn takes a room wearing Karon's face for the community night.
- ch. 285–334: In the past timeline Bjorn and Amelia stay at an inn in the 7th District before and after their time in Noark.

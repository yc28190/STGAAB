## Description
The spot in the 7th District where Noark's Urae strike landed. An enormous projectile came in on a steep arc from the direction of District 4 with a jet-like roar. When Bjorn used [Domain Declaration], black ripples turned the area into a black-and-white world, and the impact itself appeared only as pure white light and silence.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Warning signs preceded the strike: a captured Noark scout feigned cooperation, cursed Bjorn and died, and enemy watchers pulled back fast like the sea before a tsunami (ch. 748).
- Bjorn countered with [Transcendence] plus [Iron Fortress], unlocking [Mutated Exoskeleton]; the Aegis Dragon Armor passive multiplied his resistances by 10 for 10 seconds, yielding "The Hanged Man" (he takes all damage meant for others and his HP cannot fall below 1%) and [Domain Declaration] (all damage within his line of sight goes to him). When the effect ended, all stored damage hit at once (ch. 749).

## Notable events
- ch. 749: Bjorn absorbs the strike for his whole force and collapses near death.
- ch. 751: As enemies flood in and a second Urae shot appears, the District 7 summoning circle scatters everyone across the city.

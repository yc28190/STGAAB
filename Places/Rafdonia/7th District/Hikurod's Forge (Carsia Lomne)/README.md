# Hikurod's Forge (Carsia Lomne)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | shop (blacksmith) |
| **Also known as** | Carsia Lomne ("Magic Flame" in the ancient language); Hikurod's Blacksmith Workshop; Dwarkey's House; the dwarf's forge |
| **First appearance** | ch. 148 |

## Description
A blacksmith's workshop in the 7th District, originally the home of Dwarkey (Dwalke), who left it to his four Team Misfits teammates. Hikurod Murad bought out the others and turned it into a forge into which he put everything he owned. Over time it grew into Carsia Lomne, a busy commercial forge expanded into neighboring buildings, with a front lobby where displayed gear serves only as samples for custom orders, and a cramped attic upstairs (ceiling barely over 1.5 m, with a dwarf-sized sofa) for paperwork. Hikurod stopped working the forge himself, hired blacksmiths and became a money-minded businessman, though he sponsors two orphanages with the profits.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- The house was valued at 24 million stones by the Central Administrative Office (auction estimated about 20% lower); Hikurod paid each teammate 5 million (ch. 149).
- Melting mithril properly requires building a specialized forge (ch. 468).
- Exclusive supplier and fence for Clan Anabada, taking a 5% cut on selling Noark loot (ch. 449, 453).

## Notable events
- ch. 149–162: Hikurod buys the house and converts it into a workshop.
- ch. 205, 246: The struggling workshop repairs the team's gear; Bjorn worries it will lose customers after the explorer deaths.
- ch. 366: Hikurod throws out Bjorn (disguised as Lihen) for only browsing.
- ch. 449–453: Hikurod signs with Anabada and reforges the Rose Knights' mithril daggers and Ogre leather.
- ch. 498: Bjorn checks on the mithril smelting.
- ch. 649: The forge is destroyed during the Noark chaos, but its important assets, including Bjorn's Mistium, are saved; Hikurod plans a bigger forge.
- ch. 752: Hikurod jokes that he is about to lose everything again.

## Description
The broken walls of the 7th District, visible from far away, held by Noark's main force during its occupation.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Noark's main force outnumbered Bjorn's group at least twenty to one here and had to stay to watch the royal army outside (ch. 626).

## Notable events
- ch. 627–628: Bjorn meets Abet Nekrapeto, Rilanne Vivian (the Witch of Agony), a blood knight and Orculus vice leader Roland Banossant. Banossant blocks all outside sound for a private talk and offers passage over the walls as a political trap; when Bjorn names Viphron, Banossant gives himself away.

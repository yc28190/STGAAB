## Description
An old, run-down house in the war-torn 7th District, with the top of a wardrobe visible through a broken 2nd-floor window and another building directly across the street. The wardrobe is cramped and dark; a grown man has to hunch to fit inside.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Sven's divine "instinct" told him the wardrobe was absolutely safe and that he could not die inside it; his chronic anxiety vanished there. Once he left to fight, he sensed it would never be safe again (ch. 730, 740, 744).

## Notable events
- ch. 730: Sven fakes a seizure to slip away and hides in the wardrobe; Lilis Marone follows him.
- ch. 741–742: Marone kills Noark scouts to save a beastkin mother and baby and hides with them across the street; a black mage tracks the bodies and Marone is captured after killing seven soldiers.
- ch. 742–744: Sven emerges, cuts down the squad and carries all three toward the fire.

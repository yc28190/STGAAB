## Description
You enter through an iron gate and go down damp stairs into pitch darkness, where only flickering torches light the way. The tunnels are roomier than expected, and sewage flows slowly beside a walkway. The stench is overwhelming. Away from the main channels the sewers become a maze worse than any labyrinth: long straight passages, ladders, holes in the ceiling, and manhole-like metal covers leading to three-, four- and five-way junctions. Some channels were built only for water, with side walkways too narrow for a 2 m barbarian and sewage up to the shins; in others, water flows above the ceiling. Deeper toward Noark, the tunnels turn into an ant nest of low ceilings where [Giant Form] cannot be used and side passages let enemies strike from the flanks.

The sewers are a refuge for branded criminals, tax evaders and vagrants, and above all the hidden road between Rafdonia and the underground city of Noark. Criminals and evil spirits hide here before Noark takes them in. Ghost stories tell of a monster born from Magic Tower waste. During the war the sewers became the border between Noark and Rafdonia: bright red blood floated on the sewage, royal army corpses lay about still wearing expensive gear, and the tunnels shook with tremors like earthquakes.

## Mechanics
- The Administrative Office gives patrollers a map, a fixed route and a worn video recorder. The job is to patrol, with no duty to chase. A fugitive captured alive pays a 5,000-stone bonus; if the video is properly recorded, fugitives may be killed and left behind (ch. 79–80).
- The stench blunts scent tracking. Mages use an anti-smell spell (ch. 80–81).
- The route to Noark: a broker meets you at a sewer entrance, unlocks a grate and leads you for hours to a stone door disguised as a wall. It opens with a magic tool onto stairs going down (ch. 146, 156, 293).
- A secret passage below Bifron's sewers comes out in the 8th District, known only to Bjorn and Jingjing (ch. 195, 203). A hidden passage also links the sewers to the Lord's Castle (ch. 336).
- Tearing the Dragonkin chief's summons paper creates a magic circle on the sewer floor that teleports the holder to the Dragon Temple. Dragon Speech returns him to the same spot (ch. 200–203).
- Noark drew explosion magic circles in the sewers under the Dimensional Plazas (ch. 231).
- Bjorn broke the ceiling with four hammer blows, releasing a torrent that flooded the passage almost to the ceiling for about 5 minutes (ch. 324).
- So many vagrants live here that it is a poor place to hide items (ch. 332).
- A secret passage leads into Viphron even while its protective circle is active. From the Viphron side, a local's shortcut comes up right at the plaza (ch. 629).
- After the invasion, armed royal knights patrol in pairs and fewer vagrants remain. The deeper path leads to the old Noark fortress (ch. 658, 660).
- An exit grate opens onto an outlying street in District 7 (ch. 706).

## Notable events
- ch. 79–84: Team Misfits' sewer patrol catches the fugitive Hans Marcom ("Hans E"). Afterward Bjorn swears never to come back.

- ch. 194–195, 306: Amelia meets Bjorn's group here, gives them Lethe's Blessing, and shows Bjorn the secret passage.
- ch. 323–329: On D-Day, Liuhen Praha cuts off Amelia's right wrist. Bjorn counters Flameless Spirit with a Bond scroll, and Auril Gabis "erases" Jerome Saintred, who will wake at the palace. Laura's death is staged with a ceiling collapse and Gabis's hallucination.
- ch. 629: Noark holds the sewers during the occupation, and many low-rank explorers die breaking through.
- ch. 706–707: Anabada holds its vice-captain election here, and Bersil Gowland wins.

## Description
A spot that is not on the patrol map. A hole about 2 m wide drops into a 5 m pit like a water tank, reached by a creaky ladder. Beyond it, a passage is lined with dozens of mummified corpses drained of life and reeking of rot. The room at the end is furnished like a home: a makeshift bed, a table with leftover bread and coffee, and a candlestick on the wall. Hidden beneath it is a forgotten gate into Noark, abandoned since the "Karui Church incident".

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- The hole is the only entrance (ch. 80).
- Elisa left a Karui trap here, [Call of the Evil God] ("Sabaha"). A click among the corpses sets it off, unleashing hundreds of banshee-like wraiths in one direction. Each hit lowers maximum HP, and reaching 0 means instant death. The trap is fueled by the sacrifices' life force (ch. 81).
- A magically concealed gate lies in the floor, barely detectable with [Mana Detection]. Bjorn smashed it with three mace blows under [Gigantification], revealing a very deep staircase. Karui told Elisa she needed sacrifices to learn how to open it (ch. 81–82).

## Notable events
- ch. 80–81: Team Misfits tracks Hans E here and survives the trap. Noark's watch logs the destruction of the gate.
- ch. 84: The party wakes here after taking the memory pill.

## Description
Twelve food distribution points in Bifron, each with long lines of people, many of them children, waiting for supplies.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- The city's food supplies are handed out at the twelve centres every morning (ch. 200).

## Notable events
- ch. 200: The factions had monopolised the centres. Bjorn orders that minors may take what they need without conditions.
- ch. 629: Supplies are now shared equally between adults and children, and leftover goods are traded to the guards for books.

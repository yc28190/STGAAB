## Description
A towering wall guarded by soldiers who face inward toward Bifron. The checkpoint has a double-door gate that, after long disuse, opens slowly and stiffly with a creak. From the top you see forests, mountains and valleys stretching to the horizon by day, and total darkness under a black sky at night, with the wind howling.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Guards receive antidotes. Leaving requires an identity check (ch. 203).
- The District 4 checkpoint is normally shut; nobody answered even when Bjorn hammered on it and gave his name (ch. 629). It opened once, around noon, for the District 7 explorers, and only permit holders could pass (ch. 630–632).
- The protective circle stops arrows fired outward from the wall (ch. 630).
- Unlike every other wall, the illusion here is broken, so the real landscape shows (ch. 662).

## Notable events
- ch. 193: Bjorn suspects a hidden reason for guarding the wall at all.
- ch. 203: A Moselan knight who escorted Bjorn in gives him a warning on his way out.
- ch. 630: A Rose Knight meets Bjorn on the wall at night with the royal order.
- ch. 631–632: Royal knights line the path as the checkpoint opens. Bjorn makes the residents his vassals so they can pass.

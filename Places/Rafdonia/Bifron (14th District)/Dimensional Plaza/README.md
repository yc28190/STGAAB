## Description
Bifron's old dimensional plaza. It was a ruin when Bjorn first saw it and has long since stopped working. By his later visit it has been cleaned up and children play there.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- The plaza is closed and non-functional (ch. 629).
- Royal intelligence feared the mysterious mage controlling Viphron's circle could reactivate it, which is why the labyrinth was sealed (ch. 645).

## Notable events
- ch. 629: Wounded explorers are treated here.
- ch. 631: More than a thousand people gather at dawn for Bjorn's speech, and the vassal contracts are signed here.

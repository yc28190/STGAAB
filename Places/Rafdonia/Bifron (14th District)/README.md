## Description
A walled-off district that looks like an ordinary neighbourhood left alone for a century. Its run-down buildings have grimy, graffitied walls and rubbish piles up in the streets. Soldiers on top of the wall face inward rather than outward, and at night the streets are completely dark. Later, under the residents' own care, it becomes much cleaner and quietly quaint. From its walls you can see a real landscape of dense forests, mountains and valleys, because the illusion there is broken.

Bifron was once part of Ravigion. It is now a lawless exile district, nicknamed the "Holding Pen", where the royal family sends critics, revolutionaries and other "reactionaries". Residents were officially said to die of the Witch's Poison after the protective magic circle failed. Four gangs once ran it, until Bjorn united them during his exile. Jingkasar Peljain (Jingjing) later runs it fairly. In the Noark invasion it became Noark's true objective. Noark ended up sealed inside it, then holding it as occupied territory, which led to the labyrinth's year-long shutdown.

## Mechanics
- Restricted area; carriages have to go around it (ch. 127).
- There is no law and no guards inside, so abilities can be used freely. Exiles may do anything except try to escape (ch. 192, 195).
- The royal family delivers food monthly; the strong seize it and use it as currency. A meal costs 250 stones, a room 50 (ch. 192). The city sends supplies to 12 distribution centres every morning (ch. 200).
- The Witch's Poison: it does not show before age 15, and after that the chance of it manifesting grows with exposure, so adult exiles live about 8–9 years. Soldiers and short-term exiles take antidotes (ch. 193, 203).
- Children born here may leave only by passing a one-time qualification test at 14 that shows talent, usually as an explorer. Fail it and you stay for life (ch. 192, 630).
- The only other legal way out is to become a noble's vassal. Viphron vassals are executed if they lose that status, pay normal taxes and may not take any job that needs military training, including explorer (ch. 631–633).
- Exits: a double-door checkpoint with identity checks (ch. 203), the District 4 checkpoint, which normally stays shut (ch. 629–631), and a secret passage under the sewers to the 8th district (ch. 195, 203, 629).
- The protective circle actually still works (ch. 630). A mysterious old mage, probably Auril Gavis, took over its permissions and turned it black, sealing Noark inside so the royal family could not break it from outside (ch. 633, 635).
- Royal intelligence feared the mage could reactivate the district's closed Dimensional Plaza, so the labyrinth was sealed (ch. 642, 645). Later the barrier is gone (ch. 706).

## Notable events
- ch. 191–192: Bjorn is exiled for 20 days for using an ability in the city. He sees through a poisoned-meal trap set by the Western Union and makes himself its boss.
- ch. 193–195: He conquers the Western Union in 3 days, then the eastern faction, and merges them into the "Behel-la Union" of over a thousand men with Jingjing and Baldy (Philip Lazer) as vice-leaders.
- ch. 194–200: Amelia searches the district for Auril Gabis.
- ch. 200: Bjorn's reforms: minors eat free, the library is revived, the factions' tyranny ends.
- ch. 216, 355: Named as the birthplace of the Blood Knight of Orculus.
- ch. 248: The visit makes Bjorn doubt the royal account of the world beyond the walls.
- ch. 627–631: Noark's invasion targets Viphron. A Rose Knight orders the explorers out within 12 hours without the residents. Bjorn suspects the royals mean to destroy the district.
- ch. 631–632: Bjorn takes 8,712 residents out as his vassals.
- ch. 633: On the fifth day the ground shakes and the protective circle turns black, closing over the district like a dome.
- ch. 706–715: Noark-held Befron sallies toward Ecliptic Karnon and is beaten back by the royal war machines. Military Command plans an all-out purge.
- ch. 773–789: Noark occupies and fortifies Viphron, causing the year-long labyrinth shutdown. Bjorn takes in its refugees (ch. 783).

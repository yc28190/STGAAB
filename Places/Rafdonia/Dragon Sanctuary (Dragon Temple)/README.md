## Description
The Dragonkin's sacred place. Visitors pass through a gate and climb a long way to the Dragon God temple at its centre. The temple looks Greek, held up by giant pillars, with a throne at the front and cold, smooth marble floors. On Bjorn's first visit a fog behind the throne glinted with dozens of eyes; it turned out to be an illusion and a gust of wind blew it away. The temple has a hall where the chief and six elders receive guests, guest rooms, corridors to the chief's daughters' rooms and caves hidden behind its walls.

It is the seat of the Ancient Dragon, the Dragonkin chieftain Lafir (Lapir; true name Firsearaidormus), older brother of Regal Vagos. The shaman Pen (Penitasauros) is kept here, frozen in time for cursing Regal Vagos. Its exact location relative to the city is never stated, and it may lie outside it.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Normally reached only by teleport through Dragon Speech. A letter or paper from the chief burns or is torn, a blue magic circle appears on the floor and carries the visitor there. Guests are sent home by Dragon Speech (ch. 159–160, 201, 203, 473).
- To ask for another meeting, you tear the message-stone paper so the Dragonkin can locate you (ch. 160).
- The chief uses Dragon Fear to intimidate visitors and can talk telepathically to his daughters anywhere (ch. 160, 475).
- The elders' hearing: each elder asks a question or sets a test (desire, fear, love, a ring puzzle testing insight, kill count, truthfulness) (ch. 201).
- Later, Dragonman guards opened the gate for Bjorn without an identity check because Lapir had told them he was coming (ch. 642).
- Pen's time is stopped; the Dragonslayer's heart is needed to cure her (ch. 425).

## Notable events
- ch. 160: Bjorn turns down money, a Dragonkin teammate for 2 years and next year's Akro equipment, and asks instead for the Dragon's Blessing in return for the Dragonslayer (Dragon Sword).
- ch. 201–203: After the elders' hearing, he receives the Earth Dragon's Blessing in exchange for returning the Dragonslayer, on condition that he brings back Regal Vagos's heart.
- ch. 430: Bjorn keeps the preserved heart as a bargaining chip.
- ch. 474–475: He delivers Regal Vagos's heart and wins the Dragonkin's support in any conflict that does not involve the King. Pen must stay hidden here; Raviyen distances herself from the revenge plan. The Elder Council meets here.
- ch. 642–643: Lapir warns Bjorn of the royal plan to marry him to Ragna and of the coming labyrinth seal. Bjorn sees Pen, who has started growing again, and stays the night.

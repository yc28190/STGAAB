# Blessing Cavern

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | cave |
| **Also known as** | — |
| **First appearance** | ch. 203 |

## Description
A cave behind a thin cloth in the Dragon Temple wall. A few minutes' walk in, it opens into a chamber of about 10 m² with a small bed and a few pieces of furniture. A jewel in the ceiling gives off faint light and smoke, lending the place a mystical air.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- The shaman engraves blessings with a white flame on his finger, after reading the recipient's "wavelength" through conversation (ch. 202–203).
- Blessings: Earth (+20% to stats gained from essences), Volcano (about +30% skill effect), Sea (+100 Soul Power). Non-Dragonkin can receive only one; more could kill them (ch. 202–203).

## Notable events
- ch. 203: Bjorn receives the Earth Dragon's Blessing, engraved on the sole of his foot.

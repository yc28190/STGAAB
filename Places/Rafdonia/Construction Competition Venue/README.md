# Construction Competition Venue

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | arena (stage) |
| **Also known as** | building contest stage |
| **First appearance** | ch. 650 |

## Description
A huge stage surrounded by stands holding tens of thousands of spectators in a festival mood. On the final day the finished buildings stood hidden under giant cloths. Wind and heavy rain swept in at midnight on the third day.

It hosted a royal construction competition between noble families.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Five families take part, each with 100 workers (Yandel brought 90 barbarians and 10 dwarves) (ch. 652).
- The brief is drawn from boxes: four floors, reinforced stone, at least eight windows per floor, residential, theme "Happiness". Three days to build (ch. 652).
- Days 2 and 3 are closed to the public, and touching the work before judging means disqualification (ch. 652–653).
- Scoring: 25% architecture experts, 25% noble jury, 50% from 100 random citizens (ch. 653).

## Notable events
- ch. 653: Yandel builds two identical four-storey "Happy House" studio blocks of 24 units each and places 2nd with 72 points. Count Langston wins with 76; Kiembrota scores 69.
- ch. 653–656: During the ceremony, magic is fired from the stands at Marquis Terserion. Bjorn shields him and Langston with [Scales of Greed] and is burned. Ibaekho and the Doom Scholar are behind the attack.

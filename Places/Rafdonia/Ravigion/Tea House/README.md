# Tea House

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | shop (tea house) |
| **Also known as** | meeting place |
| **First appearance** | ch. 87 |

## Description
A tea house on a main street of Ravigion, with private rooms for noble guests. The owner brews the tea personally.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Baron Martoan visits once a month between 3 and 4 PM for the owner's tea (ch. 87).

## Notable events
- ch. 87–88: Bjorn agrees to accompany Baron Martoan to a Count's banquet in two months and perform feats of strength. In return he gets 1 million stones and the lifting of Dwalky's ban.
- ch. 248–249: Bjorn meets his "regular friends" Shabin Emure and Ragna. Shabin gives him the Administrative Office's delivery job, a letter to Baron Nertobin.
- ch. 292: In the past, Bjorn persuades Raven's mother here to send Raven to the Magic Tower.

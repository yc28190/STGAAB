## Description
A five-story building that reminds Bjorn of the old Seoul Station. The Facility Management Department is on the 3rd floor, where every employee wears the same uniform and most of the staff seem to be women. It is the city's bureaucracy, and it hands out official requests to vetted explorers.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Request fees and capture bonuses are paid on a signed payment confirmation (ch. 85).
- Administrative requests go only to vetted explorers and raise an explorer's credibility (ch. 79).

## Notable events
- ch. 79–85: Shabin Emuer of Facility Management gives Bjorn the sewer patrol request. It pays 180,000 stones including the Hans Marcom bonus (conflicting accounts: 200,000 is also quoted).
- ch. 452–453: Shabin's faction loses the Director election. She and colleagues from Civil Engineering and Civilian Support are fired, and Bjorn hires them.

## Description
The familiar gray city of paved roads and stone buildings, overlooked by the towering black spire of the Magic Tower. The main streets near the plaza are safe and decent. The back alleys are poorly policed, with old buildings split into dozens of rooms and wary residents who avoid eye contact; a home there can be no more than a door in a wall. On parade days the streets fill with cheering crowds and flower petals.

Ravigion (also spelled Lavigion) is the name for Rafdonia's numbered residential districts, the commoners' city where more than half the kingdom's people live. Explorers and business owners live near the central plaza. It contains the Dimension Plaza, the Magic Tower, the Administrative Office and Bjorn's inn in the 7th District. Bifron (Viphron) was once its District 14. It counts as a pricey area by explorer standards.

## Mechanics
- Viphron used to be District 14 of Lavigion (ch. 629–630).
- The Administrative Office hands out official requests to vetted explorers, and completing them raises an explorer's credibility (ch. 79).

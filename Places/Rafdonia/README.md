## Description
A gigantic gray fortress city-state, ringed by towering walls so long that their ends cannot be seen; from the air they look like prison bars, and from the outside it takes about two days on foot to reach them from the nearest high ground. Inside are well-kept roads and stone buildings, most several storeys tall, with a spire dominating the skyline (the view from the game's loading screen). The sky is usually gloomy and gray, only a crescent moon appears at night, and winters bring snow and cold winds. The city feels oddly modern: inns have running water, officials work quickly, and there is a bank, a vast underground water and sewer system, night markets and shared ten-seat carriages (250 stones a ride, double for barbarians).

Rafdonia is the last stronghold of the six races (humans, dwarves, fairies/elves, barbarians, beastmen and dragonkin), ruled by a king and a powerful nobility. It is divided into 14 walled districts: the 1st is Karnon, the Imperial City; the 2nd–5th are Commelby, the Free Market; the 6th is Gnometree; the 14th is Bifron, once part of Ravigion. Lacking rivers and farmland, the city lives on magic stones mined in the labyrinth, which also serve as the basis of its currency. Officially, a witch's curse made the world outside deadly thousands of years ago and the royal family dug a passage into another dimension (the labyrinth) to feed the survivors; this story later proves false. The ancient underground city of Noark lies beneath it.

## Mechanics
- The labyrinth opens at midnight on the 1st of each month; a month is exactly 30 days. However long explorers spend inside, only one day passes in the city, and they return at noon the next day to the Dimensional Plaza (ch. 15–16, 19, 146).
- Explorers returning pass a checkpoint where magic stones are exchanged for money; 99.9% of currency enters this way, and 1 stone equals one stone's worth of mana (ch. 384, 608).
- Taxes begin at age 20 (about 700,000 stones the first year, 800,000 plus exchange fee the second); failure to pay is punished by public execution (ch. 3, 18).
- Using essence abilities in the city is illegal (fine about 300,000 stones if no one is hurt); duels are the only permitted personal fights and require a notary (ch. 17, 174, 177).
- Evil spirits (people from Earth) are executed when discovered (ch. 74–75).
- Every district has a Dimensional Plaza except Bifron, whose plaza is gone (ch. 192).
- A protective magic circle, legacy of the Great Sage Gavrilius, covers the city; it is invisible from inside, stops physical objects at the walls, blocks messages almost like a dimensional cutoff, and appears from outside as a touchable transparent barrier. It is broken in Bifron (ch. 193, 630, 661).
- Illusion magic on the walls shows a false view to anyone looking out; the Viphron wall is the exception (ch. 662).
- A royal magic circle can cut the racial sacred sites off from the city (ch. 623).
- Calendar of the Change Era: 13 months of 30 days plus a 5-day 13th month of festivals; the last labyrinth cycle of the year lasts 35 days (ch. 231).
- There is no burial land left; commoners are cremated (ch. 774).

## Notable events
- ch. 43–45: Rumors of a "Barbarian of Liberty" spread after Bjorn's guild incident.
- ch. 173–176: The royal family launches a campaign to wipe out Noark; only about a third of the force returns, three Top Ten clans are destroyed and many smaller clans disband.
- ch. 191: The Moselan arrest Bjorn for using abilities in the city; he is exiled to Bifron for 20 days.
- ch. 231–233: Pillars of fire erupt in all seven Dimensional Plazas, gutting the high-ranking explorer population.
- ch. 338–342: Two and a half years after Bjorn's "death", the royal family has declared him an evil spirit; the royal army fights Noark on the 7th floor.
- ch. 384: Knights arrest "Lihen Schuitz" for colluding with Noark.
- ch. 622–633: Noark invades through the sewers, taking the 7th and 13th districts and holding explorers hostage; the royal army retakes them.
- ch. 647–654: The labyrinth is sealed after a royal meeting; prices collapse and protesting explorers are arrested.
- ch. 661–662: Bjorn sees the city from outside for the first time and learns the witch's-poison story is false; erosion from the gray world is projected to reach the District 10 walls in about 10 years.
- ch. 774–789: After the war, a large 13th Month festival is held amid a housing crisis; taxes are temporarily waived.

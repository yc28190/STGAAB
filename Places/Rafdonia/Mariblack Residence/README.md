## Description
A noble residence with an assembly hall and private rooms, where guests are served Rafdonia-style iced tea. It hosts the regular gatherings of Melbes (Melves), the Multiracial Nobles' Alliance.

## Notable events
- ch. 634–636: Bjorn objects to Goldbeard winning the rebuilding bid and taunts the dwarf about his height. Chairman Takuo Wellbeart (black bear, Welbait marquisate) shouts them down and sets up a building contest. Bjorn reveals that he holds a seat on the royal council.
- ch. 637: In private, the chairman offers Bjorn the leadership of Melbes on condition that he marry and have heirs.

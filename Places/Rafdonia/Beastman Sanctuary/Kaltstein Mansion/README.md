## Description
A huge, elegant mansion with a gate and a bell for calling servants, a colourful, well-tended garden and a sparkling fountain. Inside is a large ground-floor lobby with a central staircase up to the 2nd floor, and the master's study. To Bjorn the house gives off a dark malice, like a dungeon full of monsters.

It is the home of the Kaltstein family, chieftains of the Red Cat tribe: Albreniv Kaltstein (the head, a former 8th-floor explorer with [Lord of Nightmares]), Tailon Kaltstein, Misha's siblings and the assistant Veros. Misha grew up here and was bullied as a "half-breed".

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Guarded by a gatekeeper and about fifteen armed beastman guards led by a bald man, with many servants (ch. 128).

## Notable events
- ch. 128–129: Bjorn knocks out the gatekeeper Brante and forces a promise that Misha will be accepted.
- ch. 129–130: Albreniv turns out to be after the Frost Spirit Ring, having found a torn page about Skadi in his "Compendium of Artifacts VI".
- ch. 130: Misha returns alone and tells Albreniv she knows she is not his child.
- ch. 204: Misha is called back for the funeral of a sick older brother. Her father asks to see Bjorn again, but she refuses.

## Description
The beastmen's home outside the city walls, next to the 13th district. It is nothing like the Barbarian Sanctuary: mostly stone buildings, proper roads, open shops and beastman families living there. In effect it is a beastman village outside the city.

In the game, set about 150 years earlier, the beastmen here raised animals in the forest to keep their traditions alive. It is the seat of the Kaltstein family, hereditary chieftains of the Red Cat tribe, and Misha's hometown.

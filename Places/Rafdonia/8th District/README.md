## Description
A district of Rafdonia next to the 7th District, across the wall from it, with clean streets. From its carriage platform one goes straight, turns left at the candy store and takes the right fork at the statue of the Immortal King to reach the Central Plaza about 5 minutes later. Early on, Bjorn's inn (with a 1st-floor dining room) and Misha's inn were here, and it later hosted the Huntsman Clan's headquarters.

During the war the district, held by the rebels, was garrisoned by corpse armies; Bjorn's party turned it into a sea of fire, with whole streets ablaze, burning corpses and thick smoke. Afterwards it was completely incinerated, and the stench of smoke hung over the whole city even during the festival.

## Monsters
- Corpse army — stationed throughout the district by the rebels; burned as fuel
- Modified corpses — part of the Corpse Collector's forces defending the district

## Mechanics
- Exit point of the secret passage from Bifron (ch. 203).
- Raven spread the fire with [Water Cannon], converted the water to oil with [Element Conversion], and fanned it with [Wind Direction Control]; the corpses served as fuel (ch. 729, 732).
- Bjorn's Fire Orb halves fire damage within 15 m; the smoke, not the flames, nearly suffocated him (ch. 729, 738).
- The royal family never burned it because District 8 is not strategic and nothing would be left to retake (ch. 729).

## Notable events
- ch. 72–74: Bjorn and Misha lodge at inns here.
- ch. 376: The Huntsman Clan, former 6th-floor pirates, has its headquarters here.
- ch. 726: Ravigion territory, defended by the Blood Knights, Black Claw, the Witch of Screams and the Corpse Collector; royal attempts to retake it fail.
- ch. 729–733: Bjorn burns the district to draw attention from Amelia in District 4, taunting Ricardo Luhenpraha over Vivian's radio; the Marquis contacts him on the same channel. Bjorn and Astarotta fight about twenty Orculus elites under field commander Nortia, plus the Corpse Collector.
- ch. 738: Returning from the Underworld, Bjorn runs over half an hour through smoke to reach a wall.
- ch. 775–788: The district lies in ashes after the war; restoration is planned.

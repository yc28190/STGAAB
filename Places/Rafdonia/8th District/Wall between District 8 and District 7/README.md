# Wall between District 8 and District 7

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | building (city wall) |
| **Also known as** | city wall |
| **First appearance** | ch. 738 |

## Description
A tall brick wall separating the 8th and 7th Districts. From its top the whole city can be seen on a clear day. During the war its bricks were heated by the fire and black smoke rose along it; from it Bjorn saw District 8 entirely ablaze and more than a third of District 7 burning.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Bjorn climbed the hot bricks by their cracks rather than looking for a gate, then ran along the top toward the Sanctuary, a shorter and safer route than the burning streets (ch. 738).
- The wall continues to the outer perimeter of the Sanctuary, where one can jump down into the forest (ch. 743).

## Notable events
- ch. 738–740: A black-wolf-masked Hirkmuta member sent by Auril Gavis waits here and warns that a comrade will die whether Bjorn goes to the Sanctuary or to District 7. Bjorn tears off his mask, revealing an ordinary beastman in his mid-thirties, and throws him into burning District 7.

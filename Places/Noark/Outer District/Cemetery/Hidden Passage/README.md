## Description
A secret passage beneath a giant tombstone in Noark's cemetery, wide and tall enough for a carriage. It runs as a dark, narrow tunnel walked by torchlight, then widens into a slope that climbs a hill to the surface; a branch leads into the Lafdonia sewers.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Used by the Melta Trading Company to travel between Noark and the surface, guarded by a resident cemetery caretaker (ch. 322–324).
- In the present day it was unknown even to most of Noark, but the mysterious old man knew the route (ch. 256).

## Notable events
- ch. 256: Tens of thousands of Noark residents escape the royal raid in single file behind the old man, followed by the Lord and the captain of Orculus.
- ch. 322–324: On D-Day the caretaker is found cut down; Derbon's group and the Rainwales sisters flee through it.

## Description
A desolate, gloomy field of tombstones in Noark's outer district, with the Lord's Castle visible in the distance. A giant tombstone conceals a secret passage to the surface.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Hides the secret passage to the surface used by the Melta Trading Company (ch. 322, 339).

## Notable events
- ch. 322: Bjorn crash-lands here, breaking several tombstones.
- ch. 323: Auril Gavis walks out of the passage.
- ch. 329–330: The young Amelia wakes up alone here after Laura's staged "death" and is taken into the city by guards; the Fragment of Records activates and Auril Gavis vanishes, leaving his robe.

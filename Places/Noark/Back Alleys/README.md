## Description
Narrow alleys of Noark that lie outside the sight of the Lord's patrol golems. Residents watch what happens from behind closed curtains without intervening.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Looters who prey on newcomers lure or follow them into these alleys, where the golems cannot see (ch. 293).

## Notable events
- ch. 293–294: Fifteen looters corner Bjorn and Amelia in a dead end and are wiped out; Bjorn takes a Laetium chestplate set, and the loot is worth about 70 million stones on the surface.

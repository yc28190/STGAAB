# Labyrinth Management Office

| | |
|---|---|

## Description
Noark's administrative office for labyrinth expeditions, located in the outer castle of the Lord's Castle, with a separate branch in the inner castle. Inside it resembles the Explorer's Guild on the surface, with long, slow lines at the counters.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Newcomers must register, and an entrance fee is charged every time someone enters the labyrinth (ch. 294).
- There is no queue-number system and service is slow; staff do not stop fights in the lines (ch. 294–295).
- The office casts the Bonding spell before an expedition; rank adjustment is optional (ch. 296).
- The inner-castle office serves only the Lord's faction: team registration, blocking membership applications, and a roster of "runners" (13 available of 173) (ch. 304).

## Notable events
- ch. 295: Bjorn beats up "Rex's gang" in the line and takes their gear, including a subspace ring.
- ch. 304: Bjorn and Amelia register their team "Iron Mask" with official Mel's help.

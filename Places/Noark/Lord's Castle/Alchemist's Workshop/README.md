## Description
A workshop on the restricted 3rd floor of the Lord's Castle, resembling a mage's lab but not quite the same. Its owner, the alchemist Marpa Ipaello, turns magic stones into bread, iron and other materials; Noark's food supply depends on its only two alchemists. Ipaello is guarded by invisible escorts with stealth essences.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Reached through the restricted 3rd floor of the Lord's Castle (ch. 316).
- Holds the prototype of Lethe's Blessing: twice normal size, erases nearly all memories and too costly to mass-produce; only one remained in a box with over ten slots (ch. 317).

## Notable events
- ch. 316–317: Bjorn meets Ipaello, inventor of the memory pill, and names it "Lethe's Blessing".
- ch. 321: On D-Day the other, bad-tempered alchemist is caught stealing here; Bjorn kills him and takes the prototype, and Jerome Saintred finds Bjorn in the workshop.

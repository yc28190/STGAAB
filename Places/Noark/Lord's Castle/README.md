# Lord's Castle

## Description
The fortress at the center of Noark, visible from as far away as the outer district's cemetery. It has an outer castle and an inner castle and at least four floors; from a 4th-floor window the whole city can be seen. By day its giant gate stands open to crowds of workers.

The castle is the seat of the Lord and the center of Noark's economy: most jobs in the city are here, and those who do not enter the labyrinth work for pay. It contains the Labyrinth Management Office, the Dimensional Plaza, clan houses such as the Barker clan's, the Rainwales bar, the alchemist's workshop and the ancient shelter. The Lord keeps a knight order (e.g. Sir Rick Omanus).

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- The outer castle handles administration; the inner castle requires permission and is open to explorers of the Lord's faction (ch. 294, 304).
- Joining the Lord's faction grants support, protection and the right to go up to Lafdonia after enough contributions, in exchange for obeying orders in emergencies (ch. 304).
- The 3rd floor is restricted (alchemist's area) (ch. 316).
- The Dimensional Plaza is in the western part of the castle (ch. 302).
- A secret passage links the castle to the Lafdonia sewers / surface (ch. 336, 339–340).
- Only about 1 in 100 Noark knights can use Aura (ch. 318).

## Notable events
- ch. 318: On D-Day the gate is locked; Bjorn leaps the wall with [Leap] and crushes the knights on the inner castle's 1st floor with a spinning "Whirlwind" attack.
- ch. 339–340: At its door Erwen's [Rupture] arrow shatters Amelia's clone; Erwen and Bjorn then escape to the surface through the secret passage.

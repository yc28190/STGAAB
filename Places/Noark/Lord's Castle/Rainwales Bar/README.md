# Rainwales Bar

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | shop (bar) |
| **Also known as** | [Rainwales] |
| **First appearance** | ch. 304 |

## Description
A bar in the inner castle owned by the Rainwales sisters' foster father, a man of about 170 cm with an ordinary face. The interior is clean, with corner tables and a kitchen at the back; it is quiet in the daytime and busier after noon. The liquor is strong and tastes bad. The 13-year-old Amelia waits tables here while her foster father beats the sisters in the kitchen as regulars look on.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Located inside the inner castle and accessible as a perk for explorers with inner-castle access (ch. 304).
- In Noark, orphans are taken in by foster parents as cheap labor, and the foster parents hold absolute rights over them (ch. 305).

## Notable events
- ch. 305: Bjorn and Amelia see the young Amelia working and being abused here.
- ch. 315: The Barker clan holds after-parties here.
- ch. 320: The sisters are working here when they are called up on D-Day.

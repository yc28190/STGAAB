## Description
Wide stairs lead down from the inner castle into a passage broad enough for twenty people side by side, ending at a giant stone door. Beyond lies a vast chamber that can shelter thousands. It was built by the royal family long ago, when the fortress city was a royal palace and humanity lived underground during the Witch's curse, gradually pushing the barrier back until it could return to the surface.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Almost no one knew of it in the past timeline; Amelia knew from the future, and Jerome Saintred had been told about it beforehand (ch. 318, 321).
- According to the Lord, the Fragment of Records cannot be "used": it activates on its own when the Chosen One appears and returns the traveler once his task in the past is done (ch. 319).

## Notable events
- ch. 256: The Lord hides the citizens here during the royal raid.
- ch. 319: Bjorn kills the knight Rick Omanus on the stairs, then takes the Fragment of Records from the Lord and kills him; history credits Orculus with the Lord's death.
- ch. 321: Jerome Saintred arrives about 30 minutes later and finds the bodies.

# Barker Clan House

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | building (clan house) |
| **Also known as** | clan house |
| **First appearance** | ch. 312 |

## Description
The headquarters of Pelic Barker's 13-member clan, located in the Lord's Castle. It is stylishly decorated and smells of alcohol, with rough-looking members drinking and smoking on sofas; it hardly feels like a criminal hideout. The young Rainwales sisters served the clan as runners.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- An empty lot behind the house hosts "contests" between members: no essence abilities, and forcing the opponent to surrender is enough to win (ch. 312).
- The clan has three ranks below the leader, and loot is shared according to rank (ch. 312).
- Refusing an offered drink signals distrust (ch. 312).

## Notable events
- ch. 312–316: Bjorn wins three contests on his first day, becomes 3rd Team Leader, and Derbon ("Dumbo") becomes his right-hand man.

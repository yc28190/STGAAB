# Eastern District

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | district |
| **Also known as** | Eastern District of Noark; Orculus's territory |
| **First appearance** | ch. 304 |

## Description
The part of Noark controlled by Orculus, which grew increasingly independent from the Lord; outsiders know little about it. On D-Day it was a scene of flames and explosions, with smoke covering the city, collapsed buildings and mangled bodies at its edge.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Serves as Orculus's headquarters (ch. 316, 320).

## Notable events
- ch. 320–321: On D-Day the Lord and royal knights attack; hundreds of the Lord's explorers blockade the entrance and kill fleeing residents for a bounty per ear. Ricardo Liuhen Praha comes out alone and slaughters them. Amelia carries a paralyzed Pelic Barker to him so that Flameless Spirit's [Unfair Contract] is cancelled before he dies.

## Description
A giant stone gate, larger than the gate of the Lafdonia royal palace and visible from far across the cavern, standing at the end of the underground passages leading to Noark. It is engraved with dozens of ancient spells. A smaller side door beside it is used for individuals.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Newcomers show the badge from their broker to the gatekeeper, Rek (Rex) Aures, one of the strongest people in the city, who engraves their alias on it and admits them through the side door (ch. 293).
- During the first royal subjugation the gate took hours to break; during the second it was only half-repaired and royal mages blew it open with an octagonal magic circle (ch. 252).

## Notable events
- ch. 252: Royal mages blast the gate open with a wave of pure mana.
- ch. 293: Bjorn and Amelia enter Noark through the side door.
- ch. 322: Fleeing explorers avoid the gate on D-Day because of the gatekeeper.

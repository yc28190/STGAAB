## Description
A long staircase leads down from the sewers into a vast cavern with a ceiling over 6 m high. A weathered, unrecognizable stone statue stands before a passage, beyond which lies a maze of straight, narrow corridors that fork repeatedly (one fork splits into five). Ceiling heights and wall layouts vary, making it feel like a three-dimensional maze; water drips somewhere in the bottomless dark. It forms the approach to Noark.

## Monsters
- [Shadow Guards](../../../Bestiary/shadow_guard_ch55.md) — summoned by Elisa during the fight in the maze
- Gate of the Dead — Elisa's summons

## Mechanics
- No one knows why it was built this way; even Elisa got lost for hours (ch. 82).
- The narrow passages make [Gigantification] awkward (ch. 82).
- Royal knight patrols do not come past this point (ch. 658).

## Notable events
- ch. 82–83: Bjorn's party catches Elisa here; Amelia Rainwales then ambushes them with basilisk paralysis needles and [Self-Replication].
- ch. 658: Bjorn passes through again and recalls the fight.

## Description
A huge cavern beneath the sewers of Rafdonia (Lafdonia), so large it is hard to believe it is underground. The city is built of plain, featureless stone buildings along unpaved roads and is enclosed by castle walls patrolled by guards; the Lord's Castle stands at its center. Light comes from magic-tool stones set in the ceiling that burn magic stones, giving the city a day and a night but no dawn or dusk. Ventilation is poor, so a fire fills the whole city with smoke. In its heyday the streets were surprisingly clean and the residents did not look downtrodden; after the royal army's occupation and Noark's flight the city lies deserted under a thick layer of dust, eerily silent.

Originally an ancient shelter dating from the era of the Witch's curse, when the fortress was a royal palace and humanity lived underground, Noark was later settled by the Olmi (Ormy) Revolutionary Army after a failed rebellion. It became a haven for criminals, outlaws and evil spirits who cannot live in Rafdonia, and is the headquarters of the criminal group Orculus. It is ruled by a hereditary Lord whose bloodline is needed to operate the city's private labyrinth portals; through them Noark explorers secretly mined magic stones and later preyed on other explorers inside the labyrinth.

## Mechanics
- Entrances are hidden "gates" deep in the sewers, most of them guarded by Watch members; Amelia once disarmed a tripwire-like detection device at the entrance (ch. 81, 658).
- Outsiders who discover the city are not allowed to leave knowing about it; Noark's alchemists make Lethe's Blessing (also called Curse of Lethe), a pill that erases recent memories. It does not work on evil spirits, and Bjorn's soul rejects it (ch. 83–84, 146, 148, 247).
- Noark has its own portals into the labyrinth, which require someone of the Lord's bloodline to operate (ch. 146, 177, 336).
- After the failed royal attack the Lord activated an ancient sealing magic circle: a giant blue mana barrier closed the city for about two years, causing a severe food shortage. A secret floor passage led outside (ch. 176, 198, 205, 213).
- The Magic Tower found a way to break the barrier using 30 High Priests; it was broken at midnight on 1 June, sending a small earthquake to the surface, and the final engraved stone gate was blown open with a giant octagonal magic circle (ch. 238, 247, 252).
- City rules (20 years in the past): newcomers enter through a broker and the gate; patrol golems of the Lord walk the streets; nobody uses real names, only aliases on badges; harming Noark-born residents is taboo, but newcomers are fair game in the back alleys (ch. 293).
- Economy: magic stones are the currency; food is a few standardized alchemical items; special orders take about two days and cost far more than on the surface; looted gear sells at about a third of surface value (ch. 293–294, 302).
- Evil spirits are openly accepted. Children ("runners") are raised by looter teams and used as bait and labor, later absorbing essences; there is a foster-parent system (ch. 296–298, 303, 305).
- Secret passages lead to the surface from the Lord's Castle and from the cemetery (ch. 339).
- An ancient circle in the city once teleported tens of thousands of Noark troops at once; a mysterious mage (Auril Gavis) also opened a portal to the 7th floor outside the fortress walls (ch. 597, 642, 645).

- ch. 81–84: Elisa, guided by the evil god Karui, flees toward the hidden city; Amelia Rainwales ambushes Bjorn's party, kills Elisa and tries to wipe their memories.
- ch. 145–148: The Dragonslayer emerges in Noark through Karui's dimensional gate; Amelia robs him and erases his memory.
- ch. 175–176: A royal attack on Noark fails with heavy losses; the Lord seals the city with a barrier and sends agents including Amelia to the surface.
- ch. 178–185: Noark explorers using stolen ID tags ambush explorers in the labyrinth.
- ch. 247–262: The royal family breaks the barrier and the royal army under Jerome Saintred occupies the fortress; the citizens are hidden and evacuated.
- ch. 285–336: Bjorn and Amelia travel 20 years into the past and live in Noark under aliases. On D-Day the Lord, allied with the royal family, attacks Orculus; Bjorn kills the Lord and takes the Fragment of Records. A new Lord kills his relatives and makes a truce with Orculus. The pair return to their own time in a flash of light.
- ch. 337–340: Bjorn wakes in the deserted present-day city; Erwen pursues Amelia there and they escape through the Lord's Castle passage.
- ch. 342, 386: Noark now operates from the 7th floor through a portal outside the walls; Bjorn is arrested on a charge of "colluding with Noark".
- ch. 502: Noark kills the 8th-floor Floor Lord.
- ch. 573, 623: The Noark faction settles beyond the walls and later returns to invade Rafdonia.
- ch. 658–659: Bjorn's group returns to the abandoned city through the sewers, accompanied by former gatekeeper Rek Aures.
- ch. 767–773: The Lord of Noark leads forces on the surface; after losing the Marquis, Urae and District 4, Noark withdraws into a forced truce.

# Melta Trading Company

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | shop (trading company) |
| **Also known as** | — |
| **First appearance** | ch. 302 |

## Description
The only trading company in Noark, a crowded branch staffed by several appraisers, where looters sell their spoils. It is practically owned by the Lord and is the city's commercial link to the surface.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- Buys loot far below surface prices: Bjorn's gear was appraised at 21 4th-grade, 1 5th-grade and 7 7th-grade magic stones (21,570,000 stones) against a surface value over 70 million (ch. 302).
- Uses a secret passage under the cemetery through the sewers to trade with the surface (ch. 322).

## Notable events
- ch. 316: Pelic Barker uses the company to spread the rumor on the surface that barbarian hearts are valuable magic material.

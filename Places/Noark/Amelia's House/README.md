# Amelia's House

| | |
|---|---|

## Description
A small mansion in Noark, furnished only with the basics. The alchemist gave it to the Rainwales sisters during the period 20 years before the main story.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
None recorded.

## Notable events
- ch. 336: The adult Amelia visits her younger self here and makes her promise never to kill barbarians, sealing it by squeezing hands three times. Just outside the door, Bjorn and Amelia return to the future in a flash of light.

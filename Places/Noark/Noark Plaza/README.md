# Noark Plaza

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | district (plaza) |
| **Also known as** | portal plaza; underground plaza; Noark Underground Fortress |
| **First appearance** | ch. 256 |

## Description
The central plaza of Noark, where a blue portal opens at noon to return explorers from the labyrinth. Once crowded with marauders, it later stands empty and eerily silent, without even a rat. Beneath it lies an ancient teleportation (return) magic circle.

## Monsters
None recorded.

## Mechanics
- An ancient magic circle under the city floor let a mysterious old man (Auril Gavis) freeze all fighting as if by a spatial rule and teleport tens of thousands of people at once (ch. 256).
- The hidden multi-dimensional teleport circle leads to the Return Circle Corridor. Versil could not solve it, but the GM worked out the activation formula in under an hour; a 4th-rank mage can use it (ch. 659–660).
- The chant Ibaekho gave Bjorn to activate it was a prank and does nothing (ch. 659).
- The circle works for leaving, but the return route was broken until repaired; while the royal army occupied the plaza it had to be activated in secret (ch. 693, 702).

## Notable events
- ch. 256: Thousands of royal knights, soldiers, mages and priests ambush returning Noark explorers; the old man halts Jerome's sword and teleports all of Noark into a hidden passage.
- ch. 659–660: Bjorn's group finds the plaza empty and uses the circle.
- ch. 693–706: Anabada secretly activates the circle after Bjorn disappears; when they return, the royal army has left.

# Bjorn Yandel: stat reconstruction

Back to the main page: [bjorn_yandel.md](bjorn_yandel.md)

## 1. Summary

Bjorn grows his stats from five sources. The first is **essences**: each essence gives fixed stat lines, one or more passives and one active. Essence slots equal his level. The second is the **Spirit Engraving**, which follows the barbarian *Undying Imprint / Undying Engraving* path. The third is **levels**, which raise Soul Power and the slot count. The fourth is the **Earth Dragon's Blessing**, which multiplies essence stats. The last is a long tail of **achievements and permanent consumables**. His build target is the "shield barbarian": a tank built around [Gigantification], [Unification], [Transcendence] and very high resistances.

The novel shows full status windows only up to ch. 203, plus two windows in ch. 446 and ch. 449. After level 8, Bellarios and the customized essence, no window appears, so everything after that is rebuilt from individual system messages. Values below come straight from the system messages in the chapters. Anything marked **(computed)** is a sum we worked out ourselves.

## 4. Reconstructed stat totals

### 4.1 Combat Index formula (computed)
Every full window we checked fits:

> **Total Combat Index = Physical + Mental + Ability + Item Level ÷ 4**

It matches exactly at ch. 2, 5, 8, 21, 22, 25, 152, 187, 446 and 449. From ch. 34 to ch. 100 it runs 3–4 points high, which points to the displayed Mental values (see §5). The "(New +x)" deltas are measured against the live value just before the event, not the previous window. That is why ch. 21 shows +67.25 (item level 17 → 202 after the ch. 18 sales) while the windows differ by only 52.5.

## 5. Discrepancies and gaps

1. **ch. 2:** the item-level log says +12, the window says +24 (two starting items).
2. **ch. 21:** Body +21 shown against the engraving's +20, and Mind 36 against 37 at ch. 8.
3. **ch. 34–35:** Mind 39 → 46 is +7 but shown as +10. The Combat Index implies Mind 43. From ch. 34 to ch. 100 every index is 3 points (4 from ch. 69) below P+M+A+IL/4, so the displayed Mental seems inflated.
4. **ch. 35:** the index delta "+204" leaves out the −104 item-level loss; the windows actually differ by 178.
5. **ch. 69:** Mental 91 → 104 is +13, shown as +12. The Orc Hero stat list is cut off ("……"), and so is the ch. 624 removal log.
6. **ch. 89:** the level-4 window is cut off ("Bjorn Yan……") even in the source. The level-up only shows in the ch. 100 window (Ability +13 = +10 engraving + 3 level).
7. **ch. 127:** Physical **360** shown against **410 computed** (380 + 30). The index of 941 fits neither (925 expected from the ch. 100 delta, and 929 or 879 from the formula). There is no item-level line.
8. **ch. 152:** the deltas (+225 / +44 / +60) fit a baseline of 330 / 154 / 128, not the ch. 127 window (360 / 184). Item level 98 (−770): the 868 baseline is 40 above the last shown 828, which the ch. 59 and 89 gear (+45, +38, +12, with replacements) roughly explains.
9. **ch. 187 vs ch. 203:** ch. 187 shows Physical/Mental **560 / 213**, but ch. 203 shows **555 / 198** (the ch. 152 values) while its index (+176.6 over ch. 187) assumes higher totals. P+M+A = 1,140.6 by the formula. The ch. 203 window likely reprinted stale numbers. Ch. 187 also raises Ability 188 → 191 with no delta.
10. **ch. 209 vs ch. 556 Magic Resistance:** ch. 209 counts the Engraving stage 2 +20; ch. 556 drops it and adds the 8 from potions. Neither counts Ogre's All Resistances +10. Our sum with the engraving gives 523.9, against the novel's 497.9.
11. **Manticore permanent gain:** ch. 349 gives no number. The +40 comes from the 20% rule (ch. 183) and is confirmed only by the ch. 556 sum.
12. **ch. 446:** the pre-removal index (5,680.75 + 575.58 = 6,256.33) is 16.08 above P+M+A+IL/4 built from the deltas. The −575.58 delta is larger than −54.5 Physical plus −2,020/4. The −2,020 item-level drop is gear that no longer fits his larger body.
13. **ch. 449 item level:** the re-equipped pieces add up to **+3,045**, but the window shows only **+1,695** (6,285 → 7,980). Replaced pieces probably account for the gap, but the system does not itemize this.
14. **Truncated lists:** Corpse Golem (absorption and removal logs), Orc Hero (absorption and removal), Stormgush (absorption), Gachabon removal ("Accuracy −45…"; Bone Density, Control and Luck do appear afterward), the vampire sheet in ch. 44 (block missing from the source), and the ch. 427 debuff list.
15. **Vampire stats:** the absorption log has 4 lines (Natural Regeneration +40). The essence page, from the ch. 44 sheet, lists 9 lines with Natural Regeneration +45 (the translator's note calls it a raw typo).
16. **No windows** after ch. 449: nothing for level 8 (ch. 529), Bellarios (ch. 555), the customized essence (ch. 625), Engraving stage 8 (ch. 784) or the Bion corruption (ch. 803). Unquantified sources include Engraving "greatly increased" stats, the Fragment of Earth count, Ring of Circulation gains with no message, and Soul of Wildness passive threat.
17. **Main page timeline:** [bjorn_yandel.md](bjorn_yandel.md) places Undying Imprint stage 7 at ch. 660–680. The source has "Undead Engraving 7th stage activated" in **ch. 392**. The ch. 660–680 range is the stage-8 material hunt (stage 8 at ch. 784).
